### The pre-existing failure — record it, do not inherit blame for it

```
FAILED tests/test_ibkr_stream.py::
       test_read_only_connect_skips_the_startup_account_sync_when_ib_async_offers_it

assert seen["fetchFields"] is not None …
E   assert (None is not None)
```

`read_only_connect` does not pass `fetchFields` even when the underlying
`connect` signature accepts it. The test's intent is to avoid TWS error 322
(account-summary quota exhaustion) on a flapping link — a robustness measure,
not a data-correctness one. **It was failing at `5a6c4cc`, before any merge.**

If this failure is still present after the merge, that is the baseline, not a
regression. If it disappears, something changed that should be explained.

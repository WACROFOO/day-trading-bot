# What time are the classes held? Can I attend even if I'm international or busy during class times?

All of our courses are digitally recorded and available for streaming 24/7 from our site, so that members can study when it works for them and their schedules. We have members all over the world, with families, jobs and other commitments and we realize that not everyone can be around at the same time to learn. All you need to log into the classes is a decent internet connection for streaming the videos. The courses are both computer- and mobile-friendly. Courses cannot be downloaded, but we do encourage members to download the course PDFs that are available for each lesson, and keep these for personal use.

Our live trading room does currently run live during market hours, but anyone who is unable to observe and participate can catch up through the daily recaps that we post on YouTube: [https://www.youtube.com/user/DaytradeWarrior/playlists](https://www.youtube.com/user/DaytradeWarrior/playlists)

The stock market is open from 9:30 am until 4 pm EST. Scanners are available starting at 4 am EST and we open the chat at 7 am EST so that traders can build watch lists with us and prepare for the day. For more information on what trading strategies might fit best for your schedule, check out this article here: [https://www.warriortrading.com/find-your-niche-when-and-what-to-trade/](https://www.warriortrading.com/find-your-niche-when-and-what-to-trade/) 

The live mentor sessions that are available for purchase as part of the Warrior Pro membership run up to 5 days per week in our online classroom. These mentor sessions are also recorded so that members who cannot attend the live sessions can still participate, and all Pro members can re-watch sessions afterwards.

And of course, our Customer Support Team is happy to help answer any questions that cannot be asked during live sessions. Some of our trading educators and mentors assist on our support team, so our members receive high quality support in both live and email settings. Our Support Room is open for real-time technical support for members in the chat room platform during market hours.

Our trading simulator does run on real-time data, so it is not available for trading outside of market hours. For members in our course programs looking for an option to trade when the US market is closed, we recommend looking at Thinkorswim's onDemand feature which offers playback data for simulated trading.

Our community is known primarily for its day trading strategies, but we do offer swing trading and options trading courses as a supplement to our day trading classes in our Warrior Pro membership.

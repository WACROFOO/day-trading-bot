# How can I get mentoring?

Mentoring is provided at Warrior Trading in weekly scheduled group sessions to Warrior Pro members who have an active community subscription. Warrior Trading and its representatives are not licensed financial advisors and do not provide personalized financial or investment advice. No 1-on-1 mentoring is provided. You can read more [in our disclaimer here](https://www.warriortrading.com/disclaimer/).

We offer the option for group mentoring with our Warrior Pro membership, via the community subscription (live stream + chat). These sessions take place in our online Mentor Room multiple days of the week, typically at 11 AM or 1 PM ET.

During sessions, our mentors will broadcast their screen and audio/video and discuss topics for members or lead a Q&A session. Members can participate by typing in the chat feed, entering Q: in front of questions to highlight their questions for moderators to see during the session.

If you're already a Warrior Pro member, and have purchased mentoring access, [you should click here to learn how to access the Warrior Pro Mentoring Sessions](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000090711). You can also suggest a mentor session topic via the [Mentor Topic Suggestions link](https://www.warriortrading.com/mentor-suggestion-box/) on the Member's Dashboard.

If you're a Pro member who would like to purchase mentoring access, visit our [](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/)[Renewal Information & Chat Room Tools page](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/) from the Member's Dashboard.

If you're not a member yet, [you should click here to read more about our Warrior Pro ](https://www.warriortrading.com/memberships/)membership and community options. If you're looking for personalized, 1-on-1 mentoring, please note that Warrior Trading and its representatives are not licensed financial advisors and do not provide personalized financial or investment advice. You can read more [in our disclaimer here](https://www.warriortrading.com/disclaimer/).

# What strategies do you teach (swing trading, options trading, etc.)?

Warrior Trading is best known for its day trading strategies focusing on small cap stocks.

In the past, Warrior Trading did have additional strategies included in its curriculum. However, as our community has grown, we've decided to focus on what we do best, which is long-biased day trading of small cap stocks.

The current curriculum of classes can be found here: [https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000071156-class-syllabus](http://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000071156-class-syllabus)

For more info about each course, check out our Trading Courses page here: [https://www.warriortrading.com/trading-courses/](https://www.warriortrading.com/trading-courses/)

Please note that we do not trade Forex or cryptocurrency, and as US traders, we do not trade CFDs (it is not legal in the USA).

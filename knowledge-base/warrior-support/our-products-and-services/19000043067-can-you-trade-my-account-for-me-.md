# Can you trade my account for me?

Warrior Trading representatives, including Ross Cameron, cannot cannot trade your trading account for you.  All traders must actively manage their own accounts.  The only people who can trade for you are licensed stock brokers.

If you've been contacted on social media or other means by anyone claiming to be a Warrior Trading representative and offering to trade your account, please read our [safety precautions here](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000091869).

Want to learn how to trade your own account? That's where we can help. [Check out our trading education memberships here](https://www.warriortrading.com/memberships/)!

# TradeStation

To verify your profits from TradeStation, we will need to see your short-term profits (not account value). You can get this from their Gain/Loss Tax Management Report. Please keep the time period visible on that report. If your full name is available on that page, you can keep that visible so that we can verify that you're the account owner. If your full name is not on that report, please keep the last 4 digits of your account number visible. See image below for reference:

[image]

If your full name is not visible on that report, you can send us a redacted image of your TradeStation statement so that we can verify you're the owner of the broker account. Please keep the last 4 digits of the account number visible, as well as your full name. See image below for reference:

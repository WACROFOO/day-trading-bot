# Lightspeed

We'll first need to see a screenshot of your net profits. You can get this from Lightspeed's reporting feature:

[image]

We'll also need to verify you're the account owner. You can send us a copy of your last statement, which shows the account holder's name and account number (please include the last 3 digits & your full name in your submission - you can redact the rest). Either the screenshot from their portal (above) or your statements (see below) will need to function to provide a history of the trades you've made during the time period associated with your badge application.

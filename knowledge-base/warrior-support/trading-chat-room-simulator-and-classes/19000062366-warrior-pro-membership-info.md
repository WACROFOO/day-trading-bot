# Warrior Pro Membership Info

Memberships options can be found [HERE](https://www.warriortrading.com/memberships/). Memberships provide access to a select set of trading courses for at least three years. The Warrior Pro membership provides access to the full suite of courses available at Warrior Trading, from the Day Trading: The Basics course to the advanced trading classes from Ross Cameron and even Warrior Trading graduates. [You can go here](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000071156-class-syllabus) to see the full list of courses we provide and which are included in each membership.

Selecting a membership, or prior enrollment in a membership, means that members are eligible to choose from various subscription options for tools like a stock market news feed, stock scanners, and charts. We also have subscription options available for access to the Warrior Trading community, including Ross Cameron's trading live stream, access to live and recorded mentoring sessions, and access to various chat rooms from live trading rooms to a community chat (Lounge) and live technical support. Please note that participation in our chat rooms is currently only provided as an option at the Warrior Pro membership level, and is not provided to Warrior Starter members.

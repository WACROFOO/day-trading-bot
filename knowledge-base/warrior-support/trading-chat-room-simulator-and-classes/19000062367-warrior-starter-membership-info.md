# Warrior Starter Membership Info

Memberships options can be found [HERE](https://www.warriortrading.com/memberships/). Memberships provide access to a select set of trading courses for at least three years. The course that makes up the Warrior Starter membership is the Day Trading: The Basics course. [You can go here](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000071156-class-syllabus) to see the full list of courses we provide and which are included in each membership.

Selecting a membership, or prior enrollment in a membership, means that members are eligible to choose from various subscription options for tools like a stock market news feed, stock scanners, and charts. We also have subscription options available for access to the Warrior Trading community, including Ross Cameron's trading live stream and view of the chat room for Small Cap trade ideas. Please note that participation in the chat feed is currently only provided as an option at the Warrior Pro membership level.

Upgrading from Warrior Starter to Warrior Pro
Members who begin with the Warrior Starter membership can upgrade to a Warrior Pro membership at any time. We encourage members to visit our [](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/)[Renewal Information & Chat Room Tools page](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/) which is available from the Member's Dashboard.

Warrior Pro does contain the course that comes with Warrior Starter, so upgrading will not cause members to lose access to anything from the Starter membership.

In addition adding access to many more trading education classes, upgrading to the Pro membership gives members the ability to take advantage of mentoring sessions and other community benefits that are not available at the Warrior Starter membership level. [Read more about Warrior Pro memberships here.](http://support.warriortrading.com/solution/articles/19000062366)

### About the Simulator Offered by Warrior Trading

The WT Sim is a real-time trading simulator accessible via a web browser. It integrates with the Day Trade Dash news, scanners, and charting widgets offered by Warrior Trading.

[Click here to check out basic information and a walk-through of the platform](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000173324-getting-started-with-the-web-simulator) or [here for a list of system requirements](http://You%20can%20see%20full%20system%20compatibility%20requirements%20here%3A%20https%3A//support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000106183).

Interested in purchasing simulator access?

- Members: Please see the [Member Renewal & Information ](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/)page for member offers and discounts.
- Non-members: [See how to buy trading simulator access here](https://www.warriortrading.com/paper-trading/).

Traders should prove profitability in a simulator BEFORE using real money. There are limitations to be aware of. Paper trading does not accurately reflect:

- Real-market slippage
- Availability of shares to borrow
- Emotions experienced with real money
Especially if you are new to trading or still learning, we suggest supplementing your simulator practice with trading education such as what we offer through our [memberships](https://www.warriortrading.com/memberships/).

### Do I have sim access?

Members can confirm their current subscriptions via their [Orders & Memberships page](https://secure.warriortrading.com/crm/members/sales/). You may also check the [Member's Dashboard](https://www.warriortrading.com/dashboard/), under the green "Trading Simulator" section for information regarding adding simulator access to your membership.

Unless otherwise stated with bundle offers, simulator access is activated upon purchase, so only purchase it when you are ready to start using it. Subscriptions cannot be paused, due to the nature of fees charged by the exchanges for live market data. Recurring subscriptions can be cancelled anytime. [Click here to learn more about managing a subscription](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000045050).

If you have purchased a bundle offer such as the Warrior Pro Special, [click here to learn how to activate your simulator trial](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000173324-getting-started-with-the-web-simulator#How-do-I-activate-my-simulator-trial?-(Pro-bundle-offers)).

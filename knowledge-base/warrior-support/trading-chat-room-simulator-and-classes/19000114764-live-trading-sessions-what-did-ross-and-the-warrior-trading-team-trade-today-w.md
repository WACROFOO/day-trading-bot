# Live Trading Sessions: What did Ross and the Warrior Trading team trade today? What is on your watch list? Is there a recording?

Our trading team normally shares daily pre-market watch lists and trading recaps within our live trading rooms for our members.

Members who are not able to attend live can reference the chat room announcements and chat feed to see what Ross and other traders traded that day.

- [Click here to learn where to find chat room announcements.](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000108845-adjust-audio-visual-alerts-in-the-chat-room-wt#Announcements-Audio-Alert)
- [Click here to learn more about the Favoriting feature and chat room filters](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000106189).

The Live Trading Archives section of the Warrior Pro curriculum is where Pro members can find recordings of some of these recaps and live trades. We do not upload every single day of live trading, though updates can be found on our [Content Updates page](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000114894-member-educational-content-updates) when new recordings are posted. [You can click here to learn more about how to access the archives.](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000109486-live-trading-archives)

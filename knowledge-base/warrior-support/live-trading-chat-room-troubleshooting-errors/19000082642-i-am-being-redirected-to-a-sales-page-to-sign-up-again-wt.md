# I am being redirected to a sales page to sign up again. | WT

If you are landing on our trading room sales page when trying to sign into the live trading chat room platform or classes, please make sure you are clicking on the correct links on the [Member's Dashboard](https://www.warriortrading.com/dashboard/), and not the sales pages. The trading simulator is a separate software that must be downloaded onto your computer and accessed directly though the program.

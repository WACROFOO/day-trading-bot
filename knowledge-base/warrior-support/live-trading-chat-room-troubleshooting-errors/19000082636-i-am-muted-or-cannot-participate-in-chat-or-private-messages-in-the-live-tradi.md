# I am muted or cannot participate in chat or private messages in the live trading rooms. | WT

Only Pro members with chat room access will be able to chat publicly in the live trading room platform.
If you are interested in learning about upgrading to a subscription that gives you the ability to participate in the chat, please [reach out to our Support Team](http://support.warriortrading.com/support/tickets/new), and we'd be happy to help.

Direct messaging is not available in the trading chat rooms for any members. However, certain members of the WT Team are able to initiate a private message with members when necessary.

If you have an active membership and have been muted by a moderator for any reason, and would like your access reinstated, please [reach out to us](https://warrior.app/contact).

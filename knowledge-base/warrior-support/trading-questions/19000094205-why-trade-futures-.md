# Why trade futures?

There are numerous benefits to learn how to prudently trade futures. Below is a list of many of these benefits:

- It is leveraged by default (only need 3%-10% of capital)
- It helps create portfolio diversification
- It allows you to make a pure commodities play
- Futures are easy to short
- 24-hour access, 6 days a week
- Great liquidity
- No PDT rule
- Lower entry costs than many other trading strategies
- It is simple to file taxes, since you do not need to submit your full trade history

# How do stock halts work?

Curious about stock halts?

A trading halt is a temporary pause in trading for a particular stock (or, sometimes, the entire market). During a halt:

- 
No new trades can be executed.
- 
The Level 2/order book freezes and instead shows a halt code or status message.

Whether or not you are able to indicate this level on your own trading platform will depend on the availability of that feature as well as market data you are subscribed to and what data is available.

# Understanding Level 2

Level 2 market data provides a detailed look at an order book, displaying the best bid prices and ask prices for a stock alongside the current bid. This visibility allows market participants to analyze liquidity and understand potential price movements in real time.

As the name suggests, Level 2 data goes beyond Level 1, which only shows the highest bid price and the lowest ask price. Level 2 trading can reveal the full market depth, giving you a clearer picture of the number of shares available at various price levels.
Think of it like pulling back the curtain on the stock market. You’re not just seeing the current price — you’re seeing who’s buying and selling, how much they’re trading, and at what prices. Trying to trade without Level 2 is like trying to drive with one eye closed. Can you do it? Technically, yes. Does it make sense to do it? No.

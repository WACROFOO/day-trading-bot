### How to use Search:

- From the [Member's Dashboard](https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/), click the pink button, "View Courses in Education Portal".
- 
[image]
Next, select the course you are interested in from the list of "My Courses".
[image]

- Click the "Content Search" button on the top right corner of the Course:

[image]

-  In the Search Box, enter a key word, phrase, or topic you want to search for. When searching by date, enter in this format: M/D/YYYY (ex: 5/22/2018 for May 22, 2018 or 11/5/2019 for November 5, 2019).
- Click "Search". This will pull up a list of chapters or pages within this course that match your search. In this example, we searched for content with the keyword, "Level 2" from the Strategies and Scaling Course.

[image]

# Live Trading Archives | WT

Ross does not record every single day of live trading, but when he does, you'll be able to find the recordings in the Live Trading Archives. You can also stay tuned to this page for information on where members can find recently-uploaded content: [Member Educational & Content Updates](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000114894-member-educational-content-updates) 

If you're interested, you can also catch daily trading recaps on YouTube: [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL1xI23WKVWienxdYuMT2VJCSK7LicBzdr](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL1xI23WKVWienxdYuMT2VJCSK7LicBzdr)

Live Trading Archives are available for Warrior Pro members only. If you are participating in the Pro Preview, [please click here](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000162794).

How to Locate the Archives

Warrior Pro members can find Ross' live trading examples in the "Live Trading Archives" section the Education Portal. 

Start by signing into the Member Dashboard using this link: [https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/](https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/). If you need help signing on, please follow the instructions here: [Signing into the Member's Dashboard (Warrior Trading Member Site) | WT : Warrior Trading](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000052853-signing-into-the-member-s-dashboard-warrior-trading-member-site-wt)

Once you are on the Member Dashboard, Warrior Pro members can access the Live Trading Archives content by clicking the pink button, "View Courses in Education Portal" - see illustration below.

[image]

From the "My Courses" page, scroll down to and click the blue button, "Begin/Resume" course for Live Trading Archives.

[image]

Click the button, "Expand All" then scroll down to find a Live Trading Archive or use the search feature. To search, click the "Content Search" button. In the Search Box, enter a key word, phrase, or topic you want to search for. When searching by date, enter in this format: M/D/YYYY (ex: 5/22/2018 for May 22, 2018 or 11/5/2019 for November 5, 2019).

# How do I reactivate my paused account | WT

Reactivating a paused subscription can only be done by a Warrior Trading Support Team member.

The best way to make sure that your request to have a subscription unpaused gets seen as soon as possible is to send your request through our Support Portal: [https://support.warriortrading.com/support/tickets/new](http://support.warriortrading.com/support/tickets/new)

Use a subject title such as "Unpause Account" and make sure to select "I have login questions or would like to unpause my account" as the reason for your request.

# How can I get a receipt for my purchase? | WT

Any purchase made directly by a member will be emailed to the billing email address on file within minutes after payment. The email subject title will be "Order Confirmation" with sender billing@warriortrading.com (please do not reply to that email address, and use team@warriortrading.com for assistance).

Any past payments that were billed automatically do not generate email receipts; however, you can still find receipts on your member account (see details below).

Please note that we do not provide formal invoices for purchases. We are also unable to provide a receipt with our tax ID number. We are not an accredited educational institution.

Here are the steps to locate receipts for your purchases:

1. Sign into your Member's Dashboard at [https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/](https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/) . If your account is active, you will be lead to your Member's Dashboard where you can click the "My Orders & Memberships" link. (Note: the password to sign into your dashboard is the password that you chose when you created your account or last updated it.)

[image]

If your account is no longer active, you'll be lead to an Access Denied page where you'll see a link to "View My Orders & Memberships" which will allow you to view your account:

[image]

2. On your Member Control Panel, you'll be able to click the "i" information icon next to each order you have placed (under the "Completed Sales" section).

[image]

3. This will pull up your receipt from the order. You can save the webpage or print the receipt to PDF by simply right-clicking on the page and choosing "Save as" or "Print." You can also take a screenshot and save that image.

[image]

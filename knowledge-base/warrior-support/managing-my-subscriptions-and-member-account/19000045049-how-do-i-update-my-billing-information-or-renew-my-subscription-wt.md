# How do I update my billing information or renew my subscription? | WT

What would you like to know?

- [How to Update Billing Information](#How-to-Update-Billing-Information)
- [How to Renew a Warrior Trading Subscription](#How-to-Renew-a-Warrior-Trading-Membership)

### How to Update Billing Information

Members can update the billing information on their account by either updating the information directly, or by saving their new information when purchasing a renewal. If your subscription is expired or due for payment, please see the "How to Renew" section farther down on this page, as it will show you how to both renew and update your billing information.

To update the billing information or credit card on file, sign into your Warrior Trading account at [https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/](https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/) .

If you have an active membership, you'll be lead to the Member's Dashboard where you can click the "Billing & Credit Card" tab and update the information there.

[image]

NOTE: If your subscription was initially purchased via PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay, you will need to contact our team here to have your existing subscription's payment method updated. You can contact our team here: [https://warrior.app/contact](https://warrior.app/contact) or by emailing [team@warriortrading.com](mailto:team@warriortrading.com). 

If your membership is expired, you can update your billing information from the "Access Denied" page by clicking the link to view your Orders & Memberships and then proceeding to Billing Info (see below). 

[image]

### How to Renew a Warrior Trading Subscription

Our system sends email reminders 5 days before an auto-renewal is scheduled or 5 days before a non-renewing subscription expires.

Active members can view the ["Renewal Information" page](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/) from the Member's Dashboard for renewal details.

Warrior Trading monthly subscriptions that have failed to renew are also equipped with an easy renewal feature which allows members to retry a renewal directly from the Member Control Panel. To take advantage of the renewal feature:

- Go to the [Orders & Memberships section of your account](https://secure.warriortrading.com/crm/members/sales/). If your membership is active, the link is available from the Member's Dashboard, as shown below:

[image]

If your membership has expired, you can reach the "Orders & Memberships" section from the "Access Denied" page:

[image]
 
- Click Manage next to the membership you want to renew 

[image]

- If the Manage button is available, you'll see a blue button prompting you to click to renew. Click that button.

[image]

***If the Manage button is NOT available, you can find your renewal links on your [Renewal Information & Chat Tools](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/) page on your [Member's Dashboard](https://www.warriortrading.com/dashboard/) (as previously mentioned). If you do NOT have access to that page, you will need to contact our team [here](https://warrior.app/contact) or by emailing [team@warriortrading.com](mailto:team@warriortrading.com) so that we can assist you with the renewal process.***

- You'll then be able to choose your payment method on the next screen and complete your purchase. If you are paying with a new credit card, you'll see the option to save your new billing information on your account.

[image]

After completing your purchase, you should see that your expiration date has been extended.

You are always welcome to contact our team with questions about your renewal options as well! We can be reached directly via email at team@warriortrading.com or [use this portal to open a support ticket](https://warrior.app/contact).

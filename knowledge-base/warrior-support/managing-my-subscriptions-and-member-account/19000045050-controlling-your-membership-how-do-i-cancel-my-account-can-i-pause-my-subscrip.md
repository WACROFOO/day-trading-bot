### What does canceling a subscription do?

Canceling your subscription will:

- allow you access to your subscription contents until the expiration date,
- disable any autorenewal cycle, and
- indicate that you do not wish to be contacted regarding renewal options.

Please remember that an un-expired subscription is required in order to keep access to the content of that membership. 

When it comes to the class videos, members are encouraged to use the downloadable PDFs for personal use when a membership is not active. [You can learn more about such downloadable resources here.](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000174334-how-to-use-the-education-portal#Chapter-Slides-(PDFs))

If you no longer have access to the classes you have purchased in the past, please reach out to our team to inquire about discounted member renewal options. If you have made a purchase in the past 3 years, you should be able to access the current syllabus of any previously-purchased membership.

### How to Cancel a Warrior Trading Subscription

Step 1: Sign into your [Member's Dashboard](http://warriortrading.com/dashboard/). If you are having trouble signing in to the Member's Dashboard, check out [this article here](http://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000052853-getting-logged-into-the-member-dashboard-main-website-). If you do not have an active membership, you will receive an "Access Denied" message but can still proceed to the next step.

Step 2: Click the "[My Orders & Memberships](https://secure.warriortrading.com/ati_pro/ati_merchant/members/sales/)" link.

[image]

Step 3: You'll see two sections: one for your membership history, and a "Completed Sales" section for past payments. To manage your membership, click the "Manage" button next to the membership you'd like to adjust.

Step 4: Change status to "Cancelled" and choose the most relevant cancellation reason from the drop-down menu.

Step 5: Click "Save." You should see that your membership status has changed to Cancelled. You'll have access until the expiration date.

### Can I pause my access if I want to come back later?

While access cannot be placed on hold mid-payment cycle, members have full control to cancel an upcoming renewal -- please follow the cancellation instructions above. Then, when you are ready to resume access, simply [renew your access using the renewal instructions below](#Renew-a-Warrior-Trading-Subscription).

### What does paused access mean?

Our system may change an existing membership or subscription status to "Paused" when a member upgrades or switches to a new subscription. This pausing helps to ensure that members are not double-billed for both a new and older subscription. 

Members who upgrade from Starter to Pro will see their Starter membership become "paused" so that the system no longer recognizes them as a Starter member. This is expected behavior.

In the event a subscription (such as a Day Trade Dash or community subscription) is set to "paused" with remaining time on it, members are encouraged to reach out to us to let us know:

- when they are ready to unpause that subscription ([submit a request here](https://warrior.app/contact)), or
- if they prefer the previous subscription time to be credited to the new subscription (remaining time will be prorated at the new subscription price).

Some long-time members may recall having a pausing feature available to use for placing their own subscriptions on hold during extended absences. This feature was discontinued for simulator subscriptions in April 2022 and fully discontinued as of April 1, 2023 due to the nature of the fees that we pay on our members' behalf for live market data and more (read more below).

#### Why is pausing discontinued?

Warrior Trading looks very different now than when we originally placed pause capabilities in our members’ hands: access didn’t include live market data fees and other fees that we now pay, such as for streaming, software, and vendor licenses. Even when a member's account is on hold, Warrior Trading is still charged many of these fees. While we continue to strive to build features that our members find useful, we also want to be able to provide the best pricing possible. 

This change also helps us to continue to introduce more products to our community.

Members who don’t sign in regularly may find a better fit with a monthly subscription than an annual renewal. On a monthly subscription, you have control over when your next month is billed, and could wait to make that next payment until you’re ready to resume your access.

### Reactivate a Paused Warrior Trading Subscription

If you are an older member with time on hold from when we had a pausing feature available, contact us to reactivate your access. We cannot schedule this ahead of time; [just contact us here to request unpausing ](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000092400-how-do-i-reactivate-my-paused-account)within 24 hours of when you want it to be reactivated.

If you do not have any time on hold and would like to reactivate by renewing access, see the information below.

### Renew a Warrior Trading Subscription

Active members can find renewal information on the ["Renewal Information" page on the Member's Dashboard](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/). Expired members may be able to reinstate an expired subscription using the renewal feature ([learn about that here](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000045049-how-do-i-update-my-billing-information-or-renew-my-membership-)). If you cancelled your subscription, or have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us to inquire about renewal options.

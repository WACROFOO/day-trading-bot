# Removing Navigation Buttons - + &lt; > from Day Trade Dash Charts

By default, navigation buttons to allow members to zoom in and out and scroll on Day Trade Dash charts will appear when mousing over the chart.

[image]

To remove your navigation buttons from Day Trade Dash charts:

- Right click on a chart
- Go to Settings
- Click to Canvas
- Under the Buttons section, select the dropdown menu for Navigation and choose Always invisible.
- Click Ok to save your chart settings.

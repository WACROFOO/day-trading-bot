# How to Take a Screenshot or Send an Image in Live Trading Rooms

There are various ways to take a screenshot, and what you can do depends on the computer system that you have or device you are using.

Always remember to protect yourself by rigorously checking your screenshots or images to ensure they are stripped of any personally identifiable information (PII). Note images that contain PII will be rejected whenever possible. Examples of PII include (but are not limited to):

- Full Name
- Account Numbers (Broker or Simulator)
- IP Address
- Credit Card Details
- Physical Address

A popular built-in tool on Windows systems is the Snipping Tool which allows you to take a screenshot of your computer screen and save the image.

Websites like this one here will help identify your device and possible options for taking a screenshot: [https://www.take-a-screenshot.org/](https://www.take-a-screenshot.org/) 

When troubleshooting technical issues, sending screenshots can be a very effective way to help the tech team you're working with understand what you are experiencing.

If you're a member in our live trading rooms and have the ability to chat, you may be interested in sending a screenshot such as of your simulator metrics, P&L windows, or stock charts. 

Once you have your screenshot please follow these steps:

- Click the "Send File" icon that appears next to the text box.

[image]

- A prompt will appear at the top of the chat widget. Use the toggle to determine if you wish your image to send automatically once it's approved by a moderator, and then click "Upload file."

[image]

- Select the file to submit. It will go under admin review. You can delete the image before a moderator approved it if you change your mind.

- Once the image is approved, it will post to the chat automatically unless you toggled off the option to "Auto send when approved." In this case, the image will give you a prompt to send it yourself when approved. If the image is not approved, you'll receive an announcement notifying you why.

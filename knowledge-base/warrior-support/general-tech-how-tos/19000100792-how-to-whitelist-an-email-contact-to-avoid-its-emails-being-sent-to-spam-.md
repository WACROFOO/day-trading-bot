# How to "whitelist" an email contact to avoid its emails being sent to spam.

This helpful article from clean.email will help demonstrate how to add our email addresses to your spam whitelist to ensure that you properly receive our communications: [https://clean.email/blog/email-security/how-to-whitelist-an-email](https://clean.email/blog/email-security/how-to-whitelist-an-email)

Our most popular email addresses are team@warriortrading.com, ross@warriortrading.com, and [simulator@warriortrading.com](mailto:simulator@warriortrading.com). You can also whitelist the entire [warriortrading.com](//warriortrading.com) domain.

### Gmail

Here is how to add Warrior Trading's email contacts to your Spam whitelist if our emails are going to your Spam folder in Gmail:

- Click the gear icon
- Click "See all settings"

[image]

- Go to "Filters and Blocked Addresses"
- Click "Create a new filter"

[image]

- Enter warriortrading.com in the From section
- Click "Create filter"

[image]

- Check the box next to "Never send it to Spam". If you'd like to create a particular label for our emails as well (such as creating the label "Warrior Trading") this is where you can auto-assign emails from [warriortrading.com](https://warriortrading.com/) to a specific label as well.
- Click "Create filter."

[image]

This will help Gmail know not to send any emails that you receive from a [warriortrading.com](https://warriortrading.com/) email address to your Spam folder.

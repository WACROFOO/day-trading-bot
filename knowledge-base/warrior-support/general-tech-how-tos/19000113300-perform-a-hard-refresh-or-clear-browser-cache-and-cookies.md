# Perform a Hard Refresh or Clear Browser Cache and Cookies

[](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/32050?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en&oco=0)
Browser-based technical issues are very common, and many can be resolved by refreshing or clearing cache.

- [How to Perform a Hard Browser Refresh](#How-to-Perform-a-Hard-Browser-Refresh)
- [How to Clear Browser Cache and Cookies](#How-to-Clear-Browser-Cache-and-Cookies)
[Adjust Browser Settings to Automatically Clear Cookies for a Site](#%E2%80%8BAdjust-Browser-Settings-to-Automatically-Clear-Cookies-for-a-Site)

## How to Perform a Hard Browser Refresh

While on your browser window, perform a hard refresh by doing the following on Windows OS:

- Google Chrome or MS Edge: hold Shift and press F5
- Firefox: hold Ctrl and press F5

## How to Clear Browser Cache and Cookies

Click the links below that correspond to the browser you are using to learn how to clear cache and cookies:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/32050?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en&oco=0)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/how-clear-firefox-cache)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/help/4027947/microsoft-edge-delete-cookies)
- [Safari on Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/clear-your-browsing-history-sfri47acf5d6/mac)
- [Safari on iPhone / iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201265)

### Adjust Browser Settings to Automatically Clear Cookies for a Site

It can sometimes be very helpful to change your browser settings so that it cleans the website cookies after each browser exit. This can reduce/eliminate issues you might encounter down the road.

For Google Chrome:

When you are in the Education Portal, start by clicking the icon in the Chrome as per the screenshot, then click Cookies and site data.

[image]

From the next dialog box, click "Manage on-device site data".

[image]

For each of the sites, select the three vertical dots, then "Delete when you close all windows".

[image]

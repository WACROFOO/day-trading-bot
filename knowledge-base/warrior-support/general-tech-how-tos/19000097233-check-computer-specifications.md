# Check Computer Specifications

While troubleshooting or installing new software, we may ask you to provide information about your computer system or your internet connection. Below are some helpful tips for finding out this information.

Please note: If you're wondering what specifications are required for a Warrior Trading membership, note that we have separate requirements for our live trading rooms, viewing our classes, and our trading simulator (all of which are included in the Warrior Starter and Pro Programs). The requirements for the simulator are the most strict, so we recommend viewing those here: [System Requirements for New WT Simulator](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000094887-system-requirements-for-new-wt-simulator) 
Internet speed: An easy way to check your internet speed is to use a site like this one: [https://www.speedtest.net/](https://www.speedtest.net/)
Operating system and browser information: Websites like this one here ([https://www.mysupportdetails.com/](https://www.mysupportdetails.com/)) can confirm important information such as your operating system, the version of the browser you are using, and if you have cookies enabled on your browser. Note that we do not support windows computer that are older than Windows 10.
Hardware specifications: Once you have identified which operating system you have, you can figure out your computer specifications like your processor, RAM, and system type by following the instructions below.

Windows 10 & 11

- Right-click on the Windows Start Menu on the bottom left of your computer screen.
- On the menu that appears, click on System.
- Look under Device specifications for the necessary information regarding Processor, RAM (total and useable RAM), System Type, etc.

Mac Users: Go to the "About This Mac" feature for information about your system.
Graphics card: Once you have identified which operating system you have, you can figure out which graphics card (or "GPU") your computer has by following the steps below.

Windows 10 & 11

- Right-click on the Windows Start Menu on the bottom left of your computer screen.
- On the menu that appears, click on Device Manager.
- Click the arrow > next to Display Adapters. This will reveal your graphics card, or GPU.

Mac Users: Go to the "About This Mac" feature for information about your system.

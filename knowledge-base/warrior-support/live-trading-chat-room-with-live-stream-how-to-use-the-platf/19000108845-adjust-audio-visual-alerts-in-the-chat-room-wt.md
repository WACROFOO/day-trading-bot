### Adjusting Placement and Volume of Alerts

You can easily adjust your alert volume by clicking Alert Volume from the left-hand menu (under Settings) and using the - and + indicators to decrease and increase the volume of the audio alerts you receive.

[image]

For additional personalization, click Profile from the left-hand menu and adjust your notification settings there. 

- Closes after: This allows you to select how long it will take for visual alerts to disappear from your screen
- Screen Position: where visual indicators will appear on your screen
- Display Connection Errors: If toggled off, you will no longer have disconnect or connection errors displayed on your window
We do not suggest turning off this function unless you are certain there is no actual issue with your platform
- Please be sure your clock is synced ([click here for instructions](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000108691-issues-with-screen-share-or-video-broadcast-in-trading-rooms-wt#How-to-Sync-Clock-in-Windows)), which helps confirm if your connection is lagging behind.
- Alert Volume: how loud the audio alert will be (same as the Alert Volume shortcut from the left-hand menu)

The different alert types come with extra customization, so read on below!

### Announcements

Announcements in the platform might include important updates from the community or profit/loss status of the day from mentors. We also use it for known outages or links to content.

Today's past announcements for the rooms you have open can be found by clicking the Announcements button on the left of your screen. A red "New" indicator will appear if a new announcement is waiting for you.

[image]

If using the minimized toolbar, it will look like this:

[image]

While past announcements are always available to view after opening a chat room, you will only receive a real-time notification of announcements if the room is already open when the announcement is posted. So if you wish to receive real-time announcements, please first make sure the room is open on your chat room platform.

To mute all moderator alerts within a certain room, click the bell symbol on the blue toolbar at the top of the room window. When audio alerts are on, the alert symbol will look like this:

### Broadcasting and Announcement screen alerts

You will see a "top banner" alert across the top left of your monitor to showcase when a stream comes online or an announcement is posted. These will disappear unless you change the profile settings.

These can also come with an audio alert when a new broadcast has started streaming. Members can turn on or off the audio alert when broadcasts start, and can also choose how long it will take for the visual alert to disappear from their screen.

To do so, select Profile from the bottom of the left-hand menu. Under the Notification and Window settings section, you can then adjust:

[image]

- 1. On-screen Persistence: If you would like the broadcast alerts to remain onscreen until you close them (helpful if you happen to be looking away from your screen when the broadcast starts).
- 2. Audio Notification: If you want any audio notification when a broadcast is started.

Pink highlight means the setting is selected as on. No highlight and displayed as gray signals the setting is off.

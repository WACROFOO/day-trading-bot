# Mobile Experience: Day Trade Dash and Chat Room | WT

Per our compatibility settings, the full experience of the Warrior Trading chat room platform is not compatible with tablets, mobile devices, or the Safari browser. [Please click or tap here to see full compatibility requirements](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000106183-live-trading-chat-rooms-compatibility-system-requirements).

Our team has developed a mobile version that allows members to choose to view the chat, live stream, or scanners. Features unavailable on mobile at this time include the ability to chat, share images, and see the [news freshness indicator](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000129722-using-the-stock-quote-widget-and-cashtags-for-news-wt#Using-Recent-News-Headlines).

Keep in mind that users may be more likely to experience lag and latency issues on a mobile network.

### How to Use the Warrior Trading Chat Room on a Mobile Device

- The login process on mobile devices is similar to that on a computer. Please [see here for additional information](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000024073), if needed.
- Within the chat platform, you can tap the hamburger menu (the 3 horizontal lines) in the upper left-hand corner to see options for other rooms and scanners which you can choose to open one at a time.

Reminder: as mentioned above, chat abilities and the ability to view multiple rooms or scanners at once is not enabled on mobile. For the best experience, [please use a device and browser that meets the compatibility requirements listed on this page here](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000106183).

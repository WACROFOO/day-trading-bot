# Live Trading & Chat Rooms: Schedule + Audio Hours

Warrior Trading and our [live trading rooms](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000094161-live-trading-chat-rooms-schedule-audio-hours) are open every market day (Mondays through Fridays with the exception of [US market holidays](https://www.warriortrading.com/stock-market-holidays/)) at the hours described below. If you have not yet joined the community, you can learn more about the trading strategies we trade every day by enrolling in our courses and joining mentoring sessions offered to members of [Warrior Pro](http://support.warriortrading.com/solution/articles/19000062366-warrior-pro-program-info)!

### Live Trading Rooms

Our live trading rooms are for our members to have active discussions on trading only. This includes chatter about stocks moving, trades you are taking, questions on others’ active trades, and current market condition discussions.

Before you type, pause and think:  "Does this have anything to do directly with a stock or the market today?"

It does not include questions about brokerages or platforms. We consider these off-topic discussions that are best served by the community in the Lounge. 

The available live trading rooms are:

- Small Cap room
- Large Cap / Options / Swing room.
They open by 7 AM ET each morning and close at 5pm ET Monday through Thursday and at 4pm ET on Fridays. On days the market closes early, the rooms may also close early.

Live Stream:  

Ross Cameron hosts the Small Cap room and broadcasts a live stream there. 
The Large Cap / Options/ Swing Room does not currently host any regular live streams.

Live streaming audio/video/screen share normally starts by 7:00 am ET, or as late as 8 am ET, depending on pre-market activity. Ross will discuss his watch list and commentary with the community, and trade with a screenshare and audio when appropriate. 

Like many day traders, Ross typically trades just a couple of hours each morning and is done by noon, occasionally holding a mid-day recap before signing off the stream.

On the left of the platform, the Rooms list shows each chat room as well as one of the following indicators for any room that is equipped with a stream:

- Stream Off - There is no available live stream.
- Stream is Open - You have the stream open on your platform.
- Join Stream - The room is currently streaming. Clicking the red "Join Stream" will automatically open the room and stream if you have access.

### Warrior Pro Mentor Room

This room is available only to Warrior Pro members who have a community (AKA live stream + chat) subscription. When mentor sessions are scheduled, they will be hosted in this room. [You can see more about accessing mentor sessions here.](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000090711)

### Support Room

The Support Room is available on market days. The current hours are 7:00 AM ET until 12 PM ET. Hours will be posted to the away message for the room when it is closed.

The Support Room allows members to connect with our Support Team in real time to troubleshoot technical issues and get answers to general membership questions. We recommend stopping here first with questions so we can either answer them for you or point you in the right direction.

Remember that our Support Team cannot help with your brokerage account or third-party trading tools. We can only assist with our own platforms. We ask that any specific questions about billing on your account are addressed to our billing team at [team@warriortrading.com](mailto:team@warriortrading.com), or you can initiate a request via [https://warrior.app/contact](https://warrior.app/contact).

### News Room

The News Room opens and starts posting at 7:00 a.m. ET and stops around 4:30 p.m. ET (depending on market activity). This Room does have an audio stream available for top news headlines. To learn more about the News Room and Squawk, you[ can visit our guide here.](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000131769-news-room-and-alerts-wt)[](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000131769-news-room-and-alerts-wt/)

### Warrior Lounge

This room is open whenever the trading rooms are open. It gives members a place to chat amongst themselves about brokers, trading software, strategies, current struggles and successes, etc. (or sometimes more off-topic matters like food, travel, and lifestyle!).

This can be a great place to connect with other members or ask questions about their trading experiences. Please remember that no personal information should be exchanged in any of our chat rooms for the safety of the community. It can be helpful at times to filter chat comments so that you can talk more easily with another user. [Click here to learn more about ways to filter a chat feed.](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000106189) [Click here to review our Chat Room Rules](https://www.warriortrading.com/chat-room-rules/).

### Scanners & Charts

Members with scanner and charting access can access the platform 24/7. However, data does not update all day long. Market data for certain scanners is collected as early as 4 am ET when the US pre-market opens, and as late as 8 pm ET when the after-market hours close.

To learn in more detail the hours and when different scanners are in use, please see [our support article on it here.](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000117763-scanners-how-to-load-use-them-in-the-wt-chat-room)

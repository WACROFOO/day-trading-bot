# "Access Denied" Error Message | WT

Did you get an Access Denied error message when you tried to access the website? If so, one of the following may be true:

- you do not have access to the resource (ex: trying to access Warrior Pro mentor sessions as a Warrior Starter member),
- your membership is not active (it may be paused or expired),
- or you may simply need to clear your cache and cookies ([click here to clear cookies for the WT website](https://www.warriortrading.com/clear)) and then [sign in again here](https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/).

If you're unsure which of the above situations is true, start by checking your membership status. You can do this by clicking the link to "[View My Orders & Memberships](https://secure.warriortrading.com/crm/members/sales/)" and then following the instructions below according to the state of your membership.

## Expired or Failed

You may be due to make your next payment. [Click here to learn how to renew a subscription](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000045049-how-do-i-update-my-billing-information-or-renew-my-membership-wt#How-to-Renew-a-Warrior-Trading-Membership), or check your inbox for instructions via an email from [team@warriortrading.com](mailto:team@warriortrading.com) with the subject line that contains the words "Membership Renewal." If you have questions about your payment, contact us by replying to that email or [submitting a ticket here](https://warrior.app/contact).

## Active or Completed

- The issue may simply be that your login time has expired. Please try signing in again at the Access Denied page.

- If that does not work, please try accessing the Member's Dashboard directly at [https://www.warriortrading.com/dashboard/](https://www.warriortrading.com/dashboard/) and moving forward from there, as you may already be signed in.

- If the issue continues, you can determine if this error is related to cache and cookies by using a different browser to access the login page, or signing in at [https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/](https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/) using a private or "incognito" browser window. If you're not receiving the Access Denied error message on a different or incognito browser window, clear your browser cache and cookies, exit the browser, re-open it and sign in at [https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/](https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/). [Information on clearing cache and cookies can be found here](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000113300).

If you're able to sign into your account but are trying to access resources that are not included in your tools subscription, you will be denied access. For example, if you are trying to access the chat room but have not purchased a chat room subscription with your membership, you may get an Access Denied message. [You can contact us here](https://warrior.app/contact) for information on how to upgrade or review options on our [](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/)[Renewal Information & Chat Room Tools page](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/).

Still having trouble? Contact us at [https://warrior.app/contact](https://warrior.app/contact) or [team@warriortrading.com](mailto:team@warriortrading.com) and let us know what error you are receiving, what page you are trying to access, and what steps you have already tried.

## Paused or Cancelled

If your account is paused (a feature available only to older members of ours prior to April 2023): [send us a message through the support portal](http://support.warriortrading.com/support/tickets/new) with a subject line like "Unpause My Account" and we will unpause your membership for you.

### 

If you previously cancelled your membership, contact our Membership Consultants at team@warriortrading.com or via [https://warrior.app/contact](https://warrior.app/contact) and we can help with renewal options if you cannot access our [](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/)[Renewal Information & Chat Room Tools page](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/).

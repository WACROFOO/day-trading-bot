# How do I contact the Warrior Trading Team or Support Room? | WT

The best way to contact us is through our support portal (for both members and non-members) or our Support Room (if you are a member with chat access)! You can [browse our support articles](http://support.warriortrading.com/support/home) anytime or reach out to us [by submitting a support ticket](http://support.warriortrading.com/support/tickets/new).

Please note that we do not provide phone support.

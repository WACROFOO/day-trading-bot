### Basic Sign-In Steps

- Go to [our sign-in page](https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/)[https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/](https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/)
- Enter your email address and password in the Sign In section. If you just created your account and were not asked to create your own password on the checkout page, our system will have emailed you a link to reset/create your password. Please check your inbox and spam folders for that email.
- Click the Sign In button.

If you have forgotten your password, use the password lookup, and a reset link will be emailed to that email account if the email is on file with us.
[image]

Please remember that this will reset your password for the website ONLY and will not reset the password for the trading simulator. If you have purchased simulator access and are looking for information on signing into the trading simulator, [this link here will walk through the initial setup of the platform](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000093684) and [this link here will help reset a password afterwards](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000093706).

### Possible Error Messages and What They Mean

#### "Login Failed"

Meaning: Your email address or password is incorrect.

- The email address should be the one you entered when you first created your account, or an email address that a Warrior Trading team member updated for you. Please note that changing your own login email is not possible at this time. If you own multiple email addresses, you may just need to double-check the one you entered.
- You can reset your password by using the "Forgot your password" field on the login page https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/ . If the email address you entered is on file with a Warrior Trading account, you'll receive an email titled "Password Reset Request" with a link to reset your password. Be sure to check your spam folder.

#### "Too many attempts please try again later." or "An Error Occurred!"

Meaning: A security feature being triggered.

- First, try accessing the dashboard directly at [https://www.warriortrading.com/dashboard/](https://www.warriortrading.com/dashboard/) , as it may be a cache issue. 
- If you're unable to access the dashboard, try clearing your browser cache and cookies and signing in again. [More information can be found here on how to clear cache](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000113300).
- If that doesn't work, it is more likely that the error was triggered correctly after multiple sequential login attempts. It should resolve after 15 minutes of rest and then you can try signing in again.

#### "Access Denied"

Meaning: Your subscription does not provide access to the resource, or there is a browser cache issue occurring.

- [Please click here for more information.](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000024069--access-denied-error-message)

# How do I access live classes? | WT

Our Warrior Pro Mentor Sessions can often be confused for live classes. Our Live Classroom is where we occassionally host special class sessions, or use when the Mentor Room is otherwise occupied.

There are no Live Classes scheduled at this time.

When scheduled, classes in the Live Classroom can be accessed by Warrior Pro members through the "Live Classes" button on the Rooms list in the chat room platform:

[image]

If you are having trouble finding that button from the Rooms list:

- If you have a scroll wheel on your mouse: place your cursor over the list of rooms and scroll down until you see the Live Classes button. Click the button and the room will open. If it does not, you may have too many rooms open in the chat room platform. Try closing one and reopening the Live Classes room.
- If you do not have a scroll wheel on your mouse: zoom out on your browser page until you see the Live Classes button, click the button to open the room, and then adjust your zoom again to your desired view.

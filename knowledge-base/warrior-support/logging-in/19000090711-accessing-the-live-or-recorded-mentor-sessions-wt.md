# Accessing the Live or Recorded Mentor Sessions | WT

This page has information on how to:

- [Access Live Warrior Pro Mentor Sessions Sessions & View Schedule](#Access-Live-Warrior-Pro-Mentor-Sessions-Sessions-&-View-Schedule)
- [Get Your Questions Answered in Mentor Sessions](#Get-Your-Questions-Answered-in-Mentor-Sessions)
- [Access the Recorded Mentor Sessions](#Access-the-Recorded-Mentor-Sessions)

Note that the mentor sessions are accessible only to Warrior Pro members who have mentoring access (available with a Pro community subscription). These are group sessions. We do not provide 1-on-1 mentoring, as we are not licensed financial advisors.

If you are interested in adding a tools subscription to your membership, visit our [](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/)[Renewal Information & Chat Room Tools](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/)[ page](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/) for subscription options.

### Access Live Warrior Pro Mentor Sessions Sessions & View Schedule

[You should watch this video](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000106182-how-to-use-warrior-trading-s-live-trading-chat-platform) before your first sign-in to help you understand how to use the platform and properly open the live stream. Please also remember that compatible browsers are Google Chrome, Firefox, and MS Edge.

- Sign in at [https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/](https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/) using an updated and compatible browser. [Having login issues? Click here.](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000024069)
- From your Member's Dashboard, click the "Join or View Schedule" link.
[image]

- You'll notice that we have a Mentor Session Calendar available, where you can see the past and upcoming mentor sessions for the month. You can hover over a session for more details on it when details are available. For example:

[image]

- To access the live mentor classroom, click the blue button "Join Live Mentor Session."

[image]

If you are already within the live trading platform, access the Mentor Room from the Rooms list. If you have many rooms already open, you may need to close another room first to access the Mentor Room.

[image]

### Get Your Questions Answered in Mentor Sessions

#### 

When in the Mentor Room, if you have a question for the mentor, type Q: in front of your question and our system will highlight it!

### Access the Recorded Mentor Sessions

Pro Preview members: Please note your recorded mentor sessions will be available in a section of your Education Portal, titled "Warrior Pro Preview"

Warrior Pro members with community access will be able to access 

- Sign into your Warrior Pro education portal through the pink "View Courses" button in the Education section of the Member's Dashboard.

[image]

- If you are having trouble accessing the education portal, [check out this article here](https://support.warriortrading.com/a/solutions/articles/19000174334?portalId=19000047366).
- Once you are in the Education Portal, you will see a list of courses called, "My Courses". Scroll down the list of course to find the "Live Trading Archives" and click the button, "Begin Course".
- Searching for a particular mentor, date, or topic? Check out this [support article with helpful instructions](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000104329) regarding search.

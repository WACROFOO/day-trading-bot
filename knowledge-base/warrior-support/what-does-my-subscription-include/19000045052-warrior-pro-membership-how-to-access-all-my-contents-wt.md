### About the Warrior Pro Membership

When signing up for the Warrior Pro membership, you can choose to purchase the membership alone or select a bundle with subscriptions that add on access to the community (live stream and chat), Day Trade Dash (news, scanners, and charts), and/or simulator.

- Note that any subscription that is not an annual subscription does automatically renew. [](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000045050)[](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000045050)[Learn how to manage your subscriptions here.](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000045050)[](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000045050)[](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000045050)
- If you have purchased the membership only and want to add on a subscription, you can visit the [](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/)[Renewal Information page](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/) from the Member's Dashboard to review renewal options. [Get in touch](https://warrior.app/contact) with us if you have questions!

Are you a returning member? Welcome back! Members are always eligible for discounted renewal rates and do not have to purchase a membership in full from our website after they've already purchased a membership once. Similarly, members who purchased the Warrior Starter membership are able to upgrade to Warrior Pro at a discount since they've already purchased the first set of classes. Members who are able to access the Dashboard can visit the [](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/)[Renewal Information page](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/) to review renewal and upgrade options, or [get in touch](https://warrior.app/contact) with us if you have questions!

### What the Warrior Pro Membership Includes

As of November 2022, purchases of the Warrior Pro membership provide a full 3 years of course access. You can see the current course curriculum [on our syllabus page here](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000071156-class-syllabus). To extend course access beyond 3 years, any payment for a subscription can be made.

For information on how to access classes, [see this page here](https://support.warriortrading.com/a/solutions/articles/19000174334?portalId=19000047366).

Members who purchase a bundle like the Warrior Pro Special, or who add on monthly subscriptions, will have additional access as well. Read on for more information about any of these services.

### How to Access the Warrior Pro Membership Contents

#### Suggested Path for Making the Most of the Program

Each member is different in terms of how often and times of day they are available to participate in the program. Please keep in mind that this is a general suggestion for how to move along in the program. If you have questions, you're welcome to reach out to our Support Team for help!

The main page to pay attention to as a member is your Warrior Trading Member's Dashboard, which you can reach by signing in at [https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/](https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/) . We recommend bookmarking that login link!

##### Start Here:

For beginners in particular, regardless of what other subscriptions you may have purchased along with your membership, it may be helpful to bookmark the [onboarding page](https://www.warriortrading.com/welcome-to-warrior-trading/) (also available via the [Member's Dashboard](https://www.warriortrading.com/dashboard/)), and watch at least a few of the Day Trading: The Basics classes in the education portal first. It can also be helpful for beginners to refer to our pages for [day trading terminology](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading-terminology/) along the way.

- [Click here to learn how to access your classes](https://support.warriortrading.com/a/solutions/articles/19000174334?portalId=19000047366) in the education portal.
- Note: There are quizzes to go along with most of the lessons. Much of the course curriculum is in the member's hands. Feel free to go at your own pace while your membership is active.
- The Education Portal is where Warrior Pro members can also find the [Live Trading Archives](https://lms.warriortrading.ai/learning/course/course-v1:WarriorTrading+LTA+2026/home) and [Trading Tool Demos & Layouts](https://lms.warriortrading.ai/learning/course/course-v1:WarriorTrading+DE101+2026/home).

##### Available with Community Access:

With some baseline knowledge, it can be helpful to start tuning into the chat rooms to witness live trading and trade ideas, and to chat with fellow members in the Warrior Lounge. The Warrior Pro Mentor Sessions are a great place to ask trading questions in a live environment to our team of educators and moderators.

- [Click here to learn how to access the live stream and chat rooms](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000024073).
- [Click here to learn how to access the live and recorded mentoring sessions](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000090711).

##### Available with Day Trade Dash Access:

To compliment community access and the classes, it is helpful for traders who are learning to be able to use the live trading tools. Learning how to scan the stock market, how to read charts, and how to interpret breaking news are all essential to the strategies we teach in our classes.

- [Click here to learn about the news feed and audio squawk.](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000131769)
- [Click here to learn how to sign market data agreements for scanners and charts](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000118834).
- [Click here to learn more about the Day Trade Dash scanners and how to use them](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000117763).
- [Click here to learn all about Day Trade Dash charting.](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/folders/19000171870)

##### Available with Simulator Access:

While continuing with the classes in the education portal, members are then encouraged to get started with their simulator access when they are ready.

The "WT Sim" is Windows-based, not browser based, so members will need to download the software. [Check your system requirements here](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000094887). [](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/19000102013)You can learn all about the features in the platform and how to use them [by viewing our support articles here.](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/19000102013)

When your simulator access is activated on your account, you will receive an email from simulator@warriortrading.com (check your spam folder!) titled "Your Warrior Trading Simulator Account" that will have your instructions. If you purchased a bundle like the Warrior Pro Special, your simulator access is likely not activated by default. [Click here to learn how to activate it.](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000062350-trading-simulator-access#How-do-I-activate-my-simulator-trial?-(Pro-bundle-offers))

- [Can't find the simulator welcome email after access is activated? Contact our team!](https://warrior.app/contact)
- [Click here to learn how to sign the market data agreements for the WT Sim](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000093687)
- [Click here for more information on installing the WT Sim platform after signing agreements](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000093685)
- [Click here to learn how to use the WT Sim platform](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/19000102013)

Please note that the simulator platform may be inaccessible during a daily maintenance period that takes place between about 11 PM ET and 4 AM ET.

Keep in mind that, while the help articles will explain how to use the WT Sim, it is the trading classes that teach a trading platform and strategies for trading effectively. We recommend that beginners watch at least the first 6 classes of the Day Trading: The Basics course in their education portal before exploring the WT Sim more in depth, so be sure to continue your classes in the education portal as well.

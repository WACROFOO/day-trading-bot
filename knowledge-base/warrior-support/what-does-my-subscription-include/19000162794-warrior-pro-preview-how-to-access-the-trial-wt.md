# Warrior Pro Preview: How to Access the Trial | WT

The article below is designed to help Pro Preview members get started with their trial access. 

Not a Pro Preview member yet? [Click here to buy the Pro Preview](https://warrior.app/ProPrevPort).

What is the Warrior Pro Preview Trial?

The Pro Preview is $19 and gives you 14 calendar days of access to the first 6 chapters of the Basics class, 1 chapter of the Strategies & Scaling class, 4 recorded mentor sessions, 3 member interviews, and 3 live trading room archives.

It also includes access to Ross's full low latency broadcast, access to our live trading rooms (hear/view only), breaking newsfeed, scanner and charting tools, and the ability to attend live classes and mentor sessions.

Note the Warrior Trading Simulator (and Level 2) is not included in the 14-Day Pro Preview Trial.

If you are not a Pro Preview trial member and wish to learn more, click here: [https://warrior.app/pro-preview ](https://warrior.app/pro-preview)(Please note that the Pro Preview is limited to one purchase per person).

I signed up for the Pro Preview -- how do I access the trial?

Start by signing into the Member Dashboard using this link: [https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/](https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/). If you need help signing on, please follow the instructions here:  [Signing into the Member's Dashboard (Warrior Trading Member Site) | WT : Warrior Trading](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000052853-signing-into-the-member-s-dashboard-warrior-trading-member-site-wt) 

Once you are on the Member Dashboard, you can access all your Pro Preview content (see illustration below).

[image]

- My Orders and Memberships - Displays your subscriptions and memberships and their respective status and expiration date.
- Search the Member Support Portal - Your go-to location for detailed [support articles](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/home) and a link to submit a support ticket.
- Renewal Information & Chat Room Tools - Contains information to renew or add a subscription to your membership.
- Community and Day Trade Dash - Access the main page where you will find Chat Rooms, Live Streams, News, Scanners, and Charts.
- Education Portal - View your courses and continue your trading education.

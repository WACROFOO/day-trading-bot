# Class Syllabus | WT

Below is the updated list of classes that we provide in each of our memberships.

Warrior Starter Membership

Our Warrior Starter membership includes access to our course Day Trading: The Basics.

This 15-chapter course teaches the fundamentals that traders at all levels need to know to find their own trading strategy. It covers everything from the history of the stock market, to how to read a trading platform, the basics of making a trade, which trading tools are must-haves, why being in the right mindset matters, and how to determine which trading strategy might be the best fit for you.
Note that it the Basics course does not teach our own traders' strategies in-depth, as those classes are included in Plus and Pro memberships. However, any member beginning with the Starter membership can upgrade when they are ready.

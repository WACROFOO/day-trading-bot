# How can I download Ross Cameron's ebook and audio book? | WT

Click here to view the audio version of ["How to Day Trade: The Plain Truth."](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W6BnX1T3KZ0) 

Warrior Pro members can download Ross Cameron's ebook (PDF)! Follow these instructions to download the PDF:

- Sign in at [https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/](https://www.warriortrading.com/login-member/) or head to your Member's Dashboard [https://www.warriortrading.com/dashboard/](https://www.warriortrading.com/dashboard/) if you are already signed in.
- Click the pink button "View Courses in Education Portal."
- Navigate to the Strategies and Scaling course.
- From Strategies and Scaling, click 'Course' in the upper left corner, then look for the download in the Course Handouts section:

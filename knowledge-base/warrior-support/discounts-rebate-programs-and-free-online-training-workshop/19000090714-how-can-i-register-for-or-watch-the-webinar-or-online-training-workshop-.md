# How can I register for or watch the webinar or online training workshop?

In most cases, anyone who registers for one of our webinars or online training workshops will receive a replay of the presentation by email as soon as it is available! If you cannot locate the email within 3 days after the scheduled webinar or online training workshop, please [get in touch with us here](http://support.warriortrading.com/support/tickets/new) and we'd be happy to help.

If you haven't registered for a webinar/online training workshop, and are interested in doing so, you can sign up here: [](https://webinar.warriortrading.com/signup)[https://www.warriortrading.com/free-day-trading-class/](https://www.warriortrading.com/free-day-trading-class/)

The presentation is free to join, and tends to be between 2.5 - 3 hours long.

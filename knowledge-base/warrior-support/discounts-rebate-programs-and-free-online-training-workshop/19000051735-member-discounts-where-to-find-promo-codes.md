# Member Discounts & Where to Find Promo Codes

#### Discounts from Warrior Trading

Members can find available discounts on renewals via the [](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/)[Renewal Information & Chat Room Tools page](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/)[](https://www.warriortrading.com/member-renewal-information/) from the Member's Dashboard. If you are a former member unable to access that page, reach out to us at [team@warriortrading.com](mailto:team@warriortrading.com) or [via a support ticket](https://warrior.app/contact) and we'd be happy to help!

#### Discounts from Third Parties

Warrior Pro members may be eligible for discounts with select third-party platforms. We suggest not opening a brokerage account until you are ready or close to ready to trade live. 

Discounts and offers are subject to change. Members are encouraged to use whichever platforms they prefer and that work for them and their personal strategy. No member is required to use any specific trading tools or programs while enrolled in our program. Availability of a discount for Warrior Trading members does not imply affiliation with a third-party.

For more information on choosing a brokerage, [check out this page here](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000045045-broker-recommendations).

Active Warrior Pro members will find any available discounts and promo codes from third parties in the associated section in our ["Platform Demos & Layouts"](https://lms.warriortrading.ai/learning/course/course-v1:WarriorTrading+DE101+2026/home) course within the Education Portal. If you need help navigating to this course, follow the instructions in this support article: [Trading Platform Demos & Layouts](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000109487-trading-platform-demos-layouts).

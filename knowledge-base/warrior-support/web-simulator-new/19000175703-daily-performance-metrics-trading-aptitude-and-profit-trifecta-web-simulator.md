# Daily Performance Metrics, Trading Aptitude, and Profit Trifecta | Web Simulator

## Simulator Metrics

Simulator metrics provide up to 6 months of data generating helpful insights into trading patterns, tendencies, and opportunities. Armed with insights, a trader can adjust their trading plan to better align with more profitable trades and avoid those that tend to drive losses. This is a highly personalized journey and access to your data is a big step towards understanding how you trade and how you can find ways to be more profitable.

There are three main areas covered in metrics:

- Daily Performance - Popular calendar view displaying Profit/Loss for each day traded.
- Your Aptitudes - Reviews Profit/Loss against fundamental parameters, like Price, Time of Day, Opening Gap, Volume and Relative Volume.
- Profit Trifecta - Displays an overall score calculated from three key areas: Consistency, Trade Win Percentage, and Profit/Loss Ratio.

## Daily Performance

A popular snapshot of Profit/Loss for each day traded. The Daily Performance helps to identify trends and areas of weakness, like smaller green days followed by fewer but larger red days. 

Capabilities: 

- Adjust the Date Range to the current month or a max of the past 6 months of trading data.
- Data from prior days is finalized before the market opens. Data for the current day is pushed in real-time.
- Hide the Daily P&L if you wish to see just the weekly net totals.

## Your Aptitudes

Note: Data from the prior day is calculated by 2:30 AM EST daily before market open. The aptitudes do not update during the day.
 
Explore important metrics, like:

- P&L Ratio
- % of Success
- Average Winning/Losing Trade
- Average Winning/Losing Hold Time
- Largest Gain/Loss
- Identify areas of trading strength and weakness:
Profitability by Price
- Profitability by Time of Day
- Profitability by Instrument/Ticker Opening Gap; gap based upon market opening
- Profitability by Instrument/Ticker Volume  
- Profitability by Instrument/Ticker Relative Volume - see: [Relative Volume Definition: Day Trading Terminology](https://www.warriortrading.com/relative-volume-day-trading-terminology/) 
- Duration: All, Today, Year To Today, Specific Date Range

# Trading Platform Demos & Layouts

The trading educators and mentors at Warrior Trading have made their knowledge of many trading platforms available to [Warrior Pro members](https://www.warriortrading.com/warrior-pro-info/) through various demo videos and sharable layouts. 

Please see below:

- [How to Access Live Trading Demos & Layouts](#How-to-Access-Live-Trading-Demos-&-Layouts)
- [List of Available Demos & Layouts](#List-of-Available-Demos-&-Layouts)

These demos and layouts are provided on behalf of our team and community members. For personalized assistance with these platforms, please reach out to that company's support team.

### How to Access Live Trading Demos & Layouts

From the Education Portal, scroll down the list of "My Courses" until you find "Platform Demos & Layouts". Click the Course Description, or the "Start" / "Resume" button. If you need help navigating the Education Portal, see this helpful support article: [How to Use the Education Portal : Warrior Trading](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000174334-how-to-use-the-education-portal) 

[image]

- After clicking into that section, you will want to click on the "Courses" tab to present a clean list of all the Demos and Layout Downloads available.

[image]

- On this page, you can find all the Platform (1) Demos and (2) Layout Downloads.

[image]

Demos and layouts are provided as a courtesy. We recommend getting familiar with any platforms or layouts in a test environment first. [View our full disclaimer here](https://www.warriortrading.com/disclaimer/).

# Our Mentors, Their Strategies, and Popular Mentor Sessions | Additional Trading Resources for Pro Members

The following is an overview of the mentors that offer trading education through Warrior Pro courses and/or mentor sessions. You can also find additional resources below to help you learn more about their trading strategies, including the most popular mentor sessions from each mentor.

We hold mentor sessions in our Mentor Room for our Warrior Pro members. We record these sessions for later review as well! [Here is more information on how to access live and recorded mentor sessions.](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000090711) 

Keep in mind that mentor sessions listed are just a select few that highlight the strategies of these educators. To learn how to search mentor session recordings for additional sessions, click [here](https://support.warriortrading.com/en/support/solutions/articles/19000104329).

# How to Record Your Trading

### Screen-Recording Tool

Many traders ask us what we use to record our trades. When screen-recording their trading, Ross and our moderators tend to use a software called OBS: [https://obsproject.com/](https://obsproject.com/)

We at Warrior Trading do not provide support or troubleshooting for this third-party tool. To learn how to use OBS, we recommend exploring videos on YouTube as well as the OBS forum. If you'd like to add a keyboard overlay to your recordings to display your hotkeys, OBS has some tools for this as well: [https://obsproject.com/forum/resources/input-overlay.552/](https://obsproject.com/forum/resources/input-overlay.552/)

### Written Records for Recording Trades

Pro members who wish to record their trades can utilize Ross' pre-formatted Excel spreadsheet for trade-tracking, as well as any spreadsheets created by our moderators. These are available to all members. [You can learn more about those resources here ](https://www.warriortrading.com/excel-trade-sheet/)when you are signed into your Warrior Trading account.

If you'd like to consider other tools as well, we suggest taking a look at [TraderVue.com](https://www.tradervue.com/).

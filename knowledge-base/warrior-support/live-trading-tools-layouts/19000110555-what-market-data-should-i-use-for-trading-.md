### General Market Data Subscription Information

Ross typically does not subscribe to full level 2 data, as he finds that is too much information is displayed for his personal needs. He prefers top-of-book level one data since it should aggregate and display level 2 data in trading platforms. 

If you prefer the full depth of level 2, you could look into Nasdaq Total + Open View.

To receive top-of-book market data for all US Equities, similar to Ross, the following data feeds would be needed:

- NYSE Level 1- Tape A
- Amex Level 1 - Tape B
- Nasdaq Level 1- Tape C

You can reach out to your broker directly to confirm you have these three as part of your market data subscription. Some more specific information is listed below for platforms that Ross uses.

For trading platforms that are not listed below, you may consider the same market data as shown above if looking to trade the strategies we teach with similar data feeds. Some platforms, such as TD Ameritrade's thinkorswim (TOS), do not give the option for subscribers to specify their own data packages.

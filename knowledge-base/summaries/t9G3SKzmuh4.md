## Setups and patterns

- **Micro pullback** — stock spikes on news, pulls back momentarily, re-enters on resumption for next leg. [00:42:59]
- **Whole-dollar resistance/support** — stocks cluster orders at $5.00, $5.50, $6.00, etc.; pullback and punch through common. [00:39:02]
- **Half-dollar breakout** — as stock approaches half/whole dollar, it often finds resistance, consolidates, then breaks through to next level. [00:39:02]
- **News catalyst move** — stock pops on morning news (earnings, trial results, FDA approval, contracts), creates highest-probability entry opportunity. [00:30:14]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Scanner (highest percentage gainers)** — identifies stocks with biggest moves in real time (225% top mover example at [00:22:42]).
- **News feed** — cross-references catalyst for stock move (medical trial results, partnerships, acquisitions shown in examples).
- **Relative volume calculation** — today's volume ÷ 30-day average volume.
- **Float data** — shares outstanding available for trading; lower floats (1–5M) more volatile than high floats (34–100M+).
- **Level 2 order book** — displays live bid/ask orders, allows perception of buyer/seller sentiment strength.

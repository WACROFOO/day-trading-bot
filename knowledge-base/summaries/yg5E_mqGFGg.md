# Stock Scanners for Beginner Day Trading Strategies

## Summary

Walkthrough of custom stock scanner platform used daily for day trading. Built by developer team to identify stocks meeting specific momentum criteria before they've made their full move. Includes list-based scanners (top gappers, gainers, losers, RSI) and alert-based scanners (high-day momentum, breakouts). Discusses when each scanner is useful and how to prioritize alerts.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:16:16] Only enable audio alerts for highest-quality scanners: low float and squeeze up 10-in-10 minutes [00:16:36]
- [00:18:03] Disable audio for lower-conviction scanners to avoid false alerts [00:18:36]

## Setups and patterns

- **Low float breakout** — Low-float stocks with high relative volume moving fast [00:15:33]
- **Low float former momentum stock** — Stock that previously made big moves and is picking up again [00:15:49]
- **Squeeze up alert** — Stock up 10% in 10 minutes [00:16:36]
- **52-week breakout** — Stock trading at 52-week highs; may indicate blue-sky setup [00:20:01]

# How to BLOW UP Your Trading Account

## Summary

Deep analysis of account-blowing mistakes driven by emotional hijacking: revenge trading, refusing to accept small losses, FOMO, and escalating position sizes after losses. Practical framework for "successful red days," trader rehab protocols, and scaling strategies to prevent account destruction.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:02] Successful red day = losing money while following rules and respecting risk parameters (no revenge trading, no exceeding max loss).
- [00:07:20] Start each trading day with smallest position size to test market conditions.
- [00:07:52] First goal: build $1,000 cushion with small share size to confirm market is favorable.
- [00:08:06] If you achieve small goal easily, permission to increase share size to full size.
- [00:08:20] If you struggle to reach small goal, DO NOT scale up; market is not cooperative today.
- [00:08:31] Trader rehab share size = 20% of full size maximum.
- [00:09:04] First trade loss ($271): still small, no emotional trigger.
- [00:12:01] Three strikes you're out: if three losses in a row, stop trading for the day.
- [00:12:46] Do not increase share size when you are already losing.
- [00:14:18] Once at daily goal, reduce share size to preserve profit (not maintain aggressive sizing).
- [00:15:16] Practical max loss should be smaller than stated max loss (e.g., $1,000-$1,500 vs $5,000 stated).
- [00:17:01] Stop trading after 11:00 a.m. if you want to optimize results (metrics-driven rule).
- [00:18:08] Hard stop at noon: no trading after 12:00 p.m.
- [00:22:36] Focus on front-side of stock moves; exit on back-side when stock gets choppy.
- [00:23:36] When MACD crosses below signal line, first leg of move may be over; pullback expected.
- [00:24:55] Scale down after giving back 15-20% of profit; scale down again at 30%.
- [00:25:16] Walking away before losing 50% of daily profit is key discipline.
- [00:26:27] Journal every day: track emotions, general feeling, whether you stayed in control.
- [00:27:58] Identify personal emotional triggers (e.g., FOMO, frustration, anger).
- [00:28:59] Practice meditation and exercise regularly to build emotional discipline.
- [00:29:35] Practice consistently (daily meditation/exercise), not just when struggling.

## Setups and patterns

- **Successful red day protocol** — accept small loss, follow rules, manage size, avoid capitulation and revenge trading. [00:01:02]
- **Trader rehab** — scaled-down sizing, rebuild cushion, stop bleeding after big losses. [00:11:49]
- **Scaling in strategy** — start small, scale up if working, scale down if not. [00:07:20]
- **MACD divergence pullback** — when MACD first crosses signal line negative, first leg complete; expect pullback before second leg. [00:23:36]
- **Front-side vs back-side of move** — easy money first 20-30 minutes; second leg gets choppy with false breakouts. [00:24:23]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)** — indicates momentum; crossing below signal line suggests momentum fading. [00:23:36]
- **Moving averages** — identify trend direction; stock approaching moving average during pullback is normal. [00:24:48]
- **Journal/logging system** — track emotions, win rate, profit/loss, discipline adherence. [00:26:27]

# The Ultimate Beginners Candlestick Trading Strategy (Step-by-Step Guide)

## Summary

Teaches beginner traders to avoid cluttering charts with too many indicators. Introduces 11 essential candlestick shapes and explains how to recognize pullback patterns (doji + dip + break) as entry signals. Shows examples of finding first candle to make new high after a pullback for profitable momentum trades.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:00:45] Remove all technical indicators and focus on candlesticks themselves; add only: VWAP, 9/20/200 EMA, volume bars, and MACD bonus.
- [00:01:55] Long body candles getting bigger = accumulation of buying; long body candles getting smaller = trend exhaustion and reversal signal.
- [00:05:43] Buy the first candle to make a new high after a pullback (candle over candle breakout).
- [00:06:35] Upper candle wicks are bearish; lower candle wicks are bullish.
- [00:08:18] Only trade candlesticks when stock has high relative volume, catalyst bringing volume in, and stock up at least 10%.
- [00:22:29] Pull back, get reversal candles, then buy first candle over candle formation for entry.
- [00:05:38] In pullback pattern: look for bottoming tail (shows buyers came in) before entering.

## Setups and patterns

- **Long body candle** — Very bullish; one of strongest candles; [00:05:27] rated +++ bullish.
- **Small body candle** — Weaker; rated + bullish or - bearish.
- **Doji** — Opens and closes at same price; signals indecision/battle between buyers and sellers. [00:08:25]
- **Standard doji** — Opens and closes at same level. [00:13:05]
- **Long-legged doji** — Has really long candle wicks. [00:13:08]
- **Dragonfly doji** — Long tail down showing bulls rallied it back. [00:09:53]
- **Gravestone doji** — Upper wick; price squeezed up then came down; very bearish. [00:11:50]
- **Spinning top** — Small body in middle; communicates indecision. [00:13:42]
- **Hammer** — Red or green candle with bottoming tail at bottom of downtrend; bullish. [00:14:47]
- **Hanging man** — In uptrend context; red with lower wick; signals possible short-term correction. [00:15:08]
- **Shooting star** — Upper wick; price squeezed up then got pushed back down; bearish reversal. [00:16:17]
- **Candle over candle** — Green candle breaks high of previous red candle; bullish breakout formation. [00:18:20]
- **Pullback pattern** — Big candles up, smaller candles, pullback, doji/bottoming tail, then candle over candle. [00:23:36]

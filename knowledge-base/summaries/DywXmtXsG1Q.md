# The Ultimate SCALP TRADING Indicator for Active Traders

## Summary

Guide to the stochastic oscillator (0-100 range, predicts breakouts). Invented 1950s; hyper-sensitive indicator useful for detecting crossovers and divergences. Caution: can create analysis paralysis if combined with too many other indicators.

## Mechanical rules

_None stated._

## Setups and patterns

- **Stochastic crossover** — When stochastic crosses below 20 or above 80, may signal reversal. [00:182]
- **Stochastic divergence** — Price making new high but stochastic not confirming = warning sign. [00:261]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Stochastic oscillator** — Oscillator (0-100 range) based on 14-period closing price; predicts breakouts. [00:04]
- **RSI (Relative Strength Index)** — 0-100 oscillator; below 30 = oversold, above 70 = overbought. [00:178]
- **MACD** — Moving Average Convergence Divergence; more reliable than stochastic in author's opinion. [00:312]
- **Volume bars** — Shows strength of moves. [00:43]
- **20 EMA, 9 EMA, 200 EMA** — Moving averages for trend context. [00:41]
- **VWAP** — Volume-weighted average price. [00:48]

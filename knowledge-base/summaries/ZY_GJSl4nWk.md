# How I Went from RED on the Day to GREEN!

## Summary

Day trading recap showing recovery from early losses using two leading gainers (PRFX and LC). Emphasizes discipline during cold market conditions: keeping position size small, taking quick wins, exiting quickly on losses, and accepting smaller profits. Contrasts trading in hot versus cold markets and the importance of scaling back when conditions cool.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:29] When you take a loss, keep it small and don't re-enter "up here" at higher prices; wait for better setup.
- [00:05:01] During cold markets, use small share size to limit drawdowns.
- [00:05:42] On red candles, don't hold large position size; scale back.
- [00:05:51] During cold markets: "survive till you thrive. Keep your head above water. Just tread water."
- [00:06:50] Scale back and slow down when market cools off to avoid giving back all profits from hot market periods.

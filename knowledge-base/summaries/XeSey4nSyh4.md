# How to Start Investing for Beginners (Full Course)

## Summary

Guide to selecting stocks for long-term buy-and-hold investment strategies as a beginner investor. Contrasts day trading (seeking 100-300% intraday moves) with longer-term investing (seeking 50%+ annual returns), emphasizing the importance of finding trending stocks with established fundamentals rather than speculative penny stocks. Teaches practical stock screening using Finviz filters and chart-based entry/exit strategies.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — rapid move higher, consolidation period, break back through consolidation high. [00:07:26]
- **Ascending support line** — trendline drawn below successive pullback lows; stock holds this line while trending up. [00:18:50]
- **Trend trading** — waiting for pullbacks on uptrending stocks and buying breakouts through resistance levels. [00:02:32]
- **Counter-trend trading** — attempting to buy reversals after prolonged downtrends; discouraged for beginners. [00:03:15]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Finviz screener** (finviz.com) — free stock screening tool for creating watch lists; not real-time enough for day trading. [00:07:25]
- **20-day simple moving average (20 SMA)** — trailing price average; used to confirm uptrend when price is above it. [00:11:52]
- **Google Sheets** — used to maintain watch lists of candidate stocks. [00:16:50]
- **Thinkorswim platform** — charting software for technical analysis. [00:16:08]
- **Day Trade Dash** — proprietary day trading platform. [00:16:16]

# How I turned $600 into $16,013.06 in 20 days | SMALL ACCOUNT CHALLENGE

## Summary

Case study demonstrating building a small account through momentum trading and disciplined stock selection. Covers daily processes for day one trading: choosing stocks (price, float, volume, catalyst), building a cushion through consistent small gains, scaling position size, and using leverage safely. Includes emphasis on trading psychology and knowing when to walk away.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:10:20] Focus on momentum trading: buy stocks moving up, sell dips higher (buy high, sell higher).
- [00:06:40] Do not buy stocks going down hoping they turn around.
- [00:08:01] Limit trading to just the best 2-3 setups per day, not many trades.
- [00:14:30] Only trade stocks that are obvious (in top 3 percentage gainers).

- [00:27:02] Strategy: pullbacks, dips, and breakouts on the right stocks.

- [00:14:34] Breakout or bailout: if stock doesn't break out, exit immediately; no guessing.
- [00:15:20] Be a "sniper"—every trade must connect; cannot miss.
- [00:21:58] Use 10-second chart and 1-minute chart for entry timing on fast-moving stocks.
- [00:22:18] Buy micro pullbacks during strong moves (look for very small red candles).
- [00:28:38] Analyze daily chart first for resistance levels, then look for intraday entries.

## Setups and patterns

- **Pullback dips** — small retracements within uptrends. [00:26:00]
- **Breakouts** — price pushing through resistance levels. [00:27:50]
- **Micro pullback** — single small red candle within larger green move. [00:25:18]
- **Opening range breakout** — break above opening period range.

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **200-day moving average** — major trend filter on daily chart.
- **Daily chart resistance levels** — half-dollar and whole-dollar areas.
- **10-second chart** — for micro entry timing when 1-minute is too slow.
- **1-minute chart** — standard intraday timeframe.
- **Stock scanners** — identify top percentage gainers in real time.
- **Level 2 market data** — shows bid/ask and time/sales for buying pattern confirmation.

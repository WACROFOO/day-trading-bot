# The Moving Average Trading Strategy You Need! (Full Training)

## Summary

Complete training on three moving average trading strategies for beginners: the 9/20/200 EMA setup, moving average pullbacks, and MACD crossovers. Uses live examples including CR stock (up 425% with 14M volume) to show how to identify consolidation breakouts and capture mid-move pullbacks. Emphasizes using 9/20/200 exponential moving averages because they're well-respected by the market and require no custom settings—critical for reading signals the same way other traders do.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:03:44] Use 9 EMA, 20 EMA, and 200 EMA on charts; do not use 100 or 50 EMA. [00:06:48]
- [00:04:03] Price above moving average is bullish; price below moving average is bearish.
- [00:06:59] The 200 period moving average on daily chart (average price over 200 days) acts as support when price is above it and resistance when price is below it.
- [00:11:17] Alligator indicator (13/8/5 EMAs): only trade when "mouth is open" (green-red-blue alignment); do not trade when "alligator is sleeping" (crossed over). [00:12:01]
- [00:20:26] Enter moving average pullback when price dips to moving average support, uses 20 EMA as primary support level. [00:18:47]
- [00:20:53] Stop loss: place just below low of pullback; on 5-minute charts, stop at low of last 5-minute candle.
- [00:21:07] Entry trigger: first 5-minute candle to make a new high after pullback is the scalp entry.
- [00:36:38] Use MACD crosses 20/9 EMA (faster crossover than alligator indicator).
- [00:21:32] Do not trade when 9 EMA crosses below 20 EMA (negative crossover = stop trading); resume only after positive crossover.
- [00:38:46] Size management: add on dips, take partial profit on pops to reduce risk and lock in gains.

## Setups and patterns

- **Moving average pullback** — stock breaks out, pulls back to 20 EMA support, then squeezes higher. [00:16:47]
- **Bull flag** — steep move up, short pullback (3-4 candles), then continuation higher. [00:17:04]
- **MACD crossover** — when 12/26 signal line crosses = buy/sell signal slightly ahead of 9/20 cross. [00:22:54]
- **Alligator indicator** — 13/8/5 EMA combination showing trend momentum via "mouth open/closed." [00:09:40]
- **Micro pullback** — very brief dip followed by immediate push higher; trades first candle making new high. [00:16:42]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Exponential Moving Averages (EMA)** — 9, 20, 200 period; weights recent price action heavier than historical. [00:05:47]
- **Simple Moving Average (SMA)** — weights each candle equally; slower response than EMA. [00:06:14]
- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)** — standard settings 12/26/9; crosses show trend shifts. [00:22:53]
- **Alligator indicator** — uses 13/8/5 EMAs to represent jaw/teeth/lips for trend visualization. [00:09:40]
- **Level 2 market data** — shows order flow and depth; critical for active trading. [00:33:32]
- **Stock scanners** — identify leading percentage gainers and high relative volume stocks. [mentioned in context]
- **Trading software** — DayTradeDash for scanners; TradingView for analytics. [00:06:00]

# 5 Rules For Selling Losers Faster!

## Summary

Deep dive into trading psychology explaining why traders hold losing positions too long while selling winners too quickly, resulting in inverted profit-to-loss ratios that require 75% win rates just to break even. Presents five practical rules (daily max loss, green-to-red triggers, stop-loss automation, sell-half exits, and scaling discipline) to reverse this behavior and achieve positive risk-reward ratios. Narrator currently trades with 65% accuracy and achieves $1.5M+ annual profit by maintaining larger winners than losers.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:08:46] Set a daily max loss rule and follow it consistently; this is a critical guard rail for beginners. [00:09:50]
- [00:10:05] Green-to-red rule: if you turn a green day red, stop trading immediately.
- [00:10:16] If you give back half of your daily goal after hitting it, stop and walk away.
- [00:20:52] Use a sell-half hotkey (not sell-full) when taking early profits on winners, holding remainder for bigger moves. [00:20:39]
- [00:22:02] Set stop-loss orders immediately upon entry; use tight 10-cent stops to condition yourself to small losses. [00:22:16]
- [00:23:28] Scale into winners: when up more than average winner (15 cents), double position and move stop to break-even. [00:24:23]
- [00:19:50] Trade only A+ quality setups; ignore B and C quality setups even if desperate to recover losses.

## Setups and patterns

- **Micro pullback setup** — first candle making new high after pullback; classic entry pattern taught in classes. [00:05:07]
- **A+ quality stock setup** — stock meeting all five stock selection criteria (right price, right % up on day, right relative volume, news, right float). [00:15:47]
- **Psychological triggers** — specific loss thresholds (daily max loss hit, green-to-red turn, half-goal giveback) that cause emotional hijacking. [00:10:25]

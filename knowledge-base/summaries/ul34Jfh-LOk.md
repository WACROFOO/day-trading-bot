# How to Read Candlestick Charts (with ZERO experience)

## Summary

Comprehensive technical analysis course teaching candlestick shape interpretation, multi-candle pattern recognition (bull flags, flat-top breakouts, ABCD patterns), support-and-resistance drawing, and volume-weighted average price (VWAP) as a key intraday trading level. Covers multi-timeframe alignment and trend-line construction for all market types.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:12:13] Bull flag pattern: pullback should consist of one to three candles maximum; more than three indicates loss of strength.
- [00:12:28] Bull flag pullback may not retrace more than 50% of the initial move; retracing beyond 50% breaks the pattern.
- [00:18:17] Price above VWAP indicates bullish control; below VWAP indicates bearish control (intraday equilibrium).
- [00:19:02] When price breaks resistance (prior resistance), retest that level as new support to confirm trend shift.
- [00:23:15] Entry on first candle after pullback that makes a new high following a pull-back.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — strong move up with long-body green candles, light pullback of one to three small red candles, then continuation higher. [00:07:01]
- **Flat top breakout** — price consolidates just under highs with minimal pullback before breaking above resistance. [00:13:17]
- **ABCD pattern** — move up (A), pullback (B), failed continuation (C), second pullback (D) holding support, then strong breakout. [00:14:21]
- **Hammer** — long lower wick with small body, bullish reversal when appears after decline. [00:03:59]
- **Shooting star** — long upper wick with small body, bearish reversal when appears after advance. [00:03:35]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Volume weighted average price (VWAP) — orange line showing equilibrium; acts as dynamic support/resistance intraday. [00:17:09]
- 9 exponential moving average (9 EMA) — gray line serving as first pullback support; if broken, next support is 20 EMA. [00:23:01]
- 20 EMA — second support level below 9 EMA. [00:23:47]
- 200 EMA on daily chart — major trend indicator; above it is bullish, below it is bearish. [00:48:43]
- Volume bars — height indicates transaction count per candle; high volume on green candles preferred, light volume on red candles preferred. [00:25:14]

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:12:28] Pullback retracement limited to maximum 50% of the prior move; ideally much less.

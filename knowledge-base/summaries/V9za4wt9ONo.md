# How to Hold Winners Longer

## Summary

Addresses the common beginner mistake of selling winners too quickly while holding losers too long, creating a lopsided profit/loss ratio. The presenter shows how focusing on accuracy and quality stock selection creates a positive feedback loop leading to better profit/loss ratios and higher account growth without over-trading.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:08:21] Focus on accuracy and quality stock selection as the starting point of a positive feedback loop.
- [00:13:32] Reduce share size when trying to break the cycle of big losers and small winners.
- [00:14:01] Cut losses immediately rather than holding and hoping.
- [00:15:01] Focus on scaling out of winners instead of selling the entire position at once.
- [00:16:30] When scaling out, take half profits at first target, then set remaining position's stop at breakeven.
- [00:17:43] After hitting daily goal, only risk up to the day's cushion amount on subsequent trades.

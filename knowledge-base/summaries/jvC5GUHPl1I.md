# I'm a Broken Man and Here's How I'm Fixing It

## Summary

Personal account of emotionally-driven losses from FOMO and overtrading, despite years of experience. Analysis of account metrics reveals consistent afternoon losses and escalating position sizes driving drawdowns. Practical recovery plan: hard stop at noon, initial 5,000 share size cap until first green close, scaling adjustments based on market conditions.

## Setups and patterns

- **FOMO trading** — seeing good moves later in day and feeling forced to take them; causes account damage.
- **Emotional hijacking spiral** — one loss triggers another → another → account spirals from small loss to max loss.
- **Front-side vs back-side trading** — MACD crossover signals transition from front-side (clean moves) to back-side (choppy, false breakouts).
- **Hot market vs cold market** — aggressive sizing works in hot markets (July-August); must scale back in cold markets (September).

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **P&L tracker** — shows progression of wins and losses throughout trading day.
- **Monthly metrics dashboard** — analyzes profitability by time of day, day of week, stock type.
- **MACD indicator** — identifies momentum; crossing signal line indicates front-side move likely over.
- **Journal** — document emotional state, decision quality, triggers for each day.

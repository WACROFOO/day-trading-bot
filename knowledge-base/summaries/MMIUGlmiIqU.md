# Stock Scanning Software - How to find Stocks to Trade

## Summary

How to use stock scanners (powered by Trade Ideas) to find day trading candidates. Covers gap scanner, pre-market movers, high-momentum scanner, reversal scanner, and gap finder. Scanners filter for high-relative-volume, volatile stocks that match entry criteria.

## Mechanical rules

_None stated._

## Setups and patterns

- **Gap-up breakout** — Stock gaps up pre-market on news; eligible for trading at open. [00:80]
- **High-volume surge** — Stock surges on high volume intraday; entry on first pullback. [00:242]
- **Reversal setup** — Stock selling off hard on minimal RSI/volume criteria; buy first candle to new high, sell first candle to new low. [00:404]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Trade Ideas** — Proprietary scanner platform; subscription required for custom scanners. [00:08]
- **StockTwits** — Research tool to find news/social sentiment on stocks. [00:170]
- **MarketWatch** — Source for researching stock news/catalysts. [00:170]
- **Relative volume indicator** — Scanners show RV multiple (e.g., 5x typical daily volume). [00:247]
- **RSI (Relative Strength Index)** — Used in reversal scanner to filter oversold candidates. [00:424]
- **Float data** — Scanners filter by float range; helps identify potential runners. [00:138]

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:38] Typical trading day has only 5-20 candidates out of thousands of stocks. Goal: find high-probability setups.
- [00:56] Day trader seeks 10-20% intraday moves (sometimes 30-40%). Large window for profit.
- [00:128] Focus threshold: stocks with float under 100 million shares.
- [00:180] Example student: $5,000 account → +$78,000 (demonstrates skill > capital matters).
- [00:449] Counterexample: $100,000 account traders losing daily (again, skill > capital).

# One Trade / Day (Holiday Trading Strategy)

## Summary

Trading strategy for slow market conditions on holiday-adjacent days (Friday after Christmas). The trader took one quality trade on AMCI, a continuation setup that was consolidating below resistance. Emphasizes patience, waiting for clear setups, and being satisfied with base hits rather than home runs during low-volume periods.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:22] On slow volume days, accept lower expectations: if leading gainers are only up 30%, expect a slow day.
- [00:02:43] Trade continuation stocks (stocks with no breaking news) when low relative volume prevents trading news-driven setups.
- [00:03:02] Identify resistance levels on the daily chart as targets (in this case, $12.47, then $15.75).
- [00:03:45] Scale into positions: add at ascending resistance levels ($10.28, $10.40, $10.50, $10.75) as price moves higher.
- [00:03:35] Take partial profits at resistance: sell half on first test of whole dollar level ($11.00).
- [00:05:44] Avoid trading restricted stocks (marked as not-tradeable at brokers) or those with poor liquidity that halt frequently.

## Setups and patterns

- **Continuation consolidation** — stock that pulls back from recent highs and consolidates before breaking higher. [00:01:34]
- **Ascending support line** — support line gradually rising across multiple pullbacks. [00:03:50]
- **REIT patterns** — REITs tend to pop and sell off; may not be reliable for trading. [00:05:24]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- iPhone mobile scans for early morning market assessment [00:00:20]

# How I'm Making $286/Day| Day Trading with $1,000

## Summary

Multi-day small-account challenge starting with $1,000 capital and 6x leverage. Demonstrates position sizing, managing restricted leverage on sub-$5 stocks, and basic scalp trading on halt resumptions. Shows account growth from $1,000 to $2,400+ over five days via dip-and-rip setups and pullback entries.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:41] Use tight stops on scanner trades; scale in on strength.
- [00:02:07] High-day momentum scanner trade entry: watch for alert, set buy on offer, set sell on bid or offer.
- [00:02:39] Halt resumption dip-and-rip: wait for halt resumption, buy on dip, sell on rip higher.
- [00:10:27] Never use leverage on stocks under $5/share if broker restricts it; plan for smaller share size.
- [00:11:28] With leverage: press hotkey (Shift 1) to calculate auto share size (90% of buying power).
- [00:11:57] Hotkey offsets: buy on ask with +10 cent offset; sell on bid with -10 cent offset to avoid getting stuck on orders.
- [00:12:03] Do not use hotkeys without offsets; orders will not fill at intended levels.
- [00:34:38] Flat top breakout: buy breakout over resistance, especially with volume and moving average support.

## Setups and patterns

- **Dip and rip on halt resumption** — stock halts during volatility, resumes trading, dips briefly, then squeezes higher. [00:02:06]
- **Flat top breakout** — stock consolidates near high, breaks through resistance with volume. [00:10:55]
- **Pullback entries** — stock pulls back to moving average (20-MA), buyer jumps in for bounce. [00:11:00]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- High-day momentum scanner — alerts on stocks making new highs. [00:01:32]
- Top gainers scanner — lists most-up stocks by percentage. [00:02:14]
- 20-moving average — support level for pullback dips. [00:11:02]
- Level 2 — watch for green on tape/level two before entry. [00:03:32]

# The Simplest Day Trading Strategy: Gap and Go ↗️📈 #stockmarket #daytrading

## Summary

Gap and go strategy: trading stocks that gap up on news catalysts, focusing on leading gappers with low float and breaking news. Uses custom gap scanner to identify candidates. Covers stock filtering criteria (gap %, price, float), entry setups (first/second pullback, pre-market high break), and risk management. Emphasizes taking 1-2 high-quality trades rather than overtrading.

## Setups and patterns

- **Gap and go** — Stock gaps up in pre-market then continues higher at open [00:03:58]
- **First pullback breakout** — Buy pullback within the initial move on breaking news [00:14:05]
- **Pre-market high break** — Entry at break of pre-market resistance level [00:14:16]
- **Opening range breakout** — Break of opening range high (related setup) [00:17:47]
- **Red to green move** — Stock dips into red then breaks back to green [00:17:42]

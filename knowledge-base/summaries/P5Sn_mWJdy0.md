# Day Trading the Break of VWAP Setup

## Summary

Day 10 of small account challenge growing $2,000 to $11,470. Demonstrates break-of-VWAP setup on continuation stocks and break-of-high trades. Shows importance of MACD indicator as filter for avoiding trades after momentum exhaustion.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:09:38] Break of VWAP setup: stock curls back up through VWAP after pullback with positive MACD; buy the break for continuation higher.
- [00:07:22] MACD filter rule: do not trade once MACD goes red; wait for MACD to flip positive before taking trade.
- [00:08:39] High-day breakout: break of previous day's high or double-top break if previous pullback didn't hold.

## Setups and patterns

- **Break of VWAP** — stock pulls back, dips below VWAP, MACD turns positive, resumes squeeze through VWAP. [00:09:37]
- **Inverted head and shoulders** — pullback low, higher pivot on second attempt, break through previous high signals bullish reversal. [00:09:51]
- **Running up scanner alert** — stocks showing strength but not yet at high-of-day; catch second leg of move. [00:08:21]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- VWAP (volume-weighted average price) — support/resistance level; break above VWAP on positive MACD signals continuation. [00:09:37]
- MACD indicator — red MACD = no trade; green MACD = proceed with entries. [00:07:21]
- Running up scanner — identifies stocks making new moves that aren't yet at high-of-day. [00:08:21]
- Level 2 market data — confirm momentum with bid/ask spread and order book depth. [00:06:25]

# How to Choose the RIGHT Market Data for Day Trading

## Summary

Explains critical differences between market data subscription levels (Level 1 vs. Level 2) and order routing, showing how incomplete data feeds can create blind spots during trading. Different platforms offer varying levels of order book visibility and direct routing options, affecting slippage and fill quality.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:42] Subscribe to level one data at minimum to create stock charts.
- [00:01:55] Level one data includes: bid, offer, last price, volume today.
- [00:02:16] Stock scanners use level one data to identify new highs.
- [00:02:36] For active traders, level two data is strongly recommended (shows order book depth).
- [00:05:40] Level two data shows buyers and sellers lined up at different price levels.
- [00:06:38] When using offshore routes (PHLX vs. NASDAQ), expect rerouting delays and slippage.
- [00:12:46] Use direct routing to high-volume routes (NASDAQ) rather than obscure routes to reduce rerouting.

# Master the Dip Trading Strategy (Step-by-Step Guide)

## Summary

Teaches dip trading strategy for momentum stocks: buying panic selloffs on strong uptrends and selling into the bounce. Emphasizes specific entry rules tied to half dollars and whole dollar price levels, halt risk awareness, and reading level two data to confirm support holds and entry strength.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:16] Buy dips only on stocks trending up strongly; do not buy dips on stocks moving sideways—this will not work.
- [00:02:25] Enter dips near half dollar or whole dollar psychological levels (e.g., $4.00, $4.50).
- [00:02:32] Buy support, which can be ascending or descending resistance lines, horizontal levels, half dollars, whole dollars, VWAP, or moving averages.
- [00:05:37] For small dips: buy at support level with expectation of immediate reclaim of that level; use tight stop at the low of pullback.
- [00:12:48] For huge dips: buy into large red candle (one of top 10 biggest of the day) when panic flush has bottomed, with green volume confirmation on tape.
- [00:16:01] Do not trade halts down—check halt levels before taking dip trade; halt down level must be further away than your stop to avoid trap.
- [00:17:54] Confirm green on tape (buying volume) before entering; do not get in during continued red selling.
- [00:31:00] Rule 1: Need volatility and volume; stock up 30%+ on high relative volume.
- [00:31:08] Rule 2: Entries near half dollars and whole dollars.
- [00:31:50] Rule 3: Avoid halt risk; check halt levels.
- [00:41:32] Rule 4: Trade front side of move, not back side; check MACD, look for huge rejection candles or volume tops to confirm front vs. back side.

## Setups and patterns

- **Small dip** — price moving up nicely, pulls back briefly, dips down slightly; buy at support level for starter position, add on breakout. [00:12:01]
- **Huge dip** — characterized by large red candle far bigger than other candles of day; one of top 10 biggest candles; occurs on panic flush. [00:13:40]
- **Circuit breaker halts** — stock halts for 5 minutes if drops 10% in less than 5 minutes; halted up = opens higher and moves higher; halted down = opens lower and moves lower. [00:05:41]
- **Dip and rip** — price flushes down, then springs back up in bounce; can occur after multiple halts or on intraday panic pullbacks. [00:02:56]
- **Jack knife** — sharp drop followed by reversal back up; momentary panic flush. [00:04:34]
- **Bare raid** — short sellers sell aggressively to flush price down and trigger stops; creates cascade selling effect. [00:09:46]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Level two market data — shows bid/ask stack and order depth; used to confirm buyer/seller presence at levels. [00:20:10]
- Time and sales tape — displays all executed trades; red indicates selling, green indicates buying; read to gauge momentum. [00:24:05]
- Market Depth window — detailed market data showing order book. [00:33:38]

- Moving averages — can be used as support/resistance for entry levels. [00:31:42]

# The Ultimate Step-by-Step Moving Average Trading Guide (Full Training)

## Summary

Complete training on using moving averages (9, 20, 200-period) and MACD to identify trends, confirm entries, and time exits on daily and intraday timeframes. Explains simple vs. exponential moving averages, why they matter across all asset classes, and how to use multiple timeframe alignment to filter false breakouts. Targets active day traders looking for technical confirmation signals.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:04:06] 200-period moving average on daily chart acts as resistance when price is below it; acts as support when price is above it.
- [00:18:46] Buy dips off the 9-period EMA when in a strong uptrend; if price breaks below the 9 EMA, next support level is the 20 EMA.
- [00:20:31] If price breaks below the 200 EMA and holds below it, often signals the end of the uptrend; this is a sign of weakness.
- [00:21:13] Only trade when MACD is above its signal line (green histogram); avoid trades when MACD crosses below signal line (consolidation/reversal).
- [00:31:15] After a big move and pullback, wait for price to come down closer to the 9 EMA or for the 9 EMA to come up closer to price before taking the next trade.
- [00:39:48] Sell off support of the moving average if price is extended above it; this reduces risk and allows for micro pullback re-entries.

## Setups and patterns

- **Micro pullback strategy** — After initial move up, buy the first pullback that tests the 9 EMA; most profit comes from this area before MACD exhaustion. [00:02:15]
- **Moving average crossover** — When the 9 EMA crosses below the 20 EMA, signals potential end of uptrend and consolidation period. [00:19:59]
- **Breaking through moving average resistance** — Price must close above 200 EMA with strength; if it tests multiple times and pulls back, expect sellers present. [00:03:00]
- **Inverted Head and Shoulders at moving average** — Price bounces multiple times at a moving average level before finally breaking through; confirms strength. [00:34:40]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **9-period Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** — Most responsive; used on intraday timeframes for quick support/resistance. [00:09:37]
- **20-period EMA** — Secondary intraday support level; used on 1-minute, 5-minute charts. [00:10:03]
- **200-period EMA** — Most respected on daily chart; often acts as major swing high/low level; exponential version used by active traders. [00:04:30]
- **200-period SMA (Simple Moving Average)** — Used on daily chart alongside EMA; more commonly respected by beginner/meme stock traders. [00:06:40]
- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)** — Oscillator showing when 12 and 26 EMAs are converging or diverging; signal line is 9-period EMA of MACD. [00:20:14]
- **Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP)** — Intraday indicator showing average price factoring in volume; price above VWAP = bulls in control, below = bears in control. [00:11:35]

## Numbers and thresholds

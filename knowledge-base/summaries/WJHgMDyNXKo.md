## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:20] Short-term capital gains taxed at ordinary income bracket (higher than long-term).
- [00:73] Example: $100,000 short-term gain ≈ $20,000+ in taxes (exact rate varies by bracket).
- [00:77] Comparison: W-2 income on $100K = higher tax (income + Social Security + Medicare).
- [00:82] Self-employment (1099): 15% self-employment tax on top of income tax.
- [00:92] Short-term capital gains NOT subject to self-employment tax (slight advantage over 1099).
- [00:156] Wash sale: 30-day rule (sell loss, cannot buy same stock within 30 days to deduct loss).

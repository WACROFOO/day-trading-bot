# How I lost $5,000 in 2-minutes Day Trading

## Summary

Demonstrates the critical importance of having strict rules for when to stop trading after reaching profit targets. Shows real P&L examples of one bad trade giving back half the day's profits and compares this to a previous week where lack of discipline turned $10,000 profit into $17,000 loss.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:06:03] Have a system with set rules for stock selection, entry, exit, and when to walk away.
- [00:06:13] If you give back 50% of profit above daily goal, walk away.
- [00:10:18] Stop watching market action once you've decided to walk away for the day.
- [00:17:08] Once you're done trading, do not check charts for rest of day due to FOMO risk.

## Setups and patterns

- [00:21:24] Daily continuation setup — stock making big move up, consolidating, then breaking through resistance.

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- [00:14:51] 10-second time frame for micro-level entries (warned against tunnel vision from this).

# The Simple MACD Strategy to Spot Big Winners Early

## Summary

MACD-based momentum strategy for day trading high-volume stocks. Uses MACD crossovers as red-light/green-light filter to avoid false breakouts and focus on strongest momentum periods. Combines with strict stock selection (float, price, relative volume, catalyst) to identify setup quality. Case study includes stock up 5,712% in single day.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:08:01] Only trade when MACD is positive or crossing from negative to positive.
- [00:09:12] Do not trade when MACD is negative; wait for crossover back to positive.
- [00:08:25] Instrument must have at least 5x relative volume.
- [00:09:00] Micro pullbacks, bull flags, and ABCD patterns are strongest entry setups.
- [00:09:12] Do not trade the backside when MACD is negative.
- [00:22:08] Buy pullbacks when MACD is open/positive; sell when MACD goes negative or volume drops.
- [00:24:32] Avoid high-frequency trading algorithm stocks (barcode charts like Ford)—they don't work.
- [00:28:51] Scaling out: sell half at first target, adjust stop to breakeven on remainder.
- [00:30:34] Exit signal: first candle making a new low OR large topping tail OR stock no longer obvious OR large seller on Level 2.

## Setups and patterns

- **Micro pullback** — smallest retracement within strong uptrend, lowest-risk entry. [00:09:01]
- **Bull flag** — sharp up move, small consolidation, break higher. [00:09:02]
- **ABCD pattern** — named four-point reversal/breakout pattern. [00:09:03]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **MACD** (moving average convergence divergence) — indicates divergence (strong momentum) vs. convergence (consolidation); positive/negative crossover serves as filter.
- **MACD settings** — fast length: 12, slow length: 26, source: close, signal length: 9. [00:09:24]

# Best Day Trading Indicators For Beginner Day Traders (VWAP, EMA'S, Vol Bars, Candlesticks Patterns)

## Summary

Essential indicators for day traders: candlestick charts (mandatory), VWAP (equilibrium and trend filter), exponential moving averages (9, 20, 200), and volume bars. Warning against "holy grail" indicator seeking; emphasis on trend-riding as superior to contrarian entry attempts.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:00:15] Use candlestick charts, not line or bar charts. Candlesticks provide four pieces of information (open, close, high, low) that communicate market sentiment.
- [00:02:12] VWAP rule for bulls: do not buy below VWAP; buy dips that stay above it. (For trend-following; not for contrarian reversals.)
- [00:02:50] If below VWAP: trend down, sellers in control. If above VWAP: trend up, buyers in control.
- [00:02:52] Use exponential moving averages (EMA) for faster line curvature than simple MA.
- [00:05:15] Apply 9 EMA, 20 EMA, 200 EMA on one-minute, five-minute, and daily charts.
- [00:05:52] On daily charts: check if stock is approaching 200 MA resistance before buying. If above, clear skies; if approaching, may face resistance.
- [00:06:55] Volume bars (up and down) confirm candlestick signals. High volume on reversal candle = "volume top" (sell signal).
- [00:07:10] Do not overlay 20 EMA on one-minute chart just because it works elsewhere; understand timeframe nesting.

## Setups and patterns

- **Volume top** — candlestick with long upper wick (reversal signal) on high volume = confirmed reversal. [00:07:16]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Candlestick charts** — mandatory for day trading. [00:00:15]
- **VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price)** — equilibrium point of the day, defines bullish/bearish regime. Intraday only (not daily charts). [00:01:13]
- **9 EMA** — tighter than 10 EMA; used by speaker for edge. [00:05:07]
- **20 EMA** — common moving average. [00:05:09]
- **200 EMA** — widely watched by traders; used on all timeframes. [00:05:15]
- **Volume bars** — essential for confirming candlestick patterns. [00:06:49]
- **RSI** — Relative Strength Index (mentioned as alternative; not recommended by speaker). [00:10:20]
- **MACD** — Moving Average Convergence Divergence (mentioned as alternative; not recommended by speaker). [00:10:23]

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:05:22] Three moving averages used: 9, 20, 200.

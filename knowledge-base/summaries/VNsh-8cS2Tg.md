# Learn how to Day Trade Gappers and Gaps (Beginner Momentum Trading Strategies)

## Summary

Detailed guide to trading gap-up stocks in the first 1–2 hours using bull flag patterns, volume confirmation, and VWAP support. Focus on entering the first or second pullback with tight stops and trailing exits as the stock breaks new highs.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:00:33] Focus on gappers (stocks gapping up/down overnight on news) in the first 1–2 hours of the day; also trade big percentage gainers in the afternoon and breaking news stocks.
- [00:01:59] Enter on the first pullback after a gap up using a bull flag formation. On higher volume spike, wait for pullback; do not chase straight out of the gate.
- [00:04:02] Entry: a green candle that breaks the high of the previous red candle. Example: if red candle high is 1620, enter at 1621.
- [00:04:17] Stop loss: the low of the red candle (the candle before entry). Example: if red candle low is 1606, stop at 1605.
- [00:05:26] Adjust stop after profit: if green candle breaks the low of the previous green candle, sell. Tight stop allows scaling out as profit grows.
- [00:06:14] Prefer entry on the first or second bull flag of the day, typically within the first hour. Flags later in the day are less powerful.
- [00:07:34] Use the one-minute chart for first-hour trading to identify flag breaks and tight stops.
- [00:09:26] Wait for a new high of day on heavy buying volume before entering a flag breakout at resistance or trendline break.
- [00:14:30] Share size: take 5,000–15,000 shares on tight-stop bull flags because probability is high. Target is not just high of day but new highs beyond.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — sharp move up, pullback (consolidation on lighter volume), then break of consolidation high. [00:02:05]
- **Flat top** — similar to bull flag but more consolidated; enter on break to new highs with heavy volume. [00:10:54]
- **First pullback** — most reliable entry point after a gap. [00:02:03]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **VWAP** — volume-weighted average price; used as support during pullbacks and as a signal of trend direction. [00:02:59]
- **Volume bars** — heavy volume on ramp up, lighter on pullback indicates profit-taking (bullish); heavy sell volume (bearish). [00:03:02]
- **One-minute chart** — timeframe for executing first-hour trades. [00:07:34]

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:01:56] First 1–2 hours of the day: primary window for gap trading.
- [00:04:07] Example entry: 1621 (breaking high of 1620).
- [00:04:27] Example stop: 1605 (low of previous red candle).
- [00:05:48] Example profit: over 40 cents on a single move (from 1665 to high of day).
- [00:06:14] First or second bull flag most effective; avoid late-day flags.
- [00:14:30] Position size: 5,000–15,000 shares on high-probability flags.

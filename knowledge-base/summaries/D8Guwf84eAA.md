# The "Blue Sky" Day Trading Pattern

## Summary

Day 9 of small account challenge demonstrates blue sky setup where stock breaks through all-time highs with no logical resistance level above, causing potential for significant percentage moves. Uses PLSM stock (+400% to $20) and FRTT (+140%) as examples. Discusses why these patterns work better on lower-priced IPO stocks and when to take profit as topping tails appear.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:14-00:01:19] Blue sky setup: stock breaks through all-time highs, the highest price it has ever traded
- [00:01:27-00:01:35] No logical resistance level above all-time highs creates potential for unlimited upside ("the limit is the sky")
- [00:10:44-00:10:48] Buy first candle that makes new high on pullback, or buy break of previous resistance
- [00:10:44-00:10:48] Stop placement just underneath recent support level

## Setups and patterns

- **Blue sky setup** — stock breaks through all-time high with no overhead resistance [00:01:08-00:01:42]
- **Recent IPO + low float + blue sky** — combined pattern with highest probability [00:06:52-00:06:57]
- **Micro pullback entry** — enter on small pullback as stock starts breaking out [00:10:56-00:11:04]
- **Topping tail warning** — decreasing profit per trade as topping tails appear signal fatigue [00:11:19-00:11:25]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **200 moving average** — resistance reference level [00:10:39-00:10:43]
- **Volume profile** — increasing volume as stock goes higher signals strength [00:08:16-00:08:30]

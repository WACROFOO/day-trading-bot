# How To Short Stocks

## Summary

Live demonstration of taking short positions and covering for profit. Covers broker inventory (easy/hard to borrow), order entry mechanics, short sale restrictions (uptick rule), and real-world examples including high-risk scenarios like LFIN and low float squeezes.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:04:10] Short on the ask price when short sale restriction is active (SSR/uptick rule).
- [00:04:45] Cover partial position into dips after immediate profit, not requiring full position exit.
- [00:09:13] Set stop above resistance levels; false breakout wicks indicate weak pattern.
- [00:15:27] Wait for reversal confirmation (closing candle below resistance) before covering short; do not cover into the strength itself.

## Setups and patterns

- **Short resistance break failure** — Stock breaks through resistance (e.g., 200-moving average), then fades back below; short the failed break. [00:13:02]
- **Parabolic spike reversal** — Short extended moves after hitting resistance with no news catalyst. [00:13:25]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Order entry window status: "E" or "ETB" = easy to borrow, "L" = locate needed (hard to borrow), "T" = not available. [00:02:46]
- Short sale restriction (SSR) flag. [00:10:04]
- Level 2 order book for identifying seller resistance. [00:03:46]
- 200-moving average as critical resistance level. [00:13:07]

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:13:31] ABIO short example: entered at $6.38, covered partial at $6.00, profit ~$442 on 1,081 shares.
- [00:13:31] ABIO: up 9% on light volume before fading.
- [00:17:28] LFIN: moved from $5.51 to $26 on day two, then to $142 on day three; catastrophic short squeeze.
- [00:17:44] RKD (Citron short): shorted 100k shares, added up to 800k during squeeze, resulted in $15-16 million loss.
- [00:18:24] RKD peaked at $54.99 on the squeeze.

# Day Trading Strategy (reversals) for Beginners: Class 4 of 12 by Ross Cameron

## Summary

Live trade example and detailed webinar teaching reversal trading strategy for day trading. Covers identifying extreme moves (stretched rubber band), recognizing reversal signals (RSI extremes, Bollinger Bands breaks, pin bars), timing entries on first candle making new high, and trailing stops through profit zones. Emphasizes how reversals offer good profit-loss ratios because entries come at extremes with tight stops.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bottom reversal (V-shape bounce)** — Stock sells off sharply then reverses back up; catch the bounce from bottom. [00:01:08]
- **Pin bar candle** — Candle with longer wick than body; indicates indecision and potential reversal. [00:22:24]
- **Doji candle** — Candle with small body and long wicks showing buyer-seller indecision at bottoms/tops. [00:06:50]
- **Top reversal (inverse V-shape)** — Stock runs up sharply, then reverses and sells off. [00:16:38]
- **Hammer candle** — Candle with long bottom wick; shows support hold and potential reversal up. [00:37:43]
- **Bear flag pattern** — Stock goes flat after reversal attempt; indicates continued downside coming. [00:25:41]
- **Rubber band concept** — Stock stretched to extreme needs to snap back; reversal trades catch the snap. [00:17:35]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Bollinger Bands** — 20-period with 2 standard deviation; candle fully outside bands indicates extreme. [00:20:42]
- **Relative Strength Index (RSI)** — RSI above 90 or below 10 signals extreme; used to identify reversal candidates. [00:17:57]
- **9-period moving average** — On one-minute chart; used as first profit target resistance level. [00:09:16]
- **20-period moving average** — On one-minute chart; used as second profit target. [00:10:12]
- **VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price)** — Orange dotted line; third profit target level. [00:09:16]
- **V5V8 hybrid scanner** — Proprietary scanner looking for RSI extremes and reversal setups. [00:19:46]
- **Trade Ideas scanner** — Powers the V5V8 hybrid and other proprietary reversal scanners. [00:19:48]

# How to RESET after a bad month | Behind The Trades | Ep #10

## Summary

Personal case study on recovering from a $4,200 red month (September) through disciplined reset protocol. Details the mental and mechanical steps taken to rebuild confidence and consistency, including meditation, position sizing reduction, and focusing on achievable daily targets instead of home runs. Demonstrates that even with 73% accuracy, poor risk management destroys profitability and how proper rules and discipline fix it.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:31:15] Start each trading day with meditation (2+ minutes) to get focused and clear mind before market open.
- [00:32:50] Limit maximum daily loss to $1,000; if down more than $1,000 on a single day, stop trading for the rest of the day.
- [00:33:17] Limit maximum loss per trade to $1,000; if a single trade is down more than $1,000, market order out immediately.
- [00:36:07] Start with smaller position size to feel out the market for the day and avoid over-committing.
- [00:38:04] Goal for daily targets: aim for $500/day consistently rather than trying for home runs; $500 × 252 trading days = $126,000/year.
- [00:39:48] Use 10-cent offset limits on orders (10 cents above the ask for buys, 10 cents below the bid for sells) to manage slippage.

## Setups and patterns

- **Red month trigger for trader rehab** — When profit/loss ratio deteriorates and accuracy remains decent but losses exceed wins, implement strict position limits and focus on base hits. [00:24:16]
- **After big green day, be extra careful next day** — History shows that after a $7,000 green day, the next day resulted in $7,000 loss; reduce aggression after big winners. [00:24:30]

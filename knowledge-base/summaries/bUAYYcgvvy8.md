# How to Setup Your Day Trading LLC

## Summary

Explains how to structure day trading as a business using an LLC or S-Corp election to access tax deductions, wash sale exemptions, and retirement contributions available to small business owners. Covers trader tax status eligibility, mark-to-market accounting, and real-world tax savings scenarios.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:03:01] Ensure you have receipts and documentation for all trading-related expenses to claim deductions.
- [00:06:03] When creating an LLC, make an S-Corp election (Es-Corp) to avoid double taxation.
- [00:06:18] Open broker account in the business name using the company's EIN.
- [00:09:07] To be eligible for Trader Tax Status, must seek to profit from daily market movements (easy).
- [00:09:34] Activity must be substantial (done).
- [00:09:36] Must carry on activity with continuity and regularity (easy).
- [00:16:44] File Form 3115 to change accounting method, deadline is April 15th of that year.
- [00:16:11] After filing Mark-to-Market election, notify broker to update accounting method.

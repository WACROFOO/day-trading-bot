# I'm looking for this setup tomorrow!

## Summary

Day trader records from a traveling trading station in the Caribbean and breaks down a winning trade on GPUs stock driven by earnings. Demonstrates buying pullbacks on catalyst stocks and managing risk by scaling out of positions. Emphasizes importance of locking profits rather than chasing additional moves.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:08:45] On earnings catalyst: buy on the first micro pullback with a tight stop when stock breaks to new high.
- [00:09:50] Use one-minute MACD as filter: do not take trades when one-minute MACD goes negative; wait for reversal.
- [00:10:42] Scale out of position as stock pulls back; do not overstay trade when momentum slows or big buyers disappear.

- **Earnings micro pullback** — stock pops on earnings, pulls back slightly, then squeezes; buy the pullback. [00:08:30]
- **Large buyer activity** — 150,000-share buyer visible on level 2; can indicate support level. [00:11:13]

# How to use Level 2 for Beginner Day Traders

## Summary

Breaks down Level 2 market data (bid/ask depth, tape, color coding) for traders. Shows how to identify large buyers/sellers as support/resistance, read order flow colors, and execute trades using ask price for entries. Demonstrates that thin level 2 means thin float (lower volatility stock) while thick level 2 means high float and less volatility.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:04] Use Level 2 to execute trades and manage open positions; keep charts on separate monitor to understand context.
- [00:04:01] Buy on the ask when you want to get in quickly; press buy button and fill instantly.
- [00:15:48] Sell on the ask to sell into strength as momentum trader; buy high sell higher.
- [00:16:00] Hold through bulk of move rather than selling too early on ask; selling half and holding rest gives opportunity for bigger profit.
- [00:10:32] Thinly traded level 2 (1-2 orders per tier, spread out tiers) = lower float stocks = potential 10%+ quick moves.
- [00:10:46] Thickly traded level 2 (many orders per tier, tight tiers) = large float (billions of shares) = slower moves.
- [00:14:14] Large sellers on ask are resistance; until bought up, stock won't move higher.
- [00:14:19] Large buyers on bid are support; can buy with tight stop if big buyer is there.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bid-ask spread** — Difference between highest buyer price and lowest seller price; larger in illiquid markets. [00:01:40]
- **Level one** — First depth of market (best bid and best ask). [00:03:26]
- **Level two** — Multiple depths showing buyers/sellers lined up at different prices. [00:05:27]
- **Tape/Time and sales** — Every order that goes through; colored by whether at bid, ask, or between spread. [00:01:71]
- **Green orders on tape** — Orders at ask or above (buying pressure). [00:06:34]
- **Red orders on tape** — Orders at bid or below (selling pressure). [00:06:40]
- **White orders on tape** — Orders between bid/ask spread (indifference). [00:06:43]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Level 2 market data window — Shows island bid/ask with stacked buyers/sellers.
- Lightspeed platform — Used for demonstrations.
- NASDAQ/NQ (S&P 500 example) — Exchange/symbol showing bid/ask activity.
- Time and sales tape — Shows every fill with color coding.

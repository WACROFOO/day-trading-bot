# How to use Level 2 data in your Day Trading Strategy (WITH LIVE MARKET EXAMPLES)

## Summary

Level 2 market data reveals hidden buyers/sellers accumulating large positions. When large selling volume persists at a support level but price holds, a "hidden buyer" is likely supporting the level. This signals bullish breakout probability. Example: KRTX stock squeezed from $30 to $65 (220% gain) based on hidden buyer identified on Level 2 at $54 support.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:06:46] When observing Level 2: look for situations where large sell volume persists but price doesn't break support.
- [00:13:26] Accumulation signal: large selling at a level (e.g., 33,000 shares) combined with price holding suggests hidden buyer.
- [00:17:26] When price holds above critical support (e.g., $53-54), anticipate breakout above prior high (e.g., $55 resistance).
- [00:21:26] For high-priced stocks (>$30/share), trade size is limited even with large accounts due to capital constraints.
- [00:04:07] Trade during first hour of market (9:30-10:30 AM) when market reacts to overnight news.

## Setups and patterns

- **Gap and go on news** — Stock gapping up significantly (>40%) on overnight news/catalyst. [00:02:12]
- **Red doji on high volume** — Candlestick showing indecision after strong move; often followed by continued strength if buyers defend. [00:03:55]
- **Hidden buyer support** — Level 2 pattern where large sell volume at support level + price holding = accumulation. [00:12:46]
- **Gravestone doji** — Red candle with long upper tail formed when price squeezes up then sellers pull back. [00:10:42]
- **Double bottom support** — Two tests of same low level; if breaks, target VWAP or prior low. [00:15:31]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Level 2 market data — Bid side (left) and ask side (right) showing all pending buy/sell orders. [00:06:00]
- Circuit breaker halt level (LULD) — Halts triggered after >10% 5-minute move; displays on Level 2. [00:08:27]
- VWAP (volume weighted average price) — Key support/resistance level during large moves. [00:14:51]

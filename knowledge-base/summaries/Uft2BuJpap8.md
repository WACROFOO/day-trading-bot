# Buying the Break of VWAP Setup

## Summary

Trading recap on hot market day with high volume and volatility (multiple stocks up 100-300%). Focused on TWG, a stock with breaking news, using VWAP breaks and inverted head-and-shoulders pattern for entries. Emphasizes knowing when to walk away after hitting daily profit target, not overtrading, and avoiding stocks with poor liquidity.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:51] Trade the break above VWAP after stock pulls back and holds it.
- [00:03:27] Take position size cautiously on unfamiliar stocks (e.g., Chinese companies); break ice with smaller positions.
- [00:03:31] On squeeze moves, add into dips and take half profits at key resistance levels.
- [00:03:41] When stock breaks through resistance, add back and take more profit into next resistance level.
- [00:04:30] When you've squeezed as much as you can from market, don't keep going back; walk away to lock in profits.
- [00:05:37] Avoid trading stocks with very thickly traded price action (grinders); prefer clean, quick moves.
- [00:05:59] Watch for liquidity: if a stock is thickly traded despite low float, it may not provide good opportunities.

## Setups and patterns

- **VWAP break on pull back** — stock pulls back to VWAP and breaks above it; entry trigger. [00:02:51]
- **Inverted head and shoulders** — reversal pattern after pull back; provides confirmation for entry. [00:03:19]
- **Resistance at whole dollar levels** — stock hesitates at round numbers like $8.50. [00:03:54]

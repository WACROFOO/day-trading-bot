# Shooting Star & Hammer Candlesticks - Reversal Candlestick Strategy

## Summary

Technical analysis of two reversal candlestick formations: shooting star (at top of move) and hammer (at bottom of move). Explains how shooting stars close as red candles with long upper wick at tops, and hammers have long lower wick with close above open at bottoms. Emphasizes these patterns require high relative volume (5x or higher) to be reliable reversal signals.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:00:22] Doji candlestick = candle of indecision; base reversal indicator.
- [00:00:40] Gravestone doji = very clear reversal indicator at top of move.
- [00:01:20] Shooting star = gravestone doji that closes as red candle; even stronger reversal signal than gravestone doji. [00:01:27]
- [00:02:00] Confirmation of shooting star reversal = first candle to make new low vs low of shooting star.
- [00:02:58] Tweezer top (double top) after shooting star = extremely strong reversal indicator.
- [00:03:04] Take short on break of shooting star low; stop at high of shooting star candle.
- [00:03:22] Shooting star reversal uncommon = stock rarely continues higher after shooting star; more likely to drop.
- [00:04:27] Hammer candlestick = long lower wick at bottom of sell-off, close above or at open; bullish reversal.
- [00:04:20] Inverted hammer = hammer flipped; less common.
- [00:04:32] Hammer reversal confirmation = next candle continues higher (candle-over-candle pattern).
- [00:05:04] Only trade reversals at extremes (tops/bottoms); dojis/reversals during sideways are not significant.
- [00:05:04] Require relative volume of 4-5x or higher to reliably read technical patterns.

## Setups and patterns

- **Shooting star** — long upper wick, small body, closes red; bearish reversal at top. [00:01:27]
- **Inverted hammer** — long upper wick, larger body, upside-down hammer; reversal indicator. [00:01:43]
- **Hammer candlestick** — long lower wick, close above open; bullish reversal at bottom. [00:04:20]
- **Tweezer top** — double top where stock tries to reclaim high but can't; extremely strong reversal. [00:02:41]
- **Candle-over-candle pattern** — hammer followed by higher candle; two-candle reversal confirmation. [00:04:34]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Relative volume calculation — based on average daily volume over 14 days [00:04:43]
- Technical analysis — primary tool for identifying reversals [00:03:43]

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:04:43] Standard relative volume = 1 million shares average daily volume.
- [00:04:52] Double relative volume = 2 million shares = 2x relative volume.
- [00:05:04] Minimum for trading: relative volume of 5 and up (5x average volume).

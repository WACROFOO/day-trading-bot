## Setups and patterns

- **Volume imbalance** — Day with 400x or more volume compared to typical daily average indicates rare supply/demand event. [00:05:54]
- **Bag holder analysis** — Historical low-volume days mean fewer bag holders formed, allowing larger percentage moves without resistance. [00:07:02]
- **Micro pullback** — One-minute pullback during an uptrend followed by resumption of move. [00:28:35]
- **Bare flag** — Downward consolidation pattern during overall uptrend; trade when price breaks back over consolidation high. [00:36:09]
- **Short squeeze** — Short sellers forced to cover positions, creating violent upward move. [00:29:14]
- **VWAP reclaim** — When stock reclaims volume-weighted average price on high buying volume after dip, bears typically lose control. [00:27:45]

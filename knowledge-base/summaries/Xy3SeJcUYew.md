# HUGE Announcement on the PDT Rule

## Summary

Discussion of FINRA's announced change to Pattern Day Trader (PDT) rule: reducing minimum account balance from $25,000 to $2,000 for US day traders. Debate on whether rule protects or harms retail traders, alternatives like settlement margin vs. leverage, and implications for account risk management.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:00:15] PDT rule defined: taking more than three day trades in a 5-day period triggers pattern day trader classification.
- [00:00:27] Under PDT rule: must maintain $25,000 minimum account balance to day trade using margin.
- [00:00:44] With $25,000 margin account: get 4x leverage automatically.
- [00:01:28] Proposed change: FINRA approved lowering PDT minimum from $25,000 to $2,000.
- [00:01:47] Leverage = 4x account balance automatically with margin account.
- [00:02:00] Beginner trader with $25,000: can control up to $100,000 in position size due to 4x leverage.
- [00:06:50] Offshore brokers allow US residents to day trade with as little as $500 (unregulated workaround).
- [00:09:44] Settlement time: T+1 (trades settle overnight).
- [00:09:47] Margin loan: broker extends credit for one day so trader can trade multiple times before settlement.
- [00:12:32] With $2,000 PDT minimum: settlement margin alone allows unlimited daily trading without leverage.
- [00:12:38] Brokers could impose their own restrictions: e.g., require $10,000+ for 4x leverage even if PDT allows $2,000.
- [00:16:04] Small account challenge: funded with $2,000 to demonstrate new PDT rule environment.

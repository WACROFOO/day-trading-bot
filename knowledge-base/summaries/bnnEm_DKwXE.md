# How to Day Trade while working full time and NOT get fired

## Summary

Panel discussion with seven profitable traders on managing day trading alongside a full-time job. Covers practical scheduling (pre-market and lunch-hour windows), hiding trading from management, recognizing when to quit the job, and controlling environmental factors that affect trading discipline and decision quality.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:00:11] One student with million-dollar badge trades 6am-7am West Coast time before work plus 12pm-1pm lunch hour.
- [00:02:32] Block calendar 9:30am-11am for pre-market trading window when employment allows schedule flexibility.
- [00:03:17] Alternative: trade 8am-8:30am before office arrival and trading until 10:30-11am.
- [00:16:32] For Warrior founder: trade morning hours (early-to-11am) only, then shut down charts completely; do not trade afternoon.
- [00:18:04] When trading becomes more profitable than full-time job plus creates environmental stress, exit the job.
- [00:18:19] Do not quit on red (loss) day; only consider quitting after major green day or during extended profitability streak.

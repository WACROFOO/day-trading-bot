- **Gap and go** — stock gaps higher at open due to after-hours news, trades on high volume throughout the day. [00:35:56]
- **Breakout on news** — stock breaks prior day high on breaking news catalyst with 5x volume. [00:34:03]
- **Low float squeeze** — low share float combined with high demand creates 100%+ single-day moves. [00:39:50]

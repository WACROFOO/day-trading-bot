# Step-by-Step Guide to Beginner Day Trading Strategies (Full Training)

## Summary

Trading volatile stocks on breaking news using a systematic eight-step process from stock identification through risk management. The strategy focuses on small-cap stocks with low float and high relative volume triggered by news catalysts. This is practical application of active trading for volatility capture, not investment advice.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:01] Step 1: Find a stock up at least 10% on the day.
- [00:06:00] Price range: stocks must be between $2 and $20 to be worth trading.
- [00:08:03] Do not trade stocks with float larger than typical (QRX example had 960,000 share float as ideal range).
- [00:13:16] Step 2: Check the headline/catalyst; only proceed if convinced it is substantial news.
- [00:15:18] Step 3: Analyze the daily chart; look for nearby resistance and distance to 200 moving average.
- [00:17:13] Step 4: Analyze level 2 depth-of-market; check spread thickness and order sizes.
- [00:21:20] Identify if there are large sellers or buyers that would create overhead resistance or support.
- [00:22:52] Step 5: Use 10-second and 1-minute charts; look for entry on the first candle making a new high after pullback.
- [00:25:59] Use a 2:1 profit/loss ratio as minimum for trade justification.
- [00:30:38] Scale up share size only after establishing a profit cushion on the day (e.g., $1,000 cushion first).
- [00:39:35] Goal is to make $5,000 per day, starting with building a cushion.
- [00:41:00] Step 7: Stop trading aggressively or stop completely once MACD has crossed on the one-minute chart, indicating back side of the move.
- [00:42:01] Step 8: Always analyze results and update trading journal to improve.

## Setups and patterns

- **Micro pullback** — initial spike up, brief pullback, then continuation higher. Entry on the dip. [00:24:44]
- **Reversal trade** — counter-trend trade betting on a bounce after extended downside. [00:26:20]
- **High of day momentum scanner** — alerts when stock hits new daily high; used to identify setup. [00:06:25]
- **Bull flag** — initial move up followed by sideways consolidation, then break higher. [00:03:00]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Stock scanners** — custom-built scanners filtering for up 10%, price $2-$20, high relative volume, hitting new highs. [00:06:28]
- **Level 2 (depth of market)** — shows bid/ask spread, order sizes, time and sales tape. [00:17:13]
- **Time and sales tape** — shows individual orders executing; green orders are buys at ask, red orders are sells at bid. [00:21:25]
- **9 EMA (exponential moving average)** — used on 1-minute chart as moving average support/resistance. [00:25:37]
- **20 EMA** — second level of moving average; used after 9 EMA for support cascade. [00:26:00]
- **200 moving average** — on daily chart; indicates long-term resistance/support distance. [00:15:18]
- **MACD (moving average convergence divergence)** — one-minute chart indicator; when signal line crosses above average, front side of move; when crosses below, back side. [00:40:00]
- **VWAP (volume-weighted average price)** — reference level on daily chart. [00:41:00]
- **Warrior Pro scanner** — proprietary scanner system with developed filters. [00:06:31]

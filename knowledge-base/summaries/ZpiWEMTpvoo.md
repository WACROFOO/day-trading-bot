# Top 3 Candlestick Chart Mistakes Beginners Don't Know They're Making

## Summary

Three common technical analysis mistakes that undermine beginner trader success: isolating chart patterns from broader stock context, misinterpreting support/resistance levels, and overcomplicating charts with excessive indicators. The video teaches that pattern quality matters far less than trading the right stock with volatility, volume, and news catalyst.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:03:50] Buy the first candle that makes a new high after pullback in a bull flag pattern.
- [00:04:12] Enter on the first candle that makes a new high after the pullback.
- [00:04:02] Stop goes at the low of the previous candle (the pullback), never wider.
- [00:09:05] A stock must be up at least 10% on the day to be considered volatile enough to trade.
- [00:09:30] Look for relative volume—volume at least 100x the 14-day average indicates high relative volume.
- [00:10:09] Float must be less than 20 million shares; under 10 million shares is preferred.
- [00:23:03] Draw support/resistance at half dollars and whole dollars where psychological resistance congregates.
- [00:30:32] When price breaks resistance, expect a retest; if it holds above, that level becomes new support.
- [00:38:55] Keep charts simple: use only essential indicators (moving averages, VWAP, volume bars, MACD).
- [00:40:01] Use 9 EMA, 20 EMA, 200 EMA on all timeframes as foundation indicators.
- [00:41:03] MACD should be traded only when positive/open; avoid trading after crossover into negative.
- [00:45:00] Do not buy at the very top of a move; manage risk by getting in near support.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — sharp move up, sideways consolidation, break of consolidation high. [00:01:50]
- **ABCD pattern** — A-B-C-D price points that break out higher. [00:16:45]
- **Micro pullback** — smallest pullback within a move, lowest-risk entry. [00:17:31]
- **Head and shoulders** — named pattern on daily charts. [00:18:28]
- **Cup and handle** — chart pattern variant. [00:18:28]
- **Flat top breakout** — variation of consolidation pattern. [00:18:32]
- **Opening range breakout** — break above range established at market open. [00:18:25]
- **Bare flag** — pullback pattern variant. [00:18:27]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **9 EMA** (exponential moving average) — fast-moving average, primary support level.
- **20 EMA** — secondary support level.
- **200 EMA** — major trend filter; resistance when price approaches from below. [00:39:59]
- **VWAP** (volume-weighted average price) — shows equilibrium; bullish above, bearish below. [00:40:22]
- **Volume bars** — green/red coloring shows buyer vs. seller dominance.
- **MACD** (moving average convergence divergence) — indicates when moving averages diverge (strong momentum) or converge (consolidation). [00:43:06]

# A Major Turning Point in My Day Trading Strategy

## Summary

A personal trading recap focusing on shifting strategy from chasing large winners to consistently hitting base hits. The trader discusses working with a therapist/trading coach to manage emotional discipline and prevent revenge trading spirals. Demonstrates daily goal setting ($1,000 minimum, targeting $2,500-$3,000) and rebuilding confidence after high-volume months. Aimed at traders struggling with inconsistency and emotional control.

## Setups and patterns

- **Base hit trading** — Small consistent gains (10-20 cents per share repeatedly) compounding to large returns without requiring home-run moves. [00:01:26]
- **Continuation setup** — Stock with strong prior day continuing higher on Monday and subsequent days. [00:14:44]
- **Micro pullback strategy** — Buying dips during uptrends for quick scalp profits. [00:12:42]
- **Ascending support line** — Connecting sequential lows to identify support during consolidation. [00:13:14]
- **Double-top breakout** — Trading break above prior consolidation highs. [00:12:08]

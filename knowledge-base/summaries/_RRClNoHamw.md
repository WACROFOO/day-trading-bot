# Day Trading the 3-Day Squeeze Setup (Step-by-Step Guide)

## Summary

This video explains how to trade continuation setups where stocks that spiked on breaking news continue to move higher over multiple days as short sellers get forced to cover their positions. The strategy focuses on capitalizing on short-covering-driven momentum while managing inherent risks including declining relative volume, ascending resistance, and secondary offering risk. Best for traders already familiar with day trading basics.

## Setups and patterns

- **Continuation setup** — Stock makes big move on day one breaking news, then holds above those levels and continues higher on days two and three with lower volume but still showing strength. [00:06:07]
- **Double bottom** — Price pulls back twice to same level, bounces both times; signals support holding and confirmation for next leg up. [00:34:49]
- **Daily bull flag** — Pullback after large initial move followed by breakout; can occur over 3–4 days with declining intraday volume. [00:09:23]
- **Ascending resistance** — Trend line connecting resistance highs across multiple pullbacks; stock may struggle to break above this level. [00:17:26]
- **Divergence** — Price moving higher while volume declining; indicates potential weakness and failed trend sustainability. [00:09:41]

# How to use Level 2 market data for Day Trading

## Summary

Technical guide to reading and using Level 2 (market depth) data for day trading, explaining how to interpret bid/ask structure, identify support and resistance through order stacking, spot big buyers and sellers, and make better entry/exit decisions. Demonstrates real-time trading examples where Level 2 signals provide early edge.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:05:23] Use Level 2 to verify order liquidity before attempting large sells (e.g., check if buyer exists before selling 10,000 shares).
- [00:06:31] When selling, place orders on the ask price to get best execution.
- [00:07:58] Thick market (high float) stocks unsuitable for day trading; trade thin market (low float) stocks instead.
- [00:13:07] Stock selection: ideal float under 10 million shares, but under 100 million acceptable.
- [00:08:05] Spreads: tight spreads = thick market (not good for day traders); wider spreads = thin market (good for volatility).
- [00:17:53] Direct routing preferred over smart routing to avoid broker middleman and payment-for-orderflow issues.
- [00:19:00] Use dark pool pinging to find hidden liquidity between the spread.
- [00:26:34] Time and sales tape reading: green orders = buying at ask (strength); red orders = selling at bid (weakness); white = between spread.
- [00:24:18] Only use Level 2 on active stocks; Level 2 on low-volume stocks is pointless.
- [00:29:52] Entry on whole dollars and half-dollars: buy at psychological support levels.
- [00:41:42] Position management: scale out into strength, don't hold full position to absolute top.
- [00:50:05] Stop placement: low of pullback candle after breakout.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag breakout** — stock consolidates after initial move, breaks consolidation high; entry on first candle making new high. [00:41:50]
- **Large bid presence** — 87,000+ share buyer indicates strong support or potential short covering. [00:33:39]
- **Large ask seller** — 100,000+ share seller indicates overhead resistance. [00:37:53]
- **Thin market characteristics** — wider spacing between price levels, fewer market makers, potential for 50-100% daily moves. [00:09:42]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Level 2 (Market Depth) — shows bid/ask prices and share quantities at each level plus market makers. [00:00:19]
- Level 1 data — simple bid/ask quote only, insufficient for active trading. [00:00:25]
- Time and Sales — real-time transaction log showing every trade (price, size, route, time). [00:22:35]
- ECN (Electronic Communication Network) — bridges/routes for order flow; each has associated fees. [00:14:36]
- Market Makers — create spread by posting bids and asks on both sides; profit from difference. [00:08:45]
- ARCA, NASDAQ, EDGX, INET — preferred market maker routes for order routing. [00:19:08]
- Speed Trader — direct-order platform used in examples. [00:15:28]
- Thinker Swim — also used for Level 2 display.
- Dark pools — off-exchange liquidity not visible on Level 2. [00:20:30]

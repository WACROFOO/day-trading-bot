# The Pre-Market Break of VWAP Setup

## Summary

Teaches the pre-market break of VWAP (volume-weighted average price) setup that produced a big winning trade on MG stock. This setup catches short sellers off-guard during the morning squeeze phase and works best when combined with defensive risk management and understanding of market conditions. Aimed at experienced traders who can manage position-sizing discipline.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:04:12] Enter the first candle that makes a new high after the pullback has held VWAP and shown support.
- [00:07:47] Stop loss: if the stock breaks back below VWAP, that signals the setup has failed.
- [00:27:34] Only trade the first swing back up from VWAP; after the element of surprise is gone and shorts are aware, subsequent attempts are less reliable.
- [00:38:03] Use starter positions on first trades of the day to gauge market environment before increasing size.
- [00:44:47] Exit strategy: when a stock has shown pop-and-drop behavior repeatedly, reduce risk by not adding aggressively on subsequent chances, even if levels look good.

## Setups and patterns

- **Pre-market break of VWAP** — stock drops below VWAP after-hours or in pre-market, then squeezes back above it, catching short sellers who were holding it down. [00:28:14]
- **VWAP momentum reversal** — shift from bearish (holding below VWAP) to bullish (holding above it) that attracts longs and triggers stops on shorts. [00:13:29]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **VWAP** — critical level that separates bearish and bullish regimes; breaking and holding above VWAP after pulling back below it is the entry trigger. [00:08:02]
- **Level 2 market depth** — shows real-time bid/ask and order book; used to identify sell walls and determine if move has conviction. [00:17:56]
- **Bookmap** — visualization of order flow and cumulative delta; shows 20,000 and 40,000 share seller walls that resist upside. [00:17:54]
- **Trading station** — broker platform used for live trading and order execution (this video was recorded from island location). [00:22:48]

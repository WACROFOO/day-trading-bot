# The BEST Stock Market Simulators (Step-by-Step Guide)

## Summary

Comprehensive comparison of three major stock market simulators for beginners: Thinkorswim, Weeble, and Warrior Trading Sim. Addresses why brokers limit simulator features, walks through execution speed tests, demonstrates hotkey setup, and provides recommendation strategy based on account size and trading ambitions. Emphasizes importance of practicing on same software platform you'll use with real money.

- [06:52] Halt levels: Thinkorswim does NOT show them; Weeble shows them (funded account only); Warrior Trading Sim shows them.

- [24:47] Offset hotkey strategy: Create limit orders 10 cents above the ask (offset) to increase fill probability on fast-moving stocks.

- [35:05] Paper trading position tracking issue at Weeble: Simulator doesn't keep open positions; real money does.

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Thinkorswim** — Free platform by Charles Schwab; simulator mode available but slower than production; supports Level 2. [05:25]
- **Weeble** — Commission-free broker with paper trading and production accounts; $10/month subscription; owned by Chinese holding company. [05:40]
- **Warrior Trading Sim** — Powered by Sterling software; $100/month after 90-day free trial for Pro members; includes native metrics. [06:04]
- **Dastrader** — Third-party addon for Thinkorswim/Charles Schwab; $150-$175/month; adds hotkeys, halt levels, resumption quotes. [09:21]
- **Traderview** — Metrics/analytics platform; $50/month; imports trades from multiple brokers for detailed performance analysis. [38:52]
- **Lightspeed** — Commission-based platform supporting Sterling and Dastrader integrations; caters to active traders with 5,000+ share positions. [10:31]
- **Sterling** — Trading platform software used by multiple brokers (Moneymaker, Lightspeed, Guardian, Centerpoint, CME). [10:19]
- **Level 2 market data** — Shows bid/ask depth; all three platforms offer it; Weeble and Warrior include it, Thinkorswim has it native. [06:10]

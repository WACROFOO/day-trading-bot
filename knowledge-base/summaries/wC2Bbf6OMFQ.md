# The RIGHT WAY to use Volume on Candlestick Charts (with ZERO experience)

## Summary

Detailed walkthrough of volume profile indicator showing how point of control (POC) signals support/resistance and sentiment shifts. Compares volume profile to VWAP, demonstrating live trade examples (LIPO pharmaceutical squeeze, YIBO, PALI, BMR) to show how POC helps identify when to exit extended trades. Targets traders unfamiliar with volume-based technical analysis.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:06:00] Volume profile setting: use "visible range" mode with standard settings (value area 70%, row size 24, volume up vs. down).
- [00:07:01] Use volume profile on 5-minute chart, NOT 1-minute (1-minute zoom level makes profile inaccurate). Keep 5-min chart zoomed out.
- [00:14:00] When price trades above point of control: bulls in control (bullish). When below: bears in control (bearish).
- [00:13:34] Point of control acts as support when price holds above; acts as resistance when price approaches from below.
- [00:16:12] Use point of control with VWAP together: price between VWAP and POC = caution zone (mixed signals). Price above POC = strong bullish. Price below POC = weak bearish.
- [00:31:34] Exit trade when price breaks and closes below point of control after sustained rally.
- [00:38:45] Stop loss placed at low of last pullback pattern (wider stop if buying near top of extension, tighter stop if buying on pullback).

## Setups and patterns

- **Volume profile consolidation** — price oscillates above/below POC line during choppy sideways trading; typically resolves lower if unable to hold. [00:29:01]
- **Double top at resistance** — stock bounces off previous high twice; POC line helps identify when buyers are exhausted at that level. [00:28:30]
- **Blue sky breakout** — no overhead resistance on daily chart; can signal extended moves if POC holds. Example: YIBO with all-time high far below breakout. [00:32:50]
- **Cup and handle formation** — rounded consolidation then breakout of handle; POC level marks critical resistance point. [00:38:57]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Volume profile** — displays volume distributed across price axis (not time); red line = point of control (highest volume price). [00:05:04]
- **Point of control (POC)** — the single price where buyers and sellers exchanged most shares; pivot-level for sentiment. [00:13:34]
- **VWAP (volume weighted average price)** — equilibrium price factoring volume; above = bullish, below = bearish. Lags point of control in rapid moves. [00:09:01]
- **MACD** — moving average crossover helps confirm POC rejections; when MACD crosses down from overbought = likely reversal near POC. [00:25:40]
- **9 EMA** — shown as support level on pullback patterns; buyers often accumulate near EMA. [00:24:58]
- **200 EMA on daily chart** — long-term resistance level; tracks on example stocks. [00:21:08]

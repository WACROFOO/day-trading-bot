# The ONLY Technical Analysis Guide You'll Ever Need! (Beginner to Advanced)

## Summary

Comprehensive technical analysis fundamentals covering candlestick anatomy, individual candle shapes, multi-candle patterns, and practical trading setup identification. Designed for beginner to advanced traders learning to read price action and predict trend reversals on one-minute and five-minute charts used in day trading.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:08:00] Candlestick charts represent different time periods: each candle on a 1-minute chart represents 1 minute of price action; each candle on a daily chart represents one full day (9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. ET).
- [00:43:51] Buy at reversal points where price dips lower (potential support areas) using patterns like hammers and candle-over-candle formations; sell at peaks where price has resistance.
- [00:47:20] Candle-over-candle pattern: first candle makes a new high versus previous candle = entry signal; max loss is the low of the pullback; profit target is a move back to the high of day.
- [00:50:34] After breaking news catalyst, first pullback should hold at least 50% of initial leg up; green candle followed by first candle making new high confirms reversal.
- [00:52:17] Buy first and second pullback on one-minute chart; do not trade third pullback (too extended).
- [01:00:13] 9 EMA is a well-respected support level; when price comes down to 9 EMA with bottoming tail, additional confirmation to take trade.
- [01:05:28] VWAP is intraday indicator (does not work on daily charts); if stock is above VWAP it's bullish, below it's bearish; support/resistance crossovers signal control shift.
- [01:07:04] MACD used only on 1-minute timeframe (signals on 5-minute too slow, on 10-second too fast); crossover indicates front side of move may be over.
- [01:09:18] Support and resistance clustered around half-dollars and whole-dollar levels due to order book concentration; test these levels multiple times before breaking through.

## Setups and patterns

- **Hammer candle** — small body with large lower wick indicating buyers coming in after selloff; signals potential reversal [00:26:59]
- **Shooting star** — inverted hammer with upper wick at top of move; bearish reversal indicator [00:31:02]
- **Dogee candle** — opens and closes at same price showing indecision/battle; gravestone dogee (upper wick) at top is bearish, dragonfly dogee (lower wick) at bottom is bullish [00:23:06]
- **Long body candles** — communicate strong sentiment; green body = bullish strength, red body = bearish weakness [00:08:56]
- **Short body candles** — lesser sentiment; in context of declining sizes after big moves = trend exhaustion signal [00:19:33]
- **Topping tail** — upper candle wick showing sellers pulled price back down; bearish signal [00:14:29]
- **Bottoming tail** — lower candle wick showing buyers pulled price back up; bullish signal [00:15:02]
- **Candle-over-candle** — simplest pattern where next candle breaks previous high; two-candle reversal pattern [00:46:15]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)** — oscillating indicator comparing moving average relationships; crossover indicates trend reversal beginning [01:05:46]
- **9 EMA (9 Exponential Moving Average)** — well-respected support level; average of last 9 candles [01:00:13]
- **20 EMA (20 Exponential Moving Average)** — further below 9 EMA; averages last 20 candles [01:01:14]
- **200 EMA (200 Exponential Moving Average)** — significant on daily charts; below 200 = resistance, above 200 = support [01:04:01]
- **VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)** — intraday indicator showing equilibrium price including volume weighting [01:04:21]
- **Level 2 data** — shows depth of market, all current live buy/sell orders [00:17:49]
- **Time and Sales** — shows each individual order going through market creating candlestick shapes [00:25:29]
- **10-second chart** — zoomed view showing micro pullbacks within 1-minute candles [00:36:31]

# Scaling a Strategy

## Summary

Case study of Jess, a Warrior Trading community member who scaled his day trading account from under $10,000 to over $24,000 in 10 days by applying refined momentum strategy and disciplined position sizing. Explores how consistent base hits (small wins) are more sustainable than homerun-chasing, and explains the mathematical relationship between account size, share size, and returns. Lessons on strategy refinement, avoiding emotional trading mistakes, and the importance of consistency over trying to hit big single trades.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:01] Core strategy principle: Focus on consistently hitting base hits (small wins), not homerun trades.
- [00:03:01] Position sizing: Scale share size up proportionally as account grows, but accept smaller percentage returns on larger accounts.
- [00:12:15] Share size progression example: 100 shares making $20 = $0.20 per share profit. Scale to 1,000 shares = $200. Scale to 10,000 shares = $2,000 from same per-share profit.
- [00:10:01] Starting account checkpoint: Once profitable with small consistent strategy, slowly increase position size.
- [00:07:01] Do not jump from small size (100 shares) to massive size (10,000 shares) immediately; scale gradually.

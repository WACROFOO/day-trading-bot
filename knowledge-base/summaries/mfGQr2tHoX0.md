# How I Nailed Trading with the MACD Indicator (Step-by-Step Guide)

## Summary

MACD indicator usage for day trading stocks, forex, futures, and crypto. Teaches how to identify false breakouts, focus on front-side moves, and avoid overtrading on congestion. Uses 1-minute and 5-minute timeframes; applies standard MACD settings to align with broader market. Demonstrates case study from breaking news catalyst trade generating ~$5,000 profit.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:08:00] Use standard MACD settings: fast length 12, slow length 26, signal length 9 (source: close) [00:06:23]
- [00:06:07] Never adjust MACD settings from defaults; custom settings create false signals [00:06:30]
- [00:11:12] Exit rule: When MACD line crosses below signal line, stop trading that move; look for next crossover [00:11:15]
- [00:11:24] Beginner traders should only trade when MACD is open (favorable) [00:11:27]
- [00:13:58] Focus trading on front-side of move and first pullback; avoid overtrading after pullback becomes congested [00:14:23]
- [00:18:07] Consecutive days discipline: Focus on maintaining discipline for 30-40-50 days in a row rather than achieving green days consecutively [00:21:27]

## Setups and patterns

- **Breaking news momentum** — Initial spike up when catalyst news breaks [00:09:14]
- **First pullback entry** — Buy dip to 9 EMA after initial spike [00:10:38]

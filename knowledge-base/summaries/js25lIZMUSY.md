# The Simplest Day Trading Strategy for Beginners (with ZERO experience)

## Summary

This video teaches a seven-step day trading strategy focused on small-cap and biotech stocks with limited float and high relative volume. The strategy emphasizes finding stocks where supply-demand imbalances create large intraday moves, using technical analysis on multiple timeframes to identify precise entry points, and managing risk through profit-to-loss ratios. Aimed at beginner and intermediate traders ready to practice in simulators before risking real capital.

## Setups and patterns

- **Candlestick reversal patterns** — Long-body candles show strong buying sentiment; small-body or doji candles show indecision; gravestone doji shows rejection at highs; dragonfly doji shows support at lows. [00:35:48]
- **Shooting star** — Candle with long upper wick at top of trend; signals reversal coming; sellers pushed price down. [00:37:26]
- **Pullback and breakout** — Stock rises sharply, pulls back (holds above support), then breaks to new high; entry at break of high, stop at low of pullback. [00:39:36]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **200 EMA (exponential moving average)** — Used to check position relative to long-term trend; when stock is far above it becomes less relevant as immediate resistance. [00:42:01]
- **Day Trade Dash** — Custom scanning software that identifies top gainers and stocks meeting selection criteria; includes charts, news feeds, chat rooms. [00:13:23]
- **Lightspeed broker** — Used for order execution with hot keys for rapid trading. [00:40:00]
- **Volume-weighted average price (VWAP)** — Referenced as a level to watch in intraday trading.

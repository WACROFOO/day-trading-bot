# Coping with Setbacks as a Beginner Day Trader

## Summary

Reflection on early trading struggles and the emotional journey from frustration and losses to identifying three working strategies (bull flags, reversals, and gap-and-go setups). Emphasizes the importance of analyzing trade logs to find patterns and recover from extended losing periods.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:06:14] Focus on three core strategies: momentum trading (bull flags, flat-top breakouts), reversals (bottom bounces on 10-15 consecutive red candles for first green candle), and gap-and-go stocks.
- [00:04:08] Keep a trading discipline during rough patches rather than taking a break completely; get back on the horse immediately after losses.
- [00:13:20] Apply emotional restraint and accept loss rather than chase with aggressive oversizing after a defeat.
- [00:14:43] Review trade logs systematically to identify profitable patterns versus losing behaviors.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — sharp move up, consolidation pullback, break of consolidation high. [00:04:12]
- **Reversal/bottom bounce** — 10-15 consecutive red candles followed by first green candle. [00:04:48]
- **Gap and go** — stocks gapping up or down, trading those with trend or counter-trend. [00:04:54]

# A Lesson on How to Manage Risk in a HOT Market

## Summary

Daily market recap demonstrating risk management discipline after taking a $17,000 loss on FGL despite being up $65,000 on the day. Shows how to build a cushion on profitable days before taking full risk, and contrasts this failed trade with EDHL which moved 400% (from $4 to $18). Emphasizes starting small until establishing a cushion.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:05:13-00:05:39] Do not take full risk on first trade; build a cushion of profit first before accepting larger position risk
- [00:05:39-00:05:46] Once cushion is established, can take higher-risk trades

## Setups and patterns

- **Pullback entry on continuation** — wait for pullback after initial move, then enter on bounce [00:02:01-00:02:09]
- **Two-pullback squeeze pattern** — visible on EDHL where pullbacks get tighter leading to squeeze higher [00:03:35-00:04:04]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)** — used as support level [00:04:07]
- **200 moving average** — resistance level reference [00:10:39-00:10:43]

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:01:05-00:01:09] FGL loss: $17,291.27 on 35,000 share position, approximately 50 cents per share loss
- [00:05:19-00:05:27] Starting day already profitable ($65,000) before taking the $17,000 loss
- [00:06:51-00:06:54] Daily profit cushion obtained: $65,000 before taking loss trade
- [00:06:23-00:06:27] Cautious size in small account: avoided high-risk positions until confidence established

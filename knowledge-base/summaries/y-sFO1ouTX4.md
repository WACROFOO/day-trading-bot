## Setups and patterns

- **Micro pullback strategy** — stock moves up quickly, pulls back slightly, then first candle makes new high (entry signal). [00:18:03]
- **W pattern or ABCD pattern** — pop up, dip, pop up, dip structure; look for first candle making new high. [00:40:08]
- **Whole dollar/half-dollar setup** — stocks often find resistance at whole dollars and half-dollars; useful for entries and targets. [00:39:01]
- **Inverted head and shoulders** — double bottom with ascending support; shoulder reversal into upside move. [00:54:53]
- **Bull flag** — sharp initial squeeze, pullback to 50% retracement, then move higher. [_None stated in this video._]

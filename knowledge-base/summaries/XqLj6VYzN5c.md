# How to Find the Best Stocks to Day Trade

## Summary

Framework for identifying stocks with explosive intraday moves (400%+ examples shown) by analyzing news catalysts, volume, float, price range, and using technical pullback setups. Real-time case study of SLNO biotech stock demonstrates entry and exit patterns on volatile small-cap equities.

## Setups and patterns

- **Cup and handle** — previous high forms cup, pullback forms handle; break of handle high = entry. [00:18:45]
- **Pullback setup** — stock goes up, pulls back but stays above moving average, then makes new high = buy signal. [00:23:30]
- **Breakout with squeeze** — when stock breaks resistance high, short sellers covering can create 10%+ move in seconds. [00:15:18]
- **Micropl pullback** — brief dip under resistance followed by squeeze back through. [00:12:14]
- **Double top** — price tests resistance twice; second test can provide entry as it bounces from support. [00:18:20]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Stock scanner** — finds stocks up 10%+ with high relative volume that meet criteria. [00:06:12]
- **Level 2 market data** — shows bid/ask and order flow; crucial for timing entries and exits. [00:18:46]
- **Moving averages (exponential)** — identifies trend; stock should not break below during pullback setup. [00:12:48]
- **Volume bars** — shows relative volume compared to average; high volume confirms momentum. [00:08:01]

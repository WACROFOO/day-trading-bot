# I Grew 3 Small Accounts with 3 Different Brokers...Here's What I Learned

## Summary

Three parallel small account challenges with $2,000 starting balances using different account types (cash, margin 1x, margin 6x leverage) to demonstrate how PDT restrictions harm retail traders. The video argues the pattern day trader rule should be amended or abolished, comparing profitability across account types to show institutional bias against low-balance traders. Aimed at traders advocating for rule changes and those deciding between US brokers and offshore alternatives.

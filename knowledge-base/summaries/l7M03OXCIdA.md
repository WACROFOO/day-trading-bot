# Small Day Trading Account Challenge: $1K to $28K in 16 Days

Documents a cash-only Roth IRA account challenge constrained by T+1 settlement and no margin, forcing one trade per day maximum. Walks through five-step strategy for finding and executing trades with emphasis on quality setups, technical analysis, and trade execution discipline. Includes platform critique of Thinker Swim versus Light Speed.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:07:02] Trade one quality setup per day using full available buying power; maximize profit per trade rather than quantity.
- [00:15:03] Trade only between 7:00 a.m. and 10:00 a.m. ET window; this is where profitability metrics show best performance.
- [00:15:34] Trade only stocks under $10 for small account; cheaper stocks allow more share purchases and better percentage returns.
- [00:17:01] Minimum 5x relative volume on stocks; stocks up at least 10% on the day.
- [00:17:32] Look for setup potential of 40-50 cents per share before committing trade; if move not possible, skip it.
- [00:20:06] When stock reclaims VWAP and breaks above, expect additional leg higher; this is high-probability entry.
- [00:28:35] Sell immediately upon hesitation; do not hold hoping for bigger move—lock in gains as soon as momentum stalls.
- [00:27:45] Use pullback pattern and micro pullback entries on one-minute charts during uptrend resumption.
- [00:36:00] Restrict trading to 7:00 a.m.–10:00 a.m. ET even if high-probability setups appear later in day.

## Setups and patterns

- **Five-step strategy** — (1) Find strong stock, (2) Check news, (3) Technical analysis on multiple timeframes, (4) Execute trade, (5) Analytics review. [00:11:21]
- **Pullback pattern** — Uptrend with shallow pullback followed by resumption; common high-probability pattern. [00:23:13]
- **Micro pullback** — One-candle pullback during uptrend; entry as price re-accelerates upward. [00:27:42]
- **Break of VWAP** — When stock reclaims volume-weighted average price during uptrend with high buying volume. [00:20:06]
- **Pre-market squeeze** — Large gap-up move before market open (4:00 a.m.) due to European traders and early risers. [00:30:08]
- **10-second candle analysis** — Zooming into ultra-short timeframes to identify exact entry/exit points during fast moves. [00:27:35]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Day Trade Dash (Warrior Trading platform) — Proprietary scanner with real-time alerts, charts, and linked news window. [00:12:24]
- VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) — Key support/resistance level; reclaim on high volume signals strength. [00:20:06]
- 200 EMA (Exponential Moving Average) — Daily timeframe resistance/support level. [00:25:42]
- Five-minute, one-minute, and 10-second candlestick charts — Multiple timeframes for entry confirmation. [00:24:36]
- Thinker Swim platform — Broker used for challenge; noted as slower than Light Speed with platform responsiveness issues. [00:05:26]
- Light Speed Broker — Preferred platform for active trading; offers instant order execution and response versus Thinker Swim. [00:32:33]
- Level two ladder trading — Used to set buy/sell orders at specific price levels during trades. [00:26:42]

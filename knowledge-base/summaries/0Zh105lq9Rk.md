# Day 11: Scanner Alert Sends My Account +220%

## Summary

Day 11 of small account challenge growing $1,900 to over $6,000. Demonstrates breaking news scanner trading and circuit breaker halt mechanics on a Japanese stock. Shows how to trade micro pullbacks after news catalyst and manage risk on low-balance accounts.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:07:01] When news breaks, look for micro pullback and buy the dip; apply tight stop at the low of the pullback.
- [00:02:01] Position sizing: use hotkey set to 15% of buying power to automatically calculate share count for each trade.
- [00:10:03] Circuit breaker halts: stock above $3 halts on 10% move in 5 minutes; below $3 halts on 20% move in 5 minutes.

## Setups and patterns

- **Breaking news micro pullback** — stock hits news, squeezes up, dips slightly, then resumes squeeze; buy dip at support level. [00:04:27]
- **Circuit breaker halt and resumption** — stock halts, resumes, dips, then rips higher to new high; second halt possible on re-trend. [00:13:09]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Breaking news scanner — automatic audio alert when stock hits five pillars of selection (price, float, news, volume, rate of change). [00:03:25]
- Level 2 market data — shows when to send order based on green tape activity indicating buyer momentum. [00:07:21]

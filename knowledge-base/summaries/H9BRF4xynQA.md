# I made a big mistake…

## Summary

Deep dive on mistakes made trading a biotech stock (TPST) that spiked 4,693%, focusing on three critical errors: entering too aggressively near tops, ignoring halt levels and gap-risk, and continuing to trade after giving back half of profits. Demonstrates how to avoid FOMO traps and manage risk on extreme volatility.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:08:56] RULE 1: Give back half your gain = time to walk away. Stop trading if you've lost 50% of unrealized profit.
- [00:09:23] RULE 2: If you go red on the day (from positive to negative), walk away immediately.
- [00:10:51] RULE 3: Delete mobile app when emotionally hijacked to avoid checking stock prices while experiencing FOMO.
- [00:17:43] When trading highly volatile stocks, focus on dips and curls (pullback-rips) rather than chasing the main leg up.
- [00:17:55] Avoid buying at or near the high of day; wait for pullback and rip setup instead.

## Setups and patterns

- **Dip and rip** — pullback followed by squeeze higher. [00:04:43]

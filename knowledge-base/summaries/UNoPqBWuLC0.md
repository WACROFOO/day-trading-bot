# Bull Flag Pattern

## Summary

Detailed walkthrough of the bull flag pattern formation and execution. Multi-candlestick pattern combining sharp move up followed by shallow pullback. Entry is first candle making new high; trade quality depends on correct stock type, volume profile, and timeframe alignment.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:03:52] Entry: first candle to make new high above prior high after pullback.
- [00:03:02] Stop: low of pullback candle (15 cents in whiteboard example).
- [00:03:13] Target: high of day (35 cents in whiteboard example = 2:1 risk-reward).
- [00:10:21] Bull flag pullback should not retrace more than 50% of the upswing.
- [00:12:20] Pullback length: maximum 4-5 red candles before pattern deteriorates; traders lose interest in 5-6+ candle pullbacks.
- [00:12:46] No doji candles allowed at top of pattern; shooting star (red close after spike) raises red flag.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — Multiple green candles up, then 2-3 red candles pullback, then green candle breaks high. [00:06:47]
- **Micro pullback on 10-second chart forming 1-minute entry** — 10-second pullback creates opportunity within larger 1-minute candle move. [00:08:25]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- 9-period exponential moving average (EMA) as pullback support. [00:10:13]
- Volume profile: increasing volume on move up, decreasing on pullback, increasing again on break. [00:07:22]
- Five-minute and one-minute chart alignment for identifying pullback entries. [00:15:14]

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:04:04] Whiteboard example: bull flag forms at 4.15 high, pullbacks to 4.00 low (15 cent risk), high of day 4.50 (35 cent target).
- [00:13:16] Volume on move up compared to volume on pullback: lower volume on dip = cleaner pattern.
- [00:14:57] Entry point ABIO example: 3.50 level, first five-minute candle break after pullback on 1-minute shows micro-pullback entry at 4.18, max loss 4.10 (8 cent stop).
- [00:14:58] ABIO continuation: broke 4.23 and moved to 4.50 (32+ cent move).
- [00:17:20] Five-minute doji double-top example: 3.80 high, pullback formed, first candle to make new high at 3.80+ level.
- [00:17:23] ABIO final example: 4.18-4.24 entry zone, 4.10 stop, target 4.50 (4:1 risk-reward).

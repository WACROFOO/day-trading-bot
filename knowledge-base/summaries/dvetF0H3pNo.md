## Setups and patterns

- **Candlestick components** — Each candle shows open, close, high, low over a time period (1-minute, 5-minute, daily). [00:02:47]
- **Long-body candle** — Large body with small or no wicks; communicates strong sentiment and emotion. [00:07:43]
- **Short-body candle** — Small body; less emotion and volatility. [00:08:55]
- **Hammer** — Long lower wick with small body at bottom of pullback; bullish reversal at low. [00:11:58]
- **Hanging man** — Same shape as hammer but at top of uptrend; bearish, signals weakness. [00:14:54]
- **Shooting star** — Large upper wick with small body at top of move; bearish reversal. [00:22:09]
- **Gravestone doji** — Tall upper wick, small body at close; bearish indecision at top. [00:16:00]
- **Dragonfly doji** — Long lower wick; bullish indecision/reversal at bottom. [00:16:56]
- **Standard doji** — Equal wicks top and bottom; indecision candle. [00:16:42]
- **Long-legged doji** — Extended wicks both directions; maximum indecision. [00:17:01]
- **Candle-over-candle** — Second candle breaks high of first candle; breakout pattern. [00:13:46]
- **Candle-under-candle** — Candle breaks low of previous; reversal/exit pattern. [00:35:57]
- **Bull flag** — Sharp move up, pullback (flag), first candle makes new high = entry. [00:47:30]
- **ABCD pattern** — Flag fails to reach high, consolidates, tests level again, then breaks; stronger breakout expected. [00:48:36]
- **Moving average pullback** — Stock dips but holds moving average, rallies again; less dramatic than single flag. [00:50:43]

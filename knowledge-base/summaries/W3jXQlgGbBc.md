# The "Sub VWAP Trap" Trading Strategy

## Summary

Teaching the Sub VWAP Trap setup where a stock initially pops on news then pulls back below VWAP, attracts short sellers, then suddenly reverses up in a dramatic squeeze through new highs. Demonstrates step-by-step process on SCNI example up 125% in a single day. Covers technical indicators, level 2 reading, position sizing, and profit-taking philosophy. Aimed at intermediate to advanced momentum traders seeking high-probability reversal setups.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:45] Setup requirement: Stock must have squeezed up at least 10% initially before pulling back below VWAP. [00:05:27]
- [00:01:46] Stock should fail previous attempts to break over VWAP (creating supply area). [00:05:34]
- [00:04:12] VWAP definition: Calculation = (high+low+close)/3 × volume ÷ cumulative volume for the day.
- [00:06:16] Entry: Look for break back above VWAP on increased buyer demand.
- [00:08:56] Entry timing: Occurring at unusual times (lunchtime, afternoon) adds surprise element.
- [00:09:15] Halt rule: Stock going up 10%+ in less than 5 minutes triggers 5-minute volatility halt. [00:10:13]
- [00:10:23] After-halt: Shorts scramble to cover during resumption, often dips then rips higher.
- [00:13:07] Hot key buy order: Shift + number (1-10) = buy 1,000-10,000 shares at ask plus 15 cents.
- [00:13:28] Hot key sell order: Control + Z = sell full position at bid minus 15 cents.
- [00:13:42] Hot key alternative sell: Control + K/L = sell at ask for strength selling.
- [00:14:02] Technical analysis: Review daily, 5-minute, 1-minute, and 10-second charts.
- [00:14:31] Base hit profit taking: Take small profits (10-15 cents) quickly rather than waiting for 50-cent moves. [00:22:45]
- [00:22:57] Breakout or bailout philosophy: Immediate resolution expected; if stalls, likely wrong timing. [00:19:59]

## Setups and patterns

- **Sub VWAP Trap** — Stock pops on news, pulls below VWAP, fails multiple break attempts, then finally breaks VWAP on surge of buying creating violent squeeze. [00:01:45]
- **Long flag pattern** — Consolidating sideways while holding support; breakout of flag level signals continuation. [00:18:32]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Day Trade Dash scanners — Real-time volatility scanner showing top gapping stocks. [00:11:51]
- VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) — Equilibrium point showing when bulls/bears in control. [00:03:21]
- Level 2 market data — Identifying large buy/sell orders and price level support/resistance. [00:12:30]
- Moving averages (9 EMA, 20 EMA) — Support levels during reversals (referenced in analysis).
- Hot keys — Shift 1-10 for buy orders at varying shares, Control Z/K/L for sell orders.

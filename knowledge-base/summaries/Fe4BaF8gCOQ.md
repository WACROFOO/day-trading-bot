# My Day Trading Strategy for 2023 $LIPO +100%

## Summary

Video explaining the core day trading strategy: focus on leading percentage gainers (usually driven by news) in the first two hours of the market open. Uses LIPO as example of +100% gainer with multiple breakout opportunities. Emphasizes that when leading gapper fails at open, trader should slow down and scale back aggression.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:07] Focus on trading leading percentage gainers only.
- [00:02:21] Trade the first two hours of market open (8:30-10:00 a.m., extended to 11:00 on hot days).
- [00:02:31] When market is hot, push harder; when cold, ease off throttle.
- [00:03:00] First 5-minute candle to make a new high = breakout entry trigger.
- [00:03:07] 5-minute chart pullback + 1-minute candle breakout over high = multi-timeframe alignment for strong entries.
- [00:04:19] ABCD pattern: pop up, pull back, pop back up, pull back; base risk off low of second pullback and VWAP.
- [00:05:56] When stock breaks below support channel, consider trade over; avoid chasing.
- [00:06:00] When leading gapper fails at open and doesn't hold highs, slow down and be more cautious.
- [00:06:14] Sympathy momentum doesn't always shift to secondary stocks; usually everything gets choppy when leading gapper fails.

## Setups and patterns

- **5-minute to 1-minute alignment** — breakout on both timeframes simultaneously = strong entry. [00:03:27]
- **ABCD pattern** — pop, pullback, pop, pullback; A=pop, B=pullback, C=pop, D=pullback. [00:04:19]
- **Channel trading** — stock trading within channel, breakout of channel top = strong signal. [00:05:22]
- **Leading gapper momentum** — leading percentage gainer likely driven by news; focus trading around it. [00:01:29]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Scanners (gap scanner and high day momentum scanner) — identify leading gainers [00:06:33]
- VWAP (volume-weighted average price) — used for risk-based entries [00:04:41]
- 5-minute and 1-minute charts — multi-timeframe analysis [00:03:37]

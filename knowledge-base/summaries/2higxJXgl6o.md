# How I Deal With FOMO

## Summary

An exploration of personal trading psychology focusing on identifying and managing FOMO (fear of missing out) triggers that lead to costly emotional trading mistakes. Details specific emotional patterns that result in unnecessary losses and provides strategies for recognizing these triggers before they damage a trading day.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:30] Do not trade afternoon hours unless specific conditions are met.
- [00:06:00] Tag all FOMO-driven trades with the "FOMO" label for tracking and accountability.
- [00:06:40] Do not force a trade when you miss an entry; wait for the next quality setup.
- [00:07:12] If you lose money on a stock and watch it rip without you, the next entry should be a pullback entry, not a revenge chase.
- [00:09:00] Do not overcompensate for a missed entry by getting in too high.
- [00:11:22] Throttle up share size in hot markets; throttle down in cold markets (like driving a racetrack).
- [00:12:50] Do not increase share size due to emotional response; only increase based on actual market conditions.
- [00:13:23] Do not apply hot-market share size to stocks showing weakness (red doji at high of day).
- [00:13:23] Question decision-making: am I filtering data emotionally or accepting it objectively.
- [00:15:10] Set internal circuit breaker when FOMO triggers are hit; walk away from trading.

# The ULTIMATE Beginners Guide to Trading (with ZERO experience)

## Summary

Comprehensive beginner guide covering day trading fundamentals: capitalizing on intraday volatility (stocks moving 10%+ per day), identifying high-probability stocks using supply/demand imbalance, understanding leverage responsibly for small accounts, mastering micro-pullback entries, and managing risk by calculating entry-to-stop loss gap. Includes live day-one example trading $1,000 account to 18% gain using 6x leverage and disciplined exits.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:49] Day trading is capitalizing on intraday volatility; requires stocks to be moving significantly (10%+ per day).
- [00:02:37] Focus on stocks up more than 10% as primary candidates; stocks moving outside normal 4% daily range.
- [00:04:06] Scan market for leading percentage gainers; position 1-3 are usually the best candidates.
- [00:04:59] Momentum trading approach: "Buy high, sell higher"; buy stocks already moving, not stationary ones.
- [00:06:52] Five criteria for best stocks to trade: (1) up at least 10%, (2) five times relative volume, (3) news/catalyst, (4) price $2-$20 (sweet spot $5-$10), (5) float under 20 million shares.
- [00:13:38] Using leverage (6x on $1,000 account = $6,000 buying power); risk calculation is still entry-to-stop, not total position size.
- [00:14:57] Risk-to-reward target: if risking $1, should have potential to make $2 (1:2 ratio minimum; 1:2.5 or better preferred).
- [00:15:04] Break-even accuracy: 33% win rate with 1:2 risk-reward breaks even; 50% win rate goal; 65-70% accuracy is strong.
- [00:19:18] Accuracy is primary metric; avoid holding losers too long or doubling down.
- [00:27:03] Micro-pullback entry: stock squeezes up, pulls back on light volume, resumes higher; enter first candle making new high after pullback.
- [00:30:40] Pattern recognition: high volume on move up, light volume on pullback, higher volume on next move up.
- [00:34:46] Do not buy middle of candle during FOMO; wait for first red candle (pullback) to form, then green candle back to highs.
- [00:38:54] Not trading third pullback; early pullbacks (first, second) have best risk-reward; third pullback often signals end of move.
- [00:39:01] After identifying pattern, check: Is it the right stock? Can I manage risk? Where is the pattern setup?

## Setups and patterns

- **Micro pullback** — strong trending stock pulls back on light volume then resumes; provides lower-risk entry with clear stop at pullback low [00:27:00]
- **Bull flag** — stock squares up, pulls back in consolidation, breaks higher [00:34:19]
- **Head and shoulders reversal** — first move up, pullback, second higher move (head), pullback, third move up (shoulder) followed by reversal [00:38:26]
- **Stair-stepping pattern** — stock moves to psychological level (e.g., $19), pulls back, breaks that level, repeats at next level ($19.50, $20) [00:31:57]
- **Doji candle** — opens and closes at same price; indicates indecision at resistance level [00:28:38]

# Risk Management: From $2,500 Risk to $76k Profit

## Summary

Detailed risk management framework that broke a negative feedback loop in trading. Trader explains the three core profitability components: accuracy, profit-loss ratio, and consistency. Documents a critical strategic shift in June: capping initial position size at one-quarter full size until reaching a +20 cent per share profit target, then scaling to full size. This reduced daily losses from catastrophic to manageable, enabling 47 consecutive green days and high monthly profit. For traders struggling with oversize losses and emotional spiraling.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:10:43] Risk to reward ratio baseline: if risking $1 to make $1, need 50% accuracy to break even.
- [00:11:08] Risk to reward ratio: if risking $1 to make $2, only need 33% accuracy to be profitable.
- [00:15:32] Daily profit target: $5,000 per day goal (= $1.25M annually if achieved daily).
- [00:14:17] Max loss per trade example: 25,000 shares × $0.10 stop = $2,500 max loss on first trade.
- [00:25:26] Position sizing rule: start with quarter-size position until hitting +20 cent per share profit on first trade.
- [00:26:38] Full-size scaling rule: only scale to full size AFTER hitting +20 cent profit target.
- [00:29:41] Daily max loss rule: max loss equals daily profit goal (e.g., if daily goal $5k, max loss is $5k).
- [00:31:59] Consecutive winner threshold: need 2 weeks of green trading (not huge profits, just green) before increasing position size again.
- [00:33:27] Stop-loss placement: place stops at low of pullback to create good risk-to-reward ratio on entry.
- [00:36:48] Profit-taking: exit with 10-15% giveback from daily peak (hard stop at 50% giveback).
- [00:33:07] Third-loss rule: after three losing trades at quarter-size, exit trading for the day (avoid spiral).
- [00:18:08] Entry quality rule: only trade quality setups; avoid B-grade and C-grade setups in cold markets.

## Setups and patterns

- **Negative feedback loop** — big loss → emotions → desperation → larger positions → more losses → account implosion [00:08:14]
- **Positive feedback loop** — high accuracy → better ratio → consistency → confidence → larger size → higher profit [00:24:34]
- **Pullback and dip buy** — stock pops, pulls back to create support level, buy at support for next leg [00:12:48]
- **Micro pullback entry** — enter on small dips within larger uptrend after breaking through round numbers ($7, $8) [00:14:05]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- MACD indicator — confirms trend direction during pullbacks; used to identify optimal entry timing [00:05:59]
- Trading metrics software — tracks win rate, profit/loss per trade, consistency by time period to identify weaknesses [00:19:53]

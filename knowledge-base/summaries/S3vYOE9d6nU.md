# The "Stair Step" Day Trading Strategy

## Summary

Detailed breakdown of SPRC stock trade up 100%+ using the stair-step pattern: initial news spike (algo spike), pullback, second step up, pullback, third step up. Shows how to identify leading gappers, recognize when to enter, use trend line support, and avoid false breakouts. Emphasizes volume profile (high on green, light on red) and psychological level breaks ($6, $6.50, $7).

# 3 FACTS on Why Beginner Traders LOSE MONEY

## Summary

A detailed walkthrough of three critical mistakes beginner day traders make during high-pressure trading, using live examples from a small account challenge where the trader documents red days and recovery attempts. Covers forcing low-quality setups, overtrading, chasing entries with FOMO, and the emotional psychology that drives catastrophic losses.

- [00:04:55] Avoid trading stocks below $5 per share when using leverage (limits buying power efficiency).

## Setups and patterns

- **Flat-top breakout** — consolidation at highs, break through previous day resistance with volume. [00:05:24]
- **Daily breakout** — break of prior day high on intraday chart, targeting move through whole dollar level. [00:31:50]
- **Higher day bounce** — stock bounces off low of day, breaks highs with multiple green candles. [00:10:38]
- **One-minute pullback and rip** — consolidation on 1-minute chart followed by squeeze through resistance. [00:06:39]

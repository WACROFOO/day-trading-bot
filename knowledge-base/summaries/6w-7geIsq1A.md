# Day Trading the 1-Minute Dip & Rip Strategy

## Summary

Day trader teaches the "dip and rip" strategy for trading stocks with strong momentum on the one-minute timeframe. The strategy involves buying dips during strong upward moves and selling into the first squeeze toward the next whole dollar or half-dollar level. Designed for traders who want to capture momentum when pullbacks are minimal.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:14:18] First trade: use 5,000 share maximum until up $1,000 on the day; then reassess position sizing.
- [00:08:24] Buy the dip on one-minute pullbacks when stock is squeezing up; target the next whole dollar or half-dollar level for exit.
- [00:05:27] Use level two to gauge sentiment and volume strength; avoid trading when heavy selling is evident.
- [00:14:35] Do not overstay the trade; step off when the move shows signs of exhaustion or volume selling increases.

## Setups and patterns

- **Dip and rip** — stock makes strong move up, pulls back slightly, then squeezes up again to new high; buy the dip for the rip. [00:00:07]
- **Micro pullback** — very tight pullback on strong stocks; entry point for dip trades. [00:05:04]

# My #1 Indicator for HOLDING WINNERS LONGER

## Summary

Heikin-Ashi (average bar) candlestick charts smooth price action and help traders hold winning positions longer by displaying cleaner trend visualization. Use traditional candlesticks for precise entries, then reference Heikin-Ashi charts for exit signals when the first candle turns red, improving profitability by reducing premature exits.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:03:06] Use traditional candlestick charts for finding entry points on micro pullbacks; use Heikin-Ashi side-by-side as reference.
- [00:03:32] Buy on first candle that makes a new high after the pullback; stop at low of pullback.
- [00:05:16] Hold position until first Heikin-Ashi candle closes red or goes candle under candle (breaks low of prior candle).
- [00:14:09] Exit when Heikin-Ashi shows first candle going red; do not wait for full candle close.
- [00:24:27] Modify hotkey: change sell order from "sell full position" to "sell half position" to force discipline in holding winners.
- [00:26:10] Hold trailing portion of position (after selling half) until Heikin-Ashi reversal signals, then exit.

## Setups and patterns

- **Micro pullback (first pullback after initial strong move)** — Entry on first candle making new high after pullback. [00:04:33]
- **Heikin-Ashi trend continuation** — Long stretches of green candles indicate sustained uptrend; red candles indicate weakness. [00:13:46]
- **Candle under candle (breakdown)** — When current candle breaks the low of prior candle, indicates potential reversal. [00:18:34]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Heikin-Ashi candlestick charts — Smoothed candlesticks averaging prior bar and current bar for cleaner trend visualization. [00:00:05]
- Traditional candlestick charts — Best for finding precise entries; MACD for confirming trend strength. [00:09:55]
- Thinkorswim platform — Used for displaying both chart types side-by-side. [00:21:54]

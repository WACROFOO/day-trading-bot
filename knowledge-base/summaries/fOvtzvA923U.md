# The Simplest Strategy for Trading Penny Stocks #stockmarket

## Summary

A guide to trading penny stocks focusing on stocks between $1 and $20 on NASDAQ/NYSE rather than sub-dollar OTC stocks. The strategy emphasizes five times relative volume and leading gainers to reduce risk and increase profitability compared to trading illiquid sub-penny stocks.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:04:23] Focus on stocks between $1 and $3 for sweet spot volatility and speed.
- [00:04:30] Prefer price range $2 to $10, acceptable up to $20.
- [00:07:31] Stocks above $1 trade with minimum 1-penny spread.
- [00:15:34] Trade leading gainers in the market (use scanners to identify them).
- [00:17:21] Avoid OTC and pink sheet stocks; stick to NASDAQ or NYSE listed only.
- [00:17:27] Avoid sub-penny and triple-zero stocks.
- [00:14:03] Focus on stocks with 5x relative volume (89% of profits come from this metric).
- [00:16:54] Look for news/catalyst as primary driver.

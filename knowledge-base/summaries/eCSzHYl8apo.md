# How to Use Trade-Ideas Stock Scanners for Day Trading

## Summary

Tutorial on using Trade-Ideas platform and custom stock scanners for identifying day trading opportunities. Demonstrates gap scanner for pre-market screening, momentum scanner for intraday moves, and reversal scanner for fade trades. Shows how scanners feed into trading strategies and the importance of volume hunting.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:32] Market open first 5 minutes: most volatile, highest volume period; book $400-700 profit, then use as cushion for rest of day.
- [00:09:58] Reversal strategy: short the first candle making new low after consecutive green candles, stop at the high.
- [00:10:39] Pullback trade: look for stocks that made big move, now consolidating in top 25% of range; 20 cents to 2% off high.

## Setups and patterns

- **Gap and go** — stock gaps up pre-market with price action, breaks through resistance at open. [00:01:36]
- **Consecutive candle reversal** — 4+ consecutive green candles signal exhaustion and potential reversal lower. [00:10:14]
- **Pullback consolidation** — recent big move, now pulling back but staying in top of range. [00:12:44]
- **High-day momentum** — 5-minute volume spike indicating buyers stepping in; volume surge triggers entry. [00:06:09]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Trade-Ideas stock scanner software — powered by AI-based alerts; customizable layout saves. [00:00:10]
- Gap scanner — runs pre-market (8:45-9:30 a.m.) showing stocks gapping up/down; color-coded green (up) and red (down). [00:01:39]
- High-day momentum scanner — runs until 11:30 a.m.; shows stocks catching a bid with 5-minute volume spike. [00:06:08]
- Multi-strategy scanner — shows volume spikes, top/bottom reversals; some traders prefer, others skip. [00:07:38]
- Surging up scanner — shows stocks with most 5-minute volume at any given time; real-time volume indicator. [00:07:39]
- Reversal scanner (V8) — 4+ consecutive candles; stricter filter. [00:10:13]
- Reversal scanner (V5) — 3+ consecutive candles; looser filter. [00:10:30]
- Pullback scanner — stocks recently made big move, now consolidating in top 25% of range. [00:12:43]
- Relative volume leaders scanner — shows highest volume stocks of the day for volume hunting. [00:13:56]

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:01:26] Market open volume peak: occurs in first 30 minutes, then decreases throughout day.
- [00:03:36] AXN example: 1.1 million shares pre-market volume (vs. LPTH with only 300 shares at 9:15).
- [00:06:31] LPTH trade: jumped in at $2, made $1,000 profit in first 5 minutes; stock moved from $1.53 to $2.49 in one candle.
- [00:08:26] MXL surging up scanner volume: 52,000 shares in last 5 minutes.

# 3 Doji Candlesticks Patterns That are Buy/Sell Indicators

## Summary

Technical analysis instruction on three types of doji candlestick formations: gravestone doji, dragonfly doji, and standard (long-legged) doji. Explains how each represents indecision and can signal reversals when occurring at extremes (tops of rallies or bottoms of sell-offs). Includes live chart examples showing how traders use dojis to enter/exit positions.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:00:10] A doji candlestick has open and close at approximately the same price.
- [00:01:18] Doji candlesticks communicate indecision—most significant at extremes (after big moves up or down).
- [00:01:18] Dojis during sideways consolidation are not significant (already indecisive).
- [00:01:40] Gravestone doji at top of squeeze = sell signal. Appears after series of green candles topping out.
- [00:02:49] Dragonfly doji at bottom of sell-off = potential buy signal or cover short position signal.
- [00:04:01] Long-legged doji (cross with upper and lower wicks) at top of run = potential reversal but requires confirmation.
- [00:06:48] Confirmation on dragonfly doji: next candle should move higher to confirm reversal.
- [00:07:48] Use dojis with moving averages, VWAP, and volume weight to determine trade decision.

## Setups and patterns

- **Gravestone doji** — tiny body at top of candle with long upper wick; bearish reversal indicator at top of move. [00:00:46]
- **Dragonfly doji** — tiny body with long lower wick; bullish reversal indicator at bottom of move. [00:03:18]
- **Long-legged doji** — cross pattern with both upper and lower wicks; appears during strong move consolidation. [00:05:00]
- **Hammer candle** — similar to dragonfly but with larger body; stronger reversal signal. [00:04:07]
- **Shooting star** — similar to gravestone but with larger body; strong reversal signal. [00:02:56]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- VWAP (volume-weighted average price) — factored into reversal confirmation [00:04:31]
- Moving averages — considered in reversal decision-making [00:04:31]

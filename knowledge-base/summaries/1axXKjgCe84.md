# How to Cut Losers Faster

## Summary

Psychology and mechanics of cutting losing trades quickly. Demonstrates how a 1:1 profit-to-loss ratio requires only 50% win rate, while 1:2 negative ratios require 66% accuracy. Covers hotkey shortcuts (Ctrl+Z bailout), trade quality focus, smaller position sizing, and scaling strategies to reduce drawdowns.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:06:02] Keep hand on hotkeys (Ctrl+Z) positioned to exit immediately upon hesitation.
- [00:12:34] Focus on highest quality trades only; trade front-side of moves when MACD is pulling away, moving averages trending up.
- [00:13:22] Trade with smaller share size (100-3,000 shares) to reduce slippage on bail exits.
- [00:13:32] Set hard stops at 8-10 cents on small size, or set stop at break-even once up 5-10 cents.
- [00:14:20] Scale out strategy: sell half on first dip if trade loses 5 cents after entry; keeps losses small if trade continues down.

## Setups and patterns

- **Pullback to moving average on front-side of move** — Buy when stock stair-steps up on strong momentum. [00:12:41]
- **Scaling in on dips** — Enter at 4.95, add at 5.10 on confirmation, close at break-even if first entry fails. [00:14:31]

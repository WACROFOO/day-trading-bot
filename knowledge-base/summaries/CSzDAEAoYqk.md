# How to Read BUY and SELL Signals on Candlestick Charts (Beginner to Advanced)

## Summary

Analysis of identifying true vs. false buy/sell signals using candlestick pattern recognition, MACD crossover, volume profile, and market depth (level-2). Demonstrates how topping tails, high-volume selling candles, and large order walls indicate weakness to avoid false breakout traps.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:15] Enter only when stock price is between $2 and $20, the price range with most momentum.
- [00:02:23] Stock must be up at least 10% on day, breaking standard deviation of typical stock volatility.
- [00:02:32] Relative volume must be at least five times average.
- [00:02:56] Catalyst (news event) must be present.
- [00:03:25] Float must be 1.3 million shares or lower for strong moves (example: DTSS had 1.3M float).
- [00:07:47] Trade MACD only when open and moving upward; avoid trading after MACD crossover into negative territory.
- [00:08:11] Look for clean candlestick patterns (bull flag, clean pullbacks); avoid trading when topping tails appear.
- [00:10:00] High-volume red candles indicate selling pressure; avoid entry when this dominates.
- [00:15:00] When level-2 shows large seller wall (e.g., 18,000 shares at $7.10, 30,000 at $7.20), anticipate potential false breakout after short-stop squeeze.
- [00:17:53] Sit on sidelines if setup is uncertain; do not force trades into choppy price action.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — sharp move up, micro pullback, break of consolidation high. [00:08:21]
- **Topping tail** — price rises then sellers pull it back down, indicating seller dominance. [00:10:34] Multiple topping tails in a row signal consolidation before potential breakout or breakdown.
- **False breakout trap** — sellers place large orders to create wall; stock breaks above on short-stop squeeze and volume; then reverses and flushes back down as shorts cover and longs bail. [00:15:04]
- **Ascending support trend** — stock makes higher lows, indicating intermediate support level. [00:13:40]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)** — crossover indicates moving averages converging and entering consolidation period; trade opens only when MACD is positive and rising. [00:07:50]
- **Volume weight average price (VWAP)** — orange line on chart showing expected fair value based on volume; break above/below signals strength. [00:10:02]
- **Level-2 market depth** — heat map showing order book; reveals walls of sellers/buyers not visible on chart alone. [00:14:18]
- **200-moving average (daily)** — upside target on beaten-up bounce setups if stock breaks above it. [00:16:26]

# How to Trade Pre-Market & After Hours

## Summary

Complete guide to trading during pre-market (4am-9:30am EST) and after-hours (4pm-8pm EST) sessions. Covers why to trade extended hours (capture breaking news moves), technical setup on trading platforms (Thinkorswim, Lightspeed, Weeble), order types and limitations, advantages of extended hours trading, and strategies for handling micro pullbacks. Designed for day traders wanting to participate in early-morning or late-day volatility.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:04:44] Stop orders not allowed pre-market or after hours; only limit orders permitted.
- [00:43:01] Cannot place market orders pre-market or after hours; must use limit orders only.
- [00:26:32] After hours trading does not allow leverage; must liquidate all margin positions by 4:00pm ET or broker auto-liquidates.
- [00:26:40] Pre-market trading allows use of leverage (unlike after-hours).
- [00:38:28] Daily charts should not show pre-market or after-hours highs/lows; only regular trading hours candles standard.
- [00:03:07] Pre-market session: 4:00am to 9:30am EST; regular hours: 9:30am to 4:00pm; after-hours: 4:00pm to 8:00pm EST.
- [00:18:46] Most retail brokers do not allow trading before 7:00am; 7am-9:30am is most active pre-market window.
- [00:17:10] Stock hits scanner, pull up ticker to check news and catalyst before entering; wait for first pullback to form before initial entry.
- [00:16:42] Preferred strategy: wait for stock to pop, pause for micro pullback, then buy the dip for next leg higher (micro pullback strategy).

## Setups and patterns

- **Breaking news catalysts** — pre-market and after-hours sessions optimal for trading news-driven gaps and squeezes [00:00:03]
- **Micro pullbacks** — small dips within larger uptrend used as entry points for continuation moves [00:16:40]
- **High-frequency trading algorithm kickoff** — first surge of volume immediately upon news hitting wire, then pause before retail buyers jump in [00:14:22]

# The Ultimate Relative Strength Index (RSI) Trading Strategy

## Summary

RSI (Relative Strength Index) identifies overbought/oversold extremes for reversal trading. Standard settings: 14 length period, 30/70 boundaries. Best used for reversal trades (short at RSI >70, long at RSI <30) when combined with Bollinger Bands and price action. Divergences (price makes new high but RSI doesn't) signal potential reversals.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:04] RSI upper extreme: above 70; lower extreme: below 30. Only trade reversals in these extreme zones.
- [00:07:17] Trade reversals when RSI is at extreme levels AND candlestick pattern confirms (e.g., shooting star at top).
- [00:13:27] Divergence pattern: when price makes new high but RSI fails to make new high, expect reversal soon.
- [00:15:01] Short reversal setup: price outside Bollinger Bands + RSI >70 + candle showing weakness (e.g., red candle).
- [00:13:33] Hold short position with stop at high of move or wait for reversal candle.
- [00:20:25] For beginners doing reversal trading: focus on 10-15+ consecutive green/red candles, then short/long on first reversal candle.
- [00:22:09] Use RSI divergence scan (custom scanner) to find stocks at extreme RSI levels automatically.

## Setups and patterns

- **Shooting star candle** — Long body green candles followed by candle opening here, squeezing high, then closing red. [00:17:29]
- **Consecutive candle reversal** — 14+ green candles in a row; first red candle often starts pullback. [00:19:57]
- **Divergence reversal** — Price makes new high while RSI makes lower high; indicates momentum weakness. [00:13:27]
- **Bollinger Bands breakout reversal** — Price squeezes outside top/bottom Bollinger Band + RSI extreme + reversal candle. [00:15:29]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Relative Strength Index (RSI) — Oscillator ranging 0-100; measures momentum strength at 14-period standard setting. [00:01:52]
- Bollinger Bands — Channel around price; 99% of price action within bands; breakouts often lead to reversions. [00:15:39]
- MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) — Used on 1-minute charts to confirm trend changes. [00:06:42]
- Moving averages (9, 20, 200 exponential) — Trend filters on 1-min and 5-min charts. [00:01:26]
- Custom RSI Trend Scanner — Scans entire market for stocks with RSI 99-100 or 0-1 (extremes). [00:14:12]

# Year in Review: 2016 +$222,244.91 Day Trading Momentum Strategies

## Summary

Complete year-end breakdown of 2016 trading performance including broker statements, Tradingview statistics, and the three core rules that generated $222k+ profit: risk management, proper stock selection (low-float stocks), and disciplined pattern trading (bull flags). ## Mechanical rules

- [00:24:01] Define max loss on every trade before entry; knowing your max loss is mandatory before any trade executes.

- [00:30:02] Once up $1,000 daily profit, use that as "cushion" to take increased risk on subsequent trades.

- [00:35:03] Look for stocks up 5% or more to identify catalyst momentum before looking for news.
- [00:35:15] Focus scanner on low-float stocks with high relative volume (5x or higher).
- [00:36:00] Bull flag pattern is primary setup: sharp up move, pullback consolidation, break above consolidation high = entry.
- [00:37:45] Keep charts simple; use only moving averages and candlestick patterns; avoid indicator clutter.
- [00:43:32] Implement hard $1,000 max loss rule: cannot initiate new trades once down $1,000 for the day.

- [00:46:39] Taking larger size on fewer, higher-conviction setups forces selectivity and improves accuracy.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — Sharp initial move up, followed by small consolidation pullback, first candle to break consolidation high is entry. [00:36:35]
- **Breakout or bailout mentality** — Expect almost immediate resolution on trade entry; if not getting quick profit or break-even within minutes, exit. [00:07:56]
- **First candle to make new high after pullback** — Core entry pattern on momentum trades with breaking news catalyst. [00:35:25]

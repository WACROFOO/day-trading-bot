# How to Day Trade with Robinhood Legend (Platform Demo & Honest Review)

## Summary

Platform demo review of Robinhood Legend for day trading. Concludes it is unsuitable for active day traders due to lack of Level 2 data, hot keys, news feeds, and paper trading. Compares favorably to Robinhood mobile/web but significantly underperforms commission-based direct-access brokers. Recommends Webull or Charles Schwab DaTrader as better commission-free alternatives.

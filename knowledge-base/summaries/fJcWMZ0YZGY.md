# How I Became a Day Trader (from BROKE to Millionaire)

## Summary

This episode documents the speaker's path to becoming a profitable day trader despite multiple early failures and personal challenges. The video covers three critical steps: learning a proven strategy from a profitable trader, practicing in a simulator, and transitioning to real money with small position sizes. Directed at beginners who have struggled or feel discouraged by initial losses.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:13:00] Step 1: Find a proven, verified profitable trader and learn their strategy.
- [00:13:15] Step 2: Practice the strategy in a simulated trading environment for a few months.
- [00:23:00] Step 3: Switch to real money trading as soon as you've proven proficiency in the simulator.
- [00:22:51] Simulator position sizing: Trade with 100 shares and aim to make $10-$15 consistently daily before scaling up.
- [00:26:03] Walk away from trading by 10:30 a.m. ET each day when following the initial strategy.
- [00:24:43] Do not spend extended time (years) practicing in simulators; transition to real money after proving skill.

## Setups and patterns

- **Base hit trading** — Small consistent gains ($10-$20 per trade) compounded over time rather than chasing home runs. [00:24:00]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- TradeView — Software for tracking all trades and analyzing annual performance metrics. [00:05:59]
- E-Trade and Ameritrade — Brokers used for opening initial trading accounts. [00:04:21]
- Robinhood — Commission-free trading platform enabling small account entries. [00:03:41]

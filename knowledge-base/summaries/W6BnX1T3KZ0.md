# How to Day Trade: The Plain Truth by Ross Cameron (Full Audiobook)

## Summary

Ross Cameron's comprehensive audiobook narration of "How to Day Trade: The Plain Truth" covers the author's personal journey from losing money as a beginning trader to becoming consistently profitable through 15+ years of full-time trading. The book debunks myths about day trading (that it's easy, guaranteed profit, or a fast path to wealth) and instead provides a brutally honest assessment: day trading is a learnable but difficult profession requiring discipline, emotional control, and systematic risk management. Cameron emphasizes that success depends more on what's "between your ears"—knowledge, discipline, attitude—than on market patterns or tools. The 20 "guard rails" he developed form the core of his teaching: entry criteria based on volatility and catalysts, profit-to-loss ratio targets, maximum loss limits per trade and per day, position sizing rules, and the practice of "getting green and shutting down" for beginners. The audiobook serves as both a cautionary tale (his 2-3 year struggle with losses, his ex-wife's lack of support, his near-failure before discovering momentum trading) and a practical curriculum for moving from simulator to real money trading without blowing up.

## Setups and patterns

- **First and second pullback** — Enter on strong stocks after shallow pullbacks, taking the first or second pull-back setup.
- **Flat top breakouts** — Bullish pattern where stock consolidates near highs then breaks above resistance.
- **Flat bottom breakdowns** — Bearish counterpart where stock consolidates near lows then breaks below support.
- **Bull flag** — Sharp move up, followed by a small consolidation (flag), then continuation higher.
- **Bear flag** — Sharp move down, followed by consolidation, then resumption of downtrend.
- **Moving average retracements** — Entry on pullback to moving average on a strong trend.
- **Higher high after pullback on 5-minute uptrend** — Entry when stock makes new high after minor pullback within an uptrend.
- **Parabolic moves** — Extremely steep, explosive price moves; occur on highest-volatility days.
- **Head and shoulder patterns** — Classical reversal pattern; also inverse version for bullish reversals.
- **ABCD pattern** — Four-point pattern used to identify entries and profit targets.
- **Gap and go setup** — Stock gaps up at open on news/catalyst, then continues higher intraday.

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Stock scanners** — Used to identify top movers; gap scanner shows stocks set to open highest relative to previous close; high day momentum scanner shows intraday leaders. [01:55:01-01:55:10]
- **Level 2 market data** — Depth of market showing bid/ask levels and size; essential for reading order flow and making quick decisions.
- **Time and sales tape** — Shows every individual transaction, indicating whether trades occur at bid (bearish) or ask (bullish).
- **Simple and exponential moving averages** — [04:15:44-04:15:47] Common technical indicators; useful for identifying support/resistance and trend direction.
- **Volume weighted average price (VWAP)** — [04:15:46-04:15:49] Calculates average price weighted by volume; used as support/resistance and entry/exit reference.
- **Moving average convergence divergence (MACD)** — [04:15:49-04:15:52] Measures momentum and trend changes.
- **Relative strength index (RSI)** — [04:15:52-04:15:55] Oscillator measuring momentum; identifies overbought/oversold conditions.
- **Relative volume metric** — Ratio of today's volume to average volume; higher ratios indicate stronger interest and better trading conditions.
- **Trading simulator (sim)** — Paper trading platform for practicing without real money; critical for building skills, testing strategies, and recovering from losses psychologically before risking real capital. [03:52:39-03:52:56]
- **Trading journal** — [04:07:01-04:08:24] Spreadsheet tracking date, time, symbol, entry price, exit price, profit/loss, and trade notes; reviewed daily or weekly to identify patterns, strengths, weaknesses, and guard rail violations.
- **Trading community / chat room** — Provides camaraderie, more eyes on the market, honest feedback, and mentorship from experienced traders.
- **Hotkeys** — Programmable keyboard shortcuts to execute orders faster; essential for fast-moving markets.
- **Brokers mentioned** — Erade (formerly E*TRADE), Fidelity, Charles Schwab, Lightspeed, TradeZero, Thinkorswim, Robinhood.
- **Warrior Trading** — Ross Cameron's proprietary platform with custom stock scanners, chat room, live broadcasts, and educational materials.

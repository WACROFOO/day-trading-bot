# The Secret Nobody Tells You About Reading Candlestick Charts...

## Summary

Teaches candlestick reading directly from price action instead of relying on technical indicators, which lag behind the market. Shows how to identify individual candlestick shapes (letters of the alphabet) that form patterns to read buy and sell signals. Emphasizes that candlesticks matter most during trending periods, not sideways markets.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:00:10] Stop using technical indicators and learn to read candlesticks directly from the raw data.
- [00:16:38] Upper candle wicks are bearish; lower candle wicks are bullish.
- [00:16:42] A hammer (candle with bottoming tail) is bullish because it shows price declined but buyers rallied it back up.
- [00:18:00] A shooting star (candle with large upper wick) is bearish because it shows price squeezed up then got pushed down.
- [00:19:04] Candle over candle (green candle breaking high of previous candle) is a bullish signal; buy when it breaks that high.
- [00:19:15] Enter the moment a candle breaks the high of the previous candle—be faster than technical indicator traders.
- [00:14:08] A doji is a reversal warning when it comes after strong uptrend; confirmation is if the next candle goes lower.
- [00:14:14] Doji signals matter only during trend periods, not during sideways/indecisive price action.

## Setups and patterns

- **Doji** — Opens and closes at nearly the same price; signals battle between buyers and sellers. [00:13:08]
- **Gravestone doji** — Ominous pattern with upper wick; price squeezed up then sellers pulled it down. [00:15:04]
- **Dragonfly doji** — Has lower wick; bulls rallied it back after weakness. [00:15:20]
- **Hammer** — Red or green candle with bottoming tail; hammering out support base. [00:18:02]
- **Shooting star** — Topping tail then closes red; reversal beginning. [00:21:24]
- **Candle over candle** — One candle breaks the high of previous candle; bullish formation. [00:19:04]
- **Micro pullback** — Small pullback in strong uptrend; entry on first new high. [00:02:00]

# How I Made $500,000 in 7 Days of Day Trading (Full Training)

## Summary

Comprehensive day trading training covering the mechanics of profitability through risk management, stock selection criteria, technical chart patterns, and the mindset of successful traders. Aimed at beginner and intermediate traders looking to understand how to identify high-probability setups, manage risk via profit-to-loss ratios, and execute entries on momentum stocks. Taught by Ross Cameron with 25 years of trading experience and over $17.5 million in documented profits.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:43:01] Entry: Buy on the first candle to make a new high after a pullback in an uptrending stock.
- [00:45:00] Exit: Sell when a red candle closes during an uptrend (first red candle is exit signal for existing position).
- [00:49:10] Secondary exit: Sell half the position as profit is being made to pay yourself before profit potentially disappears.
- [00:40:03] Position size constraint: Trade with small size each morning until building a cushion of about a quarter of your daily goal.
- [00:40:03] Risk management: Once you have a quarter-day cushion, increase to regular share size; remain consistent each day.
- [00:11:32] Stop-loss placement: Stop-loss at the low of the pullback, not wider.
- [00:52:57] False breakout avoidance: If a trade doesn't work immediately, exit (breakout or bailout mentality).
- [00:11:32] Minimum profit-to-loss ratio: Target at least a 2:1 ratio to achieve profitability with reasonable accuracy.
- [00:08:30] Risk definition: Risk is the difference between entry price and max loss price (e.g., $8 entry minus $7.90 stop = $0.10 per share risk).
- [00:39:00] Daily stop-loss: Walk away if you hit your maximum daily loss (which equals your average daily gain).
- [00:39:00] Profit protection: Walk away if you give back half your daily profit at any point.
- [00:39:00] Post-exit discipline: Do not return to trading after walking away; stop watching for the day.
- [00:39:00] Averaging down prohibition: Do not add to losing trades.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bullish pullback** — sharp move up, pullback for profit-taking, first candle to make a new high. [00:47:25]
- **Micro pullback** — stock pops on news, pulls back briefly before surging higher; shortest pattern, minimal thinking time required. [00:53:35]
- **Bottoming tail candle** — candle dips down then closes at or near the high, showing rejection of selling; bullish signal. [00:51:22]
- **Break of VWAP setup** — price breaks above volume-weighted average price with micro pullback entry. [01:04:00]
- **High-volume rejection candle** — large red candle with high volume showing sellers taking control. [00:49:00]

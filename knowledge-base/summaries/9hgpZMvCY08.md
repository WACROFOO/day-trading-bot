# How to use Hotkeys for Day Trading

## Summary

Technical guide to setting up keyboard hotkeys for fast market entry and exit across three major platforms: Lightspeed Trader, Thinkorswim, and Webull. Covers mapping buy/sell buttons, creating partial-position hotkeys, managing slippage with limit order offsets, and practicing muscle memory for speed trading.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:00:27] Purpose of hotkeys is speed — ability to get in and out of positions quickly.
- [00:01:02] Press hotkey to instantly enter position; Ctrl+Z instantly exits full position.
- [00:01:48] Start with only three basic hotkeys (buy, sell, cancel orders) before adding advanced ones.
- [00:03:07] Offset buy orders 10 cents ABOVE the ask to ensure fill despite market movement and slippage.
- [00:05:41] For sell orders, offset 10 cents BELOW the bid to accommodate upward slippage on exit.
- [00:08:43] Test hotkeys with small size before live trading to avoid errors in mapping.
- [00:09:06] Create multiple sell hotkeys: sell full position, sell half, sell quarter, sell at midpoint, sell at ask.
- [00:09:09] Use sticky notes on keyboard labeled with what each hotkey does to build muscle memory.
- [00:11:06] With Thinkorswim pre-market, cannot use market orders; must use buy-ask and sell-bid combination.
- [00:24:00] Always offset above ask when buying and below bid when selling regardless of platform.

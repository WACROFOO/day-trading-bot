# How to Avoid Red Days...there is a trick...

## Summary

No-trade day breakdown: day two of no trades in 2025. Trader identifies ALTS (crypto stock up 100%) and BMNR (up 25%) as potential plays but passes due to market conditions, high risk, and lack of cushion. Main trick: know when not to trade.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:00:20] If scans look poor and no obvious setups exist at 8:00 a.m., 7:30 a.m., 8:00 a.m., 8:39 a.m., consider a no-trade day.
- [00:00:25] Do not force risk when reward is unclear. Risk-reward ratio must justify entry.
- [00:02:03] Before taking a high-risk trade, build a cushion/profit on the day first. Without a cushion, reduce position size or pass.
- [00:02:57] Stock too expensive (>$50–60) creates difficulty. Easy-to-borrow status (short borrow) attracts high short interest; manage expectations on tight squeezes.

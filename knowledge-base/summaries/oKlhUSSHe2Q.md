## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — sharp up move followed by sideways consolidation, then break higher. [00:47:22]
- **Pullback pattern** — the one simple pattern taught in class; stock gaps up, pulls back, breaks high of pullback candle. [00:43:50]
- **Long-body candlesticks** — very bullish, show strong momentum. [00:35:47]
- **Short-body candlesticks** — less strength than long bodies. [00:35:47]
- **Dogee candle** — open and close at same price; signals indecision, often marks pivots. [00:39:25]
- **Gravestone dogee** — larger upper tail. [00:41:20]
- **Dragonfly dogee** — larger lower tail. [00:41:27]
- **Long-legged dogee** — large upper and lower tails. [00:41:31]
- **Spinning top** — similar to dogee, shows indecision. [00:41:37]
- **Hammer candle** — in downtrend, bullish; stock dropped but rallied and closed higher, "hammering out the base." [00:42:04]
- **Shooting star** — longer upper wick; indicates potential reversal. [00:42:11]
- **Tweezer top** — two back-to-back similar highs; bearish. [00:42:32]
- **Tweezer bottom** — two back-to-back similar lows; bullish reversal. [00:42:50]
- **Higher volume selling** — red candle with heavy volume; warning sign, not a safe entry. [00:52:10]
- **Micro pullbacks** — small dips within an uptrend; can continue to trade these. [00:54:48]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **MACD** — when blue line is above orange line (positive), typically bullish; when inverted and negative, typically bearish. [00:49:40]
- **Stock scanner** — five pillars alert scanner; shows stocks meeting all five pillars in real time. [00:30:58]
- **Level 2 market data** — shows all buyers and sellers posting orders; used to read order flow and sentiment. [00:55:28]
- **Candlestick charts** — daily chart shows 9:30am to 4pm price action; one-minute chart for intraday trading. [00:33:35]
- **Volume bars** — below chart; show shares traded per candle. [00:49:00]

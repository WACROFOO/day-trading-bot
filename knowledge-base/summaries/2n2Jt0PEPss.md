## Mechanical rules

- [00:19:03] Trade ONLY when MACD is positive; do not trade when MACD is negative. Positive = easier, cleaner price action.
- [00:20:37] Buy the pullback during an uptrend; this is the front-side strategy. Do not trade the back-side (after MACD crosses negative).
- [00:19:02] Lock up profit within the first 30-minute window; this is the highest-confidence period before MACD crossover.
- [00:27:03] Buy the first and second pullback within a trend. Expect the third pullback to be a false breakout; stop trading there.
- [00:30:01] Use stair-stepping: buy pullback, ride leg up, repeat. Continue adding into strength.
- [00:33:02] Stop trading aggressively once MACD crosses below the signal line; the window has closed.
- [00:53:10] Use hot keys for rapid execution; this setup requires sub-second order entry and exit.

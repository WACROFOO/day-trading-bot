# The 7 WORST Financial Mistakes You Can Make...

## Summary

Seven costly financial mistakes that delay financial independence, including career loyalty, being too conservative when young, lack of financial goal-setting, poor budgeting, buying overpriced housing, paying high interest instead of earning interest, and failing to use tax-advantaged accounts. One mistake cost the presenter $2 million.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:28] Do not stay at one job for 40 years; change jobs every 3-5 years for career growth.
- [00:02:33] When young, invest heavily in equities (80-90% or even 100%) for growth potential.
- [00:02:47] As you age, shift from high-risk equities to conservative investments like bonds.
- [00:05:57] Set a financial independence goal-post by calculating monthly living costs × 300.
- [00:08:00] Budget expenses and allocate to savings and investing (don't live paycheck-to-paycheck).
- [00:09:14] Plan for unexpected expenses with 10% contingency allowance in budgets.
- [00:11:42] Mortgage payment should not exceed 28% of gross income (annual).
- [00:16:02] Contribute to tax-free accounts (IRA, Roth IRA, 401k) regardless of employment type.
- [00:19:57] Prioritize building trading capital in tax-free accounts over taxable accounts.

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:04:26] $200,000 inheritance invested conservatively 1993-2007 only grew to $220,000 (10% over 15 years).
- [00:05:03] Expected 15-year account return should double or triple, not just 10%.
- [00:06:33] Rule of 4%: withdraw 4% annually from investment account indefinitely.
- [00:06:35] $1.5 million account allows $60,000/year withdrawal ($5,000/month).
- [00:06:46] Multiply monthly living costs by 300 to find retirement savings goal.
- [00:11:45] Mortgage payment limit: 28% of gross income; if earning $100k/year, max $28k/year (~$2,330/month).
- [00:12:08] Many people end up paying 35-40% of income to housing (too high).
- [00:15:20] Credit card debt: 20% pre-recession, now 25-30-33% interest rates.
- [00:19:24] $6,000 annual IRA contribution limit.
- [00:19:32] $18,000 annual contribution at higher income levels.
- [00:20:04] IRA account grew to over $6 million (tax-free).
- [00:20:29] Regular taxable account: $12 million+ with $4 million profit taxed.
- [00:20:45] $2 million tax bill in 2020 on $4 million profit.

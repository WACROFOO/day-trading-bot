Quick recap of two gap-up stocks trading on day 3 of continuation (no fresh news). ## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:50] Avoid trading first five minutes after market open (9:30-9:35 ET): volatility too extreme (2-3 point swings common) to manage tight risk.
- [00:03:52] When stock oscillates wildly early session: wait for price action to settle OR demand clear higher support before re-entry.
- [00:04:08] Risk-reward check at entry: if stop loss = 50 cents and profit target = 50 cents, position is viable only if high-conviction setup. Pass if risk/reward unfavorable.
- [00:05:45] Add positions only after first trade achieves profit, not into losses. Wait for pullback completion before second entry.
- [00:07:15] Exit signals: gravestone doji rejection candles, high-volume selloff, or MACD crossover bearish. Track on intraday (1-minute and 5-minute).

## Setups and patterns

- **Easy-to-borrow continuation setup** — stock trending up without fresh news; easy-to-borrow designation suppresses initial move (shorts can freely enter) causing chop; eventual buyers overcome, causing squeeze higher. [00:00:08]
- **Double top rejection** — stock bounces to previous high (from 2-3 days prior) but fails to break higher; rejection candle signals further pullback. [00:03:20]
- **Gravestone doji** — high volume candle with long upper wick, closes low; bearish reversal signal. [00:03:34]
- **Topping tail rejection** — sharp spike up then close near open; rejection of higher prices by sellers. [00:03:32]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **MACD crossover** — when histogram crosses from positive to negative on intraday: confirms reversal lower; exit signal. [00:04:09]
- **200 EMA on daily** — key resistance level; stock must clear this for breakout continuation confidence. [00:07:09]
- **Level 2 order book** — visible seller size/prices critical for gap-up stocks; large sell stacks telegraphs resistance. [00:02:42]

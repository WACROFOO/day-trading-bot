# The #1 Day Trading Strategy for Beginners | Backtested With 24,234 Trades

## Summary

Complete breakdown of the gap-and-go strategy for trading stocks that gap up on news. Teaches entry triggers, stock selection criteria (price range, float, relative volume), and risk management. Aimed at beginners learning momentum trading on breaking news.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:05:09] Look for news as the primary catalyst for gap-and-go trades.
- [00:10:25] Focus on top two to three leading percentage gappers each day.
- [00:11:02] Stock price ideally between $2 and $20 per share.
- [00:15:30] Minimum gap requirement: stock must be up at least 10%.
- [00:15:42] Relative volume must be five times above average.
- [00:17:21] Float should be under 20 million shares.
- [00:24:20] Buy the first pullback: wait for initial squeeze, pullback, then buy first candle making new high.
- [00:39:52] Daily profit management: walk away if profit retraces more than 50%; stop trading if giving back 25% or more from peak.
- [00:41:41] Stop trading by 10:30 AM or 11:00 AM at latest; focus window 7 AM to 10 AM for news-driven moves.

## Setups and patterns

- **Gap and go** — stock gaps up overnight on news, squeezes higher on market open or news announcement. [00:05:09]
- **First pullback** — after initial squeeze up, pullback occurs (can be minor), then stock makes new high; best with high volume on green candles, light volume on red pullback candles. [00:24:20]
- **Gravestone doji** — candle that opens, squeezes up, then drops back down to close flat; signals weakness and battle between buyers and sellers. [00:28:22]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- High-day momentum scanner — alerts when stock makes new high of day in real time. [00:10:01]
- Volume-weighted average price (VWAP) — technical indicator used by day traders to identify support/resistance. [00:32:13]
- Scanners showing top gappers and top gainers. [00:10:20]

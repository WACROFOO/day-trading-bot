# Ultimate Step-by-Step Guide to Technical Analysis

## Summary

Master class on technical analysis covering candlestick charts, support/resistance, trend lines, and key indicators like VWAP and MACD. Teaches pattern recognition to predict price movements and introduces the bull flag as a high-probability setup for traders at all experience levels.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:18:53] Buy the first candle that makes a new high after a pullback.
- [00:19:38] Set stop-loss at the low of the pullback, never wider.
- [00:35:56] Pullback should not retrace more than 50% of the initial move; light pullback of 20-30% is preferable.
- [00:38:40] Bull flag entry: buy on first candle making new high after small consolidation phase.
- [00:17:52] Aim for 2:1 risk-to-reward ratio: risk $1 to make $2.
- [00:50:54] Use 9 and 20 exponential moving averages to identify levels of support during pullbacks.
- [00:53:03] MACD above signal line indicates favorable conditions; do not trade after MACD crosses below signal line.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — sharp move up, sideways consolidation, then break of consolidation high. [00:38:40]
- **Shooting star** — long upper candle wick appearing after uptrend; signals potential reversal. [00:13:03]
- **Hammer** — long lower candle wick at bottom of pullback; indicates support and possible reversal. [00:15:02]
- **Doji** — opens and closes at same price; communicates indecision and potential base. [00:21:00]
- **Candle over candle** — first candle crossing high of previous candle after pullback; classic entry signal. [00:18:53]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Volume — increasing on up moves, lighter on pullbacks indicates healthy momentum.
- VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) — major support/resistance level; stock should trade above VWAP in uptrend.
- 9 EMA and 20 EMA — exponential moving averages used as dynamic support levels and trend confirmation.
- MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) — oscillator showing when moving averages converge (top of move) or diverge (strong uptrend).

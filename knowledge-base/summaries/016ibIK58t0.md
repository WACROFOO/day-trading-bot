# A Lesson on Short Selling +$31,076.62 in 2hrs

## Summary

Risk analysis of short selling strategy following TCGL 4,000% squeeze. Advocates long-bias trading over shorting due to uncapped loss potential during squeezes. Details LRHC successful long trade through VWAP break on sympathy momentum following TCGL move, with emphasis on checking company filings and understanding float dynamics.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:07:47] Break of volume-weighted average price with cup-and-handle consolidation below VWAP = entry trigger.
- [00:10:29] Add/scale aggressively as breakout confirms and stock pulls away from VWAP.
- [00:16:07] Exit when price action and technicals no longer support trade, regardless of uninvested profit left on table.
- [00:10:06] Check SEC filings for recent shelf registrations and warrant data to assess dilution risk.
- [00:14:23] Evaluate company cash on balance sheet; if cash insufficient for operations, watch for secondary offerings.

## Setups and patterns

- **VWAP break after micro consolidation** — stock consolidates under VWAP in cup-and-handle, breaks above for entry. [00:09:54]
- **Sympathy momentum** — low-float recent reverse-split follows larger catalyst move in similar stock. [00:08:14]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

# 7 Rules of Day Trader

## Summary

Seven foundational rules distilled from 24,260 individual day trades totaling $122.5 million in gross profit. Speaker identifies timing windows, price ranges, rule discipline, and data tracking as the pillars of consistent profitability in momentum trading.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:11] Build a cushion early in the week: ease into trading on Monday with smaller profits, step up Tuesday, peak performance Wednesday and Thursday, reduce risk Friday.
- [00:02:35] Trade primarily between 8:00 a.m. and 10:00 a.m., when volume and news-driven moves peak. Volume starts at 7:00 a.m.; after 10:30 a.m. market cools.
- [00:04:24] Sweet spot for position sizing: stocks between 5 and 10 dollars. Will trade 2–20 but avoid trading under 2 or over 20 dollars in normal conditions.
- [00:06:14] Use minimal indicators: focus on 9 EMA, 20 EMA, 200 EMA, VWAP, volume bars, and candlesticks. Avoid excessive technical indicators that cause analysis paralysis.
- [00:08:17] Execute trades on Level 2, not just charts. Depth-of-market data is more important than chart patterns alone.
- [00:08:18] If you break a Max Loss rule, you have an 80% likelihood of doubling your loss if you continue trading.
- [00:09:30] Walk away if you give back more than half of your day's gains. Lock in at least half your profits.
- [00:10:47] Require five criteria for a high-probability setup: price between 2–20, news catalyst, high relative volume, stock up at least 10%, float under 20 million shares. Do not force trades.
- [00:12:01] Track your metrics and analyze trading data. Identify which months, days of week, and stock price ranges produce your best results.

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **9 EMA** — exponential moving average used for momentum confirmation. [00:07:25]
- **20 EMA** — secondary trend line. [00:07:25]
- **200 EMA/moving average** — longer-term resistance and support. [00:07:25]
- **VWAP** — volume-weighted average price, the intraday equilibrium. [00:07:28]
- **Volume bars** — essential for confirming candlestick signals. [00:08:02]
- **Level 2** — depth-of-market display where trades are actually executed. [00:08:02]

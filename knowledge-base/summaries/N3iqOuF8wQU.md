# How I Find Stocks as a Retail Trader

## Summary

Framework for finding high-probability day-trading stocks as a retail trader. Focuses on supply-demand imbalance, identifying obvious stocks that capture retail attention, and avoiding risks like secondary offerings and short selling. Emphasis on trading the most obvious leading gainers rather than obscure stocks.

- [00:01:55] Trade stocks with news and momentum as primary criteria.
- [00:02:07] Favor stocks in $2 to $20 price range; avoid stocks over $200/share as too expensive for retail interest.
- [00:02:22] Avoid stocks if company announced secondary offering (raises capital by selling shares to public).
- [00:03:36] Trade stocks with higher relative volume (5x average minimum).

- **Private placement** — company raises capital from institutional investors; reduces risk of secondary offering dilution. [00:01:37]
- **Secondary offering** — company sells shares to public market; typically pushes stock price down when announced. [00:02:47]
- **Bitcoin treasury acquisition** — recent catalyst headline driving momentum on small-cap stocks. [00:36:51]

# How to Read Candlestick Shapes & Charts (with ZERO experience)

## Summary

Beginner-focused breakdown of individual candlestick shapes, their meanings, and how they combine into multi-candle patterns to signal buying and selling opportunities. Covers anatomy of candlesticks, single-candle patterns, two-candle formations, three-candle setups, and practical pattern recognition for trading breakout moves.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:05:42] A candlestick consists of four pieces of information: open, close, high, and low; green candle = open at bottom, close at top; red candle = open at top, close at bottom.
- [00:08:21] On one-minute chart, candles are forming continuously and changing shape in real-time as price moves; a candle's shape is locked in only after 60 seconds when it closes.
- [00:42:27] First candle to make a new high after pullback = reversal/entry signal; max loss at low of pullback; profit target = retest to previous high.
- [00:46:27] Confirm reversal by checking if candle actually breaks the low (bearish) or high (bullish) of previous candle; single candle breaking high/low is the confirmation.
- [00:51:01] Morning star pattern (daily chart): stock sells off and gaps lower on next day, then opens lower but rallies with hammer formation = bullish reversal (entry at 3-4pm when daily candle nearing close).
- [00:53:47] Bullish engulfing pattern (daily chart): previous red candle followed by candle opening below and closing above it, fully engulfing = strong bullish reversal.
- [01:04:16] Entry at first candle making new high after seeing bottoming tail (hammer) pattern; wait for candle to turn green before entering (do not enter too early).
- [01:08:59] Trade stocks with breaking news and high volume; focus on leading percentage gainers with highest volatility that day for clearest pattern resolution.

## Setups and patterns

- **Long body candle** — strong sentiment; green = bullish strength, red = bearish weakness [00:10:16]
- **Short body candle** — weaker sentiment; can signal momentum building (if getting bigger) or trend exhaustion (if getting smaller) depending on context [00:18:24]
- **Hammer candle** — small body with large lower wick appearing at bottom of pullback; buyers came in and bought back up; bottoming tail indicates potential reversal [00:35:00]
- **Inverted hammer** — upper wick instead of lower; at bottom of move shows weakness with sellers pushing back down; not desirable pattern [00:40:02]
- **Shooting star** — inverted hammer (small body with topping tail) at top of move; bearish reversal indicator [00:43:48]
- **Hanging man candle** — hammer shape at top of uptrend; hammering tail bullish but position ominous; similar to shooting star [00:45:23]
- **Dogee candle** — opens and closes at same price showing indecision [00:23:08]
- **Gravestone dogee** — opens squeezed up then pulled back down to close (bearish); reversal already begun [00:26:18]
- **Dragonfly dogee** — opposite of gravestone; price opens drops down then comes back up to close (bullish) [00:27:34]
- **Spinning top** — small body with large upper and lower wicks; indecision candle; not significant during sideways price action [00:32:35]
- **Tweezer top** — two candles with topping tails at top of move; bearish reversal indicator [00:47:33]
- **Tweezer bottom** — two hammer candles back-to-back at bottom; strength shown by inability to break lower [00:48:38]
- **Three white soldiers** — three large green candles in a row; strong bullish sentiment [00:56:40]
- **Three black crows** — three large red candles in a row; strong selling pressure [00:57:27]
- **Rising three (Bull flag)** — one green candle, three pullback candles, then move higher; consolidation pattern showing support holds [00:58:29]
- **Falling three (Bear flag)** — one red candle, three bounce candles, then continue lower [01:00:00]
- **Morning star** — sell-off, gap down opening, hammer with rally at bottom on daily = bullish reversal [00:50:23]
- **Evening star** — move higher, gap up opening, shooting star with reversal to downside on daily = bearish reversal [00:51:57]
- **Bullish engulfing** — previous small candle followed by larger green candle opening below and closing above it = powerful bullish reversal [00:53:47]
- **Bearish engulfing** — previous small candle followed by larger red candle fully engulfing it = powerful bearish reversal [00:55:08]

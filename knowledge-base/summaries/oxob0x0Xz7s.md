## Setups and Patterns

- **Micro pullback** — initial squeeze up, pullback on light volume, reversal on new high; trade first and second pullbacks primarily. [01:36:50]
- **Bull flag** — steep move up, sideways consolidation, breakout of consolidation high. [00:03:50]
- **Gravestone doji** — open and close at same price, long upper wick; forms at tops after strong uptrend; bearish reversal signal. [01:05:07]
- **Dragonfly doji** — open and close at same price, long lower wick; forms at bottoms after selloff; bullish reversal signal. [01:08:02]
- **Long-legged doji** — both long upper and lower wicks; indicates indecision and tug-of-war between buyers and sellers. [01:09:17]
- **Bottoming tail** (hammer) — candle dips down then buyers pull it back up; marks potential reversal at lows. [01:10:18]
- **Topping tail** (shooting star) — opposite of bottoming tail; sellers pull price down after initial push higher; marks reversal at highs. [01:11:30]
- **Double bottom** — two lows at approximately same level; first bounce was weak, second bounce is entry if first candle makes new high. [01:30:18]

## Indicators, Tools, Platforms

- **MACD** (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) — used on 1-minute chart; positive MACD = bullish, crossing into negative = reversal warning, converging moving averages = weakening momentum
- **Volume bars** — color-coded by candle direction (green for up, red for down); high volume on red candles = selling pressure and warning signal
- **VWAP** (Volume Weighted Average Price) — orange line on chart; equilibrium price weighted by volume; price above VWAP = bullish, below = bearish; acts as support/resistance level
- **EMA 9, EMA 20, EMA 200** — exponential moving averages; uses 9 (gray), 20 (blue), 200 (purple) on all timeframes; diverging averages = momentum, converging averages = loss of momentum
- **Level 2 Market Data** — shows bid/ask depth, big buyers/sellers, current market depth; critical for tape reading
- **Time and Sales** — green (buy at ask), red (sell at bid), white (between spread); reveals order flow
- **Candlestick charts** — 10-second, 1-minute, 5-minute, daily timeframes for multi-timeframe confirmation
- **Scanners** — DayTradeDash small-cap high-momentum scanner with audio alerts; top-gainers scanner; running-up scanner
- **Platforms reviewed** — Thinkorswim, Weeble, Lightspeed Trading, Interactive Brokers, Warrior Trading Sim
- **Trader View** — paid service ($50/month) for importing trades and analyzing metrics (profit/loss, average winners, average losers, win rate, price ranges, time-of-day performance)
- **Hot keys** — programmable buttons for instant buy/sell entry (Shift+1 for full size buy, Ctrl+C for sell half, etc.)

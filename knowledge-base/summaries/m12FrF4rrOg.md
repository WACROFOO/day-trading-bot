# How to Read Candlestick Charts 📓📈 Beginner Trading Strategies 🍏

## Summary

Comprehensive guide to candlestick anatomy (open, close, high, low), timeframe relationships (one-minute nested into five-minute), and how wicks communicate market sentiment. Emphasizes multi-timeframe alignment and introduces the micro pullback strategy as a consistently profitable pattern.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:18] Green candlestick: opened low, closed high. Red candlestick: opened high, closed low. Color indicates direction; shape indicates sentiment.
- [00:03:00] A candlestick with long upper wick and small body signals reversal or topping, especially after a big move up.
- [00:03:43] A candlestick with long lower wick and small body signals reversal or bottoming, especially after a big move down.
- [00:09:17] Multi-timeframe alignment: both five-minute, one-minute, and daily charts must confirm the trade (perfect storm setup).
- [00:10:38] Micro pullback strategy: stock squeezes up, has momentary pullback, then squeezed higher. Enter on the pullback; highest-consistency pattern. (One-minute timeframe).

## Setups and patterns

- **Micro pullback** — stock rises, momentary pullback, resumes higher. This is one of the most consistent patterns for day trading. [00:10:30]
- **Doji** — open and close at same price, long upper and lower wicks, shows extreme indecision. [00:12:25]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **One-minute chart** — used for active day trading; shows recent price action. [00:05:08]
- **Five-minute chart** — intermediate timeframe; each candle contains five one-minute candles. [00:04:44]
- **Daily chart** — longer-term trend confirmation. [00:04:31]

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:05:08] Daily chart: each candle represents one full day of price action.
- [00:05:08] Five-minute chart: each candle represents five minutes of trading.
- [00:05:08] One-minute chart: each candle represents one minute of trading.

## Claims needing verification

- [00:01:59] Claims candlestick charts provide clear buy and sell indicators that line chart / bar charts do not.
- [00:10:52] Micro pullback strategy described as "one of the most consistent patterns" trader uses almost every day.

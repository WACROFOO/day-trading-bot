## Mechanical rules

- [00:07:16] Take only trades where you stand to gain at least twice what you are risking (2:1 profit-to-loss ratio minimum).
- [00:16:56] Stop loss placement: the low of the pullback, never wider. This defines your maximum risk.
- [00:15:02] Only trade stocks with five times higher volume than the 50-day average volume.
- [00:14:04] Only trade stocks gapping up at least 2% in pre-market before the opening bell.
- [00:25:02] Only trade stocks priced between $2 and $20 per share.
- [00:29:11] Only trade stocks with a float under 10 million shares.
- [00:36:36] Bull flag entry: wait for first candle to make a new high after the pullback; that is your entry point.
- [00:36:36] Entry price: buy on first candle breaking the high of the previous candle.
- [00:36:43] Stop loss: place at the low of the pullback (about $0.50 per share in the example shown).
- [00:37:06] Profit target: aim for a retest of the high of day; establish this before taking the trade.
- [01:04:53] Start each trading day with one-quarter of your full position size until you achieve one-quarter of your daily profit goal.
- [01:04:53] Once you reach one-quarter of daily profit goal (cushion), scale up to full position size.
- [01:06:00] If you have not taken a trade in 30 minutes, stop trading for the day.
- [01:06:15] If you give back your cushion (profit), reduce back to quarter size or stop entirely.
- [01:06:15] Max daily loss equals your daily profit goal (same magnitude, opposite direction).
- [01:14:26] When in a winning trade, double your position; set stop at break-even after doubling.
- [01:14:21] Never add to losing positions; cut losses ruthlessly and add to winning positions instead.
- [01:14:36] Take profit when you have 15-20 cents per share gain, unless trade is moving very fast.
- [01:16:20] Look for psychological resistance levels (whole dollars and half-dollars).

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — sharp initial move up, pullback/consolidation to 50% of initial move, then first candle makes new high (entry signal). [00:33:03]
- **W pattern (ABCD)** — pop up, pull back, pop up, pull back pattern resembling the letter W; look for break of final low. [00:40:08]
- **First candle to make a new high** — a key entry signal after a pullback; used on bull flags and other setups. [00:36:36]
- **Candlestick charts** — universal language of financial markets; each candle shows open, close, high, low. [00:28:32]

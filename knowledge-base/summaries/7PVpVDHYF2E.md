## Setups and patterns

- **MACD crossover** — 12-period and 26-period moving average convergence/divergence flipping from negative to positive indicates trend reversal and entry signal. [00:01:00]
- **Micro pullback** — first pullback after a strong initial breakout on a stock already up significantly; bought when MACD is positive and volume confirms buying pressure. [00:08:09]
- **Double top rejection** — price attempts same high twice but fails both times; indicates weakness before recovery. [00:28:35]
- **Ascending support line** — series of higher lows that can act as support on pullback. [00:29:06]
- **Curling pattern** — stock had been strong, pulls back and goes sideways, then suddenly curls back up sharply. [00:26:48]
- **Volume profile (accumulation)** — candles with higher volume on bounces back up to support level indicate institutional buying. [00:10:03]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)** — compares 12-period and 26-period exponential moving averages; diverging = momentum, converging = consolidation.
- **Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP)** — equilibrium price of stock over the course of the day; price below VWAP is bearish, above is bullish. [00:09:15]
- **9 EMA (9-period exponential moving average)** — used as support level. [00:29:24]
- **20 EMA (20-period exponential moving average)** — used for trend confirmation. [00:29:24]
- **Level 2 market data** — shows real-time buy and sell orders at each price level; used to identify large sellers/buyers that may block breakout. [00:17:02]
- **Day Trade Dash scanner** — custom scanner developed starting 2017; filters for stocks matching MACD crossover criteria. [00:16:30]
- **High of Day momentum scanner** — identifies stocks hitting new highs in real-time. [00:17:26]
- **Running Up scanner** — similar to high of day scanner with slightly lower thresholds. [00:20:42]

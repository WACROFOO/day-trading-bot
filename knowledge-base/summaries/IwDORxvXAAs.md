## Setups and patterns

- **Dip trade** — Strong stock pulls back on low volume, then rallies to new high; buy the dip on pullback low. [00:01:16]
- **Micro-pullback** — Very brief dip (seconds) before rally higher; entry on first candle making new high. [00:43:16]
- **High volume red candle** — Indicator of dump (bearish); sellers overwhelm buyers. [00:08:30]
- **Shooting star** — Large upper wick at top of move; bearish reversal signal. [00:09:25]
- **Bottoming tail** — Lower wick after price drops; bullish sentiment (buyers stepped in). [00:12:57]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)** — Must be positive during dip for buy; negative MACD signals dump risk. [00:04:56]
- **9 EMA (9-period Exponential Moving Average)** — Psychological support level; stock breaking below signals bearish control. [00:20:36]
- **VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price)** — Support level for dips; price breaking below indicates weakness. [00:20:52]
- **Volume profile** — Higher volume on buying candles vs lower on selling candles indicates dip (not dump). [00:06:01]
- **Level 2 order book** — Large sell orders or pegged orders on offer signal institutional selling pressure (bearish). [00:22:01]
- **Stock scanner** — High of day momentum scanner to identify stocks squeezing up. [00:35:49]
- **High-frequency trading platforms** — Reference to payment for order flow and ATS systems. [00:14:35]

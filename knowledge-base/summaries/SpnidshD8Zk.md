# Day Trading this ONE Scalping Strategy

## Summary

Scalping strategy targeting small 5-50 cent per share profits on high-volume momentum stocks. Optimal share size range 1,000-30,000 shares, best executed during market peak liquidity hours (8:30-10:30 AM). Discipline and tight stops are critical; requires accepting base hits over home runs.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:00:20] On scalp trades: enter when stock shows strength, exit when up 5-50 cents per share.
- [00:06:21] Focus trading on leading gappers—the most obvious stock moving in market during that period.
- [00:06:13] Peak volume trading window: 8:30-10:30 AM (approximately); most liquidity and volatility during this period.
- [00:09:33] Wait for micro pullback, buy at approximately previous high, anticipate break to new high.
- [00:09:41] Risk approximately 10 cents per share on each scalp trade; target 15-20 cent profit.
- [00:12:35] If stock is consolidating at support/resistance: wait for break of critical level before entering.
- [00:14:31] Hold at support levels; if price holds above half-dollar (e.g., $4.50), higher probability to break upside.
- [00:15:38] When stock breaks to new high, take quick partial profits; do not hold for full daily range.
- [00:23:07] During lighter volume (after 10:30 AM): reduce share size or exit strategy; difficult to scalp on light volume.

## Setups and patterns

- **High-day momentum breakout** — Stock hitting new daily highs on high volume; scalp the first pullback. [00:04:00]
- **Half-dollar/whole-dollar level holds** — Psychological support at round prices ($4.50, $5.00); breaks above often lead to quick pops. [00:14:48]
- **Micro-pullback into high-day breakout** — First candle pullback after initial move; re-entry into breakout. [00:09:00]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Gap scanner (9:15 AM scan) — Identifies opening gap up percentage leaders. [00:07:01]

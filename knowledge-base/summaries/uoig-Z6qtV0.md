# How to Trade IPOs (for Beginners)

## Summary

Guide to day trading initial public offerings, focusing on red-to-green scalping strategies on the first day of trading. Covers the mechanics of IPO pricing gaps, execution during opening volatility, and stop placement for high-momentum plays.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:11:00] Enter on the first sign of green on the tape after the initial drop from the IPO open.
- [00:13:08] Stop is set below the pullback low, typically 50 cents to a dollar away from entry.
- [00:13:18] Take partial profit on the upside squeeze; do not hold for perfect fills.
- [00:14:06] Add another tranche of position if stock holds above buyer level during pullback, rather than breaking lower.
- [00:15:52] Close down the first candle to make new high following pullback if trade breaks down.

## Setups and patterns

- **IPO red-to-green move** — Stock opens, drops on initial volume match, then squeezes back up after the first sellers clear. [00:09:27]
- **First day volatility bounce** — IPOs often experience a pullback followed by a second leg up within hours of opening. [00:05:15]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Level 2 market data for observing order flow during opening. [00:10:03]
- Trading halts and delays in opening; chart loading lags due to massive volume. [00:08:52]

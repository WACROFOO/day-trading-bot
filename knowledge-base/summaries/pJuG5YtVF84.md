# How to use Level 2 and Time & Sales as a Momentum Day Trader

## Summary

Level 2 market depth and time-and-sales data show the strength and weakness of orders beyond the top bid-ask. This video teaches traders how to read market depth to anticipate support/resistance breakouts, identify large buyers/sellers as entry/exit signals, and why thick order books (tightly spaced levels) are preferred over thin, illiquid ones for day trading.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:03:44] Look for setups with 2-to-1 profit-loss ratios; stocks with wide spreads make proper risk-reward ratios impossible.
- [00:05:31] Read Level 2: large buyers at support levels indicate that level will hold; when that buyer is absorbed by sellers, expect sharp drops.
- [00:08:02] On level 2, look at the bid-ask spread width; prefer stocks with tight spreads (1-3 cents) for better profit potential.
- [00:09:00] Float analysis: smaller float stocks move faster than larger float stocks with the same volume; bigger supply requires bigger demand for moves.
- [00:14:32] Watch for large stacked orders on level 2 (e.g., 20,000-share seller) as signals of resistance/support; when consumed, expect a move.
- [00:19:50] Use hotkeys to pre-stage buy orders at key prices; click hotkey to execute instantly when price reaches that level.
- [00:22:51] Set stops based on chart support/resistance, not on spread size; a 2-point technical stop is preferable to a 50-cent spread stop.

## Setups and patterns

- **Flat-top breakout with Level 2 confirmation** — price consolidates at a level (e.g., $14.40); Level 2 shows 20,000-share seller; once seller is absorbed, buy the breakout. [00:19:27]
- **Double-top reversal** — price makes two equal highs; expect pullback then consolidation in "cup and handle" before next breakout. [00:20:43]
- **Support-level pop** — price drops and bounces off large buyer; when that buyer is filled, expect drop to next support level. [00:10:40]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Level 2 market depth — shows all bid/ask levels and order sizes below top of book.
- Time and Sales window — displays each transaction: price, shares, and exchange route (ARCA, EdX, FADF, etc.).
- DH Trader Pro — direct access platform used; allows click-to-trade and hotkey mapping.
- Market routes: ARCA, EdX, BATS, BATS Y, NASDAQ — different pathways to the same island; some are faster than others.
- Direct access routing — traders can choose which exchange route to use (e.g., ARCA vs EdX) for speed.
- Hotkeys — pre-programmed single-key commands for buy/sell at specific prices or for auto-stops.

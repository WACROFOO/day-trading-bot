# The Story of How I Lost $20k in 90 Seconds Day Trading

## Summary

Case study of a $20,000 loss in under two minutes on a recent IPO (GUTS). Addresses five major trading mistakes common to beginners and ongoing psychological challenges even for experienced traders: oversizing position, trading with FOMO, chasing entries, not tracking metrics, and trading the P&L. Recovery protocol using account restrictions and gradual rehab trading.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:24] Start with smaller position size to condition yourself to losses; scale up gradually ($50 → $100 → $500 → $1,000).
- [00:03:01] Use stock scanner (high-day momentum, top gainers) to identify candidates with news.
- [00:05:02] Check breaking news headline when stock hits scanner.
- [00:16:25] Do not use stop orders in pre-market (not available) or on small caps during regular hours (stop hunting risk).
- [00:17:25] On large losses, respond with: (1) acknowledge heightened emotional state, (2) apply account restrictions (cap max shares via broker), (3) practice mindfulness/meditation, (4) trade small size for base hits only, (5) do not track P&L.
- [00:30:57] Recovery plan: restrict position size to 5,000 shares maximum via broker call.
- [00:31:08] Rehab trading: small size, one entry/exit, no adding, focus on building green cushion, then remove restrictions after recovering ~50% of drawdown.

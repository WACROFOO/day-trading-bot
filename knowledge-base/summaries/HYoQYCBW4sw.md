# Master This ONE Candlestick Pattern TODAY (Full Training)

## Summary

Deep dive into the micro pullback and bull flag candlestick patterns, the most universal price-action setup across all financial markets. Teaches how to recognize buyer and seller sentiment through candlestick shapes, manage risk by waiting for pullbacks after initial moves, and use volume profile to confirm momentum.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:34:04] The pullback on a bull flag should not retrace more than 50% of the initial move; favor pullbacks staying within the top 25% of the range.
- [00:39:01] Stop loss placement at the low of the pullback candle(s), which allows tighter risk management compared to stopping at the low of the entire initial move.
- [00:35:51] Wait for green orders on time-and-sales window before entering; green trades indicate buying resumption after the pullback.
- [00:33:02] Prefer high volume on green candles and light volume on red candles during the pullback to confirm buyer strength.

## Setups and patterns

- **Micro pullback** — sharp move up followed by brief pullback (seconds to one minute) on short timeframes (10-second or 1-minute charts). [00:39:57]
- **Bull flag** — sharp move up, small pullback of one to three candles, then continuation higher on five-minute or longer timeframes. [00:41:02]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Level 2 depth-of-market window for reading order flow and identifying green (buy) vs red (sell) orders. [00:35:31]
- Time-and-sales window (tape) showing individual trade executions and volume. [00:35:31]
- Volume bars at bottom of chart showing transaction count per candle. [00:31:57]

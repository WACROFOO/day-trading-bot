# Ultimate Beginners Guide to Candlestick Patterns, Support/Resistance & Technical Indicators | Ep. 4

## Summary

Reading candlestick patterns and using technical indicators (volume, moving averages, VWAP, MACD) to predict price movement. Focuses on psychological support/resistance at whole dollars and half dollars, micro pullback entries, and the relationship between candlestick shapes, volume profile, and momentum direction.

- [00:28:46] Bull flag pattern: sharp move up, 2-3 candle pullback close to 9 EMA, then break of prior high.
- [00:31:02] ABCD pattern: leg up, pullback, second leg up but failing to make new high; watch for break back up through resistance.
- [00:32:05] Bull flag is often same as failed ABCD pattern (reversal trade on break).
- [00:33:06] Ascending support trend line: drawn by connecting lows; used to identify support in ABCD patterns.
- [00:34:42] "Breakout or bailout": if no immediate squeeze after breakout, exit. Do not hold and hope or swing for big winners on small account.
- [00:37:47] Volume bars: color-code to match candle (green candle = green volume bar, red candle = red volume bar).
- [00:38:40] 9 EMA and 20 EMA: when price moves up fast, 9 EMA is closer to price than 20 EMA; if price breaks 9 EMA, check 20 EMA for next support.
- [00:42:15] VWAP (volume-weighted average price): acts like "slack tide" (equilibrium); bulls in control if above VWAP, bears if below VWAP.
- [00:43:27] Rarely short (buy) below VWAP; focus on trading above VWAP for beginners.
- [00:45:32] MACD: trade when above signal line; when crosses below, expect choppier price action and lower profit probability.
- [00:47:47] Moving average crossover (9 EMA crosses 20 EMA): some traders use it as entry signal; not primary tool here.
- [00:48:35] MACD crosses back up: can take another trade if setup is strong; avoid most trades until it crosses back positive.

## Setups and patterns

- **Whole dollar / half dollar entry** — Stock respects psychological levels at $2.00, $2.50, $3.00, etc.; dips to support, bounces up. [00:01:10]
- **Micro pullback** — Quick 1-2 candle pullback on 10-second chart during 1-minute uptrend; acts as dip-buy setup. [00:26:52]
- **Bull flag** — Sharp up move, 2-3 candle pullback close to 9 EMA, break above prior high for continuation. [00:28:46]
- **ABCD pattern** — Initial leg up, pullback, second leg up but weaker (fails to make new high), then reversal trade back up. [00:31:02]
- **Retest pattern** — Price breaks above resistance, pulls back to retest level, holds it, then continues higher. [00:02:33]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Volume (volume bars)** — Color-coded to candle color; high volume move up + light pullback indicates strong continuation; inverse indicates weakness. [00:15:02]
- **9 EMA (9-period exponential moving average)** — Reacts quickly to price; used as immediate support on all timeframes. [00:17:14]
- **20 EMA (20-period exponential moving average)** — Second level of moving average; used as fallback support if 9 EMA breaks. [00:38:40]
- **VWAP (volume-weighted average price)** — Equilibrium level; bulls above, bears below. Shown as orange dotted line. [00:42:15]
- **MACD (moving average convergence divergence)** — Oscillator on separate pane; signal line (red) and average line (blue); trade when above signal line. [00:45:32]
- **Ascending support trend line** — Drawn by connecting two or more lows; shows where support is rising; used in ABCD and bull flag patterns. [00:33:06]

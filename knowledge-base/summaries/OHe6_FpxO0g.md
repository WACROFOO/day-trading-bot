# How to Start Day Trading as a Business (Step-by-Step Guide)

## Summary

This video teaches how to structure day trading as a formal business to reduce taxes and systematize the approach. The speaker covers developing a written trading plan, establishing profit and loss targets, implementing guard rails to protect capital, and creating an S-Corp for tax advantages. Two primary failure modes in trading are addressed: lack of strategy and lack of discipline.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:14:13] Start with 1,000 shares on first trade until up $200 profit (20 cents per share gain).
- [00:15:30] Once up $200, increase share size to 2,000 shares.
- [00:16:01] Risk 10 cents per share to make 20 cents per share (2:1 profit to loss ratio).
- [00:20:09] Set maximum daily loss equal to daily profit goal; stop trading for the day once this limit is hit.
- [00:20:24] Stop if you have three consecutive losses of $200 each or bigger (about $500 down).
- [00:20:51] If up more than half daily goal, give back more than half profit on the day, stop and walk away with remainder.
- [00:28:25] Phase one: take one trade per day for 10 days minimum to build track record.
- [00:28:46] Minimum accuracy requirement in phase one: 60% (6 out of 10 trades profitable).
- [00:28:51] Minimum profit/loss ratio in phase one: 1:1 (average winners equal average losers in value).
- [00:07:01] Trade between 8:00 a.m. and 10:00 a.m., potentially extending to 11:00 a.m. in hot markets.
- [00:11:11] Focus on stocks between $1 and $10 with float under 10 million shares, up at least 25% on the day.
- [00:34:00] Trade the first pullback (bull flag) when MACD is positive.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — sharp move up followed by pullback consolidation, then break of consolidation high. [00:13:35]
- **First pullback entry** — enter when stock pulls back after strong initial move and MACD is positive. [00:13:37]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- MACD — used to confirm positive momentum on pullback trades. [00:13:37]
- Level 2 market data — referenced as available tool for traders. [00:39:58]
- Stock scanners — used to identify leading gainers. [00:08:10]

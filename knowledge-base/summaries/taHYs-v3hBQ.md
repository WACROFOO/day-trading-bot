# Top 5 Reasons Why Traders Lose Money

## Summary

Identifies five critical mistakes that cause 90% of traders to fail: trading real money too soon, opening brokerage accounts before mastery, lacking a proven strategy, not treating trading as a business, and waiting too long to start learning. Emphasizes simulator practice, verified strategy proof, risk management, and business discipline.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:05:33] Before trading real money, prove profitability in simulator: at least 3-4 weeks of green trading, good profit/loss ratio, and consistency.
- [00:06:27] Do not open a real brokerage account until you have proven consistent profitability in simulator.
- [00:06:53] Look for brokers with fast execution tools (hotkeys, direct access) not commission-free brokers designed for casual investors.
- [00:08:41] Use hotkeys (e.g., Shift+1 to buy 1000 shares, Ctrl+Z to sell full position) for speed advantage.
- [00:14:02] Maintain detailed trading log of all trades to track metrics: profit/loss ratio, win rate, consistency by day/week/month.
- [00:16:03] Expect to lose 30% of the time; losses are cost of business; detach emotionally from individual losses.
- [00:20:15] Start learning and practicing immediately, even just a few minutes daily, because knowledge compounds over time.

## Setups and patterns

- **Candle over candle** — simplest two-candle pattern; one green candle, next candle breaks its high. Entry on first candle making new high; stop at low of control candle. [00:38:21]
- **Micro pullback** — rapid move up with single or few red candles, then next green candle makes new high; occurs around half-dollar and whole-dollar levels. [00:50:09]
- **Bull flag** — sharp initial move up, pullback (2–4 candles), first candle makes new high. Works on 1-minute, 5-minute, 10-second, and daily charts. [01:01:42]
- **ABCD pattern** — bull flag pullback fails first attempt, consolidates tighter, then breaks above resistance on second or third try in wedge formation. [01:05:25]
- **Cup and handle** — extended pullback (3–5 candles) forming cup shape, then small pullback (handle), then break of double top; creates strong squeeze. [01:08:50]
- **Inverted head and shoulders** — dip down (head), pop sideways (shoulder), dip lower, pop back up (shoulder); break above resistance is entry. [01:13:02]
- **Break of VWAP** — price breaks above volume-weighted average price, indicating control shift from bears to bulls; entry on break with volume surge. [01:17:51]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Volume bars** — show share count per candle; green volume = buying strength, red volume = selling strength. [00:14:29]
- **9 EMA (exponential moving average)** — support level on intraday timeframes; first support on pullbacks. [00:20:31]
- **20 EMA** — second support level after 9 EMA fails. [00:20:31]
- **200 EMA** — strong support/resistance on daily charts; price above = bullish, below = bearish. [00:20:05]
- **MACD (moving average convergence divergence)** — settings 12, 26, 9 (standard); blue line above orange = positive/buy signal; crossover below = stop buying. [00:32:38]
- **VWAP (volume weighted average price)** — true equilibrium point of day; price above VWAP = bullish, below = bearish; resets daily at 4:00 a.m. [00:26:01]
- **Scanners** — real-time tools identifying stocks meeting five pillars of stock selection (up 10%+, price $1–$20, 5x relative volume, news catalyst, float under 20M). [00:50:28]
- **Level 2 (depth of market)** — bid/ask display; large sell orders and price action confirm support/resistance and entry timing. [02:04:46]

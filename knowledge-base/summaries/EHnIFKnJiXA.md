Covers stock selection criteria, identifying when market shifts from cold to hot, reading volume and moving averages, and the micro-pullback strategy used to trade fast-moving stocks. ## Setups and patterns

- **Momentum chasing** — riding established trends rather than trying to pick reversals [00:01:26]
- **Micro pullback strategy** — waiting for stocks to pop, pullback slightly, then buying pullback for continuation [00:25:28]
- **Short squeeze initiation** — stocks making huge moves trigger short sellers to cover, pushing prices even higher [00:10:15]
- **Hot market cluster effect** — one catalyst stock (like KOSS) ignites short squeeze that creates attention for similar stocks [00:08:02]
- **VWAP trap** — price drops below VWAP then bounces back above it to trap sellers [00:28:17]
- **High-of-day momentum scanner** — catching stocks in real-time as they reach new daily highs [00:22:08]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) — used to confirm trend direction; open histogram = uptrend valid, crossover = exit signal [00:26:21]
- VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) — support/resistance level and exit guide [00:27:46]
- 200-moving average — key resistance level; stocks breaking and holding above are strong [00:39:27]
- 9-EMA (Exponential Moving Average) — used to identify flattening (exit signal when flat) [00:30:45]
- 10-second and 1-minute charts — timeframes used for micro pullback entries during fast-moving stocks [00:26:07]
- Day Trade Dash scanner — proprietary software showing high-of-day momentum stocks, top volume, top gainers [00:16:36]
- Level 2 market data — shows bid/ask spread and order flow to identify entry/exit points [00:03:11]

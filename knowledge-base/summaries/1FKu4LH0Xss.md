# How to AVOID False Breakouts (Use This Technical Indicator)

## Summary

Ross Cameron reveals five indicators that increase the likelihood of false breakouts (bull traps) and teaches how to identify and avoid them. He emphasizes that MACD crossovers, volume profile showing high-volume red candles, historical false breakout patterns, topping tails at resistance, and consolidation periods all predict likely reversals. Early in the move is cleanest for trading; later stages see more false breakouts and insider dumping.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:04:14] Do not trade breakouts when MACD is against the trade (negative).
- [00:28:12] When MACD is against the trade, do not buy dips; risk is too high.
- [00:04:06] Exit or avoid when price breaks above resistance on high volume but immediately dumps back down (rejection candle).
- [00:06:15] After MACD crossover, expect choppy price action and avoid breakout trades.
- [00:34:35] Trade micro pullbacks only during peak volatility in the beginning of the move, not after consolidation.
- [00:34:37] Once MACD crosses over and volume profile becomes choppy with higher volume red candles, stop trading the long side.

## Setups and patterns

- **Micro-pullback strategy** — buy the pullback when stock is squeezing up, enter on the first candle making new high, set stop at low of pullback. [00:10:57]
- **Bull trap / false breakout** — stock breaks resistance high but quickly reverses lower, trapping long buyers. [00:02:26]
- **Rejection candle** — high volume red candle that pops up to resistance and dumps back down rapidly. [00:05:42]
- **Consolidation before break** — price goes sideways, squeezes higher briefly, then flushes as insiders/big sellers unload. [00:23:07]

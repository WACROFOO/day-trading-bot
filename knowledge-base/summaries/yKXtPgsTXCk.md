# 4 Lessons from Surviving a Stock Market Crash

## Summary

Brief overview of market cycles and expectations management for traders experiencing cold markets or drawdowns. Trading is non-linear; profits vary by year and month. Teaches recognizing market transition signals (smaller wins, more losses) and adapting strategy defensively (smaller positions, higher quality threshold, fewer trades) until market heats up again.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:47] LESSON 1: Trading is non-linear; expect years better and worse than prior years.
- [00:04:05] LESSON 2: Accept cold market periods and focus on base hits; do not give up mid-drawdown.
- [00:04:22] LESSON 3: When market cools, ease off throttle: reduce share size, increase quality threshold, trade less.
- [00:06:07] LESSON 4: When market heats, increase green day sizes, reduce sells too early, catch home runs, get fewer red days.

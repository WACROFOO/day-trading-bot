# How to Day Trade Bull Flag Pattern

## Summary

Detailed breakdown of bull flag (flagpole + consolidation) pattern on 5-minute and 1-minute charts. Entry on first 5-minute candle to make new high above flag; exits on first candle to make new low. Volume surge confirms entries.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:97] Entry: over the high of the flagpole (first 5-minute breakout). Stop: low of pullback.
- [00:138] After first breakout, move stop to break-even or slightly above low of flag.
- [00:158] Take profit at least half on first target; hold rest with trailing stop.
- [00:248] Exit (short): close below 20 EMA or first candle to make new low below flag.
- [00:265] Risk should be on initial entry (tight stop); subsequent pullbacks carry less risk.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — Sharp move up (flagpole), 2-3 candles sideways consolidation, break above consolidation high. [00:49]
- **First pullback entry** — Traders who missed initial pop buy dip; strong second candle. [00:163]
- **Second pullback entry** — Second dip to moving average; breakout above flag high. [00:179]
- **Flat-top breakout** — Similar to bull flag; less precise but valid. [00:394]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **20 EMA (Exponential Moving Average)** — Filter; consolidation should hold above 20 EMA for confidence. [00:128]
- **Volume bars** — Volume surge on breakout confirms strength; thin volume = weak setup. [00:203]

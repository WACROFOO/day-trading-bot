# The Simple Trading Strategy I'd Use If I Had To Start Over

## Summary

Day 1 of a cash-only small account challenge using Thinker Swim platform. Details the complete day trading workflow for accounts under $25,000: stock selection, platform setup, hotkey configuration, micro pullback entry strategy, position sizing constraints, and risk management rules for maximum compounding with minimal loss exposure.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:18] Focus on stocks already up 50%+ (not early, but on momentum with eyes on them).
- [00:04:17] Maximum position size constraint: minimum 1,000 shares per trade for small account.
- [00:05:00] Minimum profit target: $200 per trade (20 cents per share × 1,000 shares).
- [00:07:08] Position sizing: cannot trade anything over $3–5 per share range (cannot afford 1,000 shares above that).
- [00:09:02] Percentage change filter: focus on leading percentage gainers, preferably up 50%+ already.
- [00:11:00] One trade per day maximum (limited by cash settlement overnight; no margin).
- [00:14:04] Trade setup: micro pullback on front side of breakout when MACD is above signal line.
- [00:17:00] Entry technique: buy micro pullbacks (small dips during uptrend) for next leg higher.
- [00:24:28] Minimum profit target: 20 cents per share on most trades.
- [00:26:05] Profit-to-loss ratio: must have 2:1 potential before entering trade.
- [00:26:23] Risk management: never risk more than 3–5% of account per trade.
- [00:28:06] Maximum account drawdown tolerance: 10% of total account balance.
- [00:31:02] Hotkey setup: Shift+1 for buy ask, Ctrl+Z for sell bid, Ctrl+Q to cancel all orders.
- [00:31:02] Order submission settings: set limit to zero (no throttle on order rate).
- [00:31:57] Ladder auto-center: set to maximum frequency for current price visibility.
- [00:32:18] Uncheck "reset active trader order template after sending" to preserve template settings.

## Setups and patterns

- **Micro pullback** — stock squeezing on news, pulls back momentarily, entry on resumption of uptrend. [00:14:04]
- **MACD crossover setup** — trade only when MACD is above signal line (front side of move); choppier when crossed below. [00:17:32]
- **Whole dollar and half-dollar levels** — stocks tend to encounter resistance/consolidation at $5.00, $5.50, $6.00 levels. [00:42:50]

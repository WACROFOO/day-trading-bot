# Income Taxes for Traders (Step by Step Guide)

## Summary

Tax strategies used by top traders to pay zero or minimal income tax on trading profits. Covers Puerto Rico residency, Roth IRA backdoor conversions, S-corp business structures with solo 401ks, wash-sale rules, and mark-to-market elections for minimizing tax burden.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:48] Puerto Rico residents (live there 6 months and 1 day per year) pay zero federal income tax on trading profits.
- [00:04:08] Maximum annual traditional IRA contribution is $6,000 per year.
- [00:06:03] Backdoor Roth conversion: contribute $6,000 to traditional IRA (pre-tax), immediately convert to Roth IRA and pay tax on conversion amount only.
- [00:09:53] Mark-to-market election must be filed by April 15th of the CURRENT tax year for that year to apply (cannot be done retroactively for prior years).
- [00:17:02] S-corp reasonable salary: must take W2 salary before keeping profits, amount determined by accountant based on profit level.
- [00:17:38] Solo 401k contribution limit: up to $69,000 per year total (employee deferral + employer match).
- [00:20:49] Cannot do mark-to-market election retroactively if deadline passed; must plan ahead.
- [00:23:42] Wash sale rule: cannot sell at loss and buy back same or substantially similar stock within 30 days; IRS disallows the loss.

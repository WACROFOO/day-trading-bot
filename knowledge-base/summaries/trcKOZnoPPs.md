# The Bear Market Strategy Episode You NEED To Watch

## Summary

Strategic framework for trading in bear markets (low volatility, reduced opportunities) contrasted with bull markets. Explains how traders who learned during pandemic bull market often fail in bear markets due to averaging down, lower precision, and emotional breakdown. Provides five pillars of stock selection and emphasizes quality-over-quantity approach during cold market periods.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:07:36] STEP 1: Reduce position sizes when market cools (less liquidity, more slippage).
- [00:08:49] STEP 2: Reduce number of trades; only trade A-quality setups in bear market.
- [00:09:58] STEP 3: Focus on quality over quantity—trade the best, leave the rest.
- [00:13:31] STEP 4: Be aggressive taking profits quickly in bear markets (learned from 2010s commission-based trading).
- [00:15:25] STEP 5: Exit immediately if stock does not move higher upon entry; tighter exit discipline required.
- [00:14:07] During bull market: can take B and C quality setups; in bear: A-quality only.

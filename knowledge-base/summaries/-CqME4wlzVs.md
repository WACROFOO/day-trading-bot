# How to Start Day Trading (THE RIGHT WAY)

## Summary

Seven-step roadmap for beginners to start day trading, divided into three phases: gaining experience in simulators, proof of concept with disciplined trading rules, and transitioning to live trading with real money. Emphasizes discipline, stock selection criteria (five pillars), and gradual position sizing over volume of trades.

- **Bull flag pattern** — two to three candles up, two to three down, two to three back up; typical entry. [00:09:03]

# Small Account Pre-Trading Checklist

## Summary

Eight-item pre-trading checklist to use daily before starting to trade on small accounts. Covers: physical readiness (sleep/food), emotional state, stock selection criteria, market condition assessment, quality setup references, simulator track record, willingness to take no-trade days, and trade count limits. Includes printable PDF with 9 favorite chart patterns as reference guide.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:56-00:02:05] Item 1: Did you eat well and sleep well? Impacts discipline when needed
- [00:03:06-00:03:16] Item 2: Are you in a good mood? Short fuse leads to trading errors
- [00:04:44-00:05:05] Item 3: Do you have a stock that looks good based on your strategy?
- [00:05:36-00:06:01] Item 4: What's the overall market condition for your strategy? Adjust position size based on market heat
- [00:06:12-00:06:31] Item 5: Have you printed quality setups? Keep 9 favorite patterns visible
- [00:06:42-00:07:42] Item 6: Have you printed your simulator track record? Crucial for maintaining confidence through drawdowns
- [00:08:42-00:09:48] Item 7: Are you willing to take a no-trade day if nothing looks good?
- [00:09:55-00:10:27] Item 8: Are you restricting the number of trades? Recommended: 1 trade per day for small accounts

## Setups and patterns

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:05:09-00:05:12] Stock selection: gap up at least 30%, float under 20 million, price $5-$15, with catalyst
- [00:10:04-00:10:15] Recommended position scaling: 1 trade at low base profit scales up (100 shares × 20 cents = $20; 10,000 shares × 20 cents = $2,000)
- [00:10:27-00:10:36] Beginner preference: restrict to 1 trade per day to avoid emotional traps

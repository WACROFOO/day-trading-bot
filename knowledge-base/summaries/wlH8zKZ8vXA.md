# How to Pick the BEST Stocks for Trading (with ZERO experience)

## Summary

Process for identifying tradeable stocks using scanner criteria: minimum volatility (4-10% gain), relevant news catalyst, optimal price range ($1-$20), high relative volume (5x average), and low float (under 20 million shares). Explains why large-cap and penny stocks fail for active traders, and why float rotation is essential for explosive moves.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:29:49] Stock must be up at least 4% minimum; prefer 10%+ for decent trading opportunity, focusing on top 5-10 percentage gainers.
- [00:30:15] Catalyst is strongly preferred; avoid trades without clear fundamental news (buyouts, mergers cause unprofitable sideways price action).
- [00:30:24] Price range between $1 and $20 per share; this is the optimal "sweet spot" with $5-$10 being peak frequency for home runs.
- [00:30:45] Relative volume must be at least 5x above the stock's historical daily average to ensure sufficient buying pressure.
- [00:36:41] Float (shares available to trade) must be under 20 million shares; in cold markets, tighten to 10 million shares.

## Setups and patterns

- **Gap and go** — stock gaps higher at open on news, continues moving up through the session. [00:40:39]
- **Short squeeze** — stock down for extended period, then sudden short-covering breakout on technical reversal or news. [00:18:36]
- **Technical breakout** — no fundamental news, but previous chart pattern triggers automatic breakout; largest percentage gainers often result from this. [00:17:38]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Stock scanners filtered for: percent change from close, price, volume, relative volume, float, sector, news catalyst. [00:09:22]
- Pre-market and after-hours price tracking to identify leading gainers before regular trading hours. [00:44:12]
- Relative volume calculation — today's total volume divided by historical average volume for the stock. [00:30:46]

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:29:49] Minimum 4% gain to break standard deviation; 10%+ preferred; focus top gainers often 50-100%+ up.
- [00:30:24] Optimal price: $1-$20; sweet spot $5-$10 for frequency and profitability.
- [00:30:45] Relative volume minimum: 5x above average daily volume.

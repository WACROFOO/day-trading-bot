# $AURC +200% on a SPAC Setup

## Summary

Trader breaks down SPAC (special acquisition company) setup and how recent reverse split can amplify momentum potential. Discusses the five pillars of stock selection and how certain "juice factors" like SPAC status or reverse split add volatility multiplier. Demonstrates trading with small position sizes while maintaining capital preservation.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:23] Five pillars of quality setup: stock price $2-20, float under 20M shares, up 10%+ today, new fundamental catalyst, high relative volume.
- [00:05:59] SPAC trading: recognize SPAC structure (blank-check company) and its hot trading history; use same selection criteria as other stocks.
- [00:06:27] Spread management: when spread tightens (example: 12.50 by 13.80 to 13.15 by 13.80), it signals accumulation and reduces risk.

## Setups and patterns

- **SPAC pre-market gap** — SPAC gaps up from $10 level (typical trading price) on merger news or speculative interest. [00:03:27]
- **Spread compression entry** — spread tightens as buyers move orders higher; enter when spread compresses near resistance break. [00:06:40]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Level 2 market data — monitor spread and buyer order placement for accumulation signals. [00:06:17]
- Daily chart — identify previous highs and resistance levels (pre-market high: $14.88). [00:03:36]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **TradeStation Desktop Software** — Commission-free broker with Level 2 market data ($11/month), paper trading mode, and customizable hotkeys. [00:01:28]
- **Light Speed Trading** — Direct-access broker used as comparison; fastest execution, used for tape reading and scalping strategies. [00:04:32]
- **Level 2 Market Depth Window** — Shows bid/ask orders at different levels with customizable colors (green for bid, red for ask, configurable tiers). [00:12:15]
- **Time and Sales Window** — Displays executed trades in real-time with customizable columns (bid, ask, size, time). [00:21:24]
- **Order Entry Window** — Buy/sell buttons at bottom with share size entry, limit price adjustment, and routing selection. [00:18:16]
- **DAS Trader (Charles Schwab Integration)** — Third-party platform connecting to Charles Schwab brokerage for trading. [00:06:59]
- **Interactive Brokers** — Alternative direct-access broker with its own platform. [00:07:13]
- **Weeble** — Commission-free broker with mobile-first platform; allows percentage-based position sizing in hotkeys. [00:32:07]
- **Fidelity** — Commission-free broker; notably slow execution speed compared to others. [00:10:50]
- **Payment for Order Flow (PFOF)** — TradeStation routes to PFOF venues by default; average order execution speed Q3 2024: 0.44 seconds. [00:09:02]
- **Direct Access Routing (EDEX, ARCA)** — Available on TradeStation with per-share commission (50 cents per 100 shares with direct routing). [00:18:49]

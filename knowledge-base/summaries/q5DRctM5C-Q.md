# How to use Level 2 (with ZERO experience)

## Summary

Comprehensive guide to reading Level 2 market data and time and sales tape to predict short-term price movements and refine trade entries and exits. Teaches visualization of order flow imbalances, spread analysis, and how to identify support/resistance on the bid-ask side. Targets active day traders who want to master tape reading as a core skill alongside chart patterns.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:06:01] Maximum preferred spread for trading on stocks under $10/share is 15-20 cents; avoid trading if spread exceeds this.
- [00:19:02] Use 10-cent offset for buy limit orders and 10-cent offset for sell limit orders to allow some slippage.
- [00:21:12] Buy when price breaks above a large seller on the level two that is being absorbed; add to position when remaining shares drop below 10,000.
- [00:35:00] Enter on micro pullbacks to moving averages; for strong trends, sell on the ask to get best prices, buy on the ask when stock is squeezing up.
- [00:38:00] When green appears on the tape after entry, expect price to move higher; if red or white (in-between spread) appears instead, exit the trade as momentum is weak.
- [00:42:50] For pre-market and halt resumptions, monitor tape without stop orders (pre-market doesn't support stop orders).

## Setups and patterns

- **Red to Green move** — After initial sell-off to a low, price reverses with strong green orders resuming; watch for the exact high and low to estimate risk/reward. [00:15:00]
- **Psychological resistance at whole dollars and half-dollars** — Stocks often face resistance at round numbers ($750, $8.00, $8.50); look for large sellers here. [00:20:30]
- **Big buyer on bid acting as support** — When a 10,000+ share buyer sits on the bid, sellers are less willing to push past it; use this as a stop level. [00:21:40]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Level 2 data** — Shows all current bid/ask orders at different price levels and sizes; updated in real-time, not historical like candlesticks. [00:01:48]
- **Time and Sales tape** — Displays every individual order that goes through; green for ask (buy) orders, red for bid (sell) orders, white/black for between-spread fills. [00:11:15]
- **Spread analysis** — Difference between best bid and lowest ask; 1-cent spread is minimum for stocks over $1, wide spreads indicate illiquidity. [00:05:40]
- **Volume-weighted average price (VWAP)** — Used on intraday charts to show equilibrium; price above it suggests bullish control, below suggests bearish. [00:29:57]
- **ARCA, EDEX, other routing destinations** — Direct access brokers allow choosing specific market centers for order routing. [00:18:00]

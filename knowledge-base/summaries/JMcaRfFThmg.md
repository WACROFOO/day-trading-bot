# How to Trade Breaking News

## Summary

Tutorial on trading immediate market response to breaking news headlines using tight stop-losses and favorable risk-reward ratios. Demonstrates scalping approach to capitalize on the initial spike and pull back into profit before the news sentiment reverses. Shows example of celebrity endorsement news catalyst on beverage stock.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:00] Risk-reward framework: risk $700 to make $1,500-1,500 profit = 2:1 ratio; need only 33% win rate to breakeven.
- [00:12:38] Breaking news entry: wait for stock to respond to news before entering; buy on first micro pullback with tight stop at the low.
- [00:13:40] Stop placement: set stop at 7-8 cents below entry; if entry at 98 cents, stop at 89-90 cents.

## Setups and patterns

- **Breaking news micro pullback** — stock pops on news, dips slightly, buy the dip for the continuation higher. [00:06:04]
- **Circuit breaker halt level** — LULD (lower and upper limit down) level indicates where stock will halt if bid drops below it. [00:09:48]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- News scanner/desk — proprietary news feed that posts breaking headlines directly to trading platform. [00:03:39]
- Time and sales (ticker tape) — shows order flow and helps confirm buyer momentum (green on tape). [00:09:02]

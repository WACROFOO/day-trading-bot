# How to Read Technical Indicators on Candlestick Charts

## Summary

Comprehensive training on six core technical indicators and how they combine with candlestick chart patterns to predict price action. Covers moving averages, volume-weighted average price (VWAP), MACD, relative strength index (RSI), Bollinger bands, and volume profile. Emphasizes using indicators as confirmation tools (not guarantees) and avoiding indicator over-reliance. Designed for traders seeking edge in price prediction through pattern and indicator alignment.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:14:17] Moving averages to display: 9 EMA, 20 EMA, and 200 EMA on all timeframes (1-min, 5-min, daily).
- [00:14:17] Use exponential moving averages (EMA), not simple moving averages (SMA), for faster response to price.
- [00:13:01] 9 EMA usage: acts as support level on intraday charts; entering on pullback to 9 EMA is bullish.
- [00:13:41] 20 EMA usage: second level of support after 9 EMA breaks.
- [00:15:38] 200 EMA (daily chart): acts as resistance when price is below it; acts as support when price is above it.
- [00:19:15] VWAP usage: place on all intraday charts (1-min, 5-min); intraday-only indicator, not for daily charts.
- [00:25:27] VWAP interpretation: price above VWAP = bulls in control; price below VWAP = bears in control.
- [00:32:00] MACD settings: 12, 26, 9 (standard calculation; do not change).
- [00:35:01] Only trade when MACD histogram is green (positive); avoid trading when MACD is negative/red.
- [00:35:51] MACD negative crossing = stop trading that stock; take foot off the gas.
- [00:44:15] RSI length: 14 period with 14 smoothing (standard).
- [00:45:28] RSI overbought threshold: above 80 (or extended to 90s) signals potential reversal.
- [00:45:28] RSI oversold threshold: below 20 signals potential bounce; but market can remain irrational longer than solvent.
- [00:50:15] Bollinger bands settings: 20-period moving average with 2 standard deviations.
- [00:51:40] 99% of price action occurs within Bollinger bands; price outside bands is extreme.
- [00:51:47] Bollinger band breakout strategy: buy the bottom band break for move back up; short top band break for move back down.
- [00:52:45] Candle outside Bollinger bands that closes back inside = reversal signal.
- [00:58:17] Volume profile point of control: where most volume traded; act as support when price above, resistance when below.
- [01:00:01] Multi-timeframe alignment: 1-minute chart pullback to 20 EMA often aligns with 5-minute pullback to 9 EMA.
- [01:08:10] Never ignore: volume on candlestick charts (green/red volume bars showing buy/sell pressure).

## Setups and patterns

- **Doji candle** — small body with long wicks on both sides, signals indecision and potential reversal after strong move. [00:54:00]
- **Shooting star** — candle with large upper wick rejecting higher prices, signals weakness in uptrend. [00:47:30]
- **Bull flag pullback** — high-volume rally up, light-volume pullback to 9 EMA, then breakout higher. [00:21:13]
- **Classic micro pullback** — first candle makes new high, then pullback drop below VWAP, then break of VWAP with high volume. [00:29:09]
- **Topping tail candle** — candle that extends high then closes much lower, signals rejection of highs. [00:58:55]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **9 EMA (exponential moving average)** — support level on intraday; faster response than simple MA. [00:12:01]
- **20 EMA (exponential moving average)** — second support level when 9 breaks. [00:13:40]
- **200 EMA (daily chart)** — strong resistance when price below; strong support when price above. [00:15:38]
- **VWAP (volume-weighted average price)** — equilibrium point factoring volume at each price; intraday only. [00:25:06]
- **MACD (moving average convergence divergence)** — oscillator measuring divergence/convergence of moving averages. [00:31:40]
- **MACD histogram** — visual representation of MACD line above/below signal line; green = positive, red = negative. [00:34:38]
- **RSI (relative strength index)** — momentum oscillator 0-100 scale; overbought above 80, oversold below 20. [00:43:46]
- **Bollinger bands** — moving average with +/- 2 standard deviations showing price extremes. [00:50:13]
- **Volume profile** — shows cumulative volume traded at each price level; point of control = highest volume price. [00:58:07]
- **Thinkorswim platform** — charting platform used for examples; supports all indicators. [00:10:12]
- **Level 2 market data** — shows buy/sell orders at different prices (mentioned for future learning). [01:13:00]

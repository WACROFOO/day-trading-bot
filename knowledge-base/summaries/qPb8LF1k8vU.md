# How to Control Emotions as a Beginner Day Trader

## Summary

How emotional states drive profit and loss curves. Discusses tension between current P/L and desired result, creating desperation that leads to over-trading and amplified losses. Covers recognizing emotional triggers, setting hard stops, and leveraging confidence streaks while protecting downside during cold periods.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:06:02] Recognize hot streak signals (multiple green days, doubling/tripling daily goals) and increase position size aggressively.
- [00:08:31] Recognize cold market signals (flat P/L, choppy action) and reduce position size to cap downside.
- [00:12:14] Set max loss trigger at 20% giveback of daily profit; if losing more, stop trading for day.
- [00:13:19] Set max daily loss stop at $5,000 on red days; stop trading in hot market only if down more than $5,000.
- [00:13:33] Recognize emotional tipping points (frustrated, desperate, seeing red); walk away immediately.
- [00:14:46] Set hard guard rails (e.g., no more than X consecutive red trades before stopping for day).

## Setups and patterns

- **Hot streak identification** — Back-to-back days exceeding daily/monthly goals; market aligned with personal strategy. [00:05:54]
- **Cold market identification** — Choppy action, P/L flat or drifting; few clean setups. [00:07:36]

# How To Manage Position Sizing

## Summary

Discussion on balancing fixed share size vs. dynamic sizing based on trade quality. Fixed sizing (same shares every day, e.g., 3,000 shares) reduces emotional trading but may feel sub-optimal during hot markets. Dynamic sizing (smaller on weak setups, larger on A+ setups) maximizes profits but risks over-leveraging on losers.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:03:23] Dynamic sizing strategy: use smaller share size on lower-quality setups (B-grade), larger size on high-conviction (A+ grade) setups.
- [00:04:12] Consider fixed position sizing for one full month as an experiment to compare profit impact vs. dynamic sizing.
- [00:07:22] Profit calculation principle: if 70% accuracy with consistent $100-$200 risk per trade, use those fixed amounts consistently.
- [00:08:57] Avoid taking excessive share size to "make back" losses from earlier trades in same day; maintain discipline.
- [00:09:30] Fixed sizing discipline: once committed to 3,000 share size, honor it across all setups (not just small caps).

## Setups and patterns

- **A+ setup** — High-probability trade meeting multiple criteria (pre-market chart, news, technical alignment, volume). [00:03:38]
- **B-grade setup** — Lower-conviction trade; doesn't meet full criteria. [00:03:46]

# $18k in 30 minutes with this Day Trading Strategy

## Summary

Dissects an $18,538 profit trade on WETG (reverse split + AI news catalyst). Shows how to spot the setup (scanner hit, reverse split background knowledge), identify entry criteria (10%+ gain, 5x+ relative volume, AI buzzword, $2-10 price, <10M float), and execute micro pullback + candle over candle trades. Emphasizes patience over FOMO, risk management with smaller size into extended moves, and exiting before halts/flushes.

- [00:15:10] Build cushion on first entry before holding runner position for bigger move.
- [00:16:02] Reduce position size (from 4000 to 3000 shares) as price gets extended to manage risk.

- [00:21:57] On halt-down scenarios and big whips ($2-3 per share), exit position to avoid multi-million dollar loss.

# Indicators for Avoiding False Breakouts 🔪😭

## Summary

Teaches three-step system for avoiding false breakouts and bull/bear traps in volatile markets. Strategy focuses on trading only on the front-side of momentum moves when MACD is positive, price is above VWAP, and stock has no history of false breakouts.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:03:09] Only trade when stock is on front-side of move, not back-side.
- [00:05:25] Require MACD oscillator green (above signal line) before entering.
- [00:10:36] Trade only above volume weighted average price (VWAP).
- [00:13:21] Avoid stocks with history of false breakouts during same trading day.
- [00:25:39] Target modest daily goal: 20 cents per share (scale position sizing around this).
- [00:26:06] Use oscillating indicators as confirmation (green light/red light check).

## Setups and patterns

- **False breakout/bull trap** — stock breaks out then quickly reverses down [00:04:57].
- **Bear trap** — stock breaks down then quickly reverses up.
- **Topping tail** — long upper wick rejection candle [00:14:22].
- **Mountain range pattern (MACD)** — expanding oscillations indicate strengthening trend [00:04:19].
- **Moving average convergence** — averages moving closer together signals weakening trend.
- **Divergence** — averages moving apart signal strengthening trend.

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- MACD (moving average convergence divergence) displayed as colored mountain ranges [00:05:04].
- VWAP (volume weighted average price) orange line.
- Volume bars.
- Fast (9) and slow (20) moving averages.
- Oscillators (MACD configured with line and signal line).

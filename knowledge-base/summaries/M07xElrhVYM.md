## Setups and patterns

- **Dip trades on false breakouts** — stock pops above level, people sell, creates pullback to buy. [00:11:03]
- **Bull flag pattern** — three green candles, two small candles form flag, pops back up. [00:12:13]
- **Base hits** — consistent, readable price action trades; not outlier green days but foundation of daily gains. [00:57:02]
- **Parabolic squeezing** — stock ramping up hard; Danny's strong setup. [00:56:40]
- **Halts and false halts** — trading around circuit breaker halts; Danny's strength. [00:56:46]

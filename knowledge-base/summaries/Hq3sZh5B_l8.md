# Did I follow the 6 Rules of Trader Rehab?

## Summary

First day of "Trader Rehab" recovery protocol following two consecutive max-loss red days (down $20,000). Outlines six-step recovery process: acknowledge heightened emotional state, apply account restrictions, practice mindfulness, build small cushion, systematically recover to half the loss, then gradually remove restrictions. Case study shows GX reversal trade up 130% with restricted position sizing.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:42] STEP 1: Acknowledge heightened emotional state after big loss.
- [00:01:55] STEP 2: Apply account restrictions—reduce max share size to 1/4 normal (5,000 shares instead of 20,000).
- [00:03:10] STEP 3: Practice mindfulness and meditation; wear red shirt as psychological reminder of loss.
- [00:07:14] STEP 4: Build cushion with small green day; use low-risk starter positions in small account first.
- [00:15:14] STEP 5: Goal is recover half the loss (~$15,000 of $30,000) before removing restrictions.
- [00:18:03] STEP 6: After cushion built, gradually increase share size through week but stay disciplined.

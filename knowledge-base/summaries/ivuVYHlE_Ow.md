# Case Study of a 113% Gap and Go Day Trading Setup

## Summary

Daily trading recap with 4 stocks traded (2 winners, 2 losers) finishing $800 green. Demonstrates algo spike on CADL and MOMO that reversed, conservative position sizing after early losses, and successful trading of ADTX using ABCD pattern. Shows emotional discipline maintaining focus despite $4,000 swing loss late in day.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:05:39-00:05:59] When news breaks, market makers pull offers to manage risk, causing algo spike that may or may not hold
- [00:06:07-00:06:14] If stock spikes on news then reverses without fundamental reason, likely algo spike rejection
- [00:07:34-00:08:01] Position sizing after losses: reduce share count and be more conservative until proving setup works
- [00:11:38-00:12:01] After giving back profit, accept small win rather than risking complete reversal to red

## Setups and patterns

- **Algo spike rejection** — news causes aggressive market maker pullback, big spike, then flush back down [00:02:18-00:05:08]
- **ABCD pattern trade** — pop, pullback, pop fails high, pullback, break through high [00:07:17-00:08:43]

# Day Trading: How To Start From ZERO (Beginner's Guide)

## Summary

Comprehensive beginner roadmap covering the five phases of trader development, fundamentals of market structure, strategy adoption, simulator practice, tool selection, and the critical role of discipline. Addresses the two primary causes of beginner failure: trading without a defined strategy and lacking discipline to follow rules. Designed to help aspiring traders understand the realistic learning journey and avoid premature quit.

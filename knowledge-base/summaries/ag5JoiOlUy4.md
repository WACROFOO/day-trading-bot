# Surprise Daily Breakout Setup at 9:30am ET

## Summary

Daily market recap: how a stock halt triggered attention and led to profitable breakout trades. Discusses why no-news moves can be risky and the importance of having a strong daily chart setup before trading.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:92] Entry on resume from halt if stock breaks halt high with volume.

## Setups and patterns

- **Daily breakout** — Stock that pulls back after previous run, breaks back above pullback high. Requires news or catalyst. [00:184]
- **Halt setup** — Stock halts up (volatility halt), resumes with strong momentum. Riskier without clear catalyst. [00:56]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Stock halt scanner** — Identifies halts in real-time. [00:61]
- **MACD** — Used to assess momentum; can decline even as price rises. [00:244]
- **Relative volume** — High relative volume required for confidence in setup. [00:56]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)** — oscillator comparing 12 and 26 period moving averages; positive = bullish signal, negative = bearish signal. Used for entry/exit timing.
- **Level 2 market data** — shows all buyers and sellers at each price level; critical tool for experienced traders.
- **Thinkorswim** — free simulator but lacks performance metrics. [00:10:23]
- **Weeble** — simulator with decent quality; $9.99/month, no metrics included. [00:10:45]
- **Warrior Trading simulator** — included in Warrior Pro membership free for 90 days; includes aggregated performance metrics. [00:10:56]
- **Hot keys** — programmable keyboard shortcuts for rapid buy/sell execution.
- **Stock scanners** — software that searches entire market in real-time for stocks meeting specific criteria (high volume, high gains, price range).
- **Relative volume** — ratio of current volume to 50-day average volume; indicates unusual activity.
- **9 EMA (9-period exponential moving average)** — used as support/resistance level. [00:29:24]
- **20 EMA (20-period exponential moving average)** — used for trend confirmation. [00:29:24]

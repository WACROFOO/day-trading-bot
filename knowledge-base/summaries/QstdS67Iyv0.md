# How to Start Day Trading (with ZERO experience)

## Summary

A foundational guide to day trading that outlines a simple strategy: find trending stocks with volume catalyst, enter on pullback patterns, use tight stops, and scale out of winners. Includes specific chart pattern identification, scanner setup, and risk-reward calculations designed for small account traders seeking 10% daily account growth.

## Setups and patterns

- **Pullback pattern** — stock squeezes up, pulls back 1-4 red candles (not more than 50% of move), then breaks high of last pullback candle. [00:17:25]
- **Bull flag pattern** — sharp move up, consolidation pullback, break of consolidation high. [00:17:40]
- **Flat-top breakout** — consolidation at resistance level with breakout. [00:25:42]
- **ABCD pattern** — swing up (A-B), pullback to C, break of B. [00:25:40]
- **Double top / Cup and handle** — resistance level that requires handle (second pullback) before breaking through. [00:25:55]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Stock scanners** — real-time scanners to find stocks meeting criteria (five times relative volume, price $1-$20, up 10%, float under 20M). [00:14:38]
- **Finviz** — free scanner service with delayed data. [00:14:24]
- **Volume weighted average price (VWAP)** — used to confirm pullback support level. [00:19:14]
- **Level 2 market data** — bid/ask spread and volume at each price level to identify strength/weakness. [00:30:55]
- **Time and sales (tape)** — actual orders going through; look for green orders (buys) and burst of red (sells). [00:30:59]
- **High day scanner** — tracks stocks making new daily highs, shows which stocks have momentum. [00:31:36]

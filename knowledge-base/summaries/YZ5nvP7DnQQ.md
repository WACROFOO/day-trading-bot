# TOP 5 Day Trading Mistakes YOU NEED TO KNOW!!

## Summary

Five core mistakes beginner traders make: misunderstanding shadow theory with candlesticks, ignoring the 200-period moving average, not checking float, relying solely on daily charts without catalysts, and missing "juice factors" (recent IPOs, reverse splits, SPACs) that create explosive moves. Emphasizes combining technical analysis with fundamental catalyst research for better trading setup evaluation.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:50] Shadow Theory: Everything behind a candle has been broken; previous resistance/support at that price is invalidated.
- [00:02:13] When price breaks through a previous support/resistance level on a daily candle, all historical price action behind that candle is invalidated for future resistance/support purposes.
- [00:02:42] Do not draw horizontal lines at prices that have been broken through; those levels no longer matter.
- [00:05:56] Only look for next level of resistance by looking to the left and up (previous highs that haven't been broken yet).
- [00:14:32] The 200-period exponential moving average is one of the most respected levels on daily charts.
- [00:14:59] Check position of 200 EMA before considering entry—if stock is below it, it will likely run into resistance there.
- [00:16:06] Float is the supply side of supply-demand equation; low float with high demand creates explosive moves.
- [00:17:35] Supply-demand imbalance: Stock up 10%+, high relative volume, price $2-20 range, news catalyst, float under 10 million shares = strong setup.
- [00:18:15] With 1 million share float trading 30 million shares (30x float rotation), anyone wanting to sell has had opportunity; if still going higher, strong demand signal.
- [00:18:32] With 300 million share float, even if up 40-50%, there are still 270 million shares of holders who could sell, requiring 800-900 million shares of volume to push 40-50% in one day (rare).
- [00:26:14] Do not rely purely on daily charts for entry; combine with catalyst research—perfect chart pattern means nothing without catalyst.
- [00:27:45] Catalyst is more important than chart pattern—strong catalyst can override negative chart features like being under 200 EMA.
- [00:31:01] After identifying catalyst-driven stock movement, analyze daily chart for resistance areas.

## Setups and patterns

- **Shadow Theory** — Price action that occurred before a candle broke a level is invalidated and doesn't serve as future support/resistance. [00:01:45]
- **Gap setup** — Opening gap on chart showing price gap between close and next open. [00:05:00]
- **Window setup** — Large candle creating visual gap/window in chart where no trading occurred. [00:04:20]
- **Double top pattern** — Stock reaches previous all-time high twice, creating resistance at that level. [00:08:56]
- **Blue sky setup** — Stock breaks above all-time highs with no overhead resistance; occurs after recent IPOs, SPACs, or reverse splits. [00:36:01]
- **Hammer candlestick** — Long bottom wick candle showing selling pressure followed by recovery; indicates potential reversal. [00:37:43]
- **Continuation scanner patterns** — Stocks making biggest percentage range moves typically have lower floats (most under 10 million shares). [00:20:11]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **200 EMA (Exponential Moving Average)** — Critical daily chart indicator; stocks often run into resistance when approaching this level. [00:11:39]
- **200 SMA (Simple Moving Average)** — Alternative to EMA; some traders prefer; good to have both on chart. [00:12:44]
- **Bollinger Bands** — Standard 20-period with 2 standard deviation; used for identifying extremes. [00:18:05]
- **Relative Strength Index (RSI)** — Shows overbought/oversold conditions. [00:17:55]
- **VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price)** — Used to identify resistance/support levels.
- **Float data** — Must check current float before trading; displayed on scanner or trading platform. [00:16:02]
- **High-of-day momentum scanner** — Scanner setup filtering for stocks hitting new daily highs. [00:36:07]
- **Continuation scanner** — Scanner showing stocks with biggest 2-week percentage range moves. [00:20:06]

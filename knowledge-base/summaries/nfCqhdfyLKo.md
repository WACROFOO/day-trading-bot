# How Read PRICE ACTION on Candlestick Charts (with ZERO experience)

## Summary

In-depth walkthrough of recognizing quality vs. poor price action on candlestick charts. Trader demonstrates real trades from a choppy day (TVGN, TCBP, VVPR) to illustrate signs of bad price action (rejections, topping tails, huge sellers, broken patterns) versus clean price action (high-volume moves, light-volume pullbacks, pattern resolution). Emphasizes position sizing caps, patience during choppy markets, and walking away rather than overtrading weak setups.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:00:34] Good price action shows clean breakouts with traditional patterns (bull flag); poor shows rejections and false breakouts.
- [00:01:21] Volume profile during a strong move: high volume on the move up, light volume on pullback, then higher volume on next move up.
- [00:03:23] Breakouts and consolidations should resolve cleanly; if multiple rejections occur on high volume, price action is poor.
- [00:10:13] Bad price action checklist: no pattern resolution, topping tails, huge sellers on Level 2, nasty flushes/drops, unable to hold VWAP.
- [00:11:17] During choppy price action, reduce position size to cap maximum loss (e.g., 2,000-5,000 shares vs. full 15,000-20,000 share size).
- [00:13:42] Only increase position size once hitting a profit threshold (e.g., $1,000 profit minimum) before risking full daily size.
- [00:17:28] Position sizing: start each day with small cap (5,000 shares max) until breaking into profit; raise cap only after hitting profit milestone.
- [00:26:01] Exit signal: if you have three consecutive losses, walk away; do not continue trading.
- [00:31:03] Time window: trading window typically 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m.; cap position size back to small if approaching window close.
- [00:42:34] Price stalls immediately after entry = exit indicator.
- [00:44:02] Big seller appears on Level 2 = exit indicator.
- [00:45:10] Jack knife candle pattern = exit immediately.
- [00:47:56] Do not overstay; take profit and exit rather than holding for maximum gain.

## Setups and patterns

- **Micro pullback** — stock moving up quickly, gets small pullback on light volume, then resumes higher; safest entry point [00:01:21]
- **Bull flag** — initial squeeze up followed by light consolidation then break higher [00:00:43]
- **Tweezer/Chopstick rejection candles** — two mirror-image rejections indicating weakness [00:08:23]
- **False breakout** — breakout with rejection, closing as shooting star [00:01:05]
- **Jack knife pattern** — stock goes up then sharply reverses in single candle (diving board motion) [00:45:10]
- **Head and shoulders** — classic reversal pattern with first pullback, second pullback (head), third pullback (shoulder) [00:38:26]

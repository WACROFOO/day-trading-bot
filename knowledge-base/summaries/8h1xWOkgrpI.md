# Day Trading Tips: How To Build A Watch List

## Summary

Systematic pre-market watch list building: gap scanner (any stock up/down 4%+), research headlines on MarketWatch and Benzinga, assess float and daily chart resistance, identify former runners. Focus on scanner discipline plus headline confirmation to narrow candidates.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:00:41] Primary scanner: any stock gapping up or down >4%.
- [00:01:22] Check daily chart before adding to watch list; look for 200 MA as potential resistance.
- [00:01:40] Pre-market chart analysis: if stock pops up then fades back down, caution; fading = weak setup.
- [00:02:53] Low float stock (e.g., 4.28 million shares) with fresh catalyst can move quickly and break resistance.
- [00:03:30] News catalyst must exist; without news, risk is higher.
- [00:04:02] Headline quality assessment: if headline vague or unclear, skip trade. Example: mergers with outlined prices = flat trade (no volatility).

## Setups and patterns

- **Merger announcement (non-binding LOI)** — letter of intent can still produce volatility; binding deals typically price stock flat. [00:04:01]
- **Crowded setup** — when volume is extremely heavy pre-market, setup may be "crowded" with both longs and shorts creating tug-of-war; first pullback after open may be only good entry. [00:07:38]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Trade Ideas** — scanner tool used for gapper identification; includes float and info windows. [00:00:12]
- **eSignal** — charting platform used for daily and pre-market analysis. [00:00:20]

- **200 moving average (daily)** — key resistance level on daily chart. [00:01:35]

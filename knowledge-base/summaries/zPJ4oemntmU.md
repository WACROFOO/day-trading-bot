# 3 Lessons I Learned Making $60k in 1 Month : Behind The Trades Ep. 04

## Summary

An episode detailing three critical lessons learned during an exceptionally profitable month: being aggressive in strong markets, tolerating large losses without emotional trading, and waiting for pullback entries instead of chasing. Includes detailed trading statistics and breakdowns of wins and losses demonstrating how market conditions affect profitability.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:04:59] Stop at the low of the pullback, never wider.
- [00:08:07] Know your max loss and profit target before entering a trade.
- [00:17:05] Trading pullback entries only, buying the pullbacks with stop at the low.
- [00:17:12] Three-strikes rule: try a stock three times, if you lose three times, quit the stock.
- [00:44:17] Buy the pullback, do not chase stocks into highs.
- [00:44:41] Wait for the pullback entry before taking a trade.
- [00:45:12] Do not take more than 10 trades per day (recommended 3-5 trades per day).
- [00:08:10] A trade is not a trade until you know both your loss and profit target.

# Day Trading For Beginners in 3 Steps

## Summary

Three critical steps to avoid costly learning losses: paper trade first, prove profitability in a demo account before going live, and build skill-based foundation rather than gambling with real money. The video emphasizes risk management, consistent statistics, and the necessity of 6-12 months of focused practice before expecting real profits.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:00] Never trade with real money until you've proven success in a paper/demo account.
- [00:02:15] Watch only 3-4 stocks per day to avoid missing setups; avoid spreading attention across 100+ stocks.
- [00:08:10] Trade only during highest-success periods based on historical data (for the instructor: 9:30-11:30 AM).
- [00:09:07] Apply a risk-reward ratio where winners are bigger than losers; target minimum 2:1 profit-loss ratio.
- [00:09:32] Implement a daily shut-off valve; stop trading once you hit a specific loss limit (example: down $500, stop for the day).
- [00:10:04] First month: risk $50 per trade; target $125 daily profit. If you hit max loss, close trading for the day.
- [00:21:21] Month 1-3: aim for 10 trades per day, 50% success rate, 2:1 profit-loss ratio; gradually increase risk from $50 to $200 per trade.
- [00:22:14] Month 2-3: increase risk incrementally ($75-$100); don't expect profitability in first 3 months.
- [00:22:01] Scale position size only after consistent profitability in paper trading for 3+ months.

## Setups and patterns

- **Gap and go** — Sharp move up on volume, pullback, then break of consolidation high. [00:05:54]
- **First pullback after initial move** — Micro pullback entry on momentum stocks. [00:07:24]
- **Flat top breakout** — Stock consolidates sideways then breaks high. [00:07:26]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Paper trading/demo accounts — Free way to build historical trading data without capital loss. [00:01:05]

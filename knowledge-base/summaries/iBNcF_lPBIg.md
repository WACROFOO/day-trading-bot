- [00:39:54] Green-on-tape = orders executing at or ABOVE ask (buyers aggressive). Red = at or BELOW bid (sellers aggressive). White = between spread.

## Setups and patterns

- **First pullback pattern** — stock gaps up or rallies hard, pulls back 25-50% of move (NOT more than 50%), bases out for 1-3 candles, then breaks high of previous wave. Buy first candle making new high. [00:23:01]
- **ABCD pattern** — extended rally followed by two separate pullbacks and two breakout attempts, fourth wave often final setup before move higher. More complex than first pullback. [00:32:08]
- **Bull flag** — sharp vertical move, consolidation/flag, break of flag resistance. [00:20:12]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Stock scanner** — proprietary real-time scanner alerts on: stocks up 10%+, relative volume 5x+, price $5-$20, low float. Audio alert triggers when criteria hit. [00:16:22]
- **Level 2 market data (order book)** — shows bid/ask prices and share sizes queued at each level; reveals hidden buying/selling pressure before breakout. [00:36:53]
- **Time and sales (tape)** — displays every trade in real time; green = aggressive buying, red = aggressive selling, white = between bid-ask. [00:39:54]
- **9 EMA** — on 1-minute pullback chart, common support area for buying pullbacks. [00:24:58]

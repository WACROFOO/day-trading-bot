# Bollinger Bands: Why Millionaire Traders Are Using This Technical Indicator

## Summary

Comprehensive guide to using Bollinger Bands as a reversal prediction tool. Explains the self-fulfilling prophecy of technical analysis where traders following the same signals create correlated price reactions. Covers the mathematical calculation, primary reversal indicator (price outside bands), and proper indicator settings to avoid customization pitfalls. Demonstrates real stock examples (CTMX, PPAL, SCNI) with 1-minute and 5-minute applications. Suitable for traders at all levels seeking improved reversal timing.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bollinger Band reversal** — Candle outside bands with topping tail (red candle) predicts pullback to middle band/VWAP. [00:03:24]
- **Doji candle** — Small body with wicks on both sides; outside bands = high reversal probability. [00:14:66]
- **Spinning top** — Similar to doji; indicates indecision and reversal likelihood when at extremes. [00:03:57]
- **Band compression** — Very narrow bands indicate low volatility; often precedes expansion move. [00:10:30]
- **Rejection candle** — Red topping tail candle at band extreme shows buyer rejection. [00:03:56]

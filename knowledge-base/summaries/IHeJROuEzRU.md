# ABCD Pattern: A Beginners Day Trading Strategy

## Summary

Detailed breakdown of the ABCD multi-candlestick pattern, which forms when a bull flag initially fails to break through high of day (point B). Pattern consists of initial move up (A), pullback (B), another move up that fails high of day (C), and final pullback (D) before eventual breakout. Discusses entry and exit strategies, the importance of 9 EMA support, and how ABCD patterns are strong because they give traders time to recognize them.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:01:14-00:01:18] Point B: first candle to make new high but does NOT break through high of day
- [00:02:35-00:02:39] Point C: pullback cannot go lower than point A; can double bottom
- [00:03:35-00:03:51] Entry: first candle to make new high out of pattern, or break through point B high
- [00:03:51-00:04:09] Stop placement: below the double bottom at point C, use moving average support
- [00:04:09-00:04:31] Must not over-leverage into high of day break; good entry with stop means profit is captured even if breakout fails

## Setups and patterns

- **ABCD pattern (multi-timeframe)** — Move (A), Pullback (B), Secondary move up failing to break (C), Second pullback (D), then breakout [00:02:42-00:03:35]
- **Bull flag within ABCD** — initial formation can be traded as bull flag during points A-B [00:01:25-00:02:01]
- **Double bottom at point C** — pullback can touch point A level exactly [00:02:59-00:03:05]
- **Micro pullback following breakout** — small pullback after breaking point B, good re-entry [00:04:36-00:04:51]
- **Multi-timeframe alignment** — ABCD on 1-minute can form bull flag on 5-minute simultaneously [00:05:03-00:07:40]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **9 EMA (Exponential Moving Average)** — consolidation should stay above this level [00:03:28-00:03:29]
- **Volume profile** — high volume on green candles, light volume on pullback candles desired [00:08:05-00:08:23]

# How I made $22,654 in LESS THAN 90 min of Day Trading | Deep Dive

## Summary

Deep dive into trading a biotech stock that moved 600% on positive clinical trial news. Covers the screening criteria for identifying high-momentum gap stocks, the setup pattern matching from prior trades, use of level-2 data and tape reading for entries, and scaling position into winners rather than losers.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:09:02] Stock must be priced between $2 and $20 to see the biggest percentage moves.
- [00:09:07] Relative volume must be at least five times average, indicating strong demand.
- [00:09:26] Stock must be up at least 10% on the day, breaking standard deviation and indicating strong moves.
- [00:09:49] Float must be under 20 million shares, creating supply constraint.
- [00:13:31] Use breakout strategy: buy as stock breaks through resistance of prior high after pullback.
- [00:17:41] Add to winning positions as they strengthen rather than averaging down into losers.
- [00:21:51] Profit target is retest of previous high; stop loss at support holding post-breakout.
- [00:26:56] Exit partially on breakout of psychological whole-dollar resistance levels like $5 a share.

## Setups and patterns

- **Squeeze-pullback-break pattern** — stock makes sharp move up, pulls back micro-pullback on 1-minute, then breaks higher again. [00:13:31] Similar pattern occurred on SLNO two days prior, indicating this was a repeating setup at that time.
- **Tape reading with level-2** — when stock hits resistance with bid support showing multiple buyers, that signals strength to press buy button. [00:21:25]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **Gap scanner (Ross' Gap Scan)** — ranks stocks by percentage gain; used each morning to identify leading gappers and largest movers. [00:03:57]
- **Level-2 data** — shows bid/ask spread and order book depth; used to see waiting buyers and sellers that don't appear on chart. [00:18:20]
- **Tape reading** — monitoring live order flow on level-2 to recognize buying momentum (green orders) vs. selling pressure. [00:25:56]

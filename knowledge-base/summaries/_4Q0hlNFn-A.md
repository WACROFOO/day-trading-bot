# Top 3 MOST Profitable Candlestick Chart Patterns (Full Training)

## Summary

Teaches the three most profitable candlestick patterns for day trading: candle-over-candle, bull flag, and ABCD pattern. Covers the anatomy of individual candlesticks and how to identify and trade reversal patterns with proper risk-to-reward ratios at support levels.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:23:02] Enter on the first candle that breaks above the previous candle's high (candle-over-candle pattern).
- [00:27:34] Set stop-loss at the low of the pullback candle in a bull flag setup, not wider.
- [00:29:07] Minimum profit target should provide at least 2:1 risk-to-reward ratio; aim high on initial targets.
- [00:40:15] On ABCD pattern, do not break the low of the second pullback—if it does, the pattern fails and trend reverses.
- [00:41:11] On ABCD pattern, wait for break of previous resistance level (not first candle to make new high) before full-size entry.
- [00:41:17] Set stop-loss on ABCD at low of latest dip; entry distance to stop is larger than bull flag but payoff is greater.

## Setups and patterns

- **Candle-over-candle** — first candle makes large body move up, second candle breaks above first's high; entry signal at the break. [00:23:02]
- **Bull flag** — sharp move up, red pullback candle, green breakout candle that makes new high; entry on first candle break. [00:25:52]
- **Doji (standard)** — opens and closes at same or nearly same price with upper and lower wicks; indicates indecision. [00:12:10]
- **Gravestone doji** — opens and closes at same price but has only upper wick, no lower wick; bearish reversal indicator. [00:15:23]
- **Dragonfly doji** — opens and closes at same price with lower wick only; bullish reversal indicator. [00:16:42]
- **Hammer** — small body with large lower wick; hammering out a base, bullish reversal. [00:17:39]
- **Shooting star** — small body with large upper wick; bearish reversal. [00:18:27]
- **Spinning top** — small body with both upper and lower wicks; indicates indecision. [00:20:05]
- **ABCD pattern** — bull flag into stall, pullback that holds support, then breakout above previous resistance; seven to nine candles total. [00:38:02]

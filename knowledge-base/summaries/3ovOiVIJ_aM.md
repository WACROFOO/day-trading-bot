# I made a mistake today…

## Summary

Short emotional debrief of trading mistakes made in a cold market day. Covers broker restrictions on small-cap stocks, market imbalance toward short sellers, and personal emotional mistakes (getting stubborn, adding to losing positions). Discusses accuracy degradation in cold markets and solutions (position-size limits, patience, selective quality-only trading).

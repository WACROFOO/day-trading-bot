## Setups and patterns

- **Flag pattern** — series of up candles (3-10), consolidation/pullback, then move up; repeat stair-step. [00:19:16] [00:19:41]
- **Pop-up consolidation and move up** — spike up, flatten, spike up again in stair-step pattern. [00:20:41]
- **Breakout of resistance** — stock consolidating below level, buyers come in on break above; shorts cover on spike. [00:20:08]
- **Reversal rubber-band snap-back** — stock stretched to downside (oversold), snap back to upside on first candle new high. [00:29:01]
- **Wedge breakout and ascending wedge breakout** — forms during early-day pause, break triggers continuation. [00:25:39]

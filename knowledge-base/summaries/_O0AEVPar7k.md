# 7 Day Traders discuss Managing Risk & Golden Rules

## Summary

Panel discussion with seven profitable day traders (combined $18M+ in profits) revealing their core trading rules and secret weapons. Common themes: stick to one strategy, manage red days aggressively, study market conditions, maintain emotional control, and focus on conviction-based setups rather than quantity of trades.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:12] Chris's rule: Stick to one strategy and optimize it rather than constantly switching.
- [00:02:17] Chris: Decide on stock types (small caps), long/short bias, and specific setups (extensions back to 13 EMA).
- [00:05:02] Danny's rule: Decrease the amount of red days rather than maximizing green days.
- [00:05:35] Danny: When down 2-3 hundred dollars, count it as breakeven.
- [00:05:45] Danny: When down $1,000, still okay; tighten up at negative $4-5k or $10k losses.
- [00:06:36] Jess's rule: Don't have too many rules; keep trading flexible for moving stocks.
- [00:04:29] Jess: Focus on high rate of change stocks regardless of float (if moving, it has range).
- [00:09:44] Max's rule: Treat each loss as one step closer to success; learn and filter out non-working trades.
- [00:10:58] Manoli's rule: Trade the theme; capitalize on what's currently moving (pennies, reverse splits).
- [00:14:36] Ross's core rule: Simplify strategy down to one profitable niche (pre-market gappers with news/catalyst).
- [00:14:52] Only trade stocks in specific float and price range with clear catalyst.
- [00:15:13] Avoid jumping between strategies when one isn't working (small caps → penny stocks → options).

## Setups and patterns

- [00:16:35] Ross's favorite: small cap, low float, momo stock at 8:30-9:00 AM with news (Amazon investment, pharma), algos spike 50%, micro pullback, then keeps going.
- [00:16:49] Jess's favorite: stocks that squeeze up on algo, halt, get trapped, then wick flush and reclaim pattern (extreme momentum shows more to come).
- [00:17:13] Max's favorite: ABCD pattern at all-time highs with bottoming tail between C and D (very specific visual).
- [00:17:36] Danny's favorite: halt up multiple times, then "buy the dip" washout long pattern.

- [00:18:32] Manoli's favorite: washout long after parabolic move catalyst-driven (like DWE 1000%+, halt down 50-60%, then range trade).
- [00:19:09] Manoli also likes sympathy plays on similar stocks.

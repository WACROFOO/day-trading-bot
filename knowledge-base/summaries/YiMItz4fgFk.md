## Setups and patterns

- **Candlestick anatomy** — Each candle communicates four data points (open, close, high, low) showing price action sentiment within a period. [00:04:21]
- **Long-body candle** — Opens near bottom, closes near top, indicates strong bullish buying sentiment. [00:09:42]
- **Spinning top** — Small body with upper and lower wicks; signals indecision between buyers and sellers. [00:10:03]
- **Doji** — Opens and closes at same price; classic indecision candle. [00:10:55]
- **Gravestone doji** — Large upper wick only; reversal signal showing sellers pushed price back down. [00:14:32]
- **Shooting star** — Small red candle with large upper tail; indicates reversal has begun after an uptrend. [00:13:56]
- **Dragonfly doji** — Large lower wick with small body; at downtrend bottom, shows buyers stepped in. [00:14:44]
- **Hammer** — Bottoming tail with small green body; reversal already beginning at trend lows. [00:16:18]
- **Bull flag** — Sharp move up followed by small pullback (consolidation) on decreasing volume, break above consolidation high is entry. [00:36:35]
- **Micro pullback** — First pullback after initial sharp move up on high-float stocks; entry opportunity. [00:00:19]

# Day Trading a Continuation Setup

## Summary

Case study of a single continuation trade on ARTL (Biosciences stock) with recent reverse split and low float. Trader enters on daily breakout, adds on strength, faces seller resistance, manages emotion, and closes with $6,020 profit after managing near $7,300 peak.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:03:06] Enter on the break of daily breakout high from previous day. ARTL was blocked at 837 previous day; break above 837 is the trigger.
- [00:03:22] On add-on: when entry proves correct (daily breakout made), add shares on the next move up within the same breakout move.
- [00:03:36] Target after daily breakout: look for room to the next major resistance level (200 MA in this case around 1127).
- [00:04:01] Monitor MACD for negative signals; if MACD goes negative while you're in a position, reduce or exit.
- [00:05:59] Exit signal: if stock shows topping tails on repeated attempts to hold highs, it signals weakness; do not hold through topping tails.

## Setups and patterns

- **Continuation setup** — stock that was active on a previous day with catalysts, pulled back overnight, now attempting to resume the breakout. [00:00:56]
- **Daily breakout** — breaking the high from the previous day's price action. [00:03:22]
- **Topping tail** — candlestick with small body but long upper wick, indicates sellers pushing down after buyers tried to run higher. [00:05:46]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **MACD** — Moving Average Convergence Divergence; when negative, signals downside momentum and exit signal. [00:06:06]
- **VWAP** — Volume-weighted average price, used as support/resistance level. [00:06:00]
- **200 moving average** — longer-term resistance level. [00:03:34]
- **Level 2** — displays ask and bid for execution. [00:04:01]

# $TCBP $OTRK Day Trading from Gap & Momentum Scanners

## Summary

Recap of day trading TCBP and OTRK using gap and momentum scanners while traveling. The video demonstrates how the trader found leading gappers using Day Trade Dash scanner software, executed multiple dip trades on TCBP, and managed risk after a previous red day by keeping positions smaller and exiting after securing profits.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:39] Use Day Trade Dash scanners or daytrade-dash.com to find leading gappers and gainers.
- [00:03:14] Do dip trades off moving averages and VWAP rather than breakout trades when a stock is rejecting prior highs.
- [00:04:03] For low float stocks on light volume, limit position size to 2,000-5,000 shares to avoid excessive risk.
- [00:08:05] After a red day, focus on keeping position sizes small and walking away once you've made a target profit, rather than overtrading to recover losses.

## Setups and patterns

- **Dip and rip** — buying the dip off moving averages or VWAP and selling into bounces. [00:03:14]
- **Volume-weighted average price (VWAP) break** — entering trades when stock breaks above VWAP during squeeze moves. [00:05:06]
- **Halt and resume** — stocks that halt on halts due to volatility and resume trading at higher prices. [00:03:42]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Day Trade Dash scanner software — identifies leading gappers and high momentum stocks [00:00:30]
- VWAP (volume-weighted average price) — used as support/resistance level for entries and dips [00:03:14]
- Moving averages (20-period mentioned) — used to identify dips for entry [00:03:19]

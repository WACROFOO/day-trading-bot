# How to Read CANDLESTICK Charts (Step-by-Step Beginners Guide)

## Summary

Comprehensive beginner-to-intermediate guide on candlestick chart pattern reading and what individual candle shapes communicate about market sentiment. Teaches pattern recognition for identifying reversal signals, support/resistance bounces, and exhaustion patterns. Shows how to apply candle language to real trading charts.

## Setups and patterns

- **Long-body candle** — large body indicates strong directional sentiment (bullish if green, bearish if red). [00:03:07]
- **Short-body candle** — small body shows less conviction than long body; less bullish sentiment. [00:03:26]
- **Spinning top** — long upper and lower wicks with small body; communicates indecision. [00:03:44]
- **Doji** — opens and closes at basically same price; indicates indecision. [00:04:10]
- **Long-legged doji** — very long wicks in both directions; extreme indecision. [00:04:13]
- **Topping tail** — upper candle wick on red candle at resistance; bearish (sellers push price down). [00:05:32]
- **Bottoming tail** — lower candle wick on green candle at support; bullish (buyers step in). [00:05:55]
- **Dragonfly doji** — long lower wick, opens and closes at top; bullish reversal indicator at bottom of move. [00:06:18]
- **Gravestone doji** — long upper wick, opens and closes at bottom; bearish reversal indicator at top of move. [00:06:20]
- **Hanging man** — small body with long lower wick at top of uptrend; reversal indicator. [00:07:09]
- **Hammer** — same shape as hanging man but appears at bottom of downtrend; bullish reversal/support signal. [00:09:32]
- **Shooting star** — small body with long upper wick at top of uptrend; bearish reversal. [00:12:23]
- **Stair-step pattern** — alternating moves up and sideways, up and sideways; gradual climb with consolidation. [00:15:21]

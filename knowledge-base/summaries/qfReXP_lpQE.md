# The Discipline NOT to Trade Low Quality Setups

## Summary

Reflection on taking a no-trade day after a $77,000 loss over 2 days and implementing trader rehab with guard rails. Examines several stocks that hit scanners but lacked quality setup criteria (SkyQ, ONCSO, GLMD, CLIK) and explains why each was rejected despite being tempting. Emphasizes maintaining high conviction standard (95%+) for trade entries, especially after losses.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:03:01-00:03:08] Relative volume minimum: need at least 5x relative volume ratio (5 times normal average volume)
- [00:00:52-00:01:02] Guard rails after losses: max 10,000 share position size and $6,000 max daily loss
- [00:01:31-00:01:42] Trade the best, leave the rest philosophy

## Setups and patterns

- **Continuation momentum setup** — stock popping up on volume with pull-backs [00:02:13-00:02:17]

# How to use the Relative Volume Trading Strategy (with ZERO experience)

## Summary

Ross Cameron explains relative volume as his primary technical indicator for identifying stocks with homerun potential. The video demonstrates how high relative volume (today's volume compared to historical average) signals unusual market interest and identifies which stocks are worth trading. Performance data shows nearly 98% of profits over 4 years come from stocks with relative volume of 5 or higher.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:05:02] Buy the pullback after the stock makes a quick move up on high volume, wait for first candle to make new high.
- [00:34:08] Take profits early on dips when using the micro-pullback strategy.
- [00:13:18] Only trade stocks with high relative volume (at least 5x normal), not just high total volume.
- [00:17:30] Focus on top leading gappers by percentage gain to access stocks with most trader attention.

## Setups and patterns

- **Gap and continuation** — stock gaps up on news, then continues higher with sustained high relative volume. [00:11:52]
- **Red-to-green move** — stock pulls back on lighter volume, then transitions to green and rallies. [00:34:51]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Relative volume ratio indicator (compares today's volume to 30-day or 50-day average).
- Stock scanner filtering for top percentage gainers.
- Level 2 market data and time & sales for order flow analysis.

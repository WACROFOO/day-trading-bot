# How I'm RED on a stock up 300%...

## Summary

Psychology-focused breakdown of trading CYN (Cyan, up 250%+) unsuccessfully despite large account size. Demonstrates how to recognize when a stock's price action (choppy, grinding, low relative volume) doesn't fit your strategy and the discipline to walk away emotionally hijacked to avoid revenge trading and account drawdown.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:04:15] Three strikes rule: exit strategy after three consecutive losses on the same stock.
- [00:06:53] Add support at 9.40, then 9.50, then 9.75, with full 30,000-share position targeting break of $10.00.
- [00:06:59] Stop loss when breakout fails; if stock cannot break through expected resistance, exit rather than hold.
- [00:15:10] Recognize when a stock profile (choppy, grinding micro-moves) doesn't match your edge; walk away to avoid emotional hijack.
- [00:21:23] Walk away with cushion rather than chase; emotional discipline is more valuable than margin.

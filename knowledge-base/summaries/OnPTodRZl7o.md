# Trading Multi-Day Continuation Setups

## Summary

Trader discusses day two and multi-day continuation setups when stocks fail to have fresh news catalysts. Shows how volume patterns shift on continuation days and demonstrates trading the break of VWAP setup. Emphasizes that day-one moves are typically stronger and cleaner than day-two.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:03:28] Continuation trade: take break of VWAP setup on day two if stock is holding support and trending higher.
- [00:05:59] Secondary offering impact: company selling shares dilutes existing holders; stock typically drops on dilution news.
- [00:02:18] Day-two characteristics: volume typically lower, price action choppier, element of surprise gone; fewer predictable patterns.

## Setups and patterns

- **Continuation bounce** — stock had big move on day one, pulls back on day two, then rallies off support; break of VWAP on day two. [00:01:54]
- **Inverted head and shoulders** — pullback followed by higher pivot, then break through resistance; seen on EDBL. [00:07:38]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- VWAP (volume-weighted average price) — break above VWAP on day-two continuation can signal resumption of uptrend. [00:02:30]
- MACD indicator — helps confirm momentum; red MACD indicates weakness. [00:07:22]
- Level 2 market data — shows market congestion and buying/selling pressure; "thick" level 2 indicates heavy participation. [00:06:26]

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:00:06] Daily P&L: up $11,000 on day with 5 stocks traded; 2 red, 3 green trades.
- [00:00:17] Accuracy: lower than typical because no fresh hot news catalysts.
- [00:01:20] Previous two days: up $50,000 and $60,000 respectively.
- [00:04:36] EDBL float: 1.3 million shares; day-one volume 90 million; day-two volume 65 million and rising.

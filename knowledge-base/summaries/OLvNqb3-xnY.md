# How to Day Trade Stocks with Big Spreads

## Summary

Large bid-ask spreads indicate volatility but create significant risk management challenges for day traders. This video explains why stocks with bigger spreads often make big moves, the profit-loss ratio problems they create, and the criteria for selecting tradeable stocks within a manageable spread range.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:06:16] When trading a stock with a 50-cent spread at the ask, your stop must realistically be at least the width of the spread below the bid, not a tighter stop.
- [00:07:12] Require a 2-to-1 profit-loss ratio minimum on trades; if you risk $1.10 per share, you must target $2.20 per share profit potential.
- [00:11:32] Focus on stocks priced between $2 and $20 per share; it is easier for a $3 stock to move 30 cents than for a $30 stock to move 3 points.
- [00:12:30] Look for stocks with: price between $2-$20, up at least 10%, relative volume of at least 5x, news/catalyst, and float under 20 million shares.
- [00:19:07] Avoid stocks with total volume below 10 million shares; liquidity is required to take meaningful position sizes.

## Setups and patterns

- **Breakout trade on big-spread stock** — buy the break of resistance on high volume; requires extra-tight risk due to spread. [00:08:02]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Level 2 market data — used to understand bid-ask spread width and available liquidity.
- Relative volume scanner — filters for stocks with significantly above-average daily volume.
- High-frequency trading — mentioned as causing drops and pops unrelated to actual trades on low-volume stocks.

## Numbers and thresholds

- [00:12:01] Stock OMH had float of 20 million shares (upper limit for consideration).
- [00:12:32] Rule of thumb: stocks between $2 and $20 price range are preferred for tight spreads.
- [00:13:14] Float of 20 million shares is the threshold; larger floats reduce movement potential.
- [00:17:01] Benchmark threshold: 10 million shares of daily volume is preferred for position sizing.
- [00:19:05] Chinese stock HKD made 2,000-3,000% moves but on extremely light volume, making it untradeable for size.

## Claims needing verification

- [00:03:03] Claims a big spread signals stock has potential to move very quickly.
- [00:08:35] Most beginner traders will not find a single clean setup on a high-spread stock like OMH.
- [00:18:32] Lighter-volume stocks below 10 million shares are less scalable for meaningful profit.

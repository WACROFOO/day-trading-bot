# Candlestick Charts DIDN'T WORK Until I Learned This Easy ONE (1) STEP TRICK

## Summary

The breakthrough for reading candlestick charts effectively is not better pattern recognition but rather stock selection: trading the right types of stocks makes technical analysis work. The video teaches five stock selection criteria that historically produced 89% of the speaker's $1.2 million in profits over ten months.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:13:27] Stock price between $2 and $20; sweet spot for highest profitability.
- [00:14:31] Minimum 5x relative volume (10% or higher preferred).
- [00:15:39] News catalyst required; stock must have a reason to make a big move.
- [00:16:28] Float under 20 million shares; lower float provides better volatility potential.
- [00:14:59] Gap up at least 2% from previous close (10% or higher preferred).
- [00:11:04] Risk management baseline: never risk more than you stand to gain; minimum 1:1 profit-to-loss ratio.
- [00:12:00] Require 50% accuracy minimum at 1:1 ratio to break even.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — sharp move up, sideways consolidation, break of consolidation high.
- **Flat bottom breakdown** — support level holds then breaks down.
- **Flat top breakout** — resistance level holds then breaks through.
- **Ascending wedge pullback** — support trend line provides entry point.
- **Psychological resistance** — whole dollar and half-dollar price levels create natural resistance.

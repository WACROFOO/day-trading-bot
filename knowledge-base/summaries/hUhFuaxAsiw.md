# How to Draw Trend Lines the RIGHT Way on CANDLESTICK Charts

## Summary

Ross Cameron teaches how to correctly draw ascending and descending support and resistance trend lines on candlestick charts, emphasizing that trend line validity depends on multiple tests and being respected by many traders. The video explains supply/demand mechanics behind why trend lines work, the psychological significance of half-dollar and whole-dollar price levels, and how false trend lines lead to false breakouts and losses.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:44] Trend line connecting highs must be adjusted until price action respects it at multiple points, not just touching candle wicks.
- [00:05:57] When price breaks above a resistance trend line and holds that level on a retest, that resistance level becomes new support.
- [00:06:15] Price typically breaks through levels quickly once one level is broken, moving to next psychological resistance level.
- [00:10:20] Horizontal trend lines at half dollars and whole dollars represent psychological resistance from traders placing profit targets.
- [00:14:24] First pullback and second pullback are typical trading opportunities; third and fourth pullbacks become more risky as pattern extends.
- [00:26:19] Do not connect resistance and support points in the same trend line; connect resistance to resistance, support to support.
- [00:27:52] Do not draw extremely steep trend lines as they rarely hold for long.
- [00:29:07] When price breaks support/resistance and retests it, that area often becomes a good entry with limited downside risk.

## Setups and patterns

- **Cup and handle** — rounded cup formation followed by handle pullback, then breakout higher. [00:16:52]
- **Double top** — price makes two similar highs at same level, indicating resistance. [00:16:51]
- **Ascending support trend line** — support levels rising diagonally over time. [00:01:24]
- **Descending resistance trend line** — resistance levels declining diagonally over time. [00:01:24]
- **Stair-stepping pattern** — price moves higher in steps with pullbacks at half dollars and whole dollars. [00:08:09]

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — momentum up, consolidation, break higher. [00:14:41]
- **Micro pullback** — small dip on low volume within uptrend. [00:26:00]
- **Wave pattern** — oscillating price movement with pullbacks. [00:32:37]
- **Candlestick over candle** — each candle breaks high of previous. [00:34:29]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **9 EMA** — fastest exponential moving average.
- **20 EMA** — mid-speed moving average.
- **200 EMA** — slowest; major trend indicator.
- **VWAP** (volume-weighted average price) — bullish above, bearish below. [00:08:17]
- **Volume bars** — colored by close; high volume on up moves is bullish, high volume on down moves is bearish.
- **MACD** (moving average convergence divergence) — positive means open momentum; negative means closed/consolidating momentum. [00:15:35]

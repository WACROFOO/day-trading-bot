# What is Day Trading and How to Start Day Trading as a Beginner

## Summary

Day trading is buying and selling stocks intraday to capture short-term volatility, targeting stocks that move 10-50% in a single day. Key criteria include price between $2-$20, catalyst news, relative volume of 5x or higher, and high-volatility small-cap stocks. Beginners should practice in simulators first before trading real money to learn proper timing and discipline.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:03:25] Day trades are executed intraday: buy stock, sell same-day for profit (e.g., buy at $4, sell at $4.40).
- [00:04:07] Use multiple position size tiers: begin with 100 shares, scale to 200, 300, 400, 800, 1,000 as account grows.
- [00:06:38] Target stocks meeting five criteria: price $2-$20, up at least 10%, relative volume 5x or higher, news/catalyst, float under 20 million.
- [00:08:27] Pre-market screening: run gap scanners at 9:20 a.m.; filter for stocks up more than 5%.
- [00:13:09] Practice in a simulator first with real-time data before trading live capital.
- [00:10:46] Buy pullbacks on momentum waves; wait for micro-pullback before entering, then ride the next leg higher.
- [00:10:52] Never buy the absolute low; buy "high and sell higher" because the wave is already moving.

## Setups and patterns

- **Micro-pullback entry** — price squeezes up, pulls back slightly, then rides the next leg higher; entry is on the pullback. [00:10:45]
- **Wave momentum** — price rises, consolidates, then continues up; traders jump on wave before it crashes back down. [00:06:39]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Gap scanner — searches market for stocks up more than 5% pre-market; narrows thousands to ~25-30 candidates by 9:20 a.m.
- TD Ameritrade simulator — free simulator for accounts as small as $500 (limited vs. premium).
- Warrior Trading simulator — real-time market data; costs money but more accurate.
- Stock screeners / stock scanning software — real-time radar for volatility; audio alerts when stocks meet criteria.

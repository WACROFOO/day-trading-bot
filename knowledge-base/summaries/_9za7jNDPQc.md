# Don't Use Candlestick Charts Without THIS

## Summary

Technical analysis masterclass demonstrating the critical relationship between candlestick chart patterns and Level 2 market data. Teaches that candlestick charts are historical records while Level 2 reveals the future through order flow. Trader shows how large buy/sell orders on the book create support/resistance that either validates or invalidates chart patterns, and demonstrates live trade execution using both tools in concert.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:10:37] Bull flag entry: buy when stock makes new high after a pullback following an initial run-up.
- [00:11:18] Candle over candle formation: enter when next candle breaks the high of the previous candle after a pullback.
- [00:12:34] Entry point: buy at the moment the candle breaks the high of the preceding pullback candle.
- [00:14:02] Before taking a trade with a good candlestick pattern, verify Level 2 does not show large sell orders blocking price movement.
- [00:14:20] Large sell order on Level 2 (e.g., 200,000 shares) = resist taking the trade despite good chart setup.
- [00:14:30] Large buy order on Level 2 (e.g., 200,000 shares) = confirmation to size up and be more aggressive.
- [00:15:35] Never buy into resistance created by large sell orders on the ask without waiting for that order to be absorbed or canceled.
- [00:19:50] After entry, expect to see large green candles, high volume buying, and price moving higher.
- [00:20:07] If seeing large red candles, price declining, and large sellers appearing after entry, exit the trade.
- [00:24:54] Smart routing: broker algorithm selects best execution route automatically; not necessary to manually choose exchanges.
- [00:25:37] Sophisticated traders can manually select specific routes (NASDAQ, ARCA, BATS) for execution.
- [00:32:54] Focus eye on the ask (offer) side of Level 2 when waiting for price to move higher.
- [00:39:32] Watch for "bursts of green" in time and sales (transactions executed at the offer) = buy signal.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag** — initial squeeze up, pullback on lower volume, first candle making new high = entry; classic momentum pattern. [00:10:35]
- **Candle over candle** — next candle breaks high of previous pullback candle; signals shift from distribution to accumulation. [00:11:15]
- **Topping tail / large red candle** — forms when large sell order on Level 2 blocks price advance; traders bail, short sellers enter. [00:20:30]

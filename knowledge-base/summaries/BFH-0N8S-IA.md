# Master Supply & Demand Trading (Ultimate Beginners Guide)

## Summary

Visual guide to identifying supply and demand zones on candlestick charts as areas of support and resistance. Demonstrates how positioning entries near support levels creates tiny maximum losses and favorable risk-reward ratios (2:1, 5:1, or higher) even with 50% accuracy.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:02:15] Use 9, 20, and 200-period exponential moving averages (EMAs) as reference levels.
- [00:05:32] 200 moving average on daily chart often acts as strong resistance/support.
- [00:06:30] Get entry confirmation on daily chart before zooming into 5-min and 1-min charts.
- [00:08:04] Enter on micro pullbacks during breakouts (1-minute chart entries after 5-minute chart setup).
- [00:15:41] Resistance often forms at psychological whole dollar and half-dollar levels.
- [00:28:53] Buy on support at micro pullbacks following breakouts above resistance.
- [00:14:45] Entry close to support minimizes stop loss, maximizing risk-reward ratio.

## Setups and patterns

- **Double bottom** — two lows at similar level creating demand zone support [00:11:21].
- **Double top** — two highs at similar level creating supply zone resistance [00:12:19].
- **Bull flag** — rapid move up with consolidation, then breakout.
- **Cup and handle** — rounded bottom consolidation with handle pullback.
- **Breakout with follow-through** — resistance break that holds and continues higher.
- **Bottoming tail** — long lower wick indicating demand came in at that level.
- **Topping tail/shooting star** — long upper wick indicating sellers rejected that price.

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- 9/20/200 EMA (exponential moving averages).
- VWAP (volume weighted average price) — orange line showing equilibrium.
- MACD (moving average convergence divergence) — momentum crossover.
- Volume bars — shows volume at each candle.
- Volume profile indicator — shows which price levels had most volume traded.
- Multiple timeframe charts: daily, 5-minute, 1-minute, 10-second.

# High Accuracy 1 Minute Scalping Strategy (Full Training)

## Summary

In-depth scalping training for capturing short-duration volatility using 1-minute and 5-minute charts. Teaches how to participate in parabolic moves by taking small base hits repeatedly rather than home runs; includes real trading examples (ZPP up 100%, generating $10,000+ profit in 2 hours). Emphasizes strict risk management with tight stops, high accuracy trading (68.5%), and knowing when to walk away. Core philosophy: trade front side of moves aggressively, exit when trend breaks.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:12:09] Scalp trading requires at least 5 times relative volume (volume 5x higher than 14-day average).
- [00:13:24] Only trade stocks up at least 10% on the day (indicates high demand).
- [00:14:34] Preferred price range for scalping: $1 to $20 per stock (under $1 has low risk but high spreads; over $20 has too much risk per tick).
- [00:16:20] Prefer stocks with float under 10 million shares (where most profitable trades occur).
- [00:24:55] Do not trade if stock cannot potentially move up at least 30 cents per share (minimum for capturing half the move after accounting for slippage and spread).
- [00:30:13] Trade only on front side of move (above 20 EMA and MACD positive); exit immediately when moving average crosses negative or MACD flips.
- [00:33:38] First 1-minute candle to make a new high after a pullback = scalp entry pattern.
- [00:34:10] Use arbitrary 10-15 cent stop loss (not at low of pullback, which is too far).
- [00:33:05] Set profit targets: 15-20 cents for first target = 2-to-1 ratio; look for bigger moves ($1-$5) for most profit.
- [00:50:30] Exit indicators: (1) green candles getting smaller = trend exhaustion; (2) topping tail or doji = reversal imminent; (3) big sellers on level 2 = exit signal.
- [00:50:32] "Breakout or bailout" rule: if price doesn't move up quickly after entry, exit the trade.
- [00:50:39] Max loss rule: if max loss stop is hit, exit immediately.
- [00:50:52] Use hotkey Shift+1 to buy shares at ask+15c offset; Shift+2 for larger position; Control+Q to cancel all orders.
- [00:50:52] Sell using Control+Z (full position at bid-15c) or Control+X (half position at bid).

## Setups and patterns

- **First pullback** — initial move up, pullback, then first candle making new high (easiest scalp pattern). [00:33:38]
- **Micro pullback** — sharp pop, tiny dip, immediate push higher; catches continuation moves. [00:36:42]
- **Half dollar / Whole dollar resistance** — stocks respect $1, $1.50, $2.00 price levels; big sellers cluster there. [00:38:28]
- **Ascending support line** — connecting higher lows; when held, shows accumulation; when broken, shows weakness. [00:43:50]
- **Wedge breakout** — descending resistance line + ascending support line forming apex; breakout from wedge is entry. [00:52:05]
- **Psychological support/resistance** — at $6, $7, $8 whole dollars; can see order stacks on level 2. [00:38:48]

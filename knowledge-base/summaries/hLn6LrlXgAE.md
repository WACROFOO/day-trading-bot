# The Only Candlestick Pattern You'll Ever Need (full training)

## Summary

Teaches the first pullback setup after a catalyst-driven squeeze, which the instructor uses as primary strategy for small account challenges. Entry is when first candle makes new high after pullback holds 50% of initial move. Stop is low of pullback; profit target is retest of high using 2:1 risk/reward ratio. Works universally across stocks, forex, futures, and crypto. Aimed at traders who need consistent, repeatable setups with high accuracy.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:15:02] Entry: First candle to make a new high after at least one red candle (pullback); two to three red candles acceptable.
- [00:13:46] Pullback requirement: Stock must hold at least 50% of the initial move up; deeper retrace means weaker setup.
- [00:19:54] Stop loss: Always the low of the pullback candle(s); do not use arbitrary stops tighter than this level.
- [00:20:23] Profit target: Retest of the high of day using 2:1 profit-to-loss ratio (if risk is 20 cents, target is 40 cents up).
- [00:23:03] Exit on weakness: When setup works, it works instantly; if it falters on entry, exit immediately without waiting for stop to be hit.
- [00:28:29] Prefer first pullback over second or third; first has highest probability and strongest momentum continuation.

## Setups and patterns

- **First pullback after catalyst-driven squeeze** — stock pops on breaking news, pulls back holding 50%+ of move, then first candle makes new high is entry point. [00:03:41]
- **Topping tail** — bearish candlestick shape (opened, closed high, has large tail down) that signals reversal; often appears at trend tops. [00:16:52]
- **Bottoming tail** — bullish candlestick shape with tail down; indicates support rejection and potential reversal up. [00:17:29]
- **Bull flag pattern** — large move up, consolidation, then break of consolidation high; mentioned as related to pullback setup. [00:26:45]

# How to Day Trade BREAKING NEWS with a Scalping Strategy (Step-by-Step Guide)

## Summary

A full-length class on scalp trading with a focus on fast-moving breaking-news stocks using 10-second charts and level-2 market data. The instructor trades GNLN (a 300% mover) as a live case study, demonstrating five mechanical steps: stock selection, news catalyst verification, technical analysis, order execution, and trade analytics review.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:05:01] Use larger positions (2,000 to 5,000 shares typically) to scale profits from small price movements (10-15 cents per share).
- [00:07:02] Enforce strict stop-loss orders; scalp traders must manage losses carefully because they use large position sizes.
- [00:04:23] Entry is triggered on the first candle breaking a support level after a pullback (ABCD pattern example at 22 cents).
- [00:40:03] Add to positions into breaks of whole-dollar levels; move stop-loss up as position accumulates.
- [00:51:06] Stop trading a stock once the MACD crosses negative; the trend has ended.
- [00:47:04] Add on breakouts every 10 cents per share as stock pushes higher; adjust stop each time.

## Setups and patterns

- **Micro-pullback entry** — Pop up, pull back, break above the pullback low. Used repeatedly on 10-second chart. [00:22:05]
- **ABCD pattern** — Price pops, pulls back, comes back up, breaks flat-top or higher-high. [00:22:05]
- **First pullback continuation** — Buy dip during strong trend; most reliable window. [00:20:03]
- **Whole-dollar break** — Watch for holds and breaks of psychological levels (e.g., $6 hold, break to $7). [00:39:57]

- **Time and Sales tape** — Displays transaction flow; used with level 2 to anticipate moves.

# The Simplest Day Trading Strategy for Beginners

## Summary

Gap-and-go strategy focusing on the leading percentage gainer each day. Traders buy the first pullback to the 9-period moving average after a spike move and scale out on further extensions. Emphasizes trading high-volatility stocks with low floats and catalyst events over stable large-caps.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:14:21] Entry is first candle to make new high after pullback to 9-period EMA on one-minute chart.
- [00:14:46] Stop is at the low of the pullback candle.
- [00:14:55] Scale out partial position into moves higher, capture gains without giving back too much.
- [00:16:48] Limit to one or two trades per day as beginner; avoid over-trading.
- [00:08:05] Trade only leading gappers/gainers each day; ignore choppy, narrow-range stocks.

## Setups and patterns

- **Bull flag on micro pullback** — Sharp move up followed by 2-3 candle pullback, then first candle breaks prior high. [00:14:39]
- **Gap and go** — Stock gaps up at open on news, pulls back to 9-EMA, then squeezes higher. [00:04:53]
- **Five-minute to one-minute alignment** — Pullback on five-minute chart matches first entry opportunity on one-minute chart. [00:15:14]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- Scanner alerts for stocks moving into top gainer list. [00:07:13]
- 9-period exponential moving average (EMA) as pullback support. [00:14:50]
- Volume-weighted average price (VWAP) as secondary support level. [00:14:50]

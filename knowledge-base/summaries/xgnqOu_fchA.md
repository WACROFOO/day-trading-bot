# High Probability Candlestick Chart Patterns

## Summary

Comprehensive breakdown of the ABCD pattern—a failed bull flag followed by a second bullish resolution—used for momentum trading in stocks with news catalysts and low float. Pattern is most powerful on 5-minute charts when combined with multi-timeframe alignment on 1-minute charts. Teaches stock selection criteria (sub-$20 price, up 10%, 5x+ relative volume, sub-20M float) and risk management (2:1 profit-loss targets, 33-68% accuracy thresholds, stop-loss placement at support levels).

## Setups and patterns

- **ABCD pattern** — initial move up, pullback resembling bull flag, move back up failing to break high-of-day, then pullback, then final breakout through high. [00:02:40]
- **Bull flag** — sharp move up (flag pole) followed by 1-3 candle pullback with higher volume on move up, lower volume on pullback, pullback retracing less than 50% of move. [00:03:17]
- **Double bottom** — price pulls back to same level as previous low, showing support strength. [00:08:29]
- **Doji/spinning top** — candle with small body and long wicks indicating indecision; often precedes reversal. [00:36:03]
- **Topping tail** — candle with long upper wick and smaller close, showing rejection at highs. [00:17:01]
- **Multi-timeframe alignment** — 5-minute bull flag coinciding with 1-minute ABCD pattern; both timeframes confirm pattern. [00:14:32]
- **Apex/pivot level** — point where ascending support line forces stock through resistance via repeated tests and buyer accumulation. [00:18:52]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **9 EMA (Exponential Moving Average)** — moving average where pullbacks often find support in proper bull flags. [00:06:01]
- **20 EMA** — used to identify support levels during uptrends.
- **200 EMA (daily chart)** — key level separating bullish (above) from bearish (below) long-term trends. [00:27:24]
- **High-of-day momentum scanner** — real-time scanner alerting when stocks make new highs within the trading day. [00:04:16]
- **Level 2 (market depth/order book)** — shows buy/sell walls and hidden orders (icebergs); used to anticipate breakout strength. [00:34:05]
- **Trading halts** — volatility pauses triggered by 10%+ moves in 5-minute periods; lasts 5 minutes, freezes all trading. [00:41:46]

# When to BUY & SELL using Candlestick Charts (with ZERO experience)

## Summary

Universal candlestick chart buy and sell signals applicable across stocks, forex, crypto, and futures. Teaches identifying support/resistance levels, recognizing when market sentiment shifts from selling to buying, reading order flow (tape) to confirm entries, and managing risk with 2:1 profit-to-loss ratios. Core concept: buy on pullbacks near support, exit into strength near resistance.

## Mechanical rules

- [05:45] Buy on pullbacks (dips), not at highs; pullbacks establish where to enter with managed risk.
- [07:00] 200 exponential moving average (200 EMA): Major daily chart support/resistance level; check position before entering any trade.
- [07:15] Above 200 EMA = support; below 200 EMA = resistance. Price respects this level consistently.
- [08:46] Half-dollar and whole-dollar price levels: Most financial instruments show resistance at $X.50 and $X.00 price points.
- [09:10] Intraday resistance progression example: $6.50 → $7.00 → $7.50 → $8.00 → $8.50 → $9.00 (step-by-step resistance).
- [10:02] Support definition: Low of last pullback; use this level as stop-loss placement.
- [10:04] 50% retracement rule: If stock retraces more than 50% of initial move, pullback is too deep; prefer 25-75% retracement depth.
- [10:13] Entry timing: Wait for green orders on tape (buy signal) to confirm sentiment shift before entering on pullback.
- [13:32] Double top pattern: Two attempts to break resistance at same level; typically rejects on second/third attempt; avoid buying here.
- [14:34] Support/resistance reversal: Prior resistance becomes support when price moves above it and pulls back.
- [15:55] Sentiment shift indicator: Watch tape for change from red orders (selling) to green orders (buying); this is buyable moment.
- [17:23] Risk-to-reward ratio: Risk $100 to make $200 minimum (2:1); only need 33% accuracy to break even.
- [17:28] Realistic win rate: Aim for 50%+ accuracy; 33% with 2:1 ratio just breaks even psychologically.
- [21:08] Starter position: Take small initial position at support; don't wait for top of pullback.
- [21:40] Stop placement: Set stop just below support level (e.g., if bought at $6.00, stop at $5.80 = 20 cent stop).
- [22:01] Position management: If trade moves higher, consider adding to position; trail stop up as price rises.
- [22:35] Profit-taking rule: Take partial profits as price approaches resistance levels; don't be greedy.
- [23:11] Holding winners: Stay in position if trend remains strong (green tape); don't sell too early out of fear.
- [25:49] Pullback depth rule: 50% max retracement of move is limit; prefer smaller pullbacks (25% retracement = 75% of move retained).
- [27:55] First and second pullback trading: Primary focus on first pullback for entry; second pullback also valid.
- [31:28] Time-of-day strategy: Trade between 7:00 a.m. - 11:00 a.m. Eastern (based on data analysis of win rates).
- [32:02] Stock price zone: Trade stocks between $1-$20 price range (optimal for this strategy).
- [32:08] Entry pattern criteria: Buy first candle making new high after pullback from resistance level.

## Setups and patterns

- **Ascending support trend line** — Connect lows of pullbacks; line showing rising support level where to buy dips. [21:07]
- **Cup and handle pattern** — Price up sharply (cup), pulls back, comes back up (handle); buy handle break for next leg. [42:37]
- **Double top** — Price hits resistance twice at same level; typically rejects on second/third attempt; avoid buying here. [13:32]
- **Micro pullback dip-and-rip** — Stock pushes up, tiny pullback (10-30 seconds), then resumes higher; buy the dip fast. [39:40]
- **Half-dollar and whole-dollar resistance** — Psychological resistance at $6.50, $7.00, $7.50 prices where selling pressure clusters. [08:46]
- **Sentiment shift on tape** — Orders shift from red (selling) to green (buying); this crossover = buyable moment. [15:50]
- **Bull flag with confirmation** — Sharp initial move up, pullback flag formation, break of flag high on volume. [00:25]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **200 exponential moving average (200 EMA)** — Daily chart support/resistance level; price above = support, below = resistance. [07:00]
- **Level 2 market data** — Shows bid/ask depth and stacked buyer/seller orders; identifies where buyers/sellers congregate. [24:07]
- **Time and sales (tape)** — Real-time order flow; green = buying pressure at ask, red = selling pressure at bid, white = mid-spread. [10:50]
- **9-moving average** — Intraday support on lower timeframes (1-min, 5-min charts); used for support confirmation. [26:45]

# How to Trade Penny Stocks - DAY 6 | Small Account Challenge

## Summary

Beginner's step-by-step guide to trading penny stocks and high-volatility low-priced stocks with focus on risk management and account growth on a small ($1,000–$25,000) cash-only account. Covers the difference between penny stocks under $1 and stocks $1–$10, explains reverse splits, and walks through stock selection criteria, technical analysis, and order execution using KAVL and TGL as live case studies in Day 6 of a challenge.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:06:02] Penny stocks (by US regulator definition) are stocks trading below $3 per share, though literal pennies are below $1.
- [00:14:32] Buy stocks near all-time lows that have just completed a recent reverse split; this sets up explosive moves.
- [00:31:05] Stock selection rule: % up minimum 10%, price $0.75–$10, relative volume 5x+, news catalyst, float under 20M shares (ideally under 10M).
- [00:12:33] Wait for stocks below $1 to break above $1 per share before aggressively trading; decimal places thin out and trading accelerates at $1+.
- [00:51:08] Buy dips on first pullback after breakout; avoid trading the back-side (after MACD closes).
- [00:47:04] Use mental stops in pre-market (before live halts in regular hours). [00:51:36] Stop at low of pullback candle.
- [00:53:03] Trade one entry, one exit per day on small cash account; discipline prevents spiral losses.

## Setups and patterns

- **Reverse split bounce** — Stock hits all-time lows, does reverse split (12-to-1, 21-to-1, 70-to-1), price pops on news. [00:23:30]
- **Micro-pullback entry** — Sharp pop up, pullback to 80 cents, then bounces higher. Repeat pattern up. [00:46:10]
- **Double-top breakout** — Two failed attempts at resistance; break above = entry. [00:52:50]
- **All-time low reversal** — Severe beaten-down stock bounces from historic low; high-probability setup. [00:20:16]

## Indicators, tools, platforms

- **MACD indicator** — Used to mark when a move is clean (open) vs. choppy (closed/crossing). [00:54:44]
- **200 moving average (daily)** — Used to check overhead resistance. [00:39:04]
- **Level 2 market depth** — Shows bid/ask stack; critical for seeing support/resistance zones. [00:08:00]
- **Scanners** — Software that identifies high-percentage gainers in real time. [00:33:15]
- **Day Trade Dash** — Author's software platform with scanners, charts, news feed. [00:31:48]
- **ThinkorSwim** — Primary broker platform used in cash-account small-account challenge. [00:47:10]
- **TraderView** — Analytics software for importing and analyzing trades; exports from ThinkorSwim via CSV. [00:55:57]
- **Hotkeys** — Required for rapid order execution (Shift+1 buy, Ctrl+Z sell, etc.). [00:48:15]

# Ultimate Guide to Trading in a Small Account

## Summary

Complete beginner framework for trading in small accounts, covering alpha phase (simulator practice), risk management principles, stock selection criteria, candlestick chart reading, and entry patterns. Emphasizes simulator practice before real money, focusing on stocks with high relative volume and breaking news catalysts. The video breaks down position sizing and profit-to-loss ratios as keys to sustainable trading.

## Setups and patterns

- **Squeeze and pullback** — stock runs up rapidly then pulls back; enter when reversal candle forms (doji, hammer, or small body candle) followed by green candle breaking out. [00:39:10]
- **Long-body candle** — very tall green candle signals strong bullish sentiment and momentum building. [00:33:45]
- **Short-body candle** — small candle signals weaker sentiment and cooling momentum. [00:34:00]
- **Doji candlestick** — opens and closes at nearly same price; indicates indecision and potential trend change. [00:35:48]
- **Gravestone doji** — price pushed higher then dropped back down; bearish signal. [00:36:07]
- **Dragonfly doji** — price sold off then rallied back up and closed at top; bullish signal. [00:36:20]
- **Hammer candle** — lower wick at bottom of sell-off with small body; bullish reversal at support. [00:37:11]
- **Inverted hammer** — after squeeze up; bearish reversal signal. [00:37:33]
- **Shooting star** — topping tail candle after rally; bearish reversal indicator. [00:37:44]
- **Tweezer top** — two topping tails back-to-back; resistance, price cannot go higher. [00:38:01]
- **Tweezer bottom** — two bottoming tails back-to-back; support, potential reversal up. [00:38:09]

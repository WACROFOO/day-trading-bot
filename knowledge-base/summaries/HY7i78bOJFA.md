# How to Make Money Trading in a COLD Market

## Summary

Explains hot vs. cold market cycles. In hot markets, stocks make big moves and traders have FOMO; in cold markets, weak gainers fail and short sellers get aggressive. Teaches to avoid shorting due to unlimited risk (gap-up halts), focus on big floats and risky halts during cold periods, and practice patience waiting for hot cycles to return.

## Mechanical rules

- [00:06:25] In cold market, don't short because unlimited risk is not worth it; one gap-up halt can wipe out months of profits.
- [00:07:20] Avoid trading stocks with "halt pending news" risk (e.g., bankruptcy, merger news) due to halt danger.
- [00:08:01] Don't trade penny stocks under $1; commissions are per-share so cost is higher.
- [00:10:45] Only trade stocks in the $2 to $10 price range; sweet spot for leverage is $5 to $10.
- [00:11:22] Be selective in cold markets; focus on quality setups; in hot markets, can be less selective.
- [00:13:35] Show up ready to trade every day because 80% of annual profits come from 20% of trading days.
- [00:14:53] Walk away before close call; quit before giving back half of profits when halts/gaps present.
- [00:16:52] Maximum 10 cent/15 cent position loss to prevent large blowups from small early trades.

## Setups and patterns

- **Hot cycle** — Period where stocks move up a lot back-to-back-to-back with enthusiasm and FOMO. [00:06:05]
- **Cold cycle** — Period where stocks don't make big moves; traders lack FOMO; shorts dominate. [00:06:25]

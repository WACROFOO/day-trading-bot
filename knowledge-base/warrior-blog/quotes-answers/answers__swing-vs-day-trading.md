# Swing Trading vs. Day Trading Differences

## TL;DR

- 

Day traders open and close all positions within the same session.

- 

Swing traders hold positions for days or weeks.

- 

Day trading requires more screen time; swing trading requires more patience.

[ Day trading ](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/) means opening and closing all positions within the same session. Swing trading means holding for days or weeks to capture larger moves.

Day traders rely on intraday momentum and tight [ risk management ](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading-risk-management/) , whereas swing traders focus on technical setups and overnight catalysts. Day trading demands more screen time, and swing trading demands patience and tolerance for overnight risk.

The right choice depends on your schedule and risk tolerance. For more information about day trading, including resources, chat rooms, and [ day trading simulators ](https://www.warriortrading.com/paper-trading/) , check out [ Warrior Trading ](https://www.warriortrading.com/) .

### Key Takeaways

- 

Day traders close all positions before the market closes

- 

Swing traders hold overnight and sometimes for weeks

- 

Day trading requires constant attention during market hours

- 

Swing trading carries overnight and weekend gap risk

# How To Build a Day Trading Watchlist

## TL;DR

- 

Start with stocks that have a catalyst and high relative volume.

- 

Narrow your list to 3 to 5 names before the open.

- 

Premarket movers with small floats are the best candidates.

A strong [ day trading watchlist ](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading-watch-list-top-stocks-to-watch/) starts with good information and data. I [ scan for stocks ](https://www.warriortrading.com/scanners/) with a catalyst, like news or earnings, and [ high relative volume ](https://www.warriortrading.com/relative-volume-day-trading-terminology/) . I want names already moving in premarket with a clean chart and a [ small float ](https://www.investopedia.com/ask/answers/what-is-companys-float/) . I narrow my list to three to five stocks before the open.

The goal is focus. Too many stocks can lead to missed entries. A short list beats a long one every time.

To learn more about day trading, check out [ Warrior Trading ](https://www.warriortrading.com/) for resources, courses, and a community in the day trading space.

### Key Takeaways

- 

Look for a catalyst first, then confirm volume

- 

Premarket price action is a strong signal

- 

Keep your list to 3 to 5 stocks

- 

A small float increases the potential for big moves

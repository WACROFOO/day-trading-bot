# Is Day Trading Profitable?

## TL;DR

- 

Day trading can be very profitable, but the earnings aren’t easy to come by or guaranteed.

- 

Day trading profitability mostly depends on your strategy, risk management, discipline, and experience.

- 

If you’re a beginner, practice in a day trading simulator before risking real money.

Day trading can be very profitable, but only for traders who develop their skills and [ manage investment risk ](https://www.finra.org/investors/investing/investing-basics/risk) well. For beginners, profitability can be harder to lock in, especially because day trading moves so quickly and the skills and knowledge required to become profitable take time to develop.

You’ll have to get used to stock prices spiking, reversing, or halting within minutes. Without a clear entry, [ stop loss ](https://www.warriortrading.com/what-is-stop-loss/) , profit target, and max loss, one bad trade can wipe out your profits from a few good ones.

This is why the best way to [ make day trading profitable ](https://www.investopedia.com/articles/trading/06/daytradingretail.asp) is to start with a [ paper trading simulator ](https://www.warriortrading.com/paper-trading/) . This training tool lets you practice identifying momentum, [ reading charts ](https://www.warriortrading.com/how-to-read-stock-charts-for-beginners/) , managing share size, and, most of all, controlling your emotions before you put real money on the line.

If you can’t stick to your rules in a simulator, trading on the real market will most likely make the pressure even harder to handle.

### Key Takeaways 

- 

Day trading can be profitable, but you’ll need discipline and practice first.

- 

Risk management is everything; focus on perfecting your processes and rules first, then aim for profits.

- 

A trading simulator is the best way to set yourself up for profitable trading.

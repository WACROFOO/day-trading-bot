# Where Do I Start Learning Day Trading?

## TL;DR

- Start with day trading basics, risk management, and technical analysis.

- Practice in a simulator before risking real money.

- Warrior Trading offers courses, chat rooms, and tools for beginners.

Before you make live trades, you must establish a base in [technical analysis](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/t/technicalanalysis.asp), risk management, and a strategy suited to beginning investors before starting to day trade. The majority of new traders learn more quickly if they learn a structured process, simulate, and correct errors rather than following alerts.

The most realistic entry point is education and paper trading, along with community support. [Warrior Trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/) backs this method up with introductory courses and a paper-trading simulator, which may help you with execution and discipline before trading.

### Key Takeaways

- Before placing live trades, beginners should build a foundation in market rules, risk management, and a defined trading strategy.

- Learning a structured process and practicing through simulation is often more effective than simply following trade alerts.

- Warrior Trading supports this approach with introductory courses and a paper trading simulator to help traders develop execution and discipline before trading real capital.

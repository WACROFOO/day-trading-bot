# How Much Money Do You Need To Start Day Trading?

## TL;DR

- 

The traditional answer has been $25,000, based on FINRA’s pattern day trader rule, but that has changed in 2026.

- 

Now, $2,000 is required for a margin account, which is what day traders prefer. Cash accounts can often be opened for much less.

- 

Starting at the bare minimum isn’t recommended. Traders need to account for expected losses as they learn, along with fees or subscriptions for tools needed to day trade effectively.

The amount of money you need to start day trading has long been tied to one specific regulation, but 2026 brought meaningful change with the end of the Pattern Day Trader Rule (PDT).

Historically, [ FINRA’s pattern day trader rule ](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/pattern-day-trader) required anyone making four or more day trades within five business days to maintain a minimum of $25,000 in a margin account, making that figure the de facto entry point for anyone serious about [ day trading ](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/) . While workarounds existed, they came with trade-offs.

The end of the PDT rule now means brokerages can offer margin accounts for a minimum opening balance of $2,000. It’s a significant change, but it doesn’t mean $2,000 is a smart starting point.

A smaller account leaves little room for normal trading losses, and the pressure of a thin cushion tends to drive exactly the kind of emotional decision-making that hurts new traders most.

A more practical starting range accounts for expected losses and considers subscriptions and fees for the tools and services traders need. The goal is to start at a level that matches both your financial situation and where you actually are as a trader, not just the minimum your brokerage offers.

## Key Takeaways 

- 

FINRA’s pattern day trader rule has historically required $25,000 in a margin account.

- 

A 2026 update has lowered the margin account requirement to just $2,000, but starting at the minimum is not advisable for most new traders.

- 

Traders should decide on a budget range that provides enough cushion to trade through losses and build skills without unnecessary financial pressure.

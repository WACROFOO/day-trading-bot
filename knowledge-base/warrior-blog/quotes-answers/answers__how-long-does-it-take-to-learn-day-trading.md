# How Long Does It Take To Learn Day Trading?

## TL;DR

- 

Most traders need 1 to 3 years before day trading profitably.

- 

The first 6 months of day trading should be spent in a simulator.

- 

Psychological discipline takes longer than technical knowledge when it comes to day trading.

Most traders need one to three years before day trading profitably. The first six months are typically spent in a [ day trading simulator ](https://www.warriortrading.com/paper-trading/) . Real proficiency takes one to two years of screen time, structured study, and emotional discipline.

Learning often happens in two phases. The first is technical: [ chart patterns ](https://www.warriortrading.com/best-day-trading-patterns/) , order types, and [ risk management ](https://www.investopedia.com/articles/trading/09/risk-management.asp) , and most beginners cover this in three to six months. The second phase is psychological. Managing fear and greed under pressure is where most get stuck.

To [ learn how to day trade ](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/) , it can help to check out [ Warrior Trading ](https://www.warriortrading.com/) for resources, community, and courses to start your trading journey.

### Key Takeaways

- 

Technical skills take 3 to 6 months

- 

Emotional discipline takes 1 to 2 years

- 

A simulator lets you learn and practice without losing real money

- 

Skipping structure can make it take longer and result in unnecessary losses

# How Do You Start Day Trading?

## TL;DR

- 

Day trading rewards preparation over impulse. The foundational steps are education, simulation, and then funding an account.

- 

A structured course, a real-time simulator, and the right starting capital are the three essentials before placing your first real trade.

- 

While day traders can open a margin account starting at $2,000, thanks to FINRA removing the PDT rule, or a cash account often with very low minimums, there are other costs associated with day trading, such as a computer or software, meaning that aspiring traders should budget beyond just what they plan to fund an account with.

[](https://media.warriortrading.com/2026/08/03121530/0fb4fac3-3284-449b-ae50-f4d81542475d.png)

The first step in day trading is structured education. Before you can trade effectively, you need to understand how the market works: chart patterns, entries, exits, risk management, and the mechanics of a brokerage account. A [ day trading course ](https://www.warriortrading.com/warrior-starter-info/) covers these essentials in one place, guided by someone with real experience, rather than leaving you to piece it together from scattered free resources.

From there, a [ real-time trading simulator ](https://www.warriortrading.com/paper-trading/) is the most important tool a new trader can use. Practicing with actual market data builds the pattern recognition and decision-making skills that carry over to live trading.

Once you’ve built a foundation, you can open a brokerage account and fund it appropriately. The new [ FINRA day trading requirements ](https://www.finra.org/investors/investing/investment-products/stocks/day-trading) mean that margin accounts can be funded with as little as $2,000.

With that said, budgeting for the tools and software you’ll need to trade effectively, along with allotting for losses that come with developing your skills, gives traders a more reasonable roadmap to think about their overall budget while reducing unnecessary pressure from not having the right tools or the ability to absorb a loss.

## Key Takeaways 

- 

A structured day trading course is the most efficient way to build the foundational knowledge needed before placing a real trade.

- 

A real-time simulator using live market data is essential for developing skills without financial risk and should be treated as seriously as live trading.

- 

While the rule changes lower the legal minimum to $2,000 for margin accounts, starting with a larger budget provides a more practical cushion for new traders.

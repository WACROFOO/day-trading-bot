# Best Premarket Routine for Day Traders

## TL;DR

- 

Start at least one hour before the open

- 

Check futures, scan for catalysts, and build your watchlist

- 

Know your entry criteria and max loss before the you begin

My premarket routine starts with a review of the markets. I check the futures, [ scan for stocks ](https://www.warriortrading.com/scanners/) with overnight news or big gaps, and review my [ watchlist ](https://www.warriortrading.com/how-to-build-morning-watchlist/) . By the time I usually begin trading at 7 am ET for the premarket session, I know which type of stocks I want to trade, where my entries are, and [ my risk tolerance for the day. ](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading-risk-management/)

The U.S. [ market opens at 9:30 a.m. Eastern ](https://www.nyse.com/trade/hours-calendars) , but premarket trading begins as early as 4:00 a.m.

In my opinion, preparation separates consistent traders from reactive ones, so a premarket routine is essential.

### Key Takeaways

- 

Start your premarket routine at least an hour before the open — check futures, scan for news or gaps, and review your watchlist.

- 

By the time you begin trading, have a clear plan: which stocks you want to trade, your entry points, and your maximum acceptable loss.

- 

Preparation is what separates consistent traders from reactive ones — the work you do before the bell defines your edge.

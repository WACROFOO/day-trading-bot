# A Roller Coaster Week with CING

Hey everyone, [Ross Cameron](https://www.rosscameron.com/) here! We made it to the end of the week, and what a week it’s been! The market has provided some exciting action that I can’t help but talk about. We saw some significant moves, especially within large-cap, mid-cap, and penny stocks. The activity has been electric. Let’s dive into the recap!

## CING Stock Events

One standout this week has been CING. This stock showed remarkable movement, rallying up 971% over the last two days. On the first day, it soared 80%, catching the attention of traders across the board. The momentum continued early the next morning, largely influenced by European traders who spotted the news.

On Friday morning, I was back at it, [trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/) CING right at 7 a.m. The initial trade was promising, netting me about $900 (Results not typical). That set the tone for the trading day, albeit with some challenges due to increased spreads and lighter volume as the stock became pricier.

## A Day in the Life of Trading

This morning, I started the day by pulling up the scans on my phone, spotting CING once again topping the list. I jumped in early, but as the morning went on, [trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/) got trickier. Yesterday’s booming action meant bigger spreads today, and I had to be cautious to maneuver through the whipsaw price actions.

Around 7 a.m., I was watching for a key bounce and pounced on it, securing my first trade. That initial profit gave me a safety net to take on bigger positions as the day unfolded. But as CING’s price oscillated, I had to pivot, [trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/) it alongside other stocks throughout the day.

## Technical Hiccups and Missed Chances

Just as things were heating up, I ran into a frustrating technical glitch. The internet cut out! It was a brief hiccup but hit right when I had an opportunity. When the connection dropped, I missed a chance to jump back in at a critical moment. By the time I reconnected, the price had already surged past my buy point, adding a bit of angst to the day’s action.

Despite the glitch, I managed another successful trade in CING, ending with a total of $2,200 from that stock alone. By the end of the day, my overall profit amounted to $2,534.37—steady progress, though not a home run.

## Risks and Rewards in Trading

[Trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/) CING taught me a lot about managing risk, especially considering the potential for stock offerings. I learned that day two of a strong move can be unpredictable. Stock offerings could dilute the value of shares and impact traders holding onto their positions too long. It’s a vivid reminder to stay nimble and vigilant.

I’m always careful in a volatile market. It’s all about knowing when to push and when to pull back. Not every trade will be perfect, but measured risks can lead to rewarding outcomes.

# All Roads Lead To Rome

Second stop of this trading and traveling journey brought me to Rome. It’s very likely going to be the most special place I’ll be visiting among all the planned cities. And that’s not mainly because Rome is recognized to be one of the most amazing destination in the whole world, which it would have been alone a great reason to be so.

In fact, I am Italian. I was born here and that’s where I’ve spent the vast majority of my life ever since. Doing this episode of a crazy adventure in the capital of my own country makes me feeling particularly enthusiast about it. So, of course there is also a sense of pride to be an ambassador of what this beautiful city has to offer.

Also, I’m here for the first time not just as a tourist and I’ve been fortunate enough to experience what a remote job could really offer to me. During the mornings, I’m having the chance to visit all the incredible and famous sites and to be meeting with the locals (no language barrier helped me even more with that) as well as the chance to focus on my own passion in the afternoons, which is day trading of course.

## Every Life Path Is Unique, Find Your Own Way To Succeed

We have a famous saying that in Italy that goes like this: ”Tutte le strade portano a Roma”, which I have translated into the title of this article. What that basically translates into is the fact that no matter which road you take, every single one of those will get you where you want to be, if you put enough effort and commitment to succeed.

Also, it reminds you to keep in mind what the final target always is, in a way that you can always be aware that it’s going to take time to get where you want to be, especially the more ambitious your goal is. And becoming a consistently profitable trader is definitely going to be an achievable but also a very challenging target to reach.

Along the way, sometimes you just have  to take a leap of faith by believing in yourself that taking that road is going to take you closer to the ultimate target. But the point is that you’ll come to know for sure only once you see that “Rome” signal. Because persistency is the real key to get what you want and consistency will just be a natural consequence of it.

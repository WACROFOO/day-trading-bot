# 3 Tips On How To Make Money Online

In the current digital era, there are so many things that people can do to make money online. If you don’t know how to make money online, this article will take you through some of the most profitable online opportunities to keep your bank account growing.

## Online Blogging

Blogging can make you as much money as you want if you are ready and willing to put in the hard effort. However, you have to choose the topic that interests both you and your readers to succeed in blogging. When starting your blog site, you should not dive in thinking that you are going to start earning immediately. You will have to put in a lot of effort and be patient enough before you can begin yielding some good results.

It is about knowledge, trying to learn more and thinking about what your readers want to know. There are numerous blog topics that can make you money online, but you can only be successful with the topic that you are passionate about. It very easy to make money online by blogging, without even having to overstretch your mind.

Your blog can generate revenue streams through several ways such as advertising, affiliate promotions, running events, recurring payments to access premium content, promoting businesses, and selling services/products to readers.

## Selling Stuff On Amazon

Amazon started out as an online platform for selling books and has now grown into the leading online shop for consumers all over the world. In addition, some people are beginning to learn how this online giant retailer can earn them money. One of the best ways to make money on Amazon is through the site’s affiliate program bloggers can earn commissions on each sale they refer to the website. The more referrals you make the higher the commissions you earn – from 4% to 8%.

[Amazon FBA](https://services.amazon.com/fulfillment-by-amazon/benefits.html) is another great way to make money on Amazon. The retailer launched Amazon FBA which eliminates the need to deal with returns and shipping. As a seller, you send the stuff you would like to sell to the warehouse of Amazon, and they handle the sales process. However, you have to pay attention to the products you are selling to make a lot of money via Amazon FBA.

You could also make some cash of Amazon Kindle where the site allows people to self-publish eBooks. Amazon does not charge any upfront fees for this service, nonetheless, you will have to share every sale you make on your eBook on a ratio 35% to 70%.

## Day Trading

Online [day trading](/day-trade-definition-day-trading-terminology/) is another superb to make money using any internet-connected computer or smartphone. It has been an online means for the average woman or man to make money at home sitting behind their computer since the late 1990s when the internet began to grow rapidly. However, like blogging, people should not expect to start earning six-figure incomes right off the bat.

To start day trading online, you will need a computer, high-speed internet connection, trading software, capital, and a disciplined strategy. Your day trading goal should be to earn money daily, but it should not be pennies. You want to make good money so that you can relax feeling that you did not waste your time and this will take time and practice. Look at joining a trading community and at taking some kind of [trading course](https://www.warriortrading.com/trading-courses/). This will help shorten the learning curve and give you the confidence you will need to succeed.

There are a lot of different ways to trade the markets so you will have to find out which one fits your personality the best. You can look at scalping and day trading or if like slower paced trading then you can look at[ swing trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading-vs-swing-trading/) which allows traders to hold positions for multiple days.

So, the smartest strategy for online day traders is to find and join the [best online investing courses](https://www.benzinga.com/money/best-online-investing-courses/) where you can learn and trade with other, more experienced traders.

## Final Thoughts

Each of the above ways of making money online can be a rewarding experience, and you make a living doing it. And although trying to make a living off the internet can, and at times it will be very tiring and extremely frustrating, your efforts will ultimately be rewarded not only financially but they will also allow you to earn money from anywhere there is an internet connection.

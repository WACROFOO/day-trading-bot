# Best Day Trading Books

## Best Day Trading Books

The best traders never stop studying.  We want to encourage all of our members to focus on education and improving their [strategies](https://www.warriortrading.com/momentum-day-trading-strategy/) whenever possible.  For that reason, we want to make a few suggestions of great books that you can read in the evenings when the market is closed!

[](http://www.amazon.com/Trade-Mindfully-Performance-Mindfulness-Psychology/dp/1118445619/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1457191946&sr=8-1&keywords=Trade+Mindfully)

## [Trade Mindfully: Achieve Your Optimum Trading Performance with Mindfulness and Cutting Edge Psychology, by Gary Dayton PSY.D](http://www.amazon.com/Trade-Mindfully-Performance-Mindfulness-Psychology/dp/1118445619/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1457191946&sr=8-1&keywords=Trade+Mindfully)

I’ve recommended this book to thousands of our students over the years.  Anyone that struggles with selling winners too soon and holding losers too long should read this.   Gary Dayton will help you understand the emotional structure that leads to this poor trading behavior so you can begin to make changes to save your trading career from yourself!

[](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/1118858697?psc=1&redirect=true&ref_=oh_aui_detailpage_o00_s00)

## [The Little Book of Market Wizards: Lessons from the Greatest Traders, by Jack D. Schwager](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/1118858697?psc=1&redirect=true&ref_=oh_aui_detailpage_o00_s00)

This Day Trading book is a great read before bed and while you are winding down.  It’s not to heavy, but it gives great food for thought on the trading process and experience.  In contrast to a how to book like mine, this is a book that takes about theory and the big concepts of trading.  I really enjoyed it!

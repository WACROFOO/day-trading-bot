# It’s Always Day One

Good days and bad days happen. Whether we like or not, we don’t really have much room for choice about when either of those are going to happen. Sometimes it can be extremely pleasant or largely frustrating but in any case, we’ve got to deal with it. There are always going to be some backward steps even though we all love moving forward rather than having to accept punches right in the face.

As [day traders](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/), we need to take complete responsibility for our own actions. We have to fall in love with the idea there is nobody else to blame if something goes wrong. The market will never care about our feelings and that’s ok. So, the journey of being a trader is mostly about learning more about ourselves as we go along with it.

So, on one hand there is literally no point in giving up because with the right mindset we can always switch a bad trade into a great lesson learned for the future or, on the other hand, learning how to keep both feet on the ground when we manage to close a trade that resolved very well in our favor. 

As far as we know, the bottom line is that it’s always day one. There is no better approach than waking up and starting fresh every morning without plenty of expectations for what lies ahead. In a life of a trader, the greatest castle is always built brick after brick over the long run.

## There is no such thing as enjoying the journey

We live in a world where everything looks like easy to achieve from the outside. When looking at somebody else fortune, common sense is to think about how lucky that person is. Someone would experience envy, jealousy or even hate. The truth is that 99% of those people who have reached a so called wealthy status have worked really hard to get there. And only in that hustle is kept the secret of getting there. That’s why I always give to all entrepreneurs my highest respect. They chose not to survive but to put every single drop of energy into their mission.

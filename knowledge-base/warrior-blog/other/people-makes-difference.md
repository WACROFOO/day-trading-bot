# People Is What Makes the Difference

This is going to be a special blog post about the experience I’ve gone through during the two-days seminar held in Las Vegas I’ve just gotten back from. Quite honestly, I think this is one of the best benefits taken from being part of this [Inner Circle](https://www.warriortrading.com/inner-circle/) family at Warrior Trading.

It’s been such an immersive event that is really impossible to share everything we’ve gone through within these few lines below. However, I’m trying my best to get you a sense of what this one adventure has brought back to me now that I’m sitting at my home desk in Italy again.

First and foremost, it’s about people. Because I’ve once again realized, great people is what makes the greatest communities a reality. And the importance of belonging to this one has been a game-changer to me.

And mind you, I’m not only referring to the senior [day trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/) mentors or the other junior moderators but also to the profound contribution that each one of the participants delivered with such a high sense of commitment.

Especially during these days where distinguishing between what’s virtual and what’s real has become increasingly difficult, putting the name to each one of the faces and personalities has been huge.

Here it’s impossible to mention every one of you but you need to know that it’s been a great honor meeting you all and that I’ve been really fortunate to have been part of this memorable experience.

## Living Vegas from a different perspective

It has been the third time for me in Vegas, the other two times I’ve stopped through there it was for vacations trip only and I’ve never spent more than two days in the city. In fact, anyone who knows me enough will be aware that I’m not a gambler and that I don’t enjoy doing that very much.

As my friend [John L10](https://www.warriortrading.com/category/johns-blog-posts/) often says:” We’re not traders but risk managers instead”, I believe that’s what has distinguished us from the 99% of the other people hanging out in Las Vegas! And that’s what make traders very different from casino gamblers when proven to be successful strategies are applied with perseverance and discipline.

It has been awesome living the city from a total different perspective and I am leaving it with a true sense of gratitude for the outstanding opportunity that I’ve been given. I feel that I have received brand new “aces in the hole” (what’s better place to get those from than Vegas???) for bringing my trading to next level.

“If you know how to live in Vegas, you can have the best time.” – Tony Curtis

See you in chat-room.

Trade safe,

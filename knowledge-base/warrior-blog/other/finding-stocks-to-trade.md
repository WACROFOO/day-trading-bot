# How to Find Stocks to Day Trade

As a beginner trader one of the most difficult concepts to grasp is how in the world profitable traders find stocks to trade.

There are thousands of stocks, mutual funds, and [ETFs](https://www.warriortrading.com/exchange-traded-funds-etf/) that you could trade.  Somehow, a professional trader has to find 5-10 stocks worth trading each day AND maintain a 65-75% accuracy of winners vs losers.

Once you can achieve this feat, you will be a profitable trader, making a living from the stock market.

 That’s how important stock selection is!

# Featured Profile: Max

## [](https://media.warriortrading.com/2023/11/27210341/Max.jpg)

## Max’s Story

Max is a Warrior Trading member who began trading during the COVID-19 Pandemic. He was drawn to the market by peers in school who were also trading. Making money trading and boasting about the gains go hand in hand. This was the same for Ross, who was inspired by a friend in high school who made $16,000 trading a penny stock. 

For Max, he initially was skeptical about day trading, but eventually, he decided to give it a shot. But there was one problem. He didn’t have the money to fund a trading account. So he paper traded with pretend money. He did so well, he showed his parents his performance and they agreed to help him set up his first trading account with Ameritrade, where he uses the ThinkorSwim trading platform. The rest is history. 

Max became the youngest member to earn his $1 million badge.

## Max’s Strategy

Max is a long-biased momentum trader. Since he’s trading with ThinkorSwim, he has no commissions. He uses that to his advantage by scalping momentum stocks as they are moving higher. He is generally focusing on the Top 10 Gainers each day. One of Max’s favorite setups is buying the dip of an ABCD pattern to catch the re-test of the high of day.

# About Ross Cameron of Warrior Trading

Ross Day Trading at a Warrior Trading Summit

Ross Cameron is a full-time day trader and the founder of [Warrior Trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/), a trusted trading education platform. His passion lies in helping beginner traders learn the ins and outs of the stock market and develop strategies that deliver consistent results.

Having turned a small account of just $583.15 into over $12.5 million in trading profits, Ross went from unemployed during the Great Recession to a successful day trader. In addition to the [Warrior Trading YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@DaytradeWarrior) and [podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/ross-camerons-day-trading-podcast/id1637652669), Ross has contributed his expertise to [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/author/ross-cameron) and [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/author/ross-cameron-warrior-trading) and authored a book, [How To Day Trade: The Plain Truth](https://www.amazon.com/How-Day-Trade-Plain-Truth-ebook/dp/B0CM1JLZBR?ref_=ast_author_dp).

Through the Warrior Trading platform, Ross has built a community for beginner and experienced traders alike. With momentum trading strategies, live trading sessions, and [other trading courses](https://www.warriortrading.com/trading-courses/), Warrior Trading can help you go from a first-time trader to a knowledgeable veteran.

## From Vermont to the Stock Market

Ross’s first brush with the stock market occurred in the late 1990s, at the height of the dot-com bubble. A school project managing a simulated portfolio sparked his interest in how stocks worked, and a trip to New York City cemented his dream of becoming a part of the financial world.

However, Ross faced obstacles that are still common to aspiring traders. For example, his limited funds as a teenager made trading seem out of reach. His first job delivering newspapers helped him save $100 — which he hoped to invest in the market — but the high commission fees prevented him from making his first trade.

[](https://media.warriortrading.com/2023/04/04143740/Ross-Early-Trading-Station.jpg)
One of Ross’s early trading stations circa 2010-2012

In 2001, Ross opened his first trading account with Ameritrade while still in high school, funding it with $1,000 he had saved from part-time jobs. However, by the end of the summer, the stock values showed little change, reinforcing his belief that trading required either a large initial capital or deep market knowledge.

## Discovering His Momentum Trading Strategy

After years of exploration, Ross’s breakthrough came with the discovery of [momentum trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/momentum-day-trading-strategy/) — a day trading strategy focused on high-volatility stocks that make significant price movements within a single trading day.

In his early days, Ross dabbled in various trading systems, including trading [penny stocks](/penny-stocks/), small caps, and other options. None yielded consistent success. When he experienced a red day — in which his day trading account fell below $25,000, restricting his ability to trade under Pattern Day Trader Rule — he took a step back to analyze his trading history.

In doing so, he discovered a pattern: His most successful trades were in stocks experiencing rapid momentum. Typically priced between $2 and $10, these stocks offered significant profit potential if traded with discipline and focus. This discovery laid the foundation for the momentum trading strategy that would come to define his career.

## Founding Warrior Trading

Ross founded Warrior Trading in 2012 as a blog, where he posted his activity diary and detailed analysis of the stock market. Originally called the “Day Trade Warrior Blog,” he attracted like-minded young people in the business who were impressed by his authenticity.

Since then, Warrior Trading has expanded to become a full-blown educational and community platform featuring live trading chat rooms, [trading simulators](https://www.warriortrading.com/paper-trading/), and step-by-step tutorials on trading techniques.

When Ross created Warrior Trading, his goal was to build a place for traders to move through their progression, discuss it with their peers, and find the courage within themselves to be successful. As the platform has grown, this dream has become a reality.

The Warrior Trading platform also boasts breaking news updates and real-time alerts to keep traders informed, plus tutorials on unique strategies such as [candlestick patterns](https://www.warriortrading.com/candlestick-charts/) and trading psychology. These insights and offerings have ensured that the platform evolves with changes in the market.

## Turning $583 Into Millions: The Small Account Challenge

[](https://media.warriortrading.com/2023/08/04144026/Entrepreneur.png)

In 2017, Ross embarked on a small account challenge, a project to prove that his day trading strategies could work for accounts of all sizes. Starting with just $583.15, Ross demonstrated the power of disciplined momentum trading strategies by growing his account to over $100,000 in just 44 days.

The challenge didn’t stop there. Over the next two years, Ross continued to build his account, reaching the $1 million milestone in May 2019. Today, his total trading profits exceed $10 million — an extremely untypical feat that highlights his ability to navigate the market and maximize returns.

Throughout the challenge, Ross maintained total transparency by sharing detailed broker statements, and today, he continues to make his broker statements available for public review. His success proved that remarkable results are possible with the right strategies and mindset.

## Ross the Teacher

Teaching and training have been essential parts of Ross’s career. Before he became a full-time day trader, he considered becoming a full-time teacher, enrolling in college in New Hampshire with the goal of becoming a ceramics instructor. Through Warrior Trading, Ross found an outlet to combine his natural teaching skills with his deep understanding of the stock market.

Ross’s approach combines technicalities with applications. His real-time trading sessions, trade recaps, and video tutorials give his students a comprehensive view of the trading ecosystem. At the same time, he emphasizes trading psychology and how psychological factors can affect investment decisions.

## A Life Beyond Trading

Ross’s life isn’t all about day trading. Residing in the Berkshires of Massachusetts, he enjoys spending time with his wife and two sons. When he’s not trading, Ross can be found sailing, mountain biking, or restoring classic cars — a passion that inspired his blog, [Tire Kickers](https://www.tirekickers.com/).

Winters in Massachusetts provide another outlet for Ross’s creativity. He and his sons like to tap sugar maples to make homemade maple syrup, a tradition that requires similar patience and focus as trading. Ultimately, he believes that success in life — as in trading — comes from a balanced perspective.

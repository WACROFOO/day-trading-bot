# How to Day Trade Options in 2021

Day trading options has become has become increasingly popular in recent years for a number of reasons.

The chance of a much higher rate of return is the main reason. Options offer several other benefits and, if used wisely, are an excellent way to help day traders reach their financial goals.

If you are considering day trading options, you have come to the right place. In the following, you will learn all you need to know to get started, including why you should day trade options, how to trade them, and tips to keep in mind.

## What are Options?

Options are contracts that give the holder either the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell a [stock](https://www.warriortrading.com/stock-definition-day-trading-terminology/), or other financial instruments at a given price within a given period, usually a few weeks or months.

## Types of Options

There are various types of options available for traders and these can be categorized in a number of ways. The two most popular categories of options among traders, and from which all options trades are built, are [call options](https://www.warriortrading.com/call-option-definition-day-trading-terminology/) and [put options](https://www.warriortrading.com/put-option-definition-day-trading-terminology/).

- A stock call option gives the holder the right (but not the obligation) to buy a stock from the seller by a certain date (the expiry), for a certain price (the strike price). The value of a call option goes up when the price of the underlying stock price increases.

- A stock put option gives the holder the right to sell a stock at an agreed-upon strike price before the expiry date. The value of a put option goes down when the price of the underlying stock price falls.

## Why you Should Day Trade Options

Now that we have covered the basics, let us look at why you should day trade stock options.

- Ease of trading

First, trading options is simple. It’s just like buying and selling stocks. You pick a strike and expiration for the options you want to purchase and hit the buy button!

- Ability to hedge

Trading options also offers you an opportunity to work both sides of the market since you can buy and sell both call and put options. Therefore, you can shield much of your capital in the event that something goes wrong on the short or long side.

- Leverage

Second, options traders can earn a very nice return with just a little bit of money as they are able to take advantage of the leverage options offer.

- Low cash requirement

If you want to generate decent earnings as a stock day-trader, you will most likely need to start with a decent amount of cash. But you can start with much less money if you day trade options instead of stocks, thanks to leverage.

## Drawbacks to Day Trading Options

While great in many respects, day-trading options does have the following drawbacks:

- Illiquidity: Some thinly traded stock options have wide bid/ask spreads. This means that a day trader will buy at the high price of the spread and sell at the low price of the spread.

- It is also difficult to know when the huge moves are going to happen

- Day trading options also requires more prep work to be done on a daily basis since traders need to look at bigger watch lists of stocks because they don’t know which names are going to be active on a given day

- Finally, if you plan on day trading options, you will have to meet certain margin requirements that are laid out by security regulators.

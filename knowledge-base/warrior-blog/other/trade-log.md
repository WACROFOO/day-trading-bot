# Ross’s Trade Log

To promote transparency, Ross’s broker statements are uploaded at the end of each month. Links to these statements can be found in the footer of every page on Warrior Trading. These statements show Ross’s daily profit/loss and his monthly net profit/loss.

To see a trade log of Ross’s individual trades on any given day, your best bet is to check the [Daily Recaps Playlist on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL1xI23WKVWienxdYuMT2VJCSK7LicBzdr). These recaps highlight the winners and the losers and offer some words of wisdom from Ross’s years of trading experience.

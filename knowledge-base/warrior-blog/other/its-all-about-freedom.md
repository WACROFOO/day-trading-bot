# It’s All About Freedom

 It’s all about freedom. This is what the idea of trading represented to me since the very first minute I realized it was actually possible. During that moment, I began dreaming about changing my life and having a whole new opportunity to achieve new goals and to finally dedicate my time and resources to something I have a genuine passion for.

Here I am, two years into this trading journey and still eager to gain more experience. In particular, I am looking to open a new chapter about what I consider to be the ultimate freedom. Freedom is assuming a very deep meaning that I’m discovering as I go further into the trading journey of mine.

In fact, ever since I started my trading career, I’ve always dreamed to have the chance of experiencing the “nomad” lifestyle. One of the unique aspects of this career is that all you need to have is a minimal equipment setup and a strong internet connection. So, once you’ve mastered the skills required to become consistently profitable, the possibilities become endless.

I’ve discussed this topic in the past when writing some of my favorites articles. But right here, right now, there is something different that I am going to tell you. I am so excited about this that it’s still hard to realize it’s actually happening. I am a little nervous too, since this is a project that me and the Warrior Trading team are planning many months now and will put myself outside of my comfort zone, again.

## Trading Taught Me To Embrace The Unexpected

Starting from January 7th, 2018 I am beginning a whole new adventure as a traveling trader! The plan is to spend most of this year visiting and living in seventeen of the most amazing cities in Europe. And the most exciting part is that I’m going to bring you with me on this journey. In fact, we’re going to produce and publish plenty of content that will hopefully be valuable for anyone looking to get more insights about the trading & traveling experience.

I am writing this article as the emotions are coming all over myself in a mix of joy and apprehension. I’m doing this with pure intent of giving value more than just enjoying the time that is about to come. And I am so involved in this that I’m feeling ready to face the unknown. In the end, trading taught me to always embrace the unexpected, rather than trying to force what we want.

In other words, I’m pushing myself beyond those boundaries that everyone has told me were impossible to achieve. But isn’t that opportunity to break those limits exactly the purest form of freedom?

“Instead of trying to make your life perfect, give yourself the freedom to make it an adventure, and go ever upward.” ― Drew Houston

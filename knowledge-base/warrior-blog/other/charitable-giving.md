## Warrior Trading Gives Back!

Ross and the Warrior Trading Team are celebrating our anniversary by giving back!

Each Thursday in April 2021 Ross will trade in our Thankful Thursday live streams. At the end of each trading session, Ross will donate all profits to charities nominated by members of our team and student community. You can track our progress by subscribing to our YouTube channel where Ross will stream his trading and donations. 

Warrior Pro and Starter members can nominate charities by contacting our support team.

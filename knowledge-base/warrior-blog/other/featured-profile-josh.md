## Josh’s Story

Josh is a [day trader](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/) based out of New York who has earned his $100k badge at Warrior Trading. He lives just a few hours north of New York City. He was introduced to the world of day trading while still in college. Josh found his way to Warrior Trading and is a graduate of the Warrior Pro curriculum. With some initial success, he decided to drop out of college and focus exclusively on trading. 

## Josh’s Strategy

Josh is a momentum trader who focuses exclusively on scalp trading. [Scalp trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/scalp-trading-strategy-for-beginners/) is when you take quick trades with relatively large share sizes in order to generate a profit on small moves in the price of a stock.

Since Josh takes dozens of trades each day, he utilizes commission-free brokers. His current broker of choice is [Charles Schwab](https://www.warriortrading.com/charles-schwab-broker-review/), and he’s using the [DAS Trader](https://www.warriortrading.com/das-trader-review/) integration. By using DAS Trader instead of ThinkorSwim he can script customized hotkeys. These allow him to move in and out of the market faster. For an active trader, speed is is critical.

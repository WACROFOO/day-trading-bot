# 3 Popular Monthly Dividend Stocks

## What are Dividends?

The thought of living and thriving through passive income in terms of dividend checks may seem like a dream to many people. However, it can become true, particularly when you comprehend what a dividend is before you engage in this trade. Here are a few insights to help you grasp a sound understanding of dividends:

- A dividend is a regular payment made to an investor who owns stock in a company.

- It is crucial to note that not every stock pays dividends.

- Dividends must be [declared](https://www.thebalance.com/dividend-investing-introduction-357452) or authorized by a firm’s board of directors every time they are issued.

- When companies generate profits and accrue retained earnings, that income can be reinvested in the enterprise or disbursed to investors as dividends.

- [Dividend yield](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/knowledge/finance/dividend/) is calculated by dividing the dividend for each share by the price of the share.

For more of dividends, read this [post](https://www.warriortrading.com/dividend-definition/).

## Gladstone Investment Corporation ($GAIN)

[Gladstone Investment Corporation](https://www.gladstoneinvestment.com/) is a private equity fund that focuses on acquiring mature and lower middle market companies with revenue not exceeding $100 million in revenue, strong management teams, and attractive fundamentals.

Investing alongside both independent sponsors and management teams, this business development company typically offers most of the debt and [equity](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/e/equity.asp) capital to close a transaction. This greatly increases the speed and certainty of closing.

Having grown its dividends for over five years, this [buyouts](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/b/buyout.asp) and [recapitalizations](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/r/recapitalization.asp) based company experiences [rosy prospects for a robust buyout moving forward](https://www.investopedia.com/investing/monthly-dividend-stocks/). This makes the monthly income stock an attractive prospect for income-seeking investors.

As a publicly-traded fund, [Gladstone Investment Corporation](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/GAIN:US) differs from other private equity funds in that it has no partnership end-of-life deadlines and offers patient and long-term capital. Besides, it operates as a non-diversified, closed-end management investment firm and invests in:

- Preferred stock

- [Mezzanine debt](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/m/mezzaninedebt.asp)

- Subordinated loans

Additionally, it warrants purchasing common stock of small and mid-sized companies associated with:

- Acquisitions

##  Shaw Communications ($SJR)

With headquarters in Calgary, Alberta, [Shaw Communications](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Shaw_Communications) is a telecommunications company that offers mobile, television, and telephone services all backed by a fiber-optic network. This Canadian telecommunications company offers services mostly in Alberta and British Columbia with smaller systems in Northern Ontario, Manitoba, and Saskatchewan.

This company offers its services to [both institutional and retail consumers](https://www.cleverism.com/company/shaw-communications/) through the following business segments;

- WIND- provides wireless services

- [ViaWest](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/viawest)– offers colocation, public and private clouds, and managed services

- Shaw tracking- provides wireless data solutions and integrated onboard computing technology for the Canadian transportation, logistic industries and mobile workforce

- [Shaw Direct](https://www.shawdirect.ca/english/)– offers digital direct-to-home satellite TV

- Shaw Business- provides services to corporates

- Shaw Broadcast Services- offers satellite-based services

- Shaw Communications- provides home phone and internet services

Although the company faced [cash-flow](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/c/cashflow.asp) troubles from 2013 through 2015, things got better in 2016. The net income has risen over a span of four years. However, although the highest stock price in the past ten years was above $27, in November 2014, it hit a low of $16.37 in February of 2016. Since then, its share price has leveled off to a respectable $19.08, and the company has continued to offer attractive monthly payouts.

For the purposes of operations, Shaw Communications is split into four business segments as follows:

- Consumer- Offers telecommunication services for retail consumers over the phone, video, public Wi-Fi, and broadband internet. This segment takes up approximately 67% of the total revenue and 71% of operating income.

- Media- Delivers both specialty and conventional video content to retail consumers via television on online media channels.

- Business Network Services- Offers telecommunication services for businesses.

- Business Infrastructure Services- Provides IT solutions such as managed services, colocation, cloud computing, and security.

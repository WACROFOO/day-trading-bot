# The Most Difficult Decision

Whenever you’re thinking about pursuing a new career and especially if you start chasing something you can tell is quite uncommon from looking around, odds are that a number of questions like these will come to your mind:

- Am I going to fail?

- Can I make enough space (even quitting my job) to focus on this matter?

- Am I just going to waste my precious time?

- What do other people think about my choice?

Good news is, you’re in great company! You can rest assure that this way of thinking is not only ordinary, it’s about how we are built, it’s our own human nature to feel a strong resistance to change.

The point is if nobody would have found the courage to make a change, we would not have seen any progress in this world. In fact, there are so many examples of successful stories that have given invaluable contribution to move the humanity forward. I personally love the story of Thomas Edison and the invention of the light bulb.

I don’t want to write about his story again over here (if you wish to go further, just browse around the web and you’ll find tons of material) because I’d go too much off-topic. But the point is whoever has been able to make the difference has passed through all of this and has managed to find the courage to act.

Now, listen to me. I’m not saying you should leave your job like I did and become a trader right now. What I am saying is if you’ve gone this far, you may have this desire sitting somewhere within yourself. Moreover, you already are in the right place to start the transition and becoming a successful trader.

## Becoming Self-Sufficient

Also, I’m not here to deceive yourself affirming that this is going to be easy. So, don’t get fooled by anybody that promises you a fortune without any commitment. Personally, I’ve spent many hours immersing myself into [trading courses](/trading-courses/) such as the ones offered by the WarriorPro and I keep doing so.

Indeed, the best gift you can donate yourself is understanding that short-cuts in trading won’t get you far. But, joining this community is a great step forward on becoming self-sufficient, together with dedication and hard work and I can’t think of a faster way to realize this than be part of it.

Expressing yourself and getting to the point where you make your own conscious decisions into trading is the ultimate goal, rather than becoming a carbon copy of other traders. Of course, you can learn from them but, since every one of us is unique, you need to figure out what works best for yourself and adapt it at your best convenience. Again, I’m saying this one without any specific interest: this place is where you get the best knowledge from so that you can count on yourself first. You’ll be amazed of what you can reach by following a passion.

# Warrior Trading’s Inner Circle: Past and Future

Warrior Trading’s Inner Circle started in 2017 as our highest membership tier that included access to a private chat room and small discussion sessions with mentors. From 2017 through 2019, in-person events evolved from a small event in New York City to larger events in Las Vegas, Disney World, and Miami. Traders had the opportunity to experience in-person learning, to trade surrounded by other traders, and to get to know fellow members of the Warrior Trading community face-to-face.

After the December 2019 conference in Miami, which took place just before COVID-19 lockdowns, we were forced to halt meetups for years.

In the meantime, we started planning for our new office space in the [historic Castle Street firehouse in Great Barrington](https://castlestreetfirehouse.com/), the small town in Southern Berkshire County, Massachusetts that we call home. Warrior Trading offices will occupy the second floor of the building.

As construction wraps up and [tenants start to fill the building](https://www.berkshireeagle.com/news/southern_berkshires/great-barrington-castle-street-firehouse-fire-station-warrior-trading-mahaiwe-performing-arts-theater/article_c848e370-e30d-11ee-8d3c-7b5dfb0d1d1a.html), we are excited to showcase a classroom we built to host future Inner Circle conferences. Having learned what attendees liked most from previous events, we are designing the space with the community in mind; fast internet, ample power sources, and enough tabletop space for a [travel trading station](https://www.warriortrading.com/how-to-set-up-a-traveling-trading-station/) are just a few of the things that are sure to be considered.

While waiting for construction to complete in 2022, we decided to keep the conversation going by inviting small groups of verified-profitable members to our then-office, located not too far away from the new location. These groups of [All Star Traders](https://www.warriortrading.com/all-star-traders/) met in a roundtable for a three-day event, discussing the market and topics familiar to many traders, such as [trading while working full time](https://www.warriortrading.com/how-to-day-trade-while-working-full-time/) and [how much money you need to start trading](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DjRX_GJ7RBM&list=PL1xI23WKVWicsaGYpcuE19v3qzhUX6SVD&index=14).

We are strong [supporters of other small businesses in Southern Berkshire County](https://www.warriortrading.com/charitable-giving/) and hope that future events bring business to the town that we love to be in and be a part of. We believe the town truly is one of what was [once called “the best small town in America.”](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/travel/the-20-best-small-towns-in-america-of-2012-66120384/)

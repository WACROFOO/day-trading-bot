## Hawkeye Traders Indicators

According to Hawkeye Traders, it is common for traders to spend a lot of time searching for trading systems that will aid them find the best stocks. Not only that. Traders will spend time testing different indicators as well as analyzing different charts all in hopes of spotting the best [stocks](/stock-definition-day-trading-terminology/). Problem is, a lot of time and even money will end up being wasted.

With Hawkeye Traders, you don’t have to waste time nor your money. Thanks to 20+ years of research and studying [price action](/price-action-definition-day-trading-terminology/), stock trading and volume, traders can now access reliable and non-correlated indicators. These indicators are developed to help traders view the direction of the market.

They were made available to the public in 2001 and today, thousands of traders from around the world have used them to profit. What you ought to know is that the reason why the indicators work so well is that they rely on Hawkeye’s advanced algorithms.

Unlike traditional indicators like [MACD](/moving-average-convergence-divergence-macd-definition-day-trading-terminology/), [moving averages](/simple-moving-average/) or [stochastic](/stochastic-oscillator-day-trading-terminology/), Hawkeye’s stock trading system and tools are developed to work with today’s market [volatility](/volatility-definition-day-trading-terminology/). In addition, they are non-lagging.

Simply put, the indicators are based on the [standard deviation](https://www.warriortrading.com/standard-deviation-definition-day-trading-terminology/) of pattern and price recognition, as well as volume, [spread analysis](/spread-definition-day-trading-terminology/). In order for Hawkeye tools to work as expected, they must be paired with renowned trading systems such as:

- [TradeStation](https://www.warriortrading.com/tradestation-broker-review/)

- [NinjaTrader](https://www.warriortrading.com/ninjatrader-review/)

- [MetaTrader](https://www.warriortrading.com/metastock-review/)

To get started, all a trader needs to do is subscribe to Hawkeye’s suite of software and tools which are available as individual modules as indicated below:

- Volume – has three indicators namely Volume Radar, Volume PaintBar, and Volume. As the [leading indicator](/leading-indicator-definition-day-trading-terminology/), it helps to signal price movement before it happens. It works on all markets and in all timeframes for time and tick charts.

- Levels [ATR](/average-true-range-definition-day-trading-terminology/) – referred to as Levels Average True Range, this is a powerful series of pre-defined levels that act as stop-loss managers and potential exit positions. Basically, the indicator helps traders to come up with clear exit strategies. It works by displaying profit target levels using a predetermined number of price bars.

- Grabba – this is a simple mechanical system that is easy to manage. Users can manage it using their risk and reward profile. As a risk management tool, it displays a visual representation of the trader’s risk and reward profile for each trade.

- FatBoy – this is a powerful and flexible indicator that displays which markets are overbought and those that have been oversold. It is designed to show correlating markets and which are not. Traders can configure it to work with [ETFs](/exchange-traded-funds-etf/) and stocks.

- Trend+Stops – this tool is designed to give traders a true sense of the market [trends](/market-trend-definition-day-trading-terminology/) instead of opinions from the media. The indicator can inform a trader when a trend begins and when a trend ends. It’s based on Hawkeye’s Volume algorithm of pattern recognition ad ATR.

- GearChanger – the indicator helps traders to avoid a collision. Simply put, it helps to establish the correct speed of the market allowing traders to select the correct tick chart finally trading at any time. As a result, the trader can trade in harmony with the markets.

- Fatman – this is a premier [Forex](https://www.warriortrading.com/forex-definition-day-trading-terminology/) strength meter that informs the trader where to begin. As a first of its kind, the indicator helps to compare every currency to other major currencies around the world. As a result, it presents a visual image of the strengths and weaknesses of every currency.

- Roadkill – the indicator works by checking multiple timeframes simultaneously finally providing the trader with two entry signals. One of the signals is based on volume while the other is based on trends. With the two entry signals, traders are in a better position to enter a trade at a certain timeframe.

- Heatmap – the indicator takes into account three variables namely normal, aggressive and conservative. Data is then displayed when all three variables (trends) lock into place. This provides the trader with a clear view of the market. In addition, it helps to quantify risks too.

- GearBox – the indicator utilizes a complex algorithm to determine the optimal tick speed for the day ahead. This is displayed in a four-bar chart where each speed is represented in one of four colors. For instance, light blue represents ultra fast chart, dark blue represents fast chart, yellow represents medium speed and red represents slow speed.

Other individual modules that you can download and pair with your trading system include:

## Hawkeye Zones

Designed to aid active traders to visualize supply, demand, [support](https://www.warriortrading.com/support-level-definition-day-trading-terminology/), [resistance](/resistance-level-definition-day-trading-terminology/) and how they interact with price action, Hawkeye Zones helps traders to increase their chances of winning trades. Hawkeye experts designed the indicator for other reasons too namely:

- Help traders find out market breakout zones and turning points

- Help traders link support and resistance to price action

- Help traders know when to enter and exit trades

Developed to work with any type of specialty or time chart, Hawkeye Zones can be used with different timeframes. Normally, the zones may overlap even on multiple timeframe charts.

This is referred to as confluence. When supply and resistance zones are displayed on multiple charts, the degree of confluence is said to be high meaning price has been affected by that zone.

As a specialty deep discount broker, Generic Trade specially serves fee conscious and hands-on futures traders.

Simply put, Generic Trade is designed for traders interested in discount options and futures trading. It is also suitable for traders who also want to avoid common restrictions present on renowned trading platforms. Such restrictions include minimum account balances and minimum [volume](https://www.warriortrading.com/volume-definition-day-trading-terminology/) requirements.

Whether you are an individual or institution, all trades are routed using the direct connection to the exchanges.

To start trading, you must fund your account. As said earlier, traders are not subjected to minimum account balances or other requirements. At the moment, Generic Trader requires you to wire funds from your account to their account located at BMO Harris Bank.

As a new user, simply log in and from your account dashboard, select the following menu item “Click here to Fund Account.” For traders with an existing account, they need to sign up to Generic Trade Online and click “Account Services” followed by “Transfer Funds.”

At the time of writing this informative Generic Trade Review, the online discount broker was no longer accepting the following types of checks:

What you ought to know is that all ACH deposits take 5 to 7 business days for the funds to be reflected in the trader’s account. The funds are limited to $10,000 within a 30 day period.

As a trader, what you ought to know is that Generic Trade does not charge traders for accessing and using Generic Trader Online. To enjoy free access, traders must send an email to Generic Trade support that they wish to use the platform exclusively.

If not, users will be set up to use Generic Trader Platform suite.

To access all the features available on Generic Trader suite of professional trading platforms that include Generic Trader Professional, Generic Trader Online and the Generic Trade Mobile app, the online discount broker charges $59 platform fee.

Once you subscribe, you will have access to unlimited free quotes and charts. In addition, you will not be charged extra transaction fees.

Final Thoughts

As an online discount broker specializing in futures and option on futures, Generic Trade caters to both beginners and experienced traders. To help beginners learn more about Generic Trade trading platforms, the discount broker offers a free trial account.

Signing up for the demo account is free and easy. With your demo account, you can learn how to use the various features and tools offered on all trading platforms. In addition, you can try out different trading strategies without fear of losing your capital.

When it comes to fees, Generic Trade is competitive just like any other specialty brokerage firm. Traders are not charged inactivity, data or maintenance fees. This is the same with real-time quote and charting fees.

Although Generic Trade offers 24-hour support via live chat and email, it does not offer broker assisted market orders. In addition, Generic Trade does not offer educational resources which would have been an advantage, especially for beginners.

This is because beginners would have familiarized themselves with futures and options trading while relying on knowledge curated by experts.

Overall, Generic Trade serves its audience very well. It has taken steps to secure its servers, provide feature rich and cutting-edge trading platforms for beginners, and hands-on experienced traders.

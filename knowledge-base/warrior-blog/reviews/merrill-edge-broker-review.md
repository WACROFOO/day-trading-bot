## Merrill Edge Pricing

Merrill Edge does not charge traders for opening and operating an account. The minimum initial investment is $0 in exception of 529 NextGen Direct accounts where traders are charged $25.

When it comes to investing and trading, Merrill Edge charges the following rates:

- Stocks & ETFs – a flat rate of $6.95 regardless of your account balance or trading rate. For automated phone trades, Merrill Edge charges $6.95 and $29.95 for Representative assisted trades.

- Options – for online trades, traders are charged $6.95 per trade plus $0.75 per contract. For representative assisted trades, charges are $29.95 per trade plus $0.75 per contract.

- Mutual funds – For load-waived funds and no-load, no transaction fee funds, Merrill Edge charges $0 per transaction.

- Fixed income and bonds – for online trades, no charges for new issues (including Brokered CDs) and Treasuries (including Auctions and Secondary). For representative assisted trades, Merrill Edge charges $29.95 per trade service charge for Treasuries (including Auction and Secondary).

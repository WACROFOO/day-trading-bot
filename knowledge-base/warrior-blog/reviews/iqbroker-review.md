# IQBroker Trading Software Review

IQBroker is renowned as the world’s most sophisticated broker-neutral trading platform provider. The company was started by Daniel Tochner with the goal of developing an algorithmic trading platform that allows trading of forex, futures and [stocks](https://www.warriortrading.com/stock-definition-day-trading-terminology/). Daniel Tochner is the current CEO of IQBroker with over 15 years of accumulated experience in professional software developer, capital markets and business administration.

To ensure the company provides the best products and services to its customers, it has designed 18 different types of .NET scripts. These scripts are developed to control all aspects of discretionary or algorithmic trading. That is not all. The platform allows traders and investors to back test and optimize their trading strategies while trading with stocks, futures and forex.

## Top Platform Features

IQBroker has designed a platform for equities, futures and forex trading. To provide users with the best experience, a unique set of powerful features are available with the platform. They include:

1. Chart

The platform features chart trading allowing traders and investors to trade directly via its integrated trade pad. Users can change chart styles to one of the following – box, candlestick, candlestick with [trend](https://www.warriortrading.com/market-trend-definition-day-trading-terminology/), candle [volume](https://www.warriortrading.com/volume-definition-day-trading-terminology/), dot on close, equivolume and volume histogram. That is not all. Users have the chance of modifying orders directly on the carts as well as setting up sophisticated alerts.

2. Backtesting

Developers at IQBroker understand how important it is for traders to attain success with every trade. As a result, they have added a backtesting and optimization feature. This feature helps traders and investors to optimize an entire portfolio of algorithmic trading strategies using custom optimization algorithmic and goals.

3. Scripts

18 different types of .NET scripts are available with the platform. They are bundled into the following groups – strategy, chart and portfolio level scripts. The scripts are designed to customize every aspect of the traders and investors discretionary and algorithmic trading.

4. Multiple workspaces

Users have access to multiple workspaces each with its own trading tools. The workspaces are flexible and structured. They can be resized and rearranged through the merging of cells. Furthermore, users can name, delete, load and save workspaces.

5. Watchlist

With this feature, traders and investors have the ability of viewing open position details for every symbol. One can also view streaming real time quotes for every symbol as well as add watchlist columns that aid in calculating indicators, patterns and signals.

Traders interested in learning how to use IQBroker’s platform have the chance of being trained by professionals. The training is designed for non-programmers with zero programming experience. Advanced programmers can still learn new stuff which is useful in optimizing the platform for a better experience.

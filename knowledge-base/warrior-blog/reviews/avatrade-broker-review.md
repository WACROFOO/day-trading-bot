# AvaTrade Broker Review

AvaTrade is a regulated Forex trading broker providing clients with tailor made solutions. Since 2006, the company has been an innovative pioneer in online trading. It was founded through combined efforts between financial experts and specialists in web commerce. This was done with the goal of perfecting online trading experience for all traders whether experienced or not.

As one of the top Forex brokers in the world, the firm has offices in New York, Dublin, Sydney, Milan, Tokyo and other locations. It is regulated by the Central Bank of Ireland and licensed by MiFID located in the European Union. Over the last decade, AvaTrade has established itself as a major player in online Forex trading with a proven track record of positive customer satisfaction.

The company has carved a reputation as the leader of adaptable trading solutions backed by strong financial institutions. Today, it has over 200,000 registered trades from around the globe. This has resulted in over 2 million trades every month.

As one of the first Forex brokers to introduce CFD trading on commodities, indices and stocks, AvaTrade has gone ahead to introduce currency trading granting traders access to the lucrative Litecoin and [Bitcoin](https://www.warriortrading.com/bitcoin-everything-you-need-to-know/) markets too.

## Services Offered

The following services are offered to both beginners and experienced traders.

a. Free demo account

Beginners can practice trading online without risking their own capital thanks to AvaTrade’s free demo account that is also 100% risk free. You can download your demo trading account to your PC, mobile or access it via the web. Once you register, you can begin trading in different markets finally familiarizing yourself with the platforms on offer. You can also take advantage of their 24/7 customer service and educational portal too. This will allow you to learn the basics about trading.

i. MetaTrader 4

iv.MQL5

c. Access to online tutorials

AvaTrade offers training materials in form of online video tutorials, webinars, online video tutorials and a glossary. Online tutorials allows beginners to learn what MetaTrader 4 is, what a pip is, about Forex trading, how to read a Forex chart, about CFDs, Options and technical analysis among others. The tutorials are available as a step by step guide making it easy to learn.

d. Customer support service

Both beginners and experienced traders have access to customer support staff that is ready to answer any questions as well as provide additional information. Support is provided via email, phone, fax and live chat.

This is a program that awards qualifying customers with premium trading experience.

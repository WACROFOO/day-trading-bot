# Reflecting On My Recent Trading

## The Start of Day Trading

Hello everyone, [Ross Cameron](https://www.tirekickers.com/about-ross-cameron-of-warrior-trading/) here! I want to share with you a reflection on recent trading experiences. My journey with [day trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/) has been a rollercoaster of highs and lows, filled with vital lessons learnd along the way. It’s these experiences that shape our strategies and help us grow, both personally and professionally.

## The Power of Preparation

Today, I started my morning just like any other trading day, reviewing my charts and setting up my watchlist. Pre-market preparation is my sacred mantra. It’s the time when I analyze the market trends, potential movers, and set my trade expectations. I focus on [high-probability trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/high-probability-trading/) setups – those are my bread and butter. This particular day, a couple of stocks caught my eye, showing promise for substantial movement.

## Jumping Into Action

As the opening bell rang, I was already zoned in and ready to strike. The energy during those first few minutes is electrifying. I’m watching for patterns, volume surges, and trying to catch the momentum. Precision is key; one has to be swift yet calculated. This day was no different, I executed a few trades that I felt had the odds in my favor. It was a thrilling start, but not every decision is a winner, and that’s a reality I’ve come to accept.

## Adjusting Strategies

By midday, it was time to reassess. The morning session had provided some valuable insights. I had my fair share of wins, but also some setbacks. This is when I take a step back to analyze what’s working and what isn’t. Adjusting your strategy on the fly is a necessary skill in trading. I jotted down some mental notes on what I needed to improve and prepared for the second half of the day with a fresh mindset.

## Sealing the Day

Though I usually focus on trading the pre-market and market open, the final hour of trading can often hold a treasure trove of opportunities. As traders start to close out positions, I’m on the lookout for last-minute movements. On December 18th, this part of the day was particularly eventful. I managed to capitalize on a couple of quick trades based on the techniques I’ve honed over the years. These moments remind me why I love [day trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/) – the adrenaline rush, the satisfaction of a well-played trade, and, of course, the potential profits.

## Continuous Learning

As the markets closed, I reflected on the day’s events. Each trading day is a learning experience, and today was no exception. In trading, you’re continuously learning, tweaking your strategies, and striving for consistency. I’m passionate about sharing these experiences with you because I believe in the power of community and learning from one another. Remember, success in trading doesn’t come overnight. It’s the culmination of discipline, resilience, and an unwavering commitment to self-improvement. Let’s keep pushing forward, together.

## What are stock futures?

A [stock future](https://www.warriortrading.com/futures-definition-day-trading-terminology/) is a contractual agreement between a buyer and a seller. The contractgives the buyer an obligation to buy a stock (and the seller an obligation to sell a stock) at a specified price at a future point in time.

An investor with an existing position in a stock, can use a future contract to minimize a loss or protect unrealized gain. For example, you can hedge a short portfolio with a long position.

## What to look for in a futures plat in a platform for futures trading

- Commissions and margins: One of the most important considerations when choosing a futures brokerage is how their transaction fees and margins will affect you. Ask yourself whether the fees that a broker is charging in return for executing a trade are going to help or hurt you.

- Execution speed: Profitable futures trading requiresinvestors to look for brokerage firms that guarantee fast execution speeds to help the investors make the most of their trades.

- Trading and research tools: The trading platform that a broker is offering ought to give both beginning and expert traders the tools they need to successfully execute trades.

- Ease of use: When searching for a futures trading broker, always choose the one that has an easy understand user interface to help you minimize errors, which can hurt your trades.

- Client support: Last but not least, make sure that your broker is offering good customer support. Does the broker offer phone support? How about live chat service?

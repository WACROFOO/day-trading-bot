# Best Options Trading Platforms

In the [stock trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/) world, speculators buy stocks at a lower price in the hope of selling them at a higher price later in order to make a profit. Sadly, a buyer cannot know what the market price of a stock will be when the time comes to dumb it.

This is why many investors prefer trading options to protect themselves from losses that are likely to be caused by unfavorable changes in stock prices. In this post, you will get to know the ins and outs of options trading as well as a list of the best options trading platforms.

## What are stock options?

An [option](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/o/option.asp) is a contract that gives an investor the right (but not the obligation) to buy or sell an underlying stock at a pre-agreed price and date.

In other words, the contract gives the investor the ability to sell or buy shares at a fixed price before or on the expiration date irrespective of the market price of those shares.

There are two types of options, namely, call option and put option.

- A stock call option gives the holder the right (but not the obligation) to buy a stock. The value of a call option goes up when the price of the underlying stock price increases.

- A stock put option gives the holder the right to sell a stock. The value of a put goes down when the price of the underlying stock price falls.

## What to look for in a platform for options trading

Now that you understand what options trading is, let us take a look at some of the key things you should consider when looking for a brokerage platform for this kind of trading.

- Trading commissions: Look for a broker whose trading commissions and margin rates won’t weigh on your overall returns.

- Account minimums: Some brokerage firms require clients to deposit several thousand dollars when opening an account while others allow you to open with no minimum deposit.

- Research and trading tools: A good options trading platform ought to offer you access to a wide range of research and [trading tools](https://www.warriortrading.com/the-5-best-tools-for-day-trading/) to help investors make the most of their trades.

- Customer service: As a client, the options trading platform that a broker is offering you should have well trained customer service representatives that are willing and ready to respond to your call or emails.

## What is day trading and how does it work?

Day trading is the process of buying and selling of financial securities such as [stocks](https://www.warriortrading.com/stock-definition-day-trading-terminology/), f[utures](https://www.warriortrading.com/futures-definition-day-trading-terminology/) and [exchange traded funds](https://www.warriortrading.com/exchange-traded-funds-etf/) (ETFs) on the very same day. Ideally, the aim of a day trader is to try and take advantage of short-term, oftentimes small changes in the price of a security.

Day traders use certain trading strategies that are based on patterns, swings or historical trends in the market. Some day traders also buy securities on credit (also known as margin) through online brokers to leverage, thus allowing them to control more sizable positions realize higher gains or losses.

To day trade stocks, traders have to maintain at least $25,000 in their trading accounts on any day that they trade in order to comply with the [Pattern Day Trader (PDT) rule](https://www.warriortrading.com/pattern-day-trader-rule/). They also have to meet certain requirements when trading on margin. These rules are enacted by the government to protect investors.

## Risks of day trading

Like most financial vehicles, day trading also has its profit potential and a high risk of losing money if not approached properly.

In other words, there is really no way to know for sure what will happen with any particular stock or in the market. This makes trade management paramount when day trading.

For that reason, it is advisable to limit your loss potential by allocating only a small portion of your capital to day trading, unless you are planning to become a full-time day trader.

Always remember that successful day trading requires perseverance, self-discipline, and an incredible amount of knowledge.

## Profit potential

One of the most frequently asked questions about day trading is: “How much money can I make as a day trader?” When it comes to day trading, there is a massive range of profit potential.

Some day traders manage to make some little money from the market each month, but often have to work another job to supplement their income.

Then there are those who are able to make a comfortable living as day traders and there is the small group of people who make a lot. There are also people who try their luck in the day trading world and end up failing and losing money.

That said, profit potential in day trading is largely determined by:

- The market you trade (are you trading stocks, exchange-traded funds (ETFs), or futures?)

- The amount of money you start with

- Your trading strategies

- Market volatility

- Your level of patience and discipline

- The amount of time you spend in your trading education

## What makes the best day trading platform?

High execution speeds, low commissions, and reliability are some of the things that you need to place a premium weight on when looking for the best day trading platforms.

- Commissions

A trading commission is a fee that is charged by a brokerage as compensation for executing a transaction. Brokerages often calculate commissions either as a flat fee or a percentage of the transaction value.

As a day trader, the fees and commissions charged by online brokers should be important to you since you are likely going to execute a large number of trades per month. Luckily, online brokers have been rapidly cutting commissions to zero on some of their most popular offerings, including stocks and ETFs, thus giving traders the ability to access free trades.

- Execution speed

Day traders usually place a huge emphasis on trade execution speed as even a few seconds difference can leave them without a viable trade. Thus, it is important to choose an online trading platform that offers rapid order execution.

Indeed, many trading platforms understand that time is of the essence in the world of day trading and this why they often highlight execution speed as a crucial constituent of their features list.

- Advanced charting

Whatever your day trading strategy, you will need to test a few platforms to see which offers advanced charting and technical analysis. Advanced charting applications, custom indicators and technical analysis tools are important because they help day traders to interpret market data within short time frames.

- Stable platform

A general rule of thumb for day traders is to choose an incredibly stable and reliable platform for high-quality trade execution. So, ensure to pick a platform that isn’t going to become unstable when there is a surge in trading activity.

- Hotkeys available

Being able to click one key and execute an order is very important for both beginner and professional day traders. Creating your own hot keys that you will remember and execute on demand will help you on your day trading journey.

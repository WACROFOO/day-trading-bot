# Top 3 Cryptocurrency Exchanges

The most popular [cryptocurrency ](https://www.warriortrading.com/cyptocurrency-definition-day-trading-terminology/)– [Bitcoin ](https://www.warriortrading.com/bitcoin-everything-you-need-to-know/)– was introduced to the world by Satoshi Nakamoto in 2009. Back then, there was a lot of mystery surrounding cryptocurrencies. Many people feared to venture in cryptocurrency trading for several reasons some of which include: fear of losing cryptocurrencies to cyber attacks, fear of crackdown on cryptocurrency developers and other security concerns.

In 2009, all the above reasons were valid but thanks to the improvement in technology, cryptocurrencies have risen in terms of popularity and price/value. At the time of writing, Bitcoin was retailing at $4650.71 a slight drop from the $5,000 mark it had attained.

You may be wondering why cryptocurrencies have become popular. Well, their popularity is attributed to lower transaction fees, flexibility, improved security and acceptance by traders, investors and the general public around the world.

As a result, several cryptocurrency exchanges that provide tools for traders and investors to trade in cryptocurrency have been developed.

## Coinbase

[Coinbase](https://www.warriortrading.com/coinbase-broker-review/) started as a digital currency wallet provider that allowed consumers and merchants to open digital wallets in order to begin trading and transacting with cryptocurrencies. [Coinbase ](https://www.warriortrading.com/coinbase-broker-review/)was founded in 2012, three years after Bitcoin was developed. Based in San Francisco, it provides trading tools that allow the trading of different cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin, Ethereum and Litecoin among others.

Thanks to the popularity of cryptocurrencies, the company has been able to attain a market cap of $10 B having raised $117 M from top investors around the world for example Andreessen Horowitz, USV and DFJ Growth among others. Today, [Coinbase](https://www.warriortrading.com/coinbase-broker-review/) boasts of 9,900,000 users – 33,100,000 wallets, 46,000 merchants and 10,000 developer apps. (2017)

The company developed GDAX, a platform that offers traders and investors a chance to trade in cryptocurrencies. As a fully regulated US based exchange, GDAX provides trading tools such as charts, limit, stop and the ability to place orders. The chart window has an intuitive interface which is familiar with most traders. Just like other exchanges, users can modify a few attributes such as candlestick.

To access the advanced features, you have to create an account. Users are required to provide their full names, email and password. Before submitting the details, one has to certify that they are 18 years and above plus they must accept Coinbase user agreement and privacy policy.

## Kraken

The San Francisco based company is one of the largest Bitcoin exchanges in the world in terms of euro [volume ](https://www.warriortrading.com/volume-definition-day-trading-terminology/)as well as liquidity. [Kraken ](https://www.warriortrading.com/kraken-bitcoin-exchange-review/)enables traders and investors to buy and sell cryptocurrencies in Canadian dollars, US dollars, British pounds and Japanese yen. The company has been around for more than 5 years and during this period, it has been rated as the best and most secure Bitcoin exchange.

Kraken is the first Bitcoin exchange to have the trading volume and price displayed on the Bloomberg Terminal. This ensures that the platform gets a first pass to cryptographically verifiable proof of reserve audit. Furthermore, it is a partner to the first cryptocurrency bank. Today, it is trusted by many traders and as a result, it has been selected to be Tokyo’s government court appointed trustee.

When it comes to trading cryptocurrencies, Kraken provides charting tools that enable traders and investors to trade. For starters, the charting tools are intuitive. They help to provide relevant information like bids/asks and spread. Furthermore, you can watch depth which displays both cumulative volume and volume of trades.

An order book is available together with other chart tools. It displays the buying and selling transactions with real time volume and price. That’s not all, you can also check out the recent trades section and learn at what time an order was made, how much was transacted, current price and the order type.

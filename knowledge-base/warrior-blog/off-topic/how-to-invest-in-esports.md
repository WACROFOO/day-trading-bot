## What is eSports?

This is a form of competitive video gaming at the professional level where competitors from different teams face off in a tournament. Short for electronic sports, gamers play popular games such as Counter-Strike, Call of Duty, Madden NFL, and Fortnite among others.

These gamers are usually watched on popular streaming services such as YouTube and Twitch. Millions of fans from around the world watch their favorite teams or gamers compete in real-time.

In 2018, [380 million people watched eSports](https://resources.newzoo.com/hubfs/Reports/Newzoo_2018_Global_Esports_Market_Report_Excerpt.pdf?submissionGuid=b655872f-663d-4b81-80a4-5fb99aef5f2b) according to a report by Newzoo. That includes 165 million eSports enthusiasts. Majority of these viewers watch from China, South Korea, and North America.

These tournaments can attract more viewers than traditional professional sports such as the NFL and NHL. In 2017, the League of Legends World Championship attracted more than 80 million viewers. In the same year, there were 588 major eSports events.

## How Did eSports Start?

The first competitive tournaments in video games started in the 1970s and 1980s. Back then, world top gamers competed for high scores in classic games such as Donkey Kong and Pac-Man. With the help of the internet in the 1990s and the release of advanced games, eSports started to gain popularity.

This helped to pave way for gaming tournaments for games such as Counter-Strike, Warcraft, and Quake. Global popularity reached the 2000s with tournaments for games such as Warcraft III and others. Between 2005 and 2008, ESPN hosted Madden Nation, an NFL competition which gave North America the taste for eSports.

## Making Money in eSports

In traditional sports, a sports team has a venue (in most cases) and they earn revenue from ticket sales. As is the norm, traditional sports clubs charge a premium for halfway line and courtside tickets. When it comes to eSports, things are different. Teams don’t have stadiums but there are gaming houses around the world.

In eSports, 95% of the money generated comes from sponsorship deals. Apart from sponsorship deals, players and teams make money from prizes, regular salaries, and streaming. For instance, a Twitch streamer with 2,000 subscribers can earn $5,000 every month. Streamers also have the opportunity of monetizing their streams with ads. It’s no secret that eSports teams are earning millions of dollars every year. In fact, the average salary for eSports is around $60,000. In 2017, the top player earned a prize of $2.436 million

Today, gaming publishers are looking into broadcasting rights. As an interesting aspect, it is something that will help eSports teams to build relationships with tournament organizers and game developers.

### Step One – Open a Brokerage Account

A brokerage account is an investment account opened with an online broker and allows you to trade stocks and other financial instruments. As an investor’s connection to the stock market, most online brokerage firms allow you to open an account from the comfort of your home.

The process takes a few minutes and after the initial deposit, you can begin trading stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, CDs, bonds, forex, and options. The brokerage firm acts as an intermediary between you and the investments you want to buy.

To trade, most online brokerage firms allow you to fill an order ticket. What you need to know is that there is no limit on the number of brokerage accounts an investor can have. Also, there is no limit to the amount of money deposited in a brokerage account.

Before opening a brokerage account, there is one hurdle you have to jump over – choosing the right brokerage firm that meets your needs. Factors to consider when choosing a brokerage firm include:

- Cost and fees

- Good customer services

- High payout

- Deposit and withdrawal options

- Smart trading platform

- Introductory bonuses

- Low-cost commissions

- Education tools

- Stock trading research

- Investment guidance

Online brokerage firms offer cash and margin accounts. As a beginner, we recommend that you stick with a cash account at first.

# Triple Bottom Definition: Day Trading Terminology

The triple bottom pattern a bullish [reversal](/reversal-definition-day-trading-terminology/) pattern used to predict the bottoming of [stock](/stock-definition-day-trading-terminology/) that has been in a [downtrend](https://www.warriortrading.com/market-trend-definition-day-trading-terminology/). The pattern can be found on [bar charts](/bar-chart-definition-day-trading-terminology/), [line charts](/line-chart-definition-day-trading-terminology/) and [candlestick patterns](/candlestick-day-trading-terminology/) with a distinct bounce off a [support](https://www.warriortrading.com/support-level-definition-day-trading-terminology/) level three times.

Looking at the pattern, it is correct to deduce that the third bounce signifies demand for the [stock](/stock-definition-day-trading-terminology/) at this level and since it was able to hold it three times it increases the chances that prices will turn around and head higher.

This typically means there is a large buyer at those prices which is what is holding up the stock.

## Triple Bottom Components

a. Three troughs

Also referred to as the three lows, they are equally spaced and help to mark vital points on the pattern. It is important to understand that the lows don’t have to be equal but they need to be in the same range.

b. Volume

[Volume ](https://www.warriortrading.com/volume-definition-day-trading-terminology/)usually declines when the pattern develops. There are times when it will increase especially close to the lows. Once the third low is achieved, volume will experience an expansion at the [resistance ](https://www.warriortrading.com/resistance-level-definition-day-trading-terminology/)breakout.

c. Price target

As a trader, the distance measured from the lows to the resistance breakout can be added to the resistance break. If the pattern has a longer development, it will result in the ultimate breakout.

d. Resistance break

This is the highest point on the pattern.

e. Broken resistance

Broken resistance = potential support.

## Understanding the Triple Bottom

As said earlier, the Triple Bottom is composed of three lows. When the first low is formed, the price of the [security](https://www.warriortrading.com/security-definition-day-trading-terminology/) will reach peak and begin to move towards the previous support level. When this happens, buyers will enter the trade finally pushing the price of the security much higher. As a result, the second bottom and second peak will be created.

As time goes by, the final bounce will create the third bottom meaning traders will be ready to enter the long position. This happens only when the price of the security forms the third bottom usually above the previous resistance.

## Trading with Triple Bottom

By now, you already know that the Triple Bottom is a bullish reversal pattern which illustrates that a particular [asset](https://www.warriortrading.com/asset-definition-day-trading-terminology/) is trading in a [downtrend](/market-trend-definition-day-trading-terminology/) and has found a major support level.

When the downtrend hits the third low, it will result in an uptrend followed by more pressure on the traders to sell the security. As a result, the price will return to the previous low. Due to this, buyers will begin to move back into the asset resulting in the price experiencing an uptrend.

If the situation above is repeated for a third time and the price falls to a new low, then the pattern can be said to be complete once it attains a higher position than the resistance level.

If the trader has confirmed the Triple Bottom pattern, he or she is supposed to set the trade trigger at the resistance line. The trader can measure the potential breakout target which presents the opportunity of opening the long position.

At this stage, traders have the chance of purchasing the [underlying security](/underlying-asset-definition-day-trading-terminology/) thus benefiting from the downtrend. As the pattern ends, it will experience a price high which will result in more selling pressure among the traders.

Selling the security at this point will allow short term traders to profit.

## Final Verdict

Triple Bottom is a reliable [technical analysis](https://www.warriortrading.com/technical-analysis-definition-day-trading-terminology/) tool that not only indicates the reversal of the downtrend but also indicates the potential for a new trend direction. It helps to signal a shift in supply and demand of an asset and when timed properly creates a great risk/reward trade.

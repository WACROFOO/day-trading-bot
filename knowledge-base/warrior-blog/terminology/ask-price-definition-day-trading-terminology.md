# Ask Price Definition: Day Trading Terminology

The ask price (also known as the offer) represents the lowest price a seller is willing to sell their shares for. The [bid price](https://www.warriortrading.com/bid-price-definition/) is what represents all the buyers.

In the above [Level 2](https://www.warriortrading.com/level-2-definition-day-trading-terminology/) of the QQQ’s you will notice that the ask is on the right hand side and is currently sitting at $118.59 with quite a bit of orders sitting there. The top order has a size of 73 which means that someone is willing to sell 7,300 shares (73 x 100) at the price of $118.59.

[](https://media.warriortrading.com/2016/12/Ask-Price.png)

The ask will always be higher in the bid unless there is a [crossed market](https://www.warriortrading.com/crossed-market-definition/), and the difference between the bid and ask is known as the [spread](/spread-definition-day-trading-terminology/). You will also see on the Level 2 what exchange the different orders are being held at under the “MM” column ([Market Maker](https://www.warriortrading.com/market-maker-definition-day-trading-terminology/)), with most orders being routed through PHIL, BATS and EDGX, but that could be different for each security.

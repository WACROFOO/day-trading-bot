# Keltner Channel Definition: Day Trading Terminology

The Keltner Channel is a [technical indicator](https://www.warriortrading.com/technical-indicator-definition-day-trading-terminology/) that shows a central line for a [moving average](https://www.warriortrading.com/exponential-moving-average/) and [channel lines](/channel-pattern-definition-day-trading-terminology/) below and above. This indicator is named for Chester Keltner, who described the indicator in a 1960s book called How To Make Money in Commodities.

While the name came from others who heard about the indicator from Keltner, Keltner himself called it a 10 day moving average rule, and never claimed any original ownership of the idea.

## Trading With the Keltner Indicator

The central line for the indicator is simply a 10 day moving “typical average”, which is calculated by taking the average of each day’s high, low and closing price. The lines below and above are at a distance from this central line, the distance being the [simple moving averages](https://www.warriortrading.com/simple-moving-average/) of the trading ranges for the last 10 days.

Traders using this indicator consider closes that are above a higher line as a signal for strong bullishness and closes below a lower line as strongly bearish.

## Origin and Expansion

The idea’s origin is uncertain. Keltner worked as a Chicago grain trader, and many consider it to have been a common indicator back then. However, the idea of fixed width channels goes back to early days of charting, so the addition of averaging does not indicate a great leap in conventional chartist thinking.

Later chartists have suggested modifications and additions to the Keltner indicator, such as alternative averaging periods, exponential moving averages or the use of a multiple of Wilder’s average true range for constructing the bands.

These variations are still referred to as Keltner Channels, which creates some confusion about what the “true” indicator is.

## Final Thoughts

The Keltner indicator is a useful addition to a day trader’s toolkit for confirming an existing theory on a [security’s](https://www.warriortrading.com/security-definition-day-trading-terminology/) medium term [trend](https://www.warriortrading.com/market-trend-definition-day-trading-terminology/). The Keltner indicator should be used alongside other similar indicators to help identify the strongest price trends in potential trades.

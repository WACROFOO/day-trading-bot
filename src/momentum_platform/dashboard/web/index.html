How to read the desk

Feed

LIVE bars arriving. STALE no bar for 20 s (60 s extended) — the stream, not the stock. DELAYED refused. OFFLINE reconnecting. REPLAY a recorded session. FROZEN a list that stops at 09:30 by design.

Sessions

PM 04:00–09:30 ET. RTH 09:30–16:00 ET. AH 16:00–20:00 ET.

Verdict (server cascade)

REJECT a kill rule fired; no plan exists for the name. WAIT Layer 1 passed, a chart gate is red. WATCH a chart gate could not be computed yet. REVIEW every gate green — read the chart, tape and book yourself. STALE the feed is behind; no verdict. LOG outside the session window. Never an instruction to buy.

PASS · FAIL · UNKNOWN

Per gate: meets the threshold · does not · no value to judge, never counted as a pass.

Float

proxy = SEC shares outstanding, an upper bound: under 20M proves float is under; over 20M proves nothing. verified = a stated figure or one you typed.

Flames

News age only: red ≤2 h, orange ≤12 h, yellow ≤24 h. Recency, never quality.

Scanner branches

Labels on an alert, not the filter: LF/MF float band, HR/MR RVOL band, <20 / 20+ price band, 5-in-5 / 10-in-10 squeeze.

Plan

Entry over the trigger, stop under the pullback low, target at 2R. Sized only from the dollar risk you type.

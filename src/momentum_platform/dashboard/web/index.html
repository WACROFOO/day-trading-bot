How to read the desk

Feed states (header and tiles)

LIVE real-time bars are arriving. STALE no bar for 20 s in regular hours (60 s extended): the stream is dead, not the stock. DELAYED the provider sent delayed data; the desk refuses it. OFFLINE disconnected; reconnecting. REPLAY a recorded session, not today. FROZEN a list that stops updating at 09:30 by design (Top Gappers).

Session pills on alerts

PM premarket 04:00–09:30 ET: thin tape, wide spreads, a gap forming. RTH regular hours 09:30–16:00 ET: the session the course strategy is built for. AH after hours 16:00–20:00 ET: thin tape, news reactions.

Five pillars (Confirmed course)

Price $2–20. Gain ≥10% on the day. Daily RVOL ≥5× today's volume against the 20-day average. Float <20M shares — a column, never a gate; proxy means SEC shares outstanding (an upper bound), verified means a stated float figure or one you typed. News a headline in the last 24h — a column, never a gate. The desk admits a wider price band (yours) so a name just outside the pillar is seen with its price cell FAIL.

PASS · FAIL · UNKNOWN

PASS the value meets the threshold. FAIL it does not. UNKNOWN there is no value to judge (no float source, no RVOL baseline, no headline seen) — it is never counted as a pass.

Flames (news recency only)

Red ≤2 h old. Orange ≤12 h. Yellow ≤24 h. None: no qualifying headline. A flame says how recent the news is, never how good it is; the catalyst grade (hard, soft, dilutive) is a labelled heuristic on the headline text.

Scanner branches

run up up ≥3% over 10 minutes with a fresh high in the last 3, above the 10-minute VWAP, liquid. LF/MF low / medium float. HR/MR high / medium RVOL. <20 / 20+ price band. 5-in-5, 10-in-10 squeeze branches (up 5% in 5 min, 10% in 10). Branches label an alert; they are not the filter.

Setup verdict

GO every pillar and a live momentum event agree and a plan is armed. WAIT one or two conditions short — watch, do not enter. PASS reject: three or more short. Entry / Stop / Target come from the first pullback: entry over the trigger, stop under the pullback low, target at 2R. Sizing uses only the dollar risk you type; the app never assumes one.

Charts

1-minute, 5-minute and 10-second panes are IBKR real-time data with extended hours shaded, VWAP and the 9, 20 and 200 EMAs drawn; a 10-second candle exists only when both 5-second halves arrived, and empty buckets stay empty. TradingView panes (in the tray) run on TradingView's data under their rules: US stocks are 15 minutes delayed to a viewer not signed in to tradingview.com (their D badge); sign in there in this browser for their real-time feed. 09:30 and 16:00 are marked on every pane.

Clean-room approximations of the course scanners, for simulation and study. Nothing on this desk can place an order.

1. **It was not the plan's trade.** The plan said buy the break of 8.31. The
   runner sends a plain limit at the trigger; IBKR's warning 2161 says it
   capped that limit to 7.87 because the market was far below it, and the

   ledger assumes a fill when the tape touches the trigger; `missed` scores
   this plan at the trigger as −1.00 R (fixed target) and −0.59 R (trail).
   The live trade and the simulated one share a decision id and little
   else. §5 proposes the instrument that makes them the same trade.
2. **The broker's stop did not execute.** `stuck` at 10:20 ET: the stop
   rested at 7.34, `PreSubmitted`. `missed` shows the plans the detector
   armed on WHLR while the position was held: 09:58 with a stop at 7.05,

   stops sit on are the tape: WHLR printed 7.05 at about 09:58 and 6.99 at
   about 10:28, both under a stop of 7.34, and the stop did not fill for
   roughly fifty minutes. At 10:15 the runner's trail move was answered

## 4. Running Up on WHLR, measured — and my explanation corrected

`alerts.py --why WHLR --from 09:20 --to 09:45` replays the scanner over the
desk's own minute bars and prints each condition per minute. Two causes,
neither the one I gave this morning:

- **Rule 6, "right now".** On every climbing minute from 09:23 to 09:45
  the ONLY ✗ is `at_window_high`: the close sat 0.6 % to 2.5 % under the
  bar's own wick (09:33 5.82 vs 5.87 needed; 09:39 6.71 vs 6.79; 09:42 7.87
  vs 8.00) and the 0.5 % tolerance called each one a pullback. On a stock
  moving twenty cents a minute the wick is the rule's blind spot. 3.2.0:
  the bar that prints the window high, closing green, is "right now"; a red
  bar under an earlier high is not. VEEE's 18.20 red bar still fails.
- **Rule 8, once per leg, unbounded in time.** The live desk's own events
  show `running_up` fired on WHLR at 09:13 at 6.79. By 09:20 the stock was
  4.74; at 09:22 every condition reads ✓ and nothing fired, because a
  repeat needed a print above 6.79 × 1.01. A run that starts 30 % below
  the last alert is a different run. 3.2.0: an alert older than the window
  no longer gates.
- **What I said this morning** — that Running Up was silent because WHLR
  was at its high of day — was wrong. The owner's own log shows WHLR at
  7.08 at 04:37; at 5.5 to 6.7 it was well below. The rule I removed
  (3.1.0) was not the cause; it stays removed on the owner's instruction
  and is not the fix. The fix is the two measured rules above.

## 5. The entry instrument — Amendment A10, IN FORCE from tomorrow

The executor's entry is a plain limit at the plan's trigger. When the tape
is below the trigger at the moment of sending, that limit is marketable and
fills at once, well under the trigger, at the ask or at IBKR's cap. The
09-22 fills were at the trigger only because the tape was there at the
time. Every simulation, every `missed` row and every control assumes the
fill at the trigger touch.

Proposed A10: the parent becomes a **buy STOP-LIMIT** resting at the
trigger — stop price = trigger, limit = trigger + a small offset — so it
fills only when the tape reaches the plan's entry and never far above it;
DAY; cancelled by the runner if untriggered after N completed minute bars
(three proposed), the row recorded NOT_FILLED. The resting entry counts as
alive for the one-position rule, as it does now. The 09-22 delegation
covered four named decisions and nothing else, so this went back to the
owner, whose answer the same afternoon was *"fix it once at all"*. Built
that evening: `StopLimitOrder` parent, limit = trigger + 0.3 % (one-cent
floor), `Runner.expire_entries` after three untriggered minutes, decision
NOT_FILLED. From tomorrow a fill is at or within the offset of the plan's
trigger, or there is no trade — the assumption every simulation already
makes. Its first cost will show as NOT_FILLED rows in `missed`.

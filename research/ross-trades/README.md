# ross-trades/ — his trades, rebuilt on the tape

Every trade Ross Cameron describes in the video recaps, live streams and blog
recaps, one row per trade with a verbatim quote, dated by the text or by the
tape, rebuilt on Alpaca SIP bars, the bot run beside him on the same
symbol-days. Code: `scripts/edge_hunt/ross.py`, `pit.py`, `ross_analysis.py`.
Report: `REPORT.md`. Tables (CSV, regenerable) are gitignored except
`results/`.

## 1. Each study folder: the question, rules, sample, cost model and verdict, with the headline net-R numbers

### Takeaway
The repo's own strategy (the mechanical Ross Cameron small-cap first-pullback bot) is **not profitable**. Every
study reaches the same answer by a different method: the entry is about zero gross, costs are 0.39 to 1.13 R a
trade depending on sizing and stop floor, and **no configuration in any study has ever met its adoption rule**.
The best harm-reduced live rules still lose −0.430 R (train 2016-2023) and −0.502 R (test 2024-2026) a trade over ten years.

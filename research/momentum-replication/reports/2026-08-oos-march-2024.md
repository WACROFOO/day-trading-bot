# Out of sample: March 2024 — v2 fails

`2026-08-strategy-v2.md` ended with "run it forward on data that is out of
sample for every number above." March 2024 is out of sample by 2.3 years.

**It fails.** This records how, and what survives.

## Method

2,120 of the 2,486 pool symbols have March-2024 daily data (366 listed later).
Universe built from daily bars exactly as the gates specify: **open $2–20,
gap ≥10% versus the prior close, no splits**. Result: **187 qualifying
symbol-days across 20 sessions, 152 symbols.**

Yahoo serves 1-minute bars for the last 30 days only, so the intrabar sequence
is unavailable for 2024. Every bracket is therefore reported as a **bound**:
pessimistic assumes the stop filled first when both levels traded, optimistic
assumes the target did. The truth lies between; only 10% of trades hit both.

## The premise replicates. The rule does not.

| | March 2024 | July 2026 (train) |
|---|---|---|
| open → high, mean | **+13.93%** | +16.00% |
| open → low, mean | **−12.30%** | −10.88% |
| open → close, mean | **−1.86%** | +0.24% |
| closed green | **29%** | 40% |

The structural claim behind the whole strategy — *a large favourable excursion
exists and selling at the close throws it away* — holds in both periods.

## But the bracket has no edge in 2024

Mean % per trade, stop × target:

| assumption | positive cells | best cell | baseline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **pessimistic (stop first)** | **0 / 49** | −2.00% | −1.86% |
| optimistic (target first) | 3 / 49 | +0.22% | −1.86% |
| **July 2026, for comparison** | **36 / 36** | **+2.36%** | −1.34% |

Zero of forty-nine under the honest assumption. The three positive cells under
the optimistic bound peak at +0.22%, which is noise, and that bound is not
achievable anyway.

The **v2 rule specifically** (−15% / +8%): **−4.19%** pessimistic, **−1.85%**
optimistic, against a −1.86% sell-at-close baseline. It adds nothing even at
the optimistic bound.

## Why — and it is not the exit's fault

| | median MFE | median MAE | **MFE / \|MAE\|** | closed green |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| July 2026 | +6.06% | −8.34% | **0.73** | 40% |
| March 2024 | +4.14% | −10.64% | **0.39** | 29% |

**The March 2024 gapper population offered roughly half the reward per unit of
risk.** A bracket can only harvest an excursion that exists; in 2024 the
typical name gave less upside and more downside before it did.

The sign of the parameter gradient flips too. In July 2026 **wider stops were
better** (−15% beat −6%); in March 2024 the least-bad stop is the **tightest**
(−6%). That is not a rule degrading, it is a rule inverting.

## What survives, and what does not

**Survives:**

1. **Nothing predicts open→close.** gap vs close: +0.079 (2024), −0.086 (2026).
   Both indistinguishable from zero. The finding that the pillar score has
   negative information value is not a July artefact.
2. **The favourable excursion is real and large** in both periods.
3. Gap vs open→high is weakly positive and stable: +0.166 (2024), +0.183 (2026).

**Does not survive:**

1. **The bracket parameters.** −15%/+8% was fitted to July 2026's median MFE of
   +6.06%. March 2024's is +4.14%, so the target is above the median and gets
   hit less while the stop gets hit more. This was flagged as a risk in the v2
   report and it is exactly what broke.
2. **Gap predicts drawdown.** r = −0.543 in July 2026, **−0.164** in March
   2024. Not a stable relationship.

## The real conclusion

**v2 is a bet on the regime, not an edge in a rule.** The same gates, the same
equal weighting and the same exit produce +2.18% per trade in one period and
−4.19% in another, because the underlying population changed from MFE/|MAE| of
0.73 to 0.39.

That reframes what to build next. Rather than tuning stop and target, the
useful question is whether the regime is measurable **in advance**: compute the
trailing MFE/|MAE| ratio of the qualifying population over the last N sessions
and only deploy when it is above some level. That is testable on exactly this
data and it is the next thing to run.

## Caveats

- The 2024 universe is built from **today's** pool, so anything delisted
  between 2024 and 2026 is missing. That is survivorship bias in the *optimistic*
  direction — and it still failed.
- Gaps are computed from daily bars (open vs prior close), not from a live
  pre-market scan, so the universe is close to but not identical to what a
  scanner would have shown at 09:25.
- 187 symbol-days over 20 sessions. Enough to reject, not enough to characterise.

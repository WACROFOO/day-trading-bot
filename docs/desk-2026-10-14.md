## 4 · Trend lines — the rule

| | |
|---|---|
| swings | confirmed pivots, 5 bars each side — the swings the S/R fan reads; a swing is known 5 bars after it printed |
| scope | today's swings only (`ross-fp-v4.pine` V9.9: never an overnight print) |
| a down-line | an older swing high to a **lower** newer one — the breakout line |
| an up-line | an older swing low to a **higher** newer one |
| possible only if | no candle **closes** through it between its two swings, and no swing of its kind pierces it by more than 0.2 ATR there (`tolTL`, the Pine's touch tolerance); wicks may poke through |
| broken | by the first candle **closing** through it after its second swing: **`downtrend BROKEN ▲`**, **`uptrend LOST ▼`**; live price through an unbroken line reads **`BREAKING`** |
| which one | newest swing first, each with its newest possible partner first, over the last 10 swings of each kind; the first unbroken line is drawn, and a line broken in the last 20 bars stays drawn to its break, dashed |
| drawn | from its first swing to 8 bars past the newest bar, the two swings dotted |
| stable | it moves only when a swing confirms or a candle closes through it — never on a tick (`test_a_line_moves_only_when_a_swing_confirms_or_a_close_breaks_it`) |

## 6 · High of Day — how it is judged

What fires it (`src/momentum_platform/scanners/momentum_events.py`, `HodMomentumScanner`; every number the
code's own approximation, said so in its docstring):

```
✓ ① new high      the session high above the previous one by ≥ 0.25 % — the first sight of a name only seeds it
✓ ② gain          ≥ +10 % on the day
✓ ③ recent RVOL   5-minute RVOL (the day's when there is none) ≥ 3× — or, in thin tape, three pillars stand in
✓ ④ liquidity     ≥ 25,000 shares in 5 minutes, or three of the five pillars
✓ ⑤ price         ≥ $1
  label           float band (LF < 20M · MF < 100M · ?F unknown; a known float over 100M fires nothing),
                  RVOL band (HR ≥ 5×), price band (< 20 · 20+)
```

What the corpus says it is (`knowledge-base/strategies/SCANNERS.md` §B1): *"a high of day scanner is only giving
you an alert when a stock makes a new high"* (w97KlUrVDk0 [01:00:41]); *"stocks that are hitting a new intra-day
high on high relative volume"* (blog `how-to-use-stock-scanners.md`) — `EVENT = new intraday high AND high
relative volume`, CONFIRMED. And: *"a stock hitting scanners more and more times is definitely a good indicator
that it's got some momentum"* (yg5E_mqGFGg [00:17:02]).

What the recording showed at 06:12: two rows — NCPL at 04:36 and GLND at 04:01, 96 and 131 minutes old — and
neither AEHL (+163 %, its 1-minute chart's high 29.76 printed after 04:00) nor GRML (+24 %, high 7.43 on its
chart around 05:07).

**Judgement.** The event is the corpus's own: a new high on volume. Three things make the card read as a log
rather than a scanner:

1. **It lists past alerts.** One row per name, its newest alert; at 06:12 the newest was 96 minutes old and
   nothing on the row said whether the name was still near its high.
2. **It sees eight names.** The desk subscribes `maxSymbols` 8 and rescans every 120 s
   (`config/desk-profile.json`); a name outside the eight cannot alert at all.
3. **One rule to measure, not to guess.** With heavy 5-minute volume the pillars cannot stand in for RVOL, so a
   5-minute RVOL under 3× blocks the alert. AEHL's RVOL tile read 1.6×. Whether that, or something else, kept
   AEHL and GRML off the card is not established: the desk's alert log is not in the daily export.

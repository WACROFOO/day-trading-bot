## 2 · What changed

```
① ONE PRICE PER NAME   a quote is split at its source (sessions.quote_parts): a trade printed inside the desk's
                       session (04:00–20:00 ET) and not on the OVERNIGHT venue is the name's print; a trade after it,
                       on that venue, or in the eight hours before it is the night's; the book follows the clock.
                       The night travels apart (`overnight`) — the header shows it as "overnight 2.17 +181%", never
                       as the price. The card's PRICE tile is the price the gates judged, the same snapshot as GAIN.
                       The trade's own time is IBKR's tick 45 (`lastTimestamp`), its venue `lastExchange`
② CLOSED               after the session, the data chip reads "closed · Fri 19:59" (grey), the scanner tiles CLOSED,
                       the phase badge the desk's NYSE calendar (weekends, holidays), the header no print age, and
                       an empty scanner card "the session is over" instead of "the tape has not advanced in 7h"
③ THE NEWS TILE        prints the news line's word: STRONG / SOME / WEAK / NONE
④ THE TAPE             a new state, CLOSED: "the session is over — prints resume at 04:00 ET"; a print that does
                       arrive still shows and makes it LIVE; at 04:00 it waits for the first print again
⑤ THE CHART'S WORDS    S/R, POC and trend words drawn last, each on a dark plate, none on another — a word with no
                       room is skipped; a chart card's name never shrinks, its line key does first
```

Nothing here moves a gate, the window, the sizing or what the bot reads: the cascade always judged the session's
bars. What changes is which price the screen calls the name's.

## 4 · What this could not check

- **IBKR's overnight fields on a real ticker.** `lastTimestamp` and `lastExchange` are read as ib_async 2.1.0
  defines them; whether IBKR fills `lastExchange` with "OVERNIGHT" for those trades was not seen here. Without
  it the trade's time decides, which places the screenshot's 2.17 in the night either way.
- **Whether IBKR's tick-by-tick carries overnight trades.** The screenshot says it did not reach the tape; the
  tape now says the session is over, and shows a print if one comes.
- **INO's news.** A +181 % move after Friday's close with no company news found by the desk's sources: check the
  wires by hand.
- Holidays come from `holidays.py`, which lists 2026.

## 5 · Verdict

Every inconsistency in the screenshot came from one cause or a neighbour of it: the desk treated IBKR's overnight
quote as the name's price and its live connection as live data. The name now has one price, the session's; the night
is shown as the night; a closed session says CLOSED. Not seen yet on a live overnight desk. Paper only.

Defaults, per pane (each one a click in ƒ, remembered per pane):

```
① 1-MINUTE   execution · today's gate lines + MACD pane + zones, prior day, $ levels · profile off
② 5-MINUTE   structure · zones, prior day, $ levels + the visible-range volume profile
③ 10-SECOND  micro · unchanged: its 30-minute window, no older sessions, no levels
④ DAILY      room · older years on drag
```

**What none of it may decide.** The cascade, the plans and the cards are built on the server
from today's bars and never see an older session or a level: a test fetches three past sessions
through the page's own endpoint and checks the session's bars and the desk's minutes are
unchanged (`test_the_desk_serves_older_candles_and_no_gate_sees_them`). On the chart every
indicator is computed per session from 04:00 ET, as the gates compute today's, so the line drawn
on today's bars is the gate's own (`test_the_previous_session_is_on_the_chart_and_the_gate_vwap_is_unchanged`);
the lines break where a session starts.

## 4 · The levels — where each comes from

| level | what it is | origin | label |
|---|---|---|---|
| whole / half dollar | the nearest one above and below the price | FILTERS.md: "position 10–25¢ *below* a whole dollar or the pre-market high and add through it"; `at_support` lists "whole/half dollar" | the rule is the project's; drawing the nearest two is this desk's choice |
| prior day high / low / close | the last completed daily bar (regular session) | FILTERS.md `at_support`: "daily level" | Approximation — which daily levels count is not stated |
| support / resistance zone | swing highs and lows on the 5-minute bars of every loaded session, five bars each side; swings within the larger of 0.3 % of price and half the median bar range join one zone; two swings make a zone; the three nearest each side of the price, within 30 % of it | FILTERS.md `at_support` names "flipped resistance"; PARAMETERS.md §3: `level_tolerance` "**unstated** — sweep 0.1–0.5% of price" | Approximation — the swing width, the tolerance and the 30 % are this desk's picks, untested |
| `vol 2.1×` on a zone | the volume traded inside the band per dollar of price, against the average over every loaded session's range (a 120-row profile of the one-minute bars): above 1 a high-volume node, below 1 a thin area price moved through | — | Approximation |
| volume profile | volume by price over the bars in view: 24 rows, up versus down, value area 70 %, point of control in red, on the 5-minute | the corpus's volume-profile class: "I'm going to choose a visible range" (wC2Bbf6OMFQ [00:05:37]); "value area volume is 70 row size 24 volume up versus down … these are all the standard settings" ([00:06:02]–[00:06:10]); "my point of control by default in red" ([00:06:14]); "because I keep my five minute chart zoomed out I have it on my f[ive] minute time frame" ([00:07:36]–[00:07:41]) | Observed — taught in one class; built from one-minute bars, each bar's volume spread evenly over its range: an Approximation of what the prints would show |

**A register finding.** "Volume profile" has 31 hits in the stream register, and there it is
the shape of the volume bars over time, not volume by price: *"the volume profile though has been
really solid light volume pullback"* (6TrXKekFeS4 [00:04:06]–[00:04:07]); *"nice volume profile
increasing volume into that squeeze"* (67gb19mq3xg [00:09:01]–[00:09:03]). The volume-by-price indicator is the
teaching class above. Neither is in FILTERS.md: the profile is a reading aid on this desk, never
a gate.

## 5 · Older candles — how they are fetched

IBKR's page (https://interactivebrokers.github.io/tws-api/historical_limitations.html, read
2026-10-13): the hard pacing limits — no identical request within 15 seconds, no six for one
contract within two seconds, no more than 60 in ten minutes — are stated for bars of 30 seconds
or less; "Historical Data Limitations for barSize = "1 mins" and greater have been lifted", with
a "soft" slow and the warning that "requesting too much historical data can lead to throttling
and eventual disconnect of the API client"; requests should return "only a few thousand bars at
a time"; its table pairs "1 D" with "1 min - 1 day".

| | |
|---|---|
| client | its own read-only client, **30** (`IBKR_HISTORY_CLIENT_ID`), on its own thread — never the decision worker |
| a request | one session: "1 D" of one-minute TRADES ending 20:00 ET, extended hours; bars outside 04:00–20:00 ET dropped |
| pace | ≥ 2 s apart, ≤ 30 in any ten minutes — with the desk's own refresh (one every 20 s on client 27) the two stay within 60 |
| cache | every completed session on disk, one file a symbol and day in the desk's git-ignored data cache: a past day is asked for once |
| splits | a cached session whose regular-hours close is more than 20 % from the desk's fresh daily close is asked for again, once a run |
| days skipped | weekends, the 2026 NYSE holidays, and days missing from the name's daily bars; a timeout is never cached as an empty day; ten empty weekdays, or the start of a name's daily bars inside the year, end the walk |
| daily | five years before the oldest daily bar on the pane, one request |
| the page | `GET /api/v1/chart/history?symbol=SYM&tf=1m&before=YYYY-MM-DD&sessions=N` — only a name on the desk; a replay answers that it has no older candles |

## 6 · What this could not check

- **Nothing here has run against TWS.** This container has no IBKR. On the first live desk the
  log should say `IBKR chart history connected read-only, client 30`; if another program holds
  client 30, set `IBKR_HISTORY_CLIENT_ID` in `.env`.
- **Whether price turns at these levels is not measured.** They describe where trading
  happened. "From where the bounce would happen" is a hypothesis to test — on the five-year bar
  cache it is measurable: how often a pullback holds a zone against random levels at the same
  distance.
- Whether IBKR rescales one-minute history after a split as it does daily bars is unverified;
  the split check covers the cache either way.
- Volume by price from one-minute bars spreads each bar's volume over its range; the prints
  would place it exactly.
- The zones' parameters (five-bar swings, 0.3 % or half the median range, two touches, three a
  side within 30 %) are picks, not measurements.
- Tested in Chromium with up to four sessions on a pane; a trader who drags back weeks loads
  thousands of bars a pane — not measured for speed.

Mistakes caught while building: at 0.3 % of price alone, no two swings on the synthetic gapper
ever joined (its five-minute bars move 1–2 %), so the reach became the larger of that and half
the median bar range; the history client's status, first added to the health block, pushed the
session event over its own size test and was taken back out; and the legend's labels carried the
colour-swatch class (a 10 × 2 px box) — they have a class of their own now.

## 7 · Verdict

**Feasible, and built — except running a Pine script, which only TradingView's own charts can
do.** The desk now keeps older candles (IBKR, a session at a time, on its own client), draws
support/resistance zones with their touches and volume density, the prior day's levels, the
whole and half dollars, and a TradingView-style volume profile, and looks like TradingView: a
visible grid, MACD in its own pane, dates across sessions, the values legend one click away.
**It shows where trading happened; it does not know in advance where a bounce will come** — that
stays untested. Paper only.

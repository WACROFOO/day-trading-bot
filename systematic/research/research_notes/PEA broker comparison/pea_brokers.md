# PEA broker comparison for a first PEA: €3,000 lump sum, then €500/month into one MSCI World ETF (as of 10 November 2026)

Costing example (illustrative order sizes, not anyone's actual plan): a PEA holding one MSCI World ETF, a €3,000 lump-sum order, then €500 monthly orders into one PEA-eligible MSCI World ETF: WPEA (iShares MSCI World Swap PEA, IE0002XZSHO1), DCAM (Amundi PEA MSCI World / "Amundi PEA Monde", FR001400U5Q4), or the older CW8 (Amundi MSCI World Swap, LU1681043599). Goal: lowest total cost, ideally with an automatic monthly plan.

How the sources are rated:
- **[OFFICIAL]**: read directly on the broker's own fee schedule, web page or contract, or on a government site, during this session (10 Nov 2026).
- **[3RD-PARTY]**: comparators, blogs or press. These need verifying on an official page.
- **[SNIPPET]**: taken from a search-result summary only; the page itself was not opened. Weakest.

Order costs below exclude the French financial transaction tax (TTF). It does not apply to ETF purchases anyway, and BoursoBank's brochure notes TTF at 0.4% on qualifying shares.

## 1. Regulatory frame: PEA fee caps, who can offer a PEA, and protection schemes

### Takeaway
Since 1 July 2020, Article D221-111-1 of the Code monétaire et financier caps PEA fees. The caps are: opening at most €10; custody and account keeping at most 0.4% a year of the securities' value plus €5 per line; online orders at most 0.5% of the order; transfers out at most €15 per line and €150 in total. The current Légifrance version, dated 15 July 2021, still shows these amounts. The brief's assumption that a PEA must be held at a French-established institution is not quite right. German, Danish, Polish and Irish institutions now offer PEAs through French branches or EU passporting, and they handle the French tax reporting. Those providers are not covered by France's deposit-guarantee fund (FGDR).

### Cited Findings
- Article D221-111-1 CMF has been in force since 15 July 2021, as amended by Décret n°2021-925 of 13 July 2021. It was created by Décret n°2020-95 of 5 February 2020, in force 1 July 2020. Its caps [OFFICIAL] — [Légifrance, art. D221-111-1 CMF, version in force read 10 Nov 2026](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000041543443):
  - Opening fees, "notamment les frais de dossier", at most **€10**.
  - Custody and account-keeping fees at most **0.4% a year of the value of the securities plus €5 per line**, or €25 per line for unlisted securities.
  - Transaction fees at most **0.5% of the amount for online orders** and **1.2% for orders placed by any other means**.
  - Transfer fees, including the closure fees of the plan being transferred, at most **€15 per line transferred**, with "L'ensemble des frais est plafonné à 150 euros".
  - The opening, custody and transfer amounts are revalued every three years in line with INSEE's consumer price index excluding tobacco. The version read on 10 Nov 2026 still shows €10, 0.4% + €5, and €15/€150.
- Secondary sources confirm the timeline: the decree was published in the Journal officiel on 7 Feb 2020, applied from 1 July 2020, and the 2021 decree extended the transaction caps to ETFs [SNIPPET] — [La finance pour tous](https://www.lafinancepourtous.com/2020/02/19/pea-les-frais-de-transaction-de-gestion-et-de-transfert-plafonnes/); [economie.gouv.fr](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/gouvernement-acheve-reforme-plans-epargne-actions-pea). One source gives €50 per line for transferring unlisted securities [SNIPPET].
- The brokers' own fee schedules apply these caps:
  - BoursoBank: "Les frais de courtage facturés aux clients sur les PEA... seront plafonnés à 0,5%"; PEA transfer out "15€ par ligne dans la limite de 150€ par compte transféré" [OFFICIAL] — [BoursoBank brochure tarifaire, 5 Oct 2026](https://s.brsimg.com/content/brochure_tarifaire/boursorama_bt.pdf).
  - Bourse Direct: transfer out €15 per line, "plafonné à 150 € pour les comptes de type PEA" [OFFICIAL] — [Bourse Direct conditions tarifaires, 6 Jan 2026](https://www.boursedirect.fr/pdf/tarifs_bd.pdf).
  - Fortuneo: transfer out "Dans la limite du maximum légal de 150 €" [OFFICIAL] — [Fortuneo conditions tarifaires, 6 Aug 2026](https://www.fortuneo.fr/files/tarifs_fortuneo.pdf).
- The legal deposit ceiling for a standard PEA is €150,000. A young adult still attached to their parents' tax household can deposit at most €20,000 [OFFICIAL] — [Trade Republic customer agreement, Appendix 12, v07.08.01, 9/2026](https://assets.traderepublic.com/assets/files/CA_FR-en-fr.pdf); [IBKR PEA page](https://www.interactivebrokers.ie/en/accounts/plan-depargne-en-action-accounts.php).
- Non-French institutions currently offer PEAs:
  - Trade Republic Bank GmbH, a German bank, offers its PEA under articles L221-30 to L221-32-7 CMF through its French branch [OFFICIAL] — [TR contract](https://assets.traderepublic.com/assets/files/CA_FR-en-fr.pdf).
  - Saxo Banque is the "Succursale française de Saxo Bank A/S., société anonyme de droit danois", supervised by ACPR and AMF [OFFICIAL] — [Saxo PEA press release](https://www.home.saxo/fr-fr/content/commentaries/pr/press-release/saxo-banque-devoile-son-pea-sans-frais-26022026).
  - XTB operates as "XTB S.A Succursale française" of the Polish XTB S.A. [OFFICIAL] — [XTB PEA page](https://www.xtb.com/fr/pea).
  - Interactive Brokers Ireland Limited offers PEAs and provides "A single tax declaration form (IFU)" [OFFICIAL] — [IBKR PEA page](https://www.interactivebrokers.ie/en/accounts/plan-depargne-en-action-accounts.php).
- FGDR, France's deposit-guarantee fund:
  - Securities held directly "ou dans le cadre d'un PEA" are covered up to **€70,000 per client per institution**.
  - Cash linked to a securities account is covered up to €100,000 when the account is held at a bank (it falls under the deposit guarantee), or up to €70,000 when held at an investment firm [SNIPPET] — [FGDR "I own securities"](https://www.garantiedesdepots.fr/en/discover-my-guarantees/i-own-securities); [FGDR explanatory brochure 2025](https://www.garantiedesdepots.fr/sites/default/files/2026-01/FGDR-Plaquette_Explicative-2025_FR%20finale.pdf).
  - The securities guarantee only triggers if the securities have disappeared from the accounts and the institution cannot return them [SNIPPET] — same sources.
- Trade Republic states it "est assigné à l'Entschädigungseinrichtung deutscher Banken GmbH" (EdB, the German scheme), not FGDR [OFFICIAL] — [TR PEA page](https://traderepublic.com/fr-fr/pea).
- IBKR Ireland is "a member of the Irish Investor Compensation Scheme (ICS)" [OFFICIAL] — [IBKR PEA page](https://www.interactivebrokers.ie/en/accounts/plan-depargne-en-action-accounts.php). A third party says this covers €20,000 per client [3RD-PARTY] — [pea.fr IBKR](https://pea.fr/courtiers/interactive-brokers/).
- XTB's cash is said to be covered by a Polish fund up to about €20,100 [3RD-PARTY] — [Seqooia XTB review](https://www.seqooia.com/post/avis-pea-xtb).

### Inferences
- For this use case, the 0.5% online-order cap is the one that matters. It limits BoursoBank's 0.60% Découverte rate to 0.5% on a €3,000 order (€15 instead of €18), and limits any €3 minimum fee on a €500 order to €2.50.
- Custody fees are €0 at every online broker reviewed. They remain a material cost only at high-street banks, which charge close to the 0.4% cap.
- If the user wants FGDR protection, keep in mind that securities in a PEA stay the client's property and are kept apart from the institution's own assets. Guarantee schemes matter mainly if records are lost or there is fraud. Still, only the French institutions (Bourse Direct, BoursoBank, Fortuneo and the high-street banks) fall under FGDR.

### Gaps
- I could not find whether the triennial CPI revaluation has ever been applied since 2020. The Légifrance text read today still shows the 2020 amounts, so it apparently has not been.
- I could not open the FGDR page itself, only search excerpts. The €70k and €100k split comes from FGDR documents cited in those excerpts.
- I did not open the Danish (Saxo) or Polish (XTB) guarantee-scheme texts.

## 2. Bourse Direct

### Takeaway
Bourse Direct is the best fit for this brief among French-established brokers. Since 11 May 2026 it has offered official automatic investment plans inside the PEA, with **€0 brokerage on a list that explicitly includes WPEA, DCAM and CW8**. A one-off €3,000 order costs **€3.80**. There are no custody, opening or closure fees.

  | €500–1,000 | €1.90 |
  | €1,000–2,000 | €2.90 |
  | €2,000–4,400 | €3.80 |

  - Free: custody (droits de garde) on Euronext, account keeping, opening and closure of a PEA or PEA Jeunes, and cancelled or unexecuted online orders.

- Launch of the plans, press release dated 11/05/2026 [OFFICIAL] — [Bourse Direct news, 11 May 2026](https://www.boursedirect.fr/fr/actualites/categorie/opcvm/bourse-direct-lance-ses-plans-d-investissement-programmes-sans-frais-sur-etf-a-partir-de-quelques-euros-disponibles-boursier-075a2bdef153cac0b0473ca31490ae45f9f1d212):

- Plans page [OFFICIAL] — [boursedirect.fr/fr/investissements-programmes](https://www.boursedirect.fr/fr/investissements-programmes):
  - The 0€ list has 27 ETFs and includes "AMUNDI PEA MONDE MSCI World UC FR001400U5Q4 (DCAM)", "Amundi MSCI World Swap UCITS E LU1681043599 (CW8)" and "iShares MSCI World Swap PEA UC IE0002XZSHO1 (WPEA)".
  - Available on PEA, PEA Jeunes, CTO, minors' CTO and company accounts.
  - Execution is on the **5th of each month**, or the next trading day.

- Entity: Bourse Direct SA, 374 rue Saint-Honoré, 75001 Paris, RCS Paris B 408 790 608, part of the VIEL & Cie group [OFFICIAL] — [tarifs_bd.pdf](https://www.boursedirect.fr/pdf/tarifs_bd.pdf).

### Inferences
- Cost for this use case: **€3.80** for the €3,000 lump sum as a manual order, then **€0** per €500 monthly plan execution. Doing the €500 manually would cost €0.99.
- The lump sum could probably also be done at €0. One option is to set the first plan instalment to €3,000 and then lower it to €500, which the official page says is allowed. The other is the Amundi refund promotion if DCAM is eligible (not verified).
- Plans appear to buy whole units only. Fractional shares are not mentioned, and the press release says plans start "à partir de la valeur d'une [part]". With WPEA or DCAM this barely matters. With CW8 it would leave a sizeable cash remainder each month.
- The fixed execution date (the 5th) means no control over timing. That is fine for dollar-cost averaging.
- Watch the €6 fee on cash transfers to another bank. It mainly affects withdrawals after year 5.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether plan orders are for a fixed euro amount rounded down to whole units, or a fixed number of units.
- I could not confirm Bourse Direct's regulatory category (investment firm or credit institution), which determines whether cash is covered up to €70k or €100k. It is French, so FGDR applies.
- The Amundi promotion terms are not in the official fee schedule I read.
- I found no reputable recent customer-service survey; only affiliate blogs.

## 5. Fortuneo

### Takeaway

### Cited Findings
- Fee schedule "Conditions tarifaires au 6 août 2026", Euronext [OFFICIAL] — [tarifs_fortuneo.pdf](https://www.fortuneo.fr/files/tarifs_fortuneo.pdf):

# Retail crypto trader outcomes, and practical constraints for a France-based retail crypto strategy (as of 25 Nov 2026)

Research date: 25 November 2026. Investor context: French tax resident, small account, spot only, automated through an exchange API. This is factual research, not tax advice. Items marked **[UNVERIFIED]** were not confirmed on an official or primary page. Items marked **[PRIMARY]** were checked directly against an official or regulator data file.

---

## 1. Population-level outcome statistics for retail crypto trading

### Takeaway
Every population-level measure found points the same way. Most retail crypto app users lost money on bitcoin over 2015–2022: about 73–81%, per BIS. Retail tends to buy after price rises and buy into crashes while large holders sell. Leveraged retail positions are wiped out in very large liquidation waves, about $150bn in 2025, including about $19bn on 10–11 Oct 2025. Copy-trading and prop-firm "challenge" data, though thin and mostly published by the industry itself, suggest only a single-digit to low-teens percentage of participants come out ahead. In France, about 9% of households hold crypto, and perceived risk fell sharply in 2025.

### Cited Findings
**BIS (Bank for International Settlements)**
- BIS Working Paper 1049, "Crypto trading and Bitcoin prices: evidence from a new database of retail adoption" (Auer, Cornelli, Doerr, Frost, Gambacorta; **14 Nov 2022**). It uses daily crypto-exchange app usage across **95 countries, 2015–22**. It estimates that **73–81% of retail investors likely lost money on their initial bitcoin investment** (the authors call this a back-of-the-envelope estimate). About **40% of new users are men under 35**. Rises in the bitcoin price are followed by new users downloading and using crypto apps. — [BIS WP 1049](https://www.bis.org/publ/work1049.htm)
- BIS Bulletin No. 69, "Crypto shocks and retail losses" (Cornelli, Doerr, Frost, Gambacorta; **20 Feb 2023**). It uses data on major crypto trading platforms from **Aug 2015 to Dec 2022**. In nearly all economies, a **majority of crypto app users made losses on their bitcoin holdings**. After the Terra/Luna collapse and the FTX bankruptcy, trading activity rose. **Large, sophisticated holders ("whales") sold while smaller retail investors bought.** — [BIS Bulletin 69](https://www.bis.org/publ/bisbull69.htm)
- Press reports on Bulletin 69 add some details. The method assumed each new user bought **$100 of BTC in the month they first downloaded the app**. Most downloads, or most buying volume, happened when BTC was above $20k, or above $30k; the outlets disagree on which. — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/121763/bitcoin-after-2015-lost-money-bis-report); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/amp/post/213372/majority-of-bitcoin-retail-investors-lost-money-in-the-last-seven-years-bis) **[UNVERIFIED against the PDF]**

**Academic studies of retail crypto trading**
- Kogan, Makarov, Niessner & Schoar, "Are Cryptos Different? Evidence from Retail Trading" (NBER WP 31317; *Journal of Financial Economics*, 2024). It covers about **200,000 eToro retail accounts, 2015–2019**. The same traders are **contrarian in stocks and gold but trade momentum in crypto**: they buy after price rises. The authors say fees, inattention and a taste for lottery-like assets do not explain this. The paper measures behaviour, not profitability. — [NBER w31317](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31317); [MIT Sloan CFI](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/cfi/forthcoming-crypto-trading-paper)
- Li, Shin & Wang, "Cryptocurrency Pump-and-Dump Schemes" (*JFQA*; the version found is dated Dec 2024). It uses **500 hand-collected pump-and-dumps organised on Telegram**. A typical pump **lasts only several minutes**, which leaves outsiders little time to take part. — [JFQA PDF](https://jfqa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/24639_Cryptocurrency-Pump-and-Dump.pdf); [CLS Blue Sky summary](https://clsbluesky.law.columbia.edu/2019/01/07/cryptocurrency-pump-and-dump-schemes/)
- Hamrick et al. counted **2,469 pump-and-dump events on Telegram over six months in 2018**. — [arXiv 1811.10109](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.10109) (figure taken from the search summary; the PDF was not read in full)

**Leverage and liquidations (CoinGlass data)**
- **10–11 Oct 2025:** more than **$19bn** of leveraged crypto positions were liquidated in 24 hours, affecting about **1.6 million traders**. CoinGlass called it "the largest liquidation event in crypto history" and said the true figure is probably higher because some venues report late. About **85–90% of the liquidated positions were longs**. The trigger was a US announcement of 100% tariffs on Chinese imports. — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/343941/bitcoin-ethereum-dogecoin-down-record-19-billion-crypto-liquidations); [CryptoSlate, 26 Dec 2025](https://cryptoslate.com/how-150-billion-was-liquidated-from-crypto-market-in-2025-driving-bitcoin-crash/)
- **Full-year 2025:** about **$150bn** of forced liquidations of futures and perpetual positions, per CoinGlass. Open interest peaked at about **$235.9bn on 7 Oct 2025**, fell by more than **$70bn** within days, and ended the year at about **$145.1bn**. BTC and ETH fell 10–15% during the 10 Oct window, while perpetual contracts on many small-cap tokens fell **50–80%**. — [CryptoSlate, 26 Dec 2025](https://cryptoslate.com/how-150-billion-was-liquidated-from-crypto-market-in-2025-driving-bitcoin-crash/)
- One secondary account says about 70% (about $6.93bn) of the 10 Oct liquidations happened in a 40-minute window (20:50–21:30 UTC). Forklog cited a possible true total of $30–40bn. — [Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/forced-liquidations-in-2025-surpass-150-billion/) **[UNVERIFIED; not a CoinGlass figure]**

**Comparable French evidence on leveraged retail trading (CFD/Forex, not crypto)**
- An AMF study (Oct 2014) of 2009–2012 found that **about 89% of retail CFD/Forex clients lost money**. The average loss was about **€10,900** and the median **€1,843**, with total losses of about **€175m**. The AMF found **no learning effect**: clients who persisted lost more. — [Finance Magnates on the AMF study](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/amf-publishes-four-year-fx-study-89-of-investors-experiencing-losses/); [MoneyVox](https://www.moneyvox.fr/bourse/actualites/47908/cfd-forex-10.000-euros-de-perte-moyenne-pour-les-boursicoteurs-francais); [AMF Lettre de l'Observatoire n°10](https://www.amf-france.org/sites/institutionnel/files/contenu_simple/lettre_ou_cahier/lettre_observatoire/La%20lettre%20de%20l%27Observatoire%20de%20l%27epargne%20de%20l%27AMF%20-%20ndeg%2010%20-%20Octobre%202014.pdf)
- A more recent AMF page puts the loss rate on Forex CFDs at **7 to 8 clients in 10**. — [AMF, "Forex, options binaires : trading haut risque"](https://www.amf-france.org/fr/espace-epargnants/proteger-son-epargne/forex-options-binaires-un-marche-fuir/forex-options-binaires-trading-haut-risque) (figure taken from the search summary)

**Copy trading and social trading**
- **IOSCO final report on copy, mirror and social trading (May 2025):** these services can expose retail investors to significant losses, including through leveraged products, and to fee erosion from frequent trading. — [IOSCO PD793](https://www.iosco.org/library/pubdocs/pdf/IOSCOPD793.pdf)
- **Kawai et al., CHI 2024 (Carnegie Mellon):** the interface design of crypto copy-trading platforms strongly shapes copiers' decisions, and leaderboard rankings are easy to game. — [Kawai CHI24](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/nicolasc/publications/Kawai-CHI24.pdf)
- **University of Florida study (social trading platform):** traders whose follower counts changed went on to perform worse, which the authors attribute to overconfidence and social pressure. — [UF Experts](https://experts.ufl.edu/experts/spotlight/?id=11271)
- **Industry figures (weak evidence):** DWF Ventures found only **about 6% of 292,000 wallets** on the "Fomo" copy-trading app were profitable over three months. — [Yellow.com](https://yellow.com/news/fomo-copy-trading-94-percent-wallet-losses). A KuCoin blog post claims **43.6% of followers** were profitable across Binance, Bybit and MEXC over 90 days, against 97% of lead traders on their own trades. — [KuCoin blog](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/ua-is-crypto-copytrading-profitable-in-2026) **[exchange marketing; underlying study not found]**

**Prop-firm "challenges" (including crypto-paying firms)**
- Velotrade's 2026 transparency report estimates **only about 7% of challenge buyers ever receive a payout** and about 14% ever pass. — [CoinGape](https://coingape.com/blog/93-of-funded-prop-traders-never-see-a-payout-a-new-report-explains-why/)
- The Funded Trader reported a **5–10% pass rate**, of whom about **20% reached a payout**. Finance Magnates estimated that **1–2% of all its clients** make money. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/prop-firm-the-funded-trader-paid-traders-386k-in-march-could-have-earned-3x-more/)
- HyroTrader, a crypto prop firm, publishes a live dashboard. In **Sep 2026** it showed **13.49%** of one-step and **8.70%** of two-step challenges ending in a pass. — [HyroTrader](https://www.hyrotrader.com/blog/prop-firm-pass-rates/) (published by the firm itself)

**French investor surveys (AMF)**
- AMF Barometer, 9th edition (Audirep, 19 Sep–3 Oct 2025, n=2,120; published in the Lettre de l'Observatoire de l'épargne n°64, **Dec 2025**):
  - **9%** of French respondents hold crypto-assets at household level.
  - Only **48%** saw crypto risk as high, down from **66% in 2024**. The figure is 60% among holders (73% in 2024) and 57% among 25–34-year-olds.
  - **63%** of holders think crypto's potential is high, but only 33% of French people (51% of holders) think crypto suits long-term investing.
  - **51%** of crypto holders make investment decisions alone.
  - **35%** of crypto investors use AI to research investments.
  - **16%** of all respondents say they have been victims of an investment scam (27% of investors; 32% of under-35s).
  - **70%** of crypto holders know that investment scams exist, but only 59% of those who started within the last year do.
  — [AMF Lettre n°64 (PDF)](https://www.amf-france.org/sites/institutionnel/files/private/2025-12/lettre-de-lobservatoire-de-lepargne-n-64.pdf) **[PRIMARY]**
- The AMF has relayed the ESMA warning that crypto-assets are not covered by investor guarantee schemes. — [AMF](https://www.amf-france.org/fr/actualites-publications/actualites/crypto-actifs-lamf-appuie-lappel-la-prudence-lancee-par-lesma-destination-des-investisseurs)

### Inferences
- The BIS loss estimates mostly describe buy-and-hold users who entered near local tops. They are not studies of active traders. Even so, the momentum and late-entry behaviour they document (BIS; Kogan et al.) is exactly the pattern a systematic strategy must avoid copying by accident.
- The liquidation data is the clearest evidence that leverage dominates retail losses in crypto. A spot-only, unleveraged strategy removes the single largest documented failure channel.
- No study was found that measures the net returns of *automated retail spot strategies* specifically. The closest evidence, from CFD/Forex studies and prop-firm statistics, shows that most active retail traders lose money after costs, and that the share of winners shrinks the more often people trade.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed paper found that measures the realised P&L distribution of retail *spot* crypto traders, net of fees, at account level.
- No independent academic study found that measures crypto copy-trading follower returns. Only industry figures exist.
- The full BIS Bulletin 69 PDF (exact loss percentages and median loss) was not parsed. Only the summary page and WP 1049 were read.
- No AMF/ACPR survey found that reports *losses* of French crypto investors. The AMF Barometer covers only holding rates, perceptions and scams.

## 2. Common failure modes (fees, leverage, scams, exchange failures, hacks, stablecoin depegs, tax mistakes)

### Takeaway
The documented ways retail crypto money is lost are:
- leverage and liquidations (about $150bn liquidated in 2025);
- scams (at least $14–17bn received by scammers in 2025);
- hacks (about $3.4bn in 2025, $1.5bn of it from Bybit alone);
- custodial failures such as FTX, Celsius and Terra (tens of billions in 2022);
- stablecoin depegs (UST collapsed; USDC traded near $0.87–0.88 in March 2023);
- high retail fees.

For a French investor, the rules on tax and declarations themselves create expensive traps, covered in section 5.

### Cited Findings
- **Scams 2025:** scammers received **at least $14bn**, which could exceed **$17bn** as more addresses are identified. AI-enabled scam operations earned **4.5 times** more revenue than traditional ones. Total illicit flows reached **at least $154bn** (Chainalysis 2026 Crypto Crime Report). — [Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/crypto-scammers-stole-a-record-14bn-in-2025/); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/384753/crypto-crime-150-billion-usd-2025-state-actors-scale-onchain-chainalysis)
- **Hacks 2025:** about **$3.4bn** stolen. The **$1.5bn Bybit exchange hack** (Feb 2025) was about **44%** of the total. North Korea-linked hackers stole about **$2bn**. Attacks on individual wallets increased. — [The Block (Chainalysis)](https://www.theblock.co/post/382477/crypto-hack-2025-chainalysis)
- **2022 custodial and protocol failures:** Chainalysis estimated realised investor losses of **$20.5bn from Terra**, about **$33bn from the Celsius and Three Arrows Capital collapses**, and about **$9bn from FTX**. — [The Block, 15 Dec 2022](https://www.theblock.co/post/195244/crypto-markets-terra-celsius-3ac-ftx). The FTX customer-funds shortfall was reported at about **$8bn**. — [CoinJournal](https://coinjournal.net/research-center/ftx-bankruptcy-second-biggest-loss-funds-history-of-crypto). The Chicago Fed estimated Celsius's pre-run customer liabilities at **$7.46bn**, with about **20% outflows in 11 days** during the run. — [Chicago Fed Letter 479](https://www.chicagofed.org/publications/chicago-fed-letter/2023/479)
- **USDC depeg (10–11 Mar 2023):** Circle disclosed that **$3.3bn** of its reserves (out of about $40bn) were held at the failed Silicon Valley Bank. USDC traded as low as **about $0.877** (Cointelegraph) or **$0.88** (The Block). It recovered after US authorities intervened to protect SVB depositors. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/usdc-depegs-as-circle-confirms-3-3b-stuck-with-silicon-valley-bank); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/218993/usdc-and-dai-remain-at-about-0-90-following-circles-disclosure-of-funds-at-svb); [Federal Reserve FEDS Note, 17 Dec 2025](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/in-the-shadow-of-bank-run-lessons-from-the-silicon-valley-bank-failure-and-its-impact-on-stablecoins-20251217.html)
- **Pump-and-dumps:** organised on Telegram, they typically last minutes. Hamrick et al. counted 2,469 over six months in 2018. — [JFQA](https://jfqa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/24639_Cryptocurrency-Pump-and-Dump.pdf); [arXiv 1811.10109](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.10109)
- **Retail fee drag on "simple" interfaces:** Kraken's app charges **1% on instant and recurring buys** (1.5% on custom orders) plus a built-in spread, and a **3% fee on small-balance conversions**. — [Kraken fee schedule](https://www.kraken.com/features/fee-schedule). Bitpanda's standard app is described as costing **about 1.49% all-in** (spread-based). — [Financer review](https://www.financer.com/review/bitpanda) **[UNVERIFIED]**
- **Exchange closures caused by regulation (France 2026):** Binance France obtained no MiCA licence. From 1 July 2026, spot orders were cancelled, and open loans and margin positions faced **automatic liquidation (deadline 1 Oct 2026)**. Withdrawals remained possible. — [Journal du Coin, 30 Sep 2026](https://journalducoin.com/economie/crypto-en-france-trois-mois-apres-la-fin-des-psan-ce-que-mica-a-change-pour-vous/)
- **Impersonation:** the AMF updated its crypto blacklist at the end of Sep 2026 and warned that scammers impersonate the AMF, ESMA and real platforms. Unauthorised providers risk 2 years' prison and a €30,000 fine. — [Journal du Coin, 30 Sep 2026](https://journalducoin.com/economie/crypto-en-france-trois-mois-apres-la-fin-des-psan-ce-que-mica-a-change-pour-vous/)
- **Tax mistakes:**
  - Crypto losses can only offset **same-year** crypto gains. They **cannot be carried forward** or set against other gains. — [impots.gouv.fr](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/questions/comment-declarer-les-plus-ou-moins-values-sur-cessions-dactifs-numeriques)
  - Each undeclared foreign account carries a **€750** fine, plus **€125** per omission or inaccuracy. — [BOFiP BOI-RPPM-PVBMC-30-30](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/11969-PGP.html)
  - The 2026 "loi fraudes" amended the **80% surcharge** (CGI art. 1729-0 A) on reassessments tied to undeclared foreign wallets. — [CMS, 10 Jun 2026](https://cms.law/en/fra/legal-updates/loi-fraudes-plusieurs-mesures-concernent-les-crypto-actifs)

### Inferences
- For a small, spot-only, automated strategy on a MiCA-licensed venue, the failure modes that remain are: (1) fee and spread drag; (2) the venue failing or being hacked, which MiCA reduces through segregation and safeguarding rules but does not remove; (3) stablecoin depegs if idle cash is parked in USDC or EURC; (4) API-key compromise; (5) tax and declaration errors.
- Parking in a stablecoin between trades swaps market risk for issuer and reserve risk. The USDC 2023 episode shows a regulated, reserve-backed coin can still trade about 12% below par for a short time.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2025–2026 figure found for losses specific to French investors from scams or failures.
- Celsius's final customer recovery percentage and FTX distribution outcomes were not researched (out of scope).

## 3. MiCA in practice (Nov 2026): venues authorised for French residents, end of the French transition, stablecoin rules, derivatives access

### Takeaway
The French grandfathering period for PSANs (the former French registration regime for crypto providers) ended on **1 July 2026**. Only MiCA-authorised CASPs (crypto-asset service providers), French or passported in, can now serve French residents. As of ESMA's register updated to 18 Nov 2026, **Kraken (Ireland), Coinbase (Luxembourg), Bitstamp (Luxembourg), Bitpanda (Austria), Crypto.com and OKX (Malta), Bybit (Austria), Bitvavo (Netherlands), eToro and Revolut (Cyprus), Gemini (Malta) and others** are authorised and passported into France. **Binance is not on the register** and has wound down French services. USDT has been delisted from trading on EU CASPs. **USDC and EURC are MiCA e-money tokens issued by Circle's French entity**. Crypto perpetuals for EU retail are treated by ESMA as CFDs, which brings leverage caps (2:1 on crypto, per secondary sources), and almost no provider offers them to French retail.

**Derivatives and perpetuals for EU retail**
- In a statement of **24 Feb 2026**, ESMA said perpetual futures are "almost certainly" CFDs under MiFID II, and that the product name is "irrelevant". The CFD product-intervention measures therefore apply: leverage caps, margin close-out, negative-balance protection and risk warnings. A PRIIPs KID is required, and mass marketing to retail is inconsistent with the product's target market. — [Finance Magnates, 24 Feb 2026](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/regulation/esma-tells-firms-perpetual-futures-fall-under-eu-cfd-rules/)
- EU retail clients face **2:1 leverage on crypto-underlying CFDs**. — [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/crypto-traders-are-chasing-10x-leverage-in-the-us-while-europe-tightens-the-screws-behind-the-scenes/); see also [Finance Magnates, "10x Down to 2x"](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/10x-down-to-2x-has-europe-killed-crypto-perps-even-before-it-started/) (title only; not read)
- One Trading (Netherlands, AFM, MiFID II OTF licence) offered BTC/ETH-EUR perpetuals to eligible retail in **Germany, the Netherlands and Austria** with up to 10x leverage (12 May 2025, before the ESMA statement). **France was not included.** — [Finance Magnates, 12 May 2025](https://www.financemagnates.com/cryptocurrency/european-retail-gains-regulated-access-to-leveraged-crypto-trading/)
- Binance French margin positions were force-closed by 1 Oct 2026. — [Journal du Coin](https://journalducoin.com/economie/crypto-en-france-trois-mois-apres-la-fin-des-psan-ce-que-mica-a-change-pour-vous/)

### Inferences

## 5. French tax on crypto in 2026 (rate, formula, exemptions, swaps and stablecoins, occasional vs habitual trading, declarations, staking, ETNs)

### Takeaway
Gains realised by individuals managing their own wealth are taxed under art. 150 VH bis CGI at a **PFU (flat tax) of 31.4%**: 12.8% income tax plus **18.6% social levies**, after the CSG rose to 10.6% under LFSS 2026. This is confirmed on impots.gouv.fr (page updated 17 Jul 2026), and taxpayers can opt for the progressive income-tax scale instead.
- Gains are computed with the **global-portfolio formula**.
- **Crypto-to-crypto swaps without a cash adjustment (soulte), including swaps into USDC or EURC, remain tax-deferred** at the date of research.
- Attempts in the 2027 budget bill to tax swaps into MiCA e-money tokens from 1 Jan 2027 have **so far failed**: an Assembly amendment lapsed on 4 Nov 2026 and the Senate rejected one on 24 Nov 2026. The bill is still in progress until about mid-December.
- The **€305 exemption** is measured on total sale proceeds, not gains.
- Frequent, sophisticated trading can be reclassified as **BNC** (non-commercial profits).
- Staking and mining income is BNC according to impots.gouv.fr.
- Foreign accounts must be declared on **3916-bis**, at **€750 per account** if not.
- Crypto ETNs held in a securities account (CTO) are taxed as **securities**, at the same 31.4% PFU, not under 150 VH bis.

### Cited Findings
**Rate**
- impots.gouv.fr: "sont imposées au prélèvement forfaitaire unique (PFU) de **31,4 % (12,8 % d'impôt + 18,6 % de prélèvements sociaux)**". Taxpayers can opt for the progressive scale by ticking box **3CN**. That choice covers all crypto gains of the household and is separate from the option for investment income. Page published 16/06/2023, **last modified 17/07/2026**. — [impots.gouv.fr](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/questions/comment-declarer-les-plus-ou-moins-values-sur-cessions-dactifs-numeriques) **[PRIMARY]**
- Article 12 of LFSS 2026 raised the standard CSG rate on investment and capital income from **9.2% to 10.6%**, lifting total social levies from 17.2% to 18.6%. — [Actu-Juridique](https://www.actu-juridique.fr/fiscalite/fiscal-finances/la-csg-en-hausse-sur-les-revenus-du-capital/); [Mayer Brown, 27 Jan 2026](https://www.mayerbrown.com/fr/insights/publications/2026/01/budget-social-2026-augmentation-du-taux-de-csg-et-perennisation-du-regime-social-management-package)
- **Retroactivity:** for crypto gains, as a capital-income category, the higher CSG was expected to apply to **gains realised in 2025** and declared in spring 2026. — [MoneyVox, 6 Apr 2026](https://www.moneyvox.fr/impot/actualites/108257/impots-2026-une-hausse-de-la-csg-retroactive-qui-va-alourdir-la-revente-de-vos-cryptos); [Banque Populaire](https://www.banquepopulaire.fr/conseils/mesures-fiscales-sociales-2026/). The official impots.gouv page now shows 31.4% without a year qualifier.

**Global-portfolio formula (art. 150 VH bis)**
- **Gross gain = sale price − [total acquisition price × sale price / global portfolio value at the time of sale]**. Fees are deducted from the sale price only.
- A cash adjustment received or paid in a swap is added to or deducted from the sale price.
- After each taxable sale, the total acquisition price is reduced by the "fraction of initial capital" already recovered. Previous swaps made without a cash adjustment do not change the total acquisition price.
- The net household total is carried to box **3AN** (gain) or **3BN** (loss) of form 2042 C.
- Source: notice 2086 (revenus 2024). — [Notice 2086](https://static.impots.gouv.fr/tdir/static/2026-03-30T16:36:08Z/aide/notices/notices2024/2086/notice2086.pdf) **[PRIMARY]**; 2026 form (gains realised in 2025): [Form 2086 (2026)](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/formulaires/2086/2026/2086_5515.pdf)

**€305 exemption and losses**
- Gains are exempt if the household's **total gross sale proceeds** for the year, excluding tax-deferred swaps, do not exceed **€305**. The test is on sale prices, not gains. — [impots.gouv.fr](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/questions/comment-declarer-les-plus-ou-moins-values-sur-cessions-dactifs-numeriques); [Notice 2086](https://static.impots.gouv.fr/tdir/static/2026-03-30T16:36:08Z/aide/notices/notices2024/2086/notice2086.pdf) **[PRIMARY]**
- Losses offset only same-year gains of the same kind. They **cannot be carried forward** and cannot offset other gains. — [impots.gouv.fr](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/questions/comment-declarer-les-plus-ou-moins-values-sur-cessions-dactifs-numeriques) **[PRIMARY]**

**Crypto-to-crypto and crypto-to-stablecoin swaps**
- impots.gouv.fr: "les opérations d'échange sans soulte d'actifs numériques bénéficient d'un **sursis d'imposition**". — [impots.gouv.fr](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/questions/comment-declarer-les-plus-ou-moins-values-sur-cessions-dactifs-numeriques) **[PRIMARY]**
- **Scope change from 2026 (loi fraudes):** for disposals from **1 Jan 2026**, art. 150 VH bis covers **MiCA-regulated (fungible) crypto-assets** (Reg. (EU) 2023/1114). A new **art. 150 VH ter** taxes unique, non-fungible crypto-assets (NFTs) under the regime of the underlying asset. The law was adopted by the Assembly on 5 May and the Senate on 11 May 2026, and appears to be loi n° 2026-534 of 25 June 2026 (Légifrance). — [CMS, 10 Jun 2026](https://cms.law/en/fra/legal-updates/loi-fraudes-plusieurs-mesures-concernent-les-crypto-actifs); [Légifrance, loi n° 2026-534](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/article_jo/JORFARTI000054310089)
- BOFiP shows the previous 150 VH bis commentary valid until 27/06/2026, consistent with the law's publication. — [BOFiP ACTU-2023-00099](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/13985-PGP.html/ACTU-2023-00099)
- **Stablecoin and e-money-token swaps today:** swaps into USDC or EURC (e-money tokens) are treated as tax-deferred crypto-to-crypto swaps under current law. — [Kohen Avocats, 18 Oct 2026](https://kohenavocats.fr/2026/10/18/imposition-crypto-stablecoin-assemblee-vote-taxation-conversions-2027/); [Fiducia Patrimoine](https://fiduciapatrimoine.fr/actualite/articles/crypto-actifs-les-deputes-votent-la-taxation-du-passage-aux-stablecoins)
- **2027 budget bill (PLF 2027):**
  - Assembly amendment **I-3898** (N. Sansu, GDR) was adopted on **16 Oct 2026** by **38 votes to 37** (25 abstentions, per one source). It would remove the deferral for swaps into **MiCA e-money tokens** (single-currency stablecoins such as USDC and EURC) from **1 Jan 2027**. Swaps into gold-backed or basket-backed tokens (asset-referenced tokens) would stay deferred.
  - Per one law firm, the amendment would also replace the global-portfolio average with a per-asset calculation, with transitional rules.
  - **The Assembly rejected Part I of the PLF on 4 Nov 2026, so the amendment lapsed.**
  - The **Senate rejected the equivalent amendment I-2421** (Savoldelli/Barros) on **24 Nov 2026**. The Finance Minister and the rapporteure were opposed. The Senate also rejected the self-hosted-wallet declaration and exit-tax amendments.
  - Next steps: Senate debate on Part I on 28–29 Nov, final Senate vote **9 Dec**, then a joint committee (CMP). The constitutional deadline is about **17 Dec 2026**, and article 49.3 or ordinances remain possible.
  — [Assemblée nationale, amendment I-3898](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/amendements/3210A/AN/3898); [NosParlementaires](https://nosparlementaires.fr/actualites/impot-crypto-stablecoins-conversion-taxee-budget-2027); [Kohen Avocats, 22 Oct 2026](https://kohenavocats.com/crypto-stablecoin-echange-imposable-vote-16-octobre-2026-texte-reel/); [Journal du Coin, 25 Nov 2026](https://journalducoin.com/analyses/crypto-le-senat-rejette-les-amendements-crypto-du-plf-2027/)
- Because the measure relied on the MiCA EMT definition, **USDT (not an authorised EMT) would have stayed outside it**. — [search summary of PLF 2027 coverage](https://cryptoast.fr/plf-2027/) **[UNVERIFIED]**

**Occasional vs habitual (professional-like) trading**
- Trading carried out as a commercial activity is taxed as **BIC**.
- Since 1 Jan 2023, art. 92 CGI (1° bis), added by art. 70 of the 2022 finance law, taxes as **BNC** the gains from buying, selling and swapping crypto "dans des conditions analogues à celles qui caractérisent une activité professionnelle".
- BOFiP says this regime applies only in **exceptional, case-by-case** situations. By analogy it cites the Conseil d'État (21 Jun 2018, n° 412124, on poker), which allowed BNC treatment where a player "significantly masters" randomness through skill and earns significant income.
- Related BOFiP references: BOI-BNC-CHAMP-10-10-20-40.
— [BOFiP ACTU-2023-00099](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/13985-PGP.html/ACTU-2023-00099) **[PRIMARY]**
- impots.gouv.fr gives as an example "les investisseurs réalisant des **opérations nombreuses et sophistiquées** sur cryptomonnaies", using the same tools as professional traders. Under BNC there is a choice between the **micro-BNC 34% allowance** and deduction of actual expenses. — [impots.gouv.fr](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/questions/comment-declarer-les-plus-ou-moins-values-sur-cessions-dactifs-numeriques) **[PRIMARY]**

**Crypto ETPs/ETNs held in a CTO**
- An ETN is a debt security, so gains fall under the **securities capital-gains regime**: PFU by default (31.4% after the CSG rise) or the progressive scale by option. ETNs are **not eligible for the PEA**. The broker's IFU (annual tax statement) summarises gains and losses. — [Boursobank help page](https://www.boursobank.com/aide-en-ligne/bourse/etn-crypto-actifs/question/quelle-fiscalite-est-applicable-aux-etn-crypto-actifs-50316192); [Épargnant 3.0](https://www.epargnant30.fr/etf-etn-crypto-bitcoin-ether/); [Calcunet](https://calcunet.fr/articles/pea-crypto-etf-bitcoin-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Practical consequences for an automated spot strategy**:
  - Every sale to EUR is a taxable event computed with the portfolio-wide formula, and the formula needs the **global portfolio value at the moment of each sale**, across all wallets and exchanges. An automated bot must log total portfolio valuations at each EUR exit, not just per-trade P&L.
  - Rotating between risk assets and **USDC/EURC instead of EUR** keeps all swaps tax-deferred under current law. The cost is stablecoin issuer risk and exposure to a future change: the PLF 2027 measure failed for now but could return in later budgets.
  - The €305 threshold is irrelevant for any active strategy because it is tested on gross proceeds.
  - Losses cannot be carried forward, so a losing year wastes its losses for tax purposes.
- **BNC reclassification risk** rises with high frequency, professional-grade tooling and large gains. Automated API trading is the kind of "sophisticated" means impots.gouv mentions. A low-frequency strategy is easier to defend as private wealth management, but the test is case-by-case with no numeric threshold.
- **Foreign accounts:** Kraken (Ireland), Coinbase, Bitstamp (Luxembourg), Bitpanda (Austria) and Bitvavo (Netherlands) are all foreign entities. Each account needs a 3916-bis every year, including the year it is closed. Missing one costs at least €750.
- **ETN alternative:** ETNs give simpler tax reporting (broker IFU) and the same 31.4% PFU. For listed securities, losses can normally offset other securities gains and be carried forward 10 years (standard securities rule; not verified in this session). That is a structural advantage over direct crypto, offset by trading only during exchange hours and issuer/structure risk.

### Gaps
- I did not read the consolidated Légifrance text of art. 150 VH bis as in force since 1 Jul 2026 (post-loi fraudes), so the exact definition of "crypto-actifs" now covered and whether it clearly includes EMTs was not confirmed. Current official guidance (impots.gouv) still describes swaps as deferred without excluding stablecoins.
- The final outcome of PLF 2027 (CMP and final adoption around 17 Dec 2026) was unknown at the research date. **Re-check after mid-December 2026.**
- No official BOFiP guidance found on the **timing and valuation of staking rewards**: income at receipt as BNC, and the cost basis for later disposal under 150 VH bis, were not detailed.
- The €1,500 penalty and the exact €50K test date were not confirmed on Légifrance.
- No official numeric threshold exists for the occasional-vs-habitual distinction; all criteria are qualitative.

### Inferences
- For a buy-and-hold or low-frequency allocation (for example monthly trend or rebalancing), a 0.10–0.15% ETN bought through a low-commission broker is likely cheaper and far simpler for tax than spot on Kraken tier 1: one entry costs about 0.05% commission versus 0.4–0.8% on the exchange. ETNs fall short for 24/7 or intraday systematic trading and for altcoins.
- ETNs are debt securities. Even when physically backed, the holder relies on the issuer's structure, trustee and custodian. This is a different risk from holding coins on a MiCA CASP.
